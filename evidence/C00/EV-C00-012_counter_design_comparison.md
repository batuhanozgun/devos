# EV-C00-012 · Comparison of the counter-design with the plan (W-C00-09, plan C00 step 5)

## 1. Header

**What is compared.** The counter-design `evidence/C00/EV-C00-010_counter_design.md` ("CD" below; cited by section and as `EV-010:<line>`) against the plan package's design of DevOS: `plan/DevOS_Kurulum_Plani.md` (`plan:<line>`), Appendix A (`Ek A:<line>`), Appendix B (`Ek B:<line>`), Appendix C (`Ek C:<line>`), Appendix D section 2 (`Ek D:<line>`) and Appendix G (`Ek G:<line>`). All files were read at commit `6598f32491aee190f21e1d9362a0002b0ae6f193` (branch `claude/c00-w09-counter-design`), where the counter-design was added; the plan files are the binding English text of plan 2.1 with PC-01 to PC-06. What the counter-designer was shown is `evidence/C00/EV-C00-009_counter_design_brief.md` (the "brief").

**Scope.** The brief asked for DevOS's way of working and data-model scope for stage B (DevOS in operation), not for the installation (brief section 6). So the plan's stage-B design (plan sections 1–8, 10, 13 and the appendices) is compared; the stages C01–C12 appear only as the place where a disposition would be decided. Appendix E, `plan/decisions/`, the ledger and other evidence were not read (outside this comparison's sources); where Appendix E might already settle a point, the row says so.

**Independence of the counter-design: declared only, low.** Its header (EV-010:5) and first line (EV-010:9) state why: the tool list (Write only) did not ensure plan-blindness; the repository's `CLAUDE.md`, loaded into every subagent, named installation-period parts of the plan's design (the guard hook and its report tool, `tools/sync_worktree.sh`, role files in `.claude/agents/`, Accept edits, the connector block, Batu's answer channel on issue #6 in the Appendix E format); MCP server instructions appeared after its first write. It marks matching points `[overlap]`. **Agreement on an `[overlap]` point is weak evidence.** Two further limits apply to every agreement: (a) the brief carried Batu's principles, the 34 criteria and his decisions, so agreement on what those force is not independent design evidence (marked "brief-forced" below); (b) the brief told the counter-designer that the comparison would question record kinds and roles against "start simple" (EV-009:9), a cue that may have pushed it toward a small scope. Only agreements marked "convergence" are design choices neither the brief nor `CLAUDE.md` dictated.

**Who wrote this comparison.** A Producer subagent of the executor's session, which read the plan; it is not blind and its own independence is not claimed. Dispositions are proposals; the executor makes the final disposition (plan-change candidates are decided at W-C00-10 through plan section 14, plan:1115-1119).

**Size at a glance (sizes alone decide nothing; principle 2, plan:40, asks for the simpler solution at the same quality).** CD: 3 routines; 5 standing roles plus 1 on-demand role; 4 record kinds plus 1 derived index; 1 hook script; 5 Actions workflows (EV-010:52). Plan: 3 environments plus the installation environment (plan:592-597); 18 role contracts, starting set of 9 (plan:754); 27 record-family sections naming about 45 record types (Ek B:50-300); 18 function groups (Ek B:306-325); 8 database roles (Ek B:33-42); 10 scheduled jobs (Ek B:331-342).
