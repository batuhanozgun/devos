**F1. Criterion 29 heading uses a different rendering of a recurring term.** Type (h). Non-blocking.
- **Where:** English line 181 (Turkish line 179), compared with English line 646 (Turkish line 644, outside my range).
- **Text:** the Turkish "**Dış kaynak birikimi zorunludur:**" is rendered "**Drawing on the outside body of knowledge is mandatory:**". At line 646 the same term "Dış kaynak birikimi" (the library's `research/studies` category) is rendered "Accumulation of external sources".
- **Why it matters:** the obligation ("mandatory") and the body text ("known solutions and counterexamples in other fields are researched") are rendered faithfully, so DevOS is bound to the same thing. However, the two renderings hide that criterion 29 and the library category name the same concept, and "Drawing on" is an interpretive verb the Turkish heading does not have.
- **Suggested fix:** use one rendering of "dış kaynak birikimi" in both places. For example, "**The accumulated body of external sources is mandatory:**" at line 181, or keep "Drawing on …" and change line 646 to match it. Record the choice as a translation choice.
