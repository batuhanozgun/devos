- What this means for the plan: the database rule is what C10 tests and what C07 measure 6 relies on. Under the "or" rule, one counter-example alone can confirm a gap.

**F7. K-4 carries the Academy generalisation exam only in part (fidelity defect; blocking → condition 4).**
- Sources: Academy §6 (the eval artefact class) measures six behaviours. Academy §10 question 3 and §15 list the causes a system review should check, including authority and time or budget. §25 names the risks of inflating capabilities and processes.
- What the plan does:
  - K-4 Testing lists four of the six behaviours. It drops "uses outside-field prior art" and "does not add needless mechanisms" without a reason.
  - Dropping the second makes the exam one-sided: it rewards raising every error to a class rule. K-1 Testing itself rejects one-sided measurement.
  - K-4 item 3 leaves out authority and time or budget as causes. DevOS uses both on purpose: effort reductions (6.10), loop budgets (K-7 item 7) and guard denials. A failure they cause would be blamed on something else.
  - C05 claims criterion 28 but has no acceptance item that tests generalisation, so K-4's exam is not tied to any stage.
- Minor point, not blocking: Appendix A 3.2 item 4 calls the Academy's role-level examples "failure classes" (§3), although most of them are capability gaps. Criterion 28 depends on keeping the two apart; the same edit can fix this.
