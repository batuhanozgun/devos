1. **Leak check: "gönderim" narrowed to "push" (types c and e/scope). Near-blocking; this is the condition above.**
   - Where: EN line 667, TR line 665.
   - TR: "uzun eşleşme gönderimi durdurur; (2) ... eşiğin üstündeki yakınlık gönderimi incelemeye düşürür. Kontrol dala gönderim, PR gövdesi, yorum ve issue yazımını kapsar."
   - EN: "a long match stops the push; (2) ... similarity above the threshold sends the push to review. The check covers pushing to a branch, PR bodies, comments and writing issues."
   - The Turkish uses the general word "gönderim" (sending, submission) for what is stopped. It uses "dala gönderim" (push to a branch) only for one of the four covered writes. The English uses "push" for both. Read next to "pushing to a branch", the enforcement action in (1) and (2) reads as applying to the git push only. The other covered writes (PR body, comment, issue) then have no stated consequence.
   - Why it matters: Section 6.7 binds the C03 leak-check build. The C03 acceptance (EN line 880, TR line 878) requires stopping only "in a push to a branch, in a PR body and in a comment". That leaves issue writing bound by 6.7 alone, and the English does not clearly bind DevOS to stop it.
   - Fix (in words): render "gönderim" in items (1) and (2) with a general word ("write" or "submission"). Keep "pushing to a branch" for "dala gönderim".
