Suggested fix: write `devos_api.read_source(source, revision, span)` to match Ek B line 316. Not blocking, because the English binds DevOS to nothing the Turkish does not say.

**F4. Uneven obligation strength in the K-1 Testing parentheses (type c, not blocking).** English line 244 (source line 242): the source has "(1) … (bulunmalı), (2) … (gereksiz hazırlık üretilmemeli)", the English has "(it must be found)" and "(no unnecessary preparation should be produced)". Both Turkish forms use the same modality (-malı/-meli). The English renders the first as "must" and the second as "should", which makes the negative test criterion read weaker than the positive one. The next sentence of the plan says the two are measured together, so that a system that adds prerequisites to everything does not look successful. Suggested fix: "(no unnecessary preparation must be produced)" or "(it must produce no unnecessary preparation)". Not blocking, because Turkish -meli can carry "should". The asymmetry is the defect, and the sentence that follows keeps the intent.
