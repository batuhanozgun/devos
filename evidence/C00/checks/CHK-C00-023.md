### F3: Format gates layered on format gates: the 6.12 squeeze pattern applied to cognitive quality (blocking as part of C2)

- **Where:** B:26, P:771, P:234, P:272, P:693, P:701, P:710, B:294, U-2 at P:1006.
- **What:** The plan says honestly that a format gate proves only that a field was filled. Its answer to that limit (the verifier bottleneck, U-2) is to add layers: a format gate, then a "filled but meaningless" test, then sample review, then content review in the audit session, then weekly silent-failure sampling. For frame blindness itself it adds a DB trigger that fires when two proposals share a `failure_class`, which depends on agents labelling that field consistently, so it is another format field. A model asked to fill `why_needed`, `premises` or `expert_assessment_ref` will fill them.
- **Affects:** principles 2, 10 and 11.
- **Why it matters:** The records per work item and the sampling load in an audit environment that runs three times a day both grow. By the plan's own statement, none of this guarantees the property it targets.
- **Suggested change:** Keep DB gates where the DB can actually decide: authority, idempotency, claims, events and verdict staleness. For thinking quality, use fewer and stronger checks: independent review of high-impact work, a second model family, and the follow-up of results that U-2 itself names. Each format gate should earn its place through C2.
- **Severity:** blocking as part of C2.

### Single weakest point

The plan tests its reason for existing late, and its own tools against bloat later still (F2).

C07 is the first test of criterion 1.4 in real work. It comes after C02–C06 have built:
- the data model derived from P4 (about 46 record types);
- the identity chain;
- three environments;
- the exam system;
- the leak-check subsystem.

Meanwhile:
- the process limit applies only to new processes;
- the mechanism assumption inventory starts in C05 and is tested in C10;
- there is no baseline without these mechanisms to compare against;
- the system review in 6.11 does not list mechanism load as a possible cause.

If most of DevOS's value comes from the model and the subagents and the machinery mainly adds cost, the plan finds out only after spending most of the installation. This is 2.0's failure pattern one level up: 2.1 corrected the carrier but kept the P4/P5 decomposition that was built on it.

The installation itself already runs a lean counterpart: one session, six helper roles, records in git, Checker verdict files and one hook (P:784–789). The plan does not use it as a baseline. C00 step 5 is the right place to act on this, but its outcome needs to bind C02. C1–C3 make it bind.
