**F1. The plan gives the whole Foundation one authority status, but the Foundation's own standing varies inside it.**

- **What the sources show:**
  - Only the STATE/INDEX surfaces carry current acceptance. Numbered analyses keep the status they had when they were written.
  - `history/` holds superseded research and old handoffs. The Foundation says these must not override the current surfaces, even when a search finds them directly.

- **Type:** technical defect. **Blocks:** no; the fix belongs to the C04 ingestion design.

**F2. The search benchmark tests one kind of retrieval; the plan relies on three others.**
- **Plan place:**
  - K-6 Testing and C04 acceptance.
  - Appendix C C4 items 2–3.
  - K-1 item 2(d), the history scan.
  - C10, "A dead end is recalled before it is tried again".
  - Appendix B 3.8 `counter_evidence_searched`.
  - C00 task 4 itself.
- **Sources:**
  - `F:01/records/RECORDS-VERTICAL-SYNTHESIS.md`, §7.
  - `U5-SYNTHESIS-AND-PARENT-DELTA.md`, §2 and §4.
  - `07_.../SYNTHESIS.md`, §2 (I1, I4).
- **What the sources show:**
  - Topical discovery, finding the current version, high-recall or absence search, and set reconstruction are different retrieval tasks.
  - Finding a document does not settle whether it is current or sufficient.
  - Not finding one supports an absence claim only when the search's coverage, aliases/languages, recall and freshness are bounded.
  - A set of individually relevant sources can still be insufficient for a given use.
- **What it means:** Acceptance for criteria 5 and 11 is "the right source in the top 10" plus a historical-ahead-of-current count. The plan also relies on absence claims (history scan, dead-end recall, "none found" counter-evidence) and on answers that need several sources, such as a result plus its qualifier. Appendix D D5 states the hit/miss distinction as a thinking discipline, but no acceptance test of the infrastructure checks it.
- **Type:** technical defect (Section 8 item 4, the test does not represent the claim). **Blocks:** no; the fix is needed before the C04 threshold is written.

**F4. Library use is measured from reported uses, so missed uses leave no record.**
- **Plan place:** K-6 item 9 (plan:366), K-2 item 7, Appendix B 3.7 UseReceipt, C07 measures 3 and 8, Appendix D D9 (Ek_D:282–298).
- **Sources:**
  - `08_.../DISCUSSION-HANDOFF.md`, section 3.
  - `research/soul-context/evidence/CONTEXT-SYNTHESIS.md`, section 6 (recurring problem classes in predecessor systems).
  - `U5-SYNTHESIS-AND-PARENT-DELTA.md`, section 6 (U5.6).

- **What the sources show:** The failure observed in the Foundation's own use, and repeatedly in predecessor systems, is that relevant knowledge exists but is not called on when it is needed. - **Type:** insufficient test. **Blocks:** no; the fix is needed before the C05 exam setup.

**F5. The first cognitive measurement comes after five infrastructure stages, a pattern the library records from an earlier attempt.**
- **Plan place:** Section 9 ordering principle (plan:844: C07 "at the earliest point where it can be tested"), 0.3 item 8 (plan:49), K-1 Conditions. C05 holds the first cognitive exams.
- **Sources:**
  - `C:.../2026-09-07-g1/REVIEW.md`, section 3.
  - `.../2026-09-07-method/REVIEW.md`, sections 5–6.
  - `.../2026-09-06-direction/REVIEW.md`, sections 2 and 6.
  - `research/soul-context/evidence/transition-to-soul.md`, §2.
- **What the sources show:**
  - In the earlier DevOS bootstrap, launch and isolation tooling grew step by step, each step justified on its own, and the purpose pilot never ran on a real model.
  - The reviews concluded that safeguards which are each justified do not show that all of them are prerequisites of the first meaningful work.
  - As evidence of progress, they proposed a real, bounded SOUL development question: the actor picks the work, researches it, produces a sourced result, and another session continues from that result.
  - Predecessor history shows mechanisms being hardened while the open problem moved up to framing.
  - The reviews call this a recorded pattern, not a verified root cause.
- **What it means:** U-1 is the plan's central open problem, and its own principle is to test the most uncertain thing first. Yet several C07 measures (for example 1, 2, 3, 6 and 9) need no database, rule gate or decision channel, and phase A already reads the library directly and runs role subagents. The plan asserts that C07 comes as early as possible but does not show what each measure depends on.
- **Type:** conflicts with the plan's own ordering principle; risk of repeating a recorded path. It is not a FAIL: the plan has a gate, criteria fixed in advance, and a stop before product work. **Blocks:** no; the fix is needed before C02.

**F6. "DevOS's success is the first evidence for the SOUL method" claims more than the Foundation found.**
- **Plan place:** 1.2 item 2. Item 1 has the PC-10 bound (plan:152); item 2 has none. Also 10.3.
- **Sources:**
  - `F:02/findings/06-...md`, part A (why the four grounds fit) and part B.
  - `02_adequacy/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`, Q10.
  - `08_.../CASE-AUDIT.md`, case C5.
- **What the sources show:**
  - In software work, most of the subject is visible through repository records and the technical environment. That hides the gap between a record and the reality it describes, a gap general SOUL work cannot avoid.
  - Which concerns are universal for SOUL and which are specific to DevOS is still an open question in the Foundation.
  - Work can be complete on the records and still fail in operating reality.
- **What it means:** As written, item 2 invites over-reading C07 and C11 results as evidence for SOUL. Checking outcomes against reality outside the records is not among the 10.3 hand-overs.
- **Type:** overreach (missing qualification). **Blocks:** no.

**F7. The portability tests do not say what must carry over.**
- **Plan place:** C11 task 5 and acceptance (criterion 4); C08 acceptance (criterion 2).
- **Source:** `F:01/work-environment/WORK-ENVIRONMENT-VERTICAL-SYNTHESIS.md`, §7 and §17.
- **What the source shows:** Portability is not a yes/no property. A claim has to name which equivalence it protects: content, configuration, behaviour, performance, or security and policy.
- **What it means:** C11 accepts criterion 4 when the tested part "runs" with no Claude-specific code. That is substitution at the code level. Criterion 4 asks that SOUL can "work" with another model, and criterion 33 sets a quality floor. The plan fixes the scope before results, but not the equivalence.
- **Type:** technical defect (the test does not match the claim). **Blocks:** no; the fix is needed before C08 and C11.
