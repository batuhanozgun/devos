**What this is.** The decisions of plan C00 step 7 ("Decisions are made on the results; the plan is updated if necessary (Section 14)") on the 56 topics of the findings register `evidence/C00/EV-C00-013_findings_register.md`, which maps all 270 findings of W-C00-03, W-C00-07, W-C00-08 pass A, W-C00-09, the premise inventory, the translation proposals and the open notes to a topic or to "no change" (its part 3, 39 findings). The executor (the working session) decides; these are technical decisions (plan 0.3 item 11), except where a row says the matter goes to Batu. Pass B of W-C00-08 (the library) comes in round 2.

**Disposition words.**
- **PC-nn**: a plan change made in this round through plan section 14 (`plan/decisions/PC-nn.md` with old text, new text, reason and affected stages, then the plan text), each checked by a fresh-context checker before it merges.
- **W-C00-nn**: new work, an item of C00 with its acceptance condition written and merged before it starts.
- **Stage note**: decided at the named stage, before the work that needs it; carried by a note on that stage's file (`plan/work/<stage>.md`) with the topic and its deadline. An acceptance condition is only ever strengthened, never loosened (working order section 6).
- **Batu**: one of his matters; it goes to him on issue #6 in the Appendix E format, after a fresh-context checker has weighed the draft.
- **Waits**: depends on an answer or access not yet there; the dependency is named.
- **No change**: with the reason.

**Grouping of the plan changes.** PC-07 leak control and safeguards for C00–C04; PC-08 the scope of what C02 builds (after the table and inventory of T-22 and T-23); PC-09 the plan's text before C01 (probe setups, effect channels, Section 12); PC-10 principles and mechanisms decided now for later stages; PC-11 editorial and consistency changes with no intended change of meaning.
