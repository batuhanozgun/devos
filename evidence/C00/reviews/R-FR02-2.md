### C2 · The result test is formal; the reason is named but not tested; P2 keeps "covertly" (criterion 3; major)

**Location:** §11 denial row ("a result that contains the denied change or its equivalent (the same text, file state or permission reached another way)"; "names the reason it gives"). FR-02 P2 ("the action is never retried or pursued covertly").

**Problem.**
1. **Equivalence is by text, file state or permission, not by effect.**
   - The L-066 refusal concerned edits that would let the brief gate accept a bare hash line, so the check would stop checking what it was built to check.
   - Concrete scenario: an exit design keeps W-R6's text and the hook unchanged. It changes only the generator, so that the "whole generated brief" becomes a one-line pointer to a file.
   - The gate then passes a message that is, in effect, a bare hash line.
   - None of these is "the same": not the text, not the file state, not the permission.
   - The fixed review question asks only about "the denied outcome". A reviewer bound to the row's definition can answer "no".
   - FR-02 item 2's own design moves in this direction: it points to the frontier instead of copying the not-ready list. Pushed further, it reaches that state.
   - R-FR02-1 B1 asked the frame review to *show the new design does not have* the property the denial objected to. The head only requires it to *name* the reason. The disposition table reports B1's fix as accepted in full.
2. **The P2 premise contradicts the restored row.**
   - P2 still reads "never retried or pursued covertly", the narrowing that B1 rejected.
   - It also says "no for the goal", while the row says "Never … pursue its goal".
   - The row needs P2 as its rationale. As written, P2 justifies the reading B1 ruled out.

**Fix.**
- In the row, after "(the same text, file state or permission reached another way", add: ", or the same effect on the check or property the denial protected, for example a gate that passes what it was built to stop".
- Replace "names the reason it gives" with "names the reason it gives and shows that the new design does not have that property as it applied to the denied change".
- Add the same words to the fixed review question: "…, reach it by a route the denial names, or have the same effect on the protected check?"
- Begin the exit sentence with "Except through this exit, the ban above holds."
- In P2, replace "never retried or pursued covertly" with "never retried, and its outcome never reached by another route".

### C3 · A forced choice to Batu could carry the denied outcome as an option (criterion 3; major)

**Location:** §6 Precedence; the last sentence of the §11 row.

**Problem.**
- After a failed exit, "a choice it forces on one of Batu's matters goes to him … never as a permission".
- Nothing stops one of the options from being the refused change. Scenario: a scope decision whose option A reads "keep unattended hand-over: needs the rule-text change the check refused". Batu chooses A, and a run treats his answer as "the user decided", the words of L-066 ("Let the user decide how to proceed").
- That is D-007 inside a scope wrapper. It routes the refusal through him, which both the refusal rule and his own words ("beni bahane ederek kuralı delebiliyorsun") reject.
- "Never as a permission" names the form, not the content.

**Fix.** Add to Precedence: "No option may contain a denied outcome or a way to it, and his answer never authorises an action a check refused; the options are what can be built without it."

### C4 · Bullet 3 can send an exception to a builder implementation rule back to Batu (criterion 2; major)

**Location:** §6 "Never his", bullet 3: "A rule or barrier that carries out one of his decisions or requirements … routes by the matter it protects."

**Problem.**
- PC-01 (each stage under a `/goal` target) and PC-02 (branch management) are recorded as class `batu`, answered (decision index).
- W-R6, the `/goal` brief gate, is a builder rule that carries out PC-01.
- Read literally, an exception to W-R6, which is departure 8 itself, "routes by the matter it protects", the matter being Batu's PC-01. So it goes to him. That is D-007 again, the case he declined.
- Almost every builder rule carries out *some* answered decision of his. The clause is wide enough to undo the bullet.

**Fix.** Replace the sentence with: "A rule or barrier that carries out one of his decisions goes to him only where the change would alter what that decision decided on one of his matters (the class above): for example, using a connector that D-003 bars. How the builder carries out his decision stays the builder's."

### C5 · Access to his accounts is missing from the precedence list (criteria 2 and 4; major)

**Location:** §6 Precedence: "When a blocker or a limit forces a choice of purpose, scope, acceptance or money …".

**Problem.**
- Bullet 1 makes any "permission check of the platform" never his. The list of forced choices that still reach him leaves out access, although the class row now includes it (Ek E §3 "yetki", PC-05 "hesapları").
- Scenario: work needs a repository or a service that the platform refuses until the account owner installs an app or grants access, and only Batu can do that.
- Under the head this is a platform permission check, so it becomes a blocker shown as information. But granting access to his account is his authority.
- The first Precedence sentence ("one of his matters") arguably covers it. The second sentence lists the matters and leaves access out, and readers follow the list.

**Fix.** Make the list read "purpose, scope, acceptance, money, or access to or a change of his accounts, data or other work".
