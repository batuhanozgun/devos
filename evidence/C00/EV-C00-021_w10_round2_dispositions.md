**Disposition words** (as round 1 defines them, `evidence/C00/EV-C00-014_w10_round1_dispositions.md`).
- **PC-nn**: a plan change made in this round through plan Section 14 (`plan/decisions/PC-nn.md` with old text, new text, reason and affected stages, then the plan text), each checked by a fresh-context checker before it merges.
- **W-C00-nn**: new work, an item of C00 with its acceptance condition written and merged before it starts.
- **Stage note <stage>**: decided at the named stage, before the work that needs it; carried by a note on `plan/work/<stage>.md` with the topic and its deadline. In this round a note also records the direction decided now. The plan text is then changed through Section 14 at the start of that stage, before its work and before any result the change bears on. A topic's note sits on its earliest deadline stage, and that stage's plan change writes all the topic's places, later stages' included, unless the row names a second stage. An acceptance condition is never loosened after its result has been seen (working order section 6; plan Section 14). This round strengthens conditions, with one stated exception made before any result exists: T-81 relaxes the head-commit binding of the audit verdict on one axis, and its row gives the reason.
- **Batu**: one of his matters; it goes to him on issue #6 in the Appendix E format. None in this round.
- **Waits**: depends on an answer or access not yet there. None in this round.
- **No change**: with the reason.
- **T-nn: adds** (or **unchanged**): for a round-1 topic, what round 2 adds and how.

**The rule this round follows.** This round adopts B3-C2's exit rule (T-60, PC-15), and the rule applies from this round. A finding changes the plan now only if it changes an action of C01 or C02, or needs a decision of Batu's. Every other finding gets a stage note with its deadline. A condition whose deadline is before C01 or C02 is carried whole by this round's plan changes, never in part by a stage note (B4-C5 by PC-16 and PC-17 together). A condition that names two deadlines is split at them where its row says so (B2-C2, B2-C4, B7-C6); otherwise it is written at the earlier one. PC-15 to PC-17 are made within W-C00-10, before it is accepted, so before C01 begins.
