   - **Matched:**
     - `Result: done` and `Result: partial (…)`;
     - bare `Result: partial`;
     - `Result: done.` and `Result: done (with one denial)`;
     - a CRLF line ending;
     - `Result: partially done`, which is harmless.
   - **Correctly not matched:** `Result: not started (…)` and `Result: not done`.

     - bold forms: `**Result:** done` and `**Result: done**`;
     - list, numbered and quoted forms: `- Result: done`, `9. Result: done`, `> Result: done`;
     - the value in backticks, or both label and value in backticks;
