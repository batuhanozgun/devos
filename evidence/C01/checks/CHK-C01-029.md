## Findings

**F1. Blocks. Under the recommended option (b) the audit and exam environments still share one account, and the record does not say so.**

Where: plan/decisions/D-020.md lines 19 ("What is asked"), 25 (option b), 29 (Recommendation) and 31 ("creating the two environments in it").

What the record says:
- The question frames the need as "DevOS's audit and exam environments must be kept apart from the working environment".
- Option (b) puts both environments in one second account.
- The recommendation says (b) is chosen because "(a) leaves it on a check DevOS can edit".

What the plan requires:
- The separation row 17 asks for applies between any two environments: "A session in one environment cannot start a session in another environment …" (plan/DevOS_Kurulum_Plani.md line 960).
- The exam environment must be kept apart from the audit environment too:
  - plan line 708: "only the exam environment can see exam answers";
  - plan line 813: "The exam tasks of the roles in the audit environment also come from the exam environment; the audit environment cannot prepare its own exam";
  - criterion 30 (line 215): "exams that the role under test cannot see".
- EV-C01-003 section 5(a) records the unfavourable case for environments in one account: separation rests on the guard hook alone. No layer DevOS controls is counted as separating environments over the account's control surface.

So under (b), the audit environment's separation from the exam environment is still the residual that the recommendation gives as the reason to leave (a), and it bears on the very criterion 30 claim the recommendation invokes. The record does not state this residual. It also offers no option that would close it, and does not explain why none is offered. That breaks:
- plan 0.3 item 3 and the last rule of Appendix E section 3 (the need silently cut back; the quality option not offered);
- Appendix E 4.2 (more confidence than the evidence carries).

What a passing revision needs (described, not applied):
- State in the question, under (b) and in the recommendation that (b) moves the account-scope separation only between the working environment and the other two, and that audit-to-exam separation stays on the guard.
- Either add an option that gives the exam environment an account of its own, or state why that residual is accepted and where it is re-assessed. Such an option would be, for example, a third Pro account, $20 more a month. Its cost and effect come from the evidence already filed: the limit of "a maximum of three Claude accounts" per phone number (evidence file line 42).
- Re-derive the recommendation on that basis.
