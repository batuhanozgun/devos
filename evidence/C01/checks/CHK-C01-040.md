     - what it forces is case-specific (a time and an event), so it is harder to template than a discipline answer.
   - The missing fact in this case was the duration, which no record computed.

   - Read-only evidence: on the head, the newest entry is L-195 and the logic finds 0 Wait lines. With a Wait line piped in, it finds 1.
