     - A notification counts only with origin kind `task-notification` or a `queued_command` of that mode, and its `<task-id>` and `<status>` are read before `<summary>` or `<result>`.

     - The last of these decides.
     - For a workflow agent, a journal `result` record with a non-null result.
   - **Report order.** The first non-refused, non-blank `SubagentHandback` comes first, then the last `StructuredOutput`, then the last text.

One agent that was NOT FINISHED at 14:26Z was finished, with identical output, at 14:29Z.
   - **"Over a minute".** Gaps between an agent's text and its next tool call reach 99 s (527 pairs measured), which confirms the N-149 line's "over a minute".
