## Purpose

Accumulate what changes when separately investigated videos are compared: complementary mechanisms, conflicting explanations, revised claims, missing prerequisites, use conditions, simpler alternatives and implications worth revisiting for SOUL or Development OS.

Create a synthesis only for an actual bounded question supported by sufficiently investigated source material. A channel headline list or several statements from the same author are not enough to claim independent corroboration.

## Entry requirements

Each real synthesis should identify its question, video/source IDs, exact finding/evidence routes, actual coverage, supporting and conflicting evidence, our inference versus source claims, applicable conditions and unresolved boundaries. Use the original research home's findings for linked videos; do not copy them into a second authority.

A synthesis may span several Foundation relevance hints without becoming a Foundation node. Separate conceptual learning, candidate mechanisms, rejected options and adoption decisions. Refresh current project context before consequential SOUL/DevOS projection.
