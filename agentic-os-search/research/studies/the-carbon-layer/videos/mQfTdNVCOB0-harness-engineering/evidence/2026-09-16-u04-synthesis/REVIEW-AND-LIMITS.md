## 2. Reverse challenges and their effect on the synthesis

**Ten components imply ten mandatory layers.** Retrieval, tool use, observation and persistence form cycles; a small workflow can combine responsibilities. The synthesis retains the author's map but rejects a deployment-count interpretation. It preserves the video/article grouping difference and attributes the author's cautions correctly.

**Updating memory means the method learned to improve.** Work-state change, reusable knowledge change and executable-policy change now have separate objects and success claims. The same storage system may support them, but their effects need different evidence. This avoids treating ordinary task progress as optimizer efficacy.

**A better candidate means a dependable improvement system.** Selected-output quality, search procedure repeatability, causal attribution, transfer, acceptance and materialization remain separate. Classification's local interface evidence does not become coding's causal experiment; coding's repeated tasks do not become unseen-task evidence; math's all-model average does not replace its transfer subset.

**The paper supplies all acceptance and deployment controls.** Its native search is presented first. The extension from selected candidate through acceptance to actual consumer is explicitly our analytical contribution, informed by selective studies. No source is credited with a control we have not established there.

**Staging protects every replacement.** The GSTACK primary file is a test source with structural checks, extracted-helper success fixtures and a reconstructed failure branch. It was not run. Its intended preservation behavior is not proof of arbitrary crash atomicity, migration success or live-process freshness. Earlier stronger study language is not promoted into new execution evidence.

**A used skill improved the task.** The HERMES registered handler increments usage on successful loading. This is directly observed source behavior, not a measured task outcome. Its meaning is legitimate for maintenance; it is insufficient as an efficacy oracle. No whole-product absence of evaluation is inferred.

**More records make an improvement loop reliable.** Logging, missing populations, summaries, relevance and criterion changes can modify which evidence is visible. The combined account therefore includes the possibility of apparent improvement through measurement change, without alleging such manipulation in the reported experiments. An archive's size or a search hit is not sufficient.

**Every failure must become a permanent rule.** No-change, simpler method, better model, repaired source, revised objective and changed environment remain options. A control's cost and false rejections matter. The example permits preauthorized low-risk changes and does not prescribe one universal approval topology.

**Connecting the sources means assembling their products.** The comparison borrows bounded distinctions, not dependencies. No shared integration, compatible schema or performance synergy was tested. The relation to SOUL is a candidate research-use explanation, not U06's current architecture assessment.
