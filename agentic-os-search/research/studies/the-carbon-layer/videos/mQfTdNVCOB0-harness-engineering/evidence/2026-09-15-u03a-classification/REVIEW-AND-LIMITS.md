## Positive evidence retained

The reported selected policy improves the mean against several baselines; the richer interface ablation is more discriminating than a mere success story; and additional-dataset evaluation offers an actual transfer check. The appropriate judgment is not that every limitation cancels every result. The source supports a promising candidate at its reported scope.

The stronger claim of a universally superior improvement system would require more. No control was added after observing a bad run and then presented as an independent confirmation. There was no new agent run, and none of the analytical counterexamples is labeled a passing or failing product test.

## Interpretation alternatives

Better diagnostic access is one supported local explanation. Proposer priors, input selection, feedback summarization quality, unequal work outside the counted evaluation budget, task mix, metric choice, candidate selection noise and source/code-version differences remain material alternatives or moderators. The sources recovered here do not permit assigning causal percentages to them.

A simple few-shot policy is a meaningful baseline, not an inferior option by definition. Fewer calls or more context are neither mandatory benefits nor automatic defects. A procedure's gain on one distribution can justify a bounded use hypothesis without authorizing use on another.

The protocol's stated test holdout is preserved as positive author-reported design. We did not infer that historical test leakage occurred from a weakness previously observed in the cleaned-up release. Conversely, the publication's holdout statement does not validate all recovery paths in that release.

## Limits not closed

Historical source/configuration/skill/adapter identities; exact sample lineage and exposure; per-example paired results; independent search repeats; summary construction and complete proposal/evaluation budgets; native metric correspondence; exact tokenizer/context/call accounting; total cost, causality and transfer to production work all remain unresolved at the indicated depth.

The 40-versus-60 presentation issue is now localized within the paper as well as its project page. Figure 3's axis/caption unit difference and the one-call family claim are explicit source inconsistencies. We do not invent a historical run to reconcile them or treat them alone as evidence of fabricated scores.

Raw data, full baseline implementations, all transfer loaders and all repository history were not searched. Do not turn the bounded recovery limit into an absence claim. MCE/ACE/GEPA method reads are not independent reproductions of the Meta-Harness adaptations.
