# Agentic AI Systems Roadmap — INDEX

## Role

Retrieval router for the candidate study seeded from the user-supplied **“AGENTIC AI SYSTEMS ROADMAP”** visual.

This package is intentionally discussion-friendly. It preserves the source artefact and an initial systems comparison, then allows later conversation to generate bounded research questions. It is not Foundation authority and is not an adopted architecture roadmap.

## Start here

1. `META.md` — classification, reuse value, authority boundary and related studies
2. `STATE.md` — current discussion/research state and what is or is not authorised
3. `INITIAL-ASSESSMENT.md` — first comparison against Foundation and existing candidate studies
4. `SOURCE-VISUAL.md` — source identity, transcription and visible topology
5. `source/agentic-ai-systems-roadmap.jpg` — repository visual copy

## Working rule

When discussion raises a new topic, do not immediately create another study. First classify it as one of:

- already well covered by an existing study;
- fragmented across studies and needing synthesis;
- genuinely under-researched and worthy of a new focused-topic package;
- later-phase design/implementation concern that should wait for authorization.

Record future discussion outputs with their epistemic status intact: observation, interpretation, hypothesis, candidate research question or accepted research result must not collapse into one another.
