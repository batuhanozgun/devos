- model/runtime policy that chooses among models, context budgets, latency/cost trade-offs and deterministic wrappers;
- retrieval/memory/context mechanisms and their validation/grounding requirements;
- agent and multi-agent organization as task-conditioned structures rather than one fixed topology;
- evaluation + observability + governance as a possible operational feedback/control system rather than isolated utilities;
- runtime/deployment constraints that shape what an agent can actually do;
