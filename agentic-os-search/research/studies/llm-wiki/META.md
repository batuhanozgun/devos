## Why this candidate is registered

The source describes an LLM-maintained, interlinked Markdown wiki that accumulates synthesis as sources and questions arrive. It distinguishes raw sources, the generated wiki and a schema/workflow document, and describes ingest, query and lint operations with separate content indexing and chronological logging.

This is an abstract pattern proposal, not a supplied implementation. These are source-description signals for later investigation, not findings about correctness, reliability, maintenance cost or superiority over retrieval-based alternatives.
