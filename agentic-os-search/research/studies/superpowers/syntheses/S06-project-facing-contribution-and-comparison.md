## 2. PC1 — A development method is a candidate means, not the goal of SOUL

Superpowers supplies a specific software-development method with alternative entry routes, roles, artifacts and host-dependent helpers. It does not thereby establish the general ability to discover and construct whichever working system a future outcome requires. Its own `writing-skills` method broadens its adaptability, but still does not prove universal working-system synthesis. [S01; S03; S05]

The distinction changes its reuse value rather than making it irrelevant. For Development OS, Superpowers is a comparatively direct SDLC reference: agreed intent, task boundaries, review, tests, correction, integration and procedure development. For SOUL, the transferable material is more conditional: how actors receive applicable guidance, how decisions and evidence move, and how methods are selected or revised under actual constraints. This is a judgment about research relevance, not comparative performance.

Foundation contributes substantive distinctions, countercases and applicability conditions. It is neither an empty checklist nor an engineering recipe. A route in its tree does not choose a service, agent count, memory store or workflow engine. Conversely, technology-noncommitted research can be changed by a concrete technical counterexample. No result below makes Foundation the sole source of construction knowledge. [P06–P10]

## 3. PC2 — Completing selected tasks is different from discovering all necessary work

Superpowers' plan self-review checks specification coverage; SDD's preflight compares tasks, globals and shared interfaces. These are meaningful controls against contradictions and omissions within the represented work. They do not prove that the specification already contains every prerequisite, affected party or necessary production activity. [S03 §§1–3; U02 F13–F17]

Foundation's Work result preserves discovery-driven pursuit, admission, situated replanning and meta-work rather than treating a task list as the whole pursuit. The later question-bank discussion supplies a concrete warning about research application: the assistant initially emphasized output/use and declared closure before sufficiently considering how the output would be produced. The owner exposed the missing perspective. That is a recorded conversational omission, not a benchmark or proof of its complete cause. [P07; P13]

**Constructed contrast:** every assigned worker can satisfy its brief while a necessary but unassigned integration activity remains absent. Local task compliance does not settle whole-goal coverage. This is an analytical countercase, not an observed Superpowers incident.

The reuse consequence is to distinguish coverage of the chosen plan from adequacy of the chosen pursuit. It does not prescribe recursive planning for every task, require another AI agent, or claim Superpowers lacks all discovery behavior. The appropriate depth depends on the actual outcome, uncertainty and cost of a missed dependency.

## 4. PC3 — Context handoff needs a common adequate and available view

The accepted Information integration result offers a sharper test than asking whether all information is stored somewhere. An adequate view can exist but be unavailable to the worker; an available brief can omit a binding condition. Separate positive statements about adequacy and availability do not establish the same compatibly qualified view or strategy. The union of information across agents or possible execution branches is not automatically any actor's usable history. [P17, I1–I2]

Superpowers provides a direct mechanism case. The plan contains Global Constraints and interfaces; the task helper extracts the local task; the controller supplies the additional binding context to the implementer and reviewer. The extractor's successful operation does not discharge the controller's selection responsibility. Another reviewer given the same incomplete input can share the omission. [S03 §3; U02 F17; U03 F20–F21]

This does **not** imply that every worker must receive the entire plan or conversation. I3 permits use-appropriate substitutions and established complementary side information. A compact brief plus reliably available interfaces and relevant prior rulings can be enough; an assumed private memory or another worker's knowledge cannot silently serve as that complement. [P17, I3; S03]

The actual research contribution is therefore two-sided: preserve the value of narrow contexts, while testing the adequacy and actual availability of the chosen context for the designated actor and use. A fixed token budget or successful file read is not a universal adequacy measure. No natural-language completeness oracle was established by Foundation's finite examples or by this comparison.

## 5. PC4 — Responsibility, authority, capability and acceptance must survive delegation separately

The Actors ground includes performers, principals, recipients and affected parties. It separates competence, participation, decision authority, technical reach and accountability. A hierarchy position or role label does not combine these into one entitlement. [P14–P16]

Superpowers' controller illustrates why this matters. It interprets spec/plan context, resolves unverified requirements and makes recorded rulings. Workers implement; task reviewers assess; humans retain integration and destructive-action choices. These are different responsibilities. A reviewer's read-only instruction is not proof that the host removed write capability; a shared access key is not human design approval. [S02 §5; S03 §4; S04 §§2–3/6]

The projection preserves rather than solves the source's open compositions: scope upgrade/reapproval versus SDD rulings (Q13), human-versus-controller wording for plan-mandated defects (Q20), and fresh-command versus reused-evidence obligations (Q24). Foundation explains why the distinctions matter; it does not select a hidden universal precedence rule for Superpowers.

For later target work, the question is who must decide what, with which evidence and which permitted effect—not whether a particular number of agents is fashionable. A more capable model cannot supply absent authorization. Adding a reviewer can improve challenge opportunities without independently validating the common requirements or oracle.

## 6. PC5 — Completion is qualified progress, not a universal green state

Foundation separates Work closure, actual outcome, evidence and authorized acceptance. Composition further distinguishes exact binding, criterion-relative standing, realized control and propagation. Those are interacting questions, not a mandatory sequence of software modules. [P13; P20]

Superpowers supplies concrete instances:

- DONE begins task review; a task may later be complete with a clean review or with explicitly parked findings.
- Tests can pass on a branch while the merged result fails; the finishing method requires retesting that new object.
- A successful merge can coexist with incomplete cleanup; a failed command can follow earlier writes without rolling them back.
- Code history, disclosed rulings and detailed ignored evidence have different retention paths.

[S04; U05 F36–F41; U07-B F76]

These examples make a reusable distinction between a decision to progress and a claim that all defects disappeared. A cost cap does not refute a remaining defect; recording a ruling does not by itself make the ruling sound. Equally, knowingly retaining non-blocking risk is not automatically a process error.

A future reuse should identify the particular object, criterion, unresolved condition and authorized next action. This does not require one universal status schema or indefinite evidence storage. The needed retention is determined by later decisions, recovery and audit uses. Earlier local helper/Git observations remain bounded examples, not demonstrated production losses or failure rates.

## 7. PC6 — Recovery and portability depend on the realized consumer path

The Environment finding distinguishes resource existence, access, availability and sufficiency; situated actionability additionally depends on the actor and work. Superpowers shows why this is operationally consequential: host-native skill discovery, callbacks, caches, worktrees and installed packages have different lifetimes and permissions. [P21–P25; S02]

A restored file is not necessarily the current applicable plan. Reusing a port/key is not restoring the previous screen. Acquiring a new package is not proving that an existing model session loaded it. A skill label or source SHA need not identify the external metadata and working-copy inputs in a distributed artifact. [S02 §§3–6; S03 §5]

The earlier continuity mechanisms should still be credited: SDD has a ledger, reports and commit references; adapters provide re-entry; the visual companion provides reconnection; distribution tools have preview/preflight checks. The stronger claim that these automatically restore valid current work requires additional object/revision, actor and consumer evidence.

The official Git worktree documentation was consulted only for its narrow shared-repository/per-worktree-state distinction. It does not turn a linked worktree into a security boundary or identify cleanup authority. No new Git command or host was run. [X10]

## 8. PC7 — Changing guidance, measuring use and improving outcomes are different transformations

Superpowers' authoring/evaluation account already distinguishes choosing an intervention, comparing candidate guidance, collecting evidence, assessing it and admitting a change. Its selected review rubric evaluates grounded delivered findings without requiring a particular skill invocation. Gauntlet's structural/read-before-cite controls do not independently establish sound judgment, and a parser repair can improve accepted-report counts without improving the coding agent. [S01; U06-B1/B2]

Foundation's Returned Influence and Persistent Adaptation results sharpen the later inference: stored lessons, changed bytes, a current response or a passing test do not alone establish operative use, retained response change, benefit or authority. A method revision and a measuring-instrument revision can alter the same metric for different reasons. [P20]

This does not require every small wording change to trigger a large benchmark. It requires matching evidence to the claim: availability, actual invocation, behavior, outcome quality, retained benefit and permission to distribute are different claims. Appropriate comparison may retain the existing method, simplify it, strengthen a mechanical check, revise the task framing or add no new mechanism. The source's failure-specific intervention choices support keeping these alternatives open.

### gstack: an artifact's downstream consequence is conditional

The complete recorded delivery synthesis was recovered, then `ship/sections/plan-completion.md` was directly re-read at the exact gstack pin. The source distinguishes discovery, actionable-item extraction, verification modes and user disposition. Missing/unreadable plans can skip the audit; extraction is capped; plan verification depends on a usable verification section, server and QA skill. It preserves negative and unverifiable outcomes rather than equating related code with a deliverable. [C01–C04; C10]

This is useful evidence for a downstream consumer making a durable artifact consequential. It is not grounds to call all gstack gates fail-closed or all controls mechanical. Nor should gstack's current-standing preference erase a genuinely historical Superpowers claim such as test-first chronology: some claims require what happened earlier, not merely an equivalent current result. Both systems mix procedure, tools and human judgment.

### Hermes: library use is not outcome evaluation

The recovered U15 evidence distinguishes name-based listing, full-view ambiguity rejection, several caches and explicit content loading. A setup-needed response can still be a successful view; recorded view/use reflects that loading path, not successful task completion. Archive/rollback and sidecar reconciliation have separate effects. [C05–C08]

Superpowers' instructions for authoring and using procedures and Hermes' library lifecycle mechanisms therefore inform different responsibilities. Their apparent complementarity is a research comparison, not proof that integration is necessary, feasible or beneficial. The full Hermes runtime and its next evaluation research were not resumed.

### Flowable: a persisted transition is different from a persisted description

The Flowable study explains engine-owned process/case state and explicit in-flight migration. Superpowers' ledger supports an actor reconstructing work; a durable engine can instead own modeled waits and activation. Both can support continuity, but the responsibility and validation objects differ. [C09]

A durable engine still executes the chosen model: it cannot certify that the model includes every necessary task or that a completed approval is true. Conversely, interpreting procedures can accommodate judgment without proving every transition is reliable. No single option is selected for SOUL; the comparison prevents treating skill instructions and a workflow engine as interchangeable merely because both contain plans and states.

## 11. Bounded conclusion and next work

The strongest contribution is not a Superpowers-shaped SOUL architecture. It is a source-linked set of mechanisms and countercases connecting working agreements, context selection, authority, evidence, actual effects and procedure evolution. Foundation makes the distinctions more precise; the study shows concrete places where they become consequential.

The inspected cases did not establish a new first-order Foundation region or justify restarting generic deepening. That is a bounded interpretive result, not independent proof of Foundation completeness. Lower-level omissions, invalid premises or a forced fit remain legitimate reopening grounds. Likewise, common themes across same-assistant studies do not establish independent empirical prevalence.
