## Purpose and bounded result

The user authorized a detailed source-grounded study with a gstack-like method, not installation or adoption. The objective was to explain how Superpowers works, where its guarantees stop and what can be reused later.

[CORE](CORE.md) gives the compact result: **a portable software-development methodology expressed through skill/role contracts, interpreted by host agents/tools, supported by concrete adapters and file/Git helpers plus an optional visual runtime, with a separate method-development/evaluation process.** It is neither only prompts, a complete autonomous runtime nor one universal linear SDLC.

The controller performs semantic coordination, not only scheduling. Three build routes, alternate execution/parallel paths, qualified completion, human integration and separate method change remain visible.
