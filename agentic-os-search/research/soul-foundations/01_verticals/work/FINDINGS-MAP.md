## 1. What is the relevant unit of Work?

**Problem:** task, purpose, method, process, case, activity and system flow are easy to collapse into one work item.

**Current result:** no universal task atom is justified. Purpose/outcome, action/task and method/refinement can differ; the useful analysis unit may be an item, network/process/case, durable activity or system-level flow.

**L3:** `findings/WORK-FRAMES-AND-UNITS.md`

## 2. Does Work begin only when execution starts?

**Problem:** possible demand, selection, commitment and actual execution can be conflated.

**Current result:** preserve candidate/possible, eligible/applicable, selected/committed/admitted and in-progress distinctions when they matter. Demand is not automatically commitment; backlog is not automatically WIP; rejection, deferral and supersession are not execution failure. Priority is comparative/contextual under scarcity.

**L3:** `findings/INTAKE-SELECTION-COMMITMENT-AND-PRIORITY.md`

## 3. How does planned Work relate to actual Work?

**Problem:** fixed plans and isolated item state can hide adaptation, dependencies, concurrency and coordination effort.

**Current result:** distinguish planned/prescribed from actual situated work; distinguish normal elaboration/adaptive replanning from uncontrolled drift. Work may use imperative, constraint-based or event/situation-driven control. Dependencies, articulation/coordination and system flow are first-order phenomena.

**L3:** `findings/COORDINATION-FLOW-AND-SITUATEDNESS.md`

## 4. What does Work state, time and closure mean?

**Problem:** one `todo → doing → done` status plus timestamps collapses readiness, requiredness, failure, closure, effort and recovery semantics.

**Current result:** applicability/requiredness/readiness/execution/suspension/failure/completion/termination can diverge. Effort differs from duration, waiting and elapsed time. Execution completion differs from verification, validation and acceptance. Termination, compensation, repair and retry are distinct. Continuing work may have completed episodes without one final terminal state.

**L3:** `findings/CONTROL-STATE-CLOSURE-AND-RECOVERY.md`

## 5. What failure surfaces did the research expose?

**Problem:** implementation-looking shortcuts can erase the actual foundation distinctions.

**Current result:** important framing failure classes include task atomism, demand–commitment conflation, plan/execution conflation, imperative over-specification, coordination invisibility, flow blindness, status collapse, effort–duration conflation, completion leakage, checklist fallacy, cancellation-as-erasure, failure-as-terminal, support-work erasure and false terminality.

**L2/L3:** start with `synthesis/WORK-VERTICAL-SYNTHESIS.md` section 15, then descend to the relevant L3 finding above.

## 6. Why did the vertical stop?

**Problem:** interdisciplinary Work research can expand indefinitely.

**Current result:** early counterframes repeatedly produced new first-order distinctions. Later targeted omission probes increasingly mapped onto established dimensions. The final feedback/control-loop probe also composed existing planning/evaluation/adaptation/recovery dimensions rather than opening a new first-order family. Marginal first-order information gain therefore fell materially.

**L4A:** `synthesis/COMPLETENESS-OMISSION-CHECK.md`

This is same-model adversarial self-review, not independent verification.
