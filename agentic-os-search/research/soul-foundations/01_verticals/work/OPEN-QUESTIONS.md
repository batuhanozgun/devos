# Work — Open Questions

**Document role:** CURRENT_RESIDUAL  
**Vertical revision:** `work-foundation-2026-09-01-r1`  
**Status:** bounded unresolved Work-local questions; not a reason to reopen the vertical by default.

## Purpose

Preserve what remains uncertain after bounded completion without forcing later readers to reconstruct residual uncertainty from the full synthesis or research history.

These are **not** currently identified missing first-order Work dimensions. They are refinements or modeling questions that become relevant only if a later task requires them.

## Current bounded residuals

### 1. Universality versus domain-specific projection

Which of the preserved Work distinctions survive across most domains, and which are better treated as optional/domain-specific analytical projections?

The current research establishes that the distinctions can matter; it does not establish one universal field/schema set.

### 2. Formal dependency and temporal semantics

If later design requires machine-operational semantics, which dependency types, temporal constraints, partial orders, recurrence rules or synchronization conditions need explicit formal treatment?

Foundation research intentionally stopped before selecting a universal workflow or constraint grammar.

### 3. Uncertainty and stochastic Work

How should uncertain duration, uncertain outcomes, probabilistic failure, discovery-driven scope, risk exposure or stochastic capacity be represented if later synthesis/design needs them?

Current research establishes adaptive/evolving work but does not provide a universal uncertainty calculus.

### 4. Resource and capacity semantics

How should effort, resource demand, bottleneck capacity, contention and admission policies be modeled without collapsing actor capability/authority or implementation-specific scheduling into the Work vertical?

### 5. Measurement validity across Work levels

Which metrics legitimately measure item progress, flow, outcome achievement or continuing-service health without confusing local activity with end-to-end system performance?

The vertical identifies the level-confusion risk but does not prescribe one metric family.

### 6. Boundary of investigation / decision / sensemaking

Investigation, diagnosis, evaluation and decision can change the epistemic state and generate subsequent work. Later synthesis may need to decide whether these remain ordinary Work forms or deserve a different cross-vertical treatment.

No current evidence requires a separate first-order Work family.

## Reopen rule

Do not reopen substantive Work research merely because one of these questions becomes interesting.

Reopen only when a later task needs one of them resolved for a concrete decision, or when evidence produces a material falsifier such as:

- a common Work phenomenon cannot be expressed through the current dimensions without systematic information loss;
- a major alternative discipline yields a genuinely new first-order unit/control/closure family;
- independent review identifies a first-order omission;
- a load-bearing source claim fails and materially changes the synthesis;
- cross-vertical evidence shows that the provisional `Work` boundary itself is misleading.
