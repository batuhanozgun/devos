# Work — Completeness / Omission Countercheck

**Status:** current bounded-completion assessment  
**Verdict:** sufficiently complete for the current foundation purpose; return to user for evaluation  
**Verification status:** same-model adversarial self-review, **not independent verification**

## 1. Question

Has the independent Work vertical reached the point where continued search for new **first-order** distinctions has materially diminishing information value, after challenging the strongest alternative framings and plausible omission risks?

This is not a claim that the scholarship on work is exhausted. It is a bounded research-stop judgment for the current SOUL Foundations Layer-1 purpose.

## 2. Starting frame that was challenged

The weak initial frame risked treating Work as bounded things-to-do with lifecycle/control state.

The first research orientation explicitly challenged:

- task atomism;
- lifecycle primacy;
- plan fidelity;
- single-case identity;
- closure universality;
- item-local progress/control;
- simple failure/termination semantics.

The strongest competing orientation treated Work instead as situated, interdependent activity and flow toward purposes/outcomes under constraints, evolving information and recovery conditions.

### Result

The narrow `task/work-item + scalar lifecycle` account did not survive. Tasks and lifecycles remain useful projections, but they are not justified as the universal foundation unit or grammar.

## 3. First-order dimensions that emerged

The research currently preserves the following families of distinction without asserting that they are ontology primitives:

1. purpose/outcome versus action/task versus method/refinement;
2. plural units/levels of analysis, including task, network/process/case, durable activity and system flow;
3. candidate/possible work versus eligibility, selection/commitment/admission and execution;
4. planned/prescribed work versus actual situated work and progressive/adaptive replanning;
5. imperative, declarative/constraint-based and event/situation-driven control regimes;
6. dependency, concurrency, synchronisation, articulation and coordination work;
7. item-local state/progress versus population-level flow, capacity and congestion;
8. applicability/requiredness/readiness/activation/execution/suspension/failure/completion/termination as separable state questions;
9. effort/work quantity versus processing duration, waiting, elapsed time, timing constraints and recurrence;
10. execution closure versus verification, validation/fitness and acceptance/readiness;
11. termination/cancellation versus rollback, compensation, reconciliation, retry and failure-generated recovery work;
12. bounded work versus continuing/recurring/condition-maintaining work, including support/enabling activity.

Investigation/decision/sensemaking appears as a Work form that can change epistemic state and determine later activity; it does not currently require a separate top-level family beyond purpose/action, adaptive control and evaluation.

## 4. Targeted omission probes and outcomes

### 4.1 Candidate demand / intake / commitment / prioritisation

**Risk:** research may begin too late, at execution, and miss the decision to take Work on at all.

**Evidence:** CMMN discretionary planning, Kanban pre-commitment options/commitment point, PMI portfolio selection and resource allocation [S02][S19][S20].

**Outcome:** **new first-order distinction found and integrated.** Candidate/eligible/selected-or-committed/in-progress Work cannot safely be collapsed into one execution lifecycle. Priority is relational/contextual under scarcity.

### 4.2 Maintenance / recurring / preventive and corrective Work

**Risk:** temporary project/task examples may hide standing or recurrent Work.

**Evidence:** ISO maintenance terminology plus existing ongoing-operation/activity and recovery evidence [S21][S08][S14].

**Outcome:** important counterexample, but **no additional first-order family**. Preventive maintenance maps to continuing/recurring/condition-triggered Work; corrective maintenance maps to failure-generated repair/recovery; retained/restored function maps to purpose/outcome.

### 4.3 Effort versus duration and support Work

**Risk:** time and progress may be over-modeled as timestamps/duration; non-deliverable-producing support activity may disappear.

**Evidence:** PMI scheduling guidance distinguishes work/effort labor units from duration and identifies level-of-effort activities [S15].

**Outcome:** **new orthogonal distinction found and integrated.** Effort, processing duration, waiting and elapsed time vary independently. Support/enabling Work remains real Work even without direct terminal deliverable production.

### 4.4 Classic workflow-pattern complexity

**Risk:** declarative/adaptive evidence might cause the research to under-examine rich imperative workflow semantics.

**Evidence:** Workflow Patterns covers branching/synchronisation, multiple instances, temporal, state-based, cancellation and inter-workflow patterns [S22].

**Outcome:** **no missing first-order family.** The patterns mapped to structure/decomposition, concurrency, time, state, cancellation/recovery and coordination already present in the frame.

### 4.5 Work-design autonomy / interdependence

**Risk:** coordination framing might collapse all Work into dependence and miss discretion/autonomy or broader work-design levels.

**Evidence:** Reiche's work-design typology distinguishes interdependence and autonomy and spans task/process/organisation levels [S23].

**Outcome:** interdependence reinforces current coordination findings. Autonomy/discretion reinforces adaptive-control variability, but the holder, scope and legitimacy of authority/capability cross into Actors. **No additional isolated-Work first-order family was required.**

### 4.6 Feedback / closed-loop control

**Risk:** adaptation findings may describe plan change without exposing the monitoring-feedback-control loop that drives change.

**Evidence:** ASQ PDCA repeats plan → do → check → act; NASA closed-loop process assessment connects non-conformance, causal assessment, corrective action and verification of effectiveness [S24][S25].

**Outcome:** feedback is load-bearing but **composes already identified dimensions**: plan/actual distinction, monitoring/evaluation, adaptive replanning, corrective/recovery Work and continuing iteration. It did not expose a new independent first-order family.

## 5. Why stopping is now reasonable

The information-gain pattern changed during the vertical:

- early alternative-frame research repeatedly exposed new first-order distinctions;
- the first targeted omission checks still exposed two substantive gaps: pre-execution commitment/selection and effort-versus-duration/support Work;
- later targeted probes — maintenance, classic workflow patterns, autonomy/interdependence and feedback/control loops — predominantly mapped onto the now-established dimensions rather than opening new problem families.

This is the expected signal for bounded completion: new evidence still refines, exemplifies and strengthens the frame, but the marginal probability of discovering another foundation-level axis has fallen materially relative to the cost of indefinite literature expansion.

Stopping is therefore based on **diminishing first-order information gain**, not on completion pressure or the claim that no more Work literature exists.

## 6. Evidence adequacy

The source ledger currently spans 25 source anchors across:

- case/process/declarative workflow;
- hierarchical planning;
- coordination and CSCW;
- object-centric process analysis;
- activity theory / situated work;
- queueing/flow;
- long-running recovery/compensation;
- systems engineering verification/validation/acceptance;
- project, scheduling and portfolio management;
- interruption/task-transition research;
- maintenance terminology;
- work design;
- quality/closed-loop improvement.

Load-bearing distinctions preferentially use full primary/authoritative views where feasible, and several major conclusions are triangulated across different traditions rather than relying on one formalism.

### Evidence limits

- This is interdisciplinary foundations research, not a systematic review or meta-analysis.
- Some sources are grade B abstract/institutional views; claims from them are bounded to the inspected view.
- Some older standards/papers are used for stable conceptual distinctions, not current compliance rules.
- Formalisms and professional methods are existence proofs for distinctions, not proposed SOUL implementations.
- Source-view fidelity is tracked, but source correctness is never guaranteed merely because the source is primary or authoritative.

## 7. Verification limitation

The omission/countercheck process was performed by the same model conducting the research.

It therefore increases confidence as an adversarial **self-review**, but it does not provide epistemically independent verification. The research does not claim otherwise.

A later independent reviewer or genuinely differentiated verification path could still find:

- an omitted first-order phenomenon;
- an over-broad synthesis claim;
- a source claim that was too strong for the inspected view;
- a cross-vertical issue misclassified as Work-local or vice versa.

Such a finding would be a valid reason to reopen the vertical.

## 8. Remaining uncertainty

The vertical is sufficiently complete for its current purpose while several questions intentionally remain unresolved.

### Work-local refinements

- Which dimensions are universal enough to survive later synthesis versus domain-specific projections?
- Which dependencies or temporal constraints require formal semantics in a later model?
- How should uncertainty, risk and stochastic duration/capacity be modeled if implementation/design later requires them?
- What measurement regimes are valid for different Work levels without confusing local and system progress?

These are refinements; no current evidence shows that they require a new first-order foundation family.

### Deferred cross-vertical questions

- Who/what may select, commit, reprioritise, accept, terminate, waive or reactivate Work? — Actors/authority.
- What persistent evidence establishes plans, commitments, execution, verification, validation, acceptance, failure or compensation? — Records relation.
- Where should purposes, obligations, resources, effects and commitments live if the Layer-1 decomposition changes? — later cross-vertical synthesis.

These unresolved relations are not defects in the isolated Work vertical; solving them here would violate its methodological boundary.

## 9. Reopen conditions

Reopen Work substantive research if later evidence produces a material falsifier, for example:

- a common Work phenomenon cannot be represented through the current dimensions without systematic information loss;
- a major disciplinary frame yields a genuinely different unit/control/closure family rather than a refinement;
- cross-vertical evidence shows that `Work` itself is the wrong or misleading problem boundary;
- an independent review identifies a first-order omission;
- a load-bearing source claim is invalidated and removing it materially changes the synthesis.

Do not reopen merely because more examples, methods, workflow patterns, domain taxonomies or implementation choices can be found.

For the current SOUL Foundations purpose:

Therefore the Work vertical is **SUFFICIENTLY COMPLETE FOR THE CURRENT FOUNDATION PURPOSE**.
