# Work — Deferred Relations

**Document role:** CURRENT_DEFERRED_RELATIONS  
**Vertical revision:** `work-foundation-2026-09-01-r1`  
**Status:** material relations observed during Work research but intentionally not solved inside the isolated vertical.

## Purpose

Give later Actors and cross-vertical work a focused entry point into Work without requiring full-corpus rereading or silently treating Work findings as cross-vertical conclusions.

These entries are **questions/relations to revisit**, not accepted architecture or ontology.

## Records / persistent-information relations

Later synthesis may need to determine what persistent information establishes or reconstructs:

- candidate requests and the transition into commitment/admission;
- plans, constraints, dependencies and changes to them;
- actual situated execution versus prescribed work;
- readiness, blockage, suspension, failure and closure state;
- effort, timing, waiting, flow and relevant measurement history;
- verification, validation, acceptance/readiness judgments;
- termination, partial effects, compensation, repair, retry and reconciliation;
- continuing/recurring obligations and episode history.

### Boundary preserved

Work research established that these distinctions can matter. It did **not** decide what must be persisted, which evidence is authoritative, or how provenance/currentness should work. Those are Records/cross-vertical questions.

## Actors / authority relations

Later Actors research or synthesis may need to determine who/what has:

- capability to perform particular Work;
- responsibility for outcomes or continuing obligations;
- authority to select, reject, defer, commit or reprioritise candidate Work;
- authority to allocate scarce capacity/resources;
- authority to waive dependencies/requirements or change constraints;
- responsibility for coordination/handoffs;
- authority to accept, validate, terminate, reactivate or supersede Work;
- accountability for recovery, compensation or unresolved effects;
- discretion/autonomy to adapt planned Work in context.

### Boundary preserved

The Work vertical preserved the existence of these decision points but deliberately did not model actor identity, delegation, authority, capability or accountability.

## Cross-vertical boundary questions

Later synthesis should explicitly revisit where the following concepts belong or whether the starting decomposition should change:

- **purpose / outcome:** Work objective, persistent claim/state, actor intention/obligation, or another concept?
- **commitment / obligation:** Work admission state, actor/authority relation, persistent institutional fact, or composite relation?
- **resources / capacity:** Work constraint, actor capability, external environment, or separate model concern?
- **externally visible effects:** part of Work state, persistent Records state, domain state outside both, or a relation among them?
- **acceptance / validation:** Work predicate, actor judgment/authority event, persistent evidence, or composite?
- **continuing responsibility:** continuing Work, actor role/obligation, persistent policy/record, or composite?

## Retrieval guidance for later synthesis

When a later cross-vertical question touches one of these relations:

1. start here to identify the specific boundary question;
2. read `KEY.md` or `FINDINGS-MAP.md` only if broader Work orientation is needed;
3. descend to the relevant L3 Work finding for the Work-local distinction;
4. use `evidence/SOURCE-LEDGER.md` only if the Work-local claim itself is load-bearing or contested;
5. separately recover the current Records/Actors evidence rather than importing another vertical's conclusion through this file.

## Anti-drift rule

Do not interpret this file as permission to solve cross-vertical architecture from the Work side. Its purpose is to preserve **where Work research encountered a boundary** so later authorised synthesis can return to the exact question efficiently.
