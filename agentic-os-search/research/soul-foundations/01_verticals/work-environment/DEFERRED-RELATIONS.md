### Environment ↔ Work

- Which environment properties determine whether particular Work is technically possible, ready or blocked?

- How should Work that provisions, configures, observes, repairs, migrates or retires an environment be related to environment lifecycle without collapsing the two domains?
- When does environmental degradation create new Work such as repair/rebuild/recovery?

- How should shared environments represent distinct user/session/access views without collapsing Actor identity into environment identity?
- How should provider/operator administration differ from environment-user control?
