## Source-strength rule

Prefer, in descending order when a claim is load-bearing and equivalent evidence is available:

1. current official standard/specification or authoritative institutional definition;
2. peer-reviewed research / established technical report;
3. current official product/platform documentation used as a concrete counterexample or mechanism instance;
4. practitioner/preprint/historical surfaces only as supporting or adversarial evidence.

Do not generalize a product mechanism into a universal domain rule merely because it is concrete.
