# Actors — Deferred Relations

**Status:** current cross-vertical deferral surface  
**Rule:** these are relation questions discovered during Actors research. They are **not** solved here and are not evidence about Records or Work.

If a later authorized synthesis needs one of these relations, recover the relevant vertical's own current evidence chain independently rather than treating this file as cross-vertical evidence.

## DR1 — Work authority and decision participation

Questions:
- Who/what may select, approve, commit to, cancel or close a Work item?
- Can performer, decider, requester, approver and accountable party differ for the same Work?
- How do joint/separation-of-duty authorization rules interact with a Work decision?

Actors contribution only:
- these participation/authority positions are semantically separable.

Deferred because:
- what counts as a Work decision or lifecycle relation belongs to later Work recovery / cross-vertical synthesis.

## DR2 — Work obligation, omission and expected conduct

Questions:
- When does failure to perform expected Work become an Actor-relevant omission?
- Which Work facts establish what conduct was available, expected, required or due?

Actors contribution only:
- meaningful omission cannot be represented solely by absence of an action event; capability/context/expectation can matter.

Deferred because:
- the source of expectations, commitments and Work state is cross-vertical.

## DR3 — Commitments and represented principals

Questions:
- When an agent/representative commits to Work, to whom is the commitment institutionally bound?
- Which actor is performer versus principal, beneficiary or accountable party?

Actors contribution only:
- acting on behalf of another and acting for oneself are distinct;
- delegation and attribution can preserve a principal distinct from performer.

Deferred because:
- the semantics of the commitment itself may belong to Work and/or later synthesis.

## DR4 — Records as evidence of identity, role and authority

Questions:
- What persistent information establishes identity, appointment, role membership, authority, delegation, revocation or apparent-authority notice?
- What evidence is sufficient to reconstruct who was authorized at a historical time?

Actors contribution only:
- identity/role/authority are time- and context-sensitive relations whose historical state can matter.

Deferred because:
- evidence persistence, provenance, validity and retrieval are Records questions.

## DR5 — Records as evidence of action and attribution

Questions:
- What record demonstrates that a person/system performed, decided, approved, represented or failed to act?
- How are competing causal, provenance, legal and accountability attributions evidenced?

Actors contribution only:
- these attribution types are distinct and should not be inferred from one generic `actor` field.

Deferred because:
- record truth, provenance and evidence strength belong to Records / later synthesis.

## DR6 — Delegation lifecycle and persistent evidence

Questions:
- How are delegation scope, chain, activation, expiry, revocation and notice represented over time?
- How can apparent authority persist after actual authority ends and what persistent evidence is needed to know the difference?

Actors contribution only:
- delegation/authority have temporal and perspective-sensitive lifecycle semantics.

Deferred because:
- persistence/audit history is Records; triggering activities may be Work; combined semantics need later synthesis.

## DR7 — Organizational decisions and Work realization

Questions:
- How does a corporate/group-level decision relate to the individual or system activities that realize it?
- How should shared action differ from corporate decision attribution in Work coordination?

Actors contribution only:
- aggregate, shared/joint and corporate agency are not interchangeable;
- organizational decision procedure can be load-bearing.

Deferred because:
- coordination/execution topology belongs to Work and cross-vertical synthesis.

## DR8 — Non-human systems, tools and Work performance

Questions:
- When should a software/AI system be treated as performer, tool, delegated representative, provenance agent or component of a joint system for a given Work episode?
- Who retains authority, obligation and accountability around automated Work?

Actors contribution only:
- operational participation and normative/legal attribution are separable.

Deferred because:
- the exact relationship to Work units, tools and environments exceeds this independent vertical.

## DR9 — Affected/beneficiary/rights-holder relations

Questions:
- Are parties affected by Work or benefiting from it part of the future Actor surface, Work context, governance, or another relation layer?

Actors contribution only:
- a semantically relevant party need not be the performer or decision-maker.

Deferred because:
- deciding the owning vertical would itself be cross-vertical architecture/synthesis.
