**Status:** preferred shortest current orientation

## Current result in one sentence

`Actors` is useful as a provisional problem-region label, but the research does **not** support one self-sufficient `Actor` primitive; later work must preserve a relational, multi-anchor separation among **bearers/identity, roles/positions, context/time, situations and meaningful non-action, participation/decision roles, capability/competence, permission/authority/obligation, representation/delegation, typed attribution/responsibility/accountability, and shared/corporate/non-human agency levels**.

## Most important non-collapse rules

- identity ≠ role ≠ current participation;
- capability/competence ≠ permission/authority ≠ obligation;
- performer ≠ decider ≠ authorizer ≠ principal/representative ≠ accountable party;
- own-behalf action ≠ delegation ≠ impersonation;
- positive action ≠ the whole agency surface: omission/refraining can matter;
- causal/provenance/legal attribution ≠ responsibility ≠ accountability;
- mere aggregation ≠ shared/joint action ≠ organized corporate/group agency;
- operational software/AI action ≠ legal or moral personhood/attribution;
- these relations can be context- and time-sensitive.

## What changed most

The strongest non-Actor-centred alternative initially used `action event + participants`. Targeted omission research falsified literal event-centrism: meaningful failure to act can matter without a positive event. The stronger current frame is **situation/episode + available/expected conduct + typed action/decision/omission relations**, while persistent bearers and standing relations remain separate anchors.
