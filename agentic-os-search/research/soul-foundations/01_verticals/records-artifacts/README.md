5. **L4A — adversarial audit**: counterchecks + exact historical pass blob when the result must be challenged.
6. **L4B — evidence trace**: source ledger when claim provenance/source scope matters.
7. **L5 — primary source**: standard/specification/paper/guidance for exact or load-bearing claims.

`STATE.md` is a currentness/status gate, not a depth layer. `INDEX.md` is the routing table. `DOCUMENT-STATUS.md` classifies direct-found files. `CURRENT-REVISION.md` binds the current surfaces to exact blobs.

## Core reading rule

Higher layers are deliberately lossy derived access views. They make reading cheaper; they do not replace lower layers or become stronger evidence because they are easier to retrieve.

Descend only as far as the question requires. If an omitted caveat can change a decision, a claim is load-bearing, two views conflict, or exact source scope matters, descend rather than filling the gap by assumption.
