## Shortest answer

The strongest current top-level research map is **four broad problem grounds**, not five peer grounds:

```text
Environment / Operating Reality                 [working label]
    ├─ Operating World / Work Domain
    └─ Work-Support Environment
         └─ Technical Work Environment

Information / Representation State
    └─ Records / Persistent Information

Work

Actors / Agency–Participation–Authority

+ typed relations / control / transitions
```

The user accepted this on 2026-09-02 as the project's **working research hypothesis**. This closes the adequacy-evaluation gate only. It is not accepted as architecture, final ontology, schema or universal truth.

## What changed from the original independent studies

The first adequacy work found:
- actual domain/world state was missing from the original four independent research questions;
- Records was too narrow for the full top-level information problem;
- relation/control is essential composition fabric.

A later hierarchy challenge then found a better level alignment:

> **World/domain and Work-support are not the same thing, but they are better treated as explicit sibling branches/roles under one wider Environment-like problem ground.**

Do **not** infer that Technical Work Environment is universally inside the product/system's operating world. Enabling/development/test systems may sit outside the system-of-interest's operational environment.

## Environment branch distinction

### Operating World / Work Domain
What actual reality is the Work trying to understand, change, maintain, navigate or affect?

### Work-Support Environment
What realized conditions, capabilities, resources and interfaces make the Work possible, constrained, observable, reproducible, safe or unavailable?

`Technical Work Environment` is the existing deep technical specialization of the support branch.

## Information correction

Records correctly studies persistent information, but top-level reasoning also needs observations/signals, beliefs/models/claims, transient/selective context, uncertainty/conflict, evidence/decision representations and decisions about persistence.

Therefore Records remains a deep branch of the broader `Information / Representation State` working hypothesis.

## Work and Actors

Work and Actors remain supported as top-level problem grounds. Their completed independent corpora are strong evidence inputs, but the acceptance of the four-ground hierarchy does **not** automatically prove that their present research depth is the final depth required for SOUL/Development OS.

## Relation/control result

Authority, provenance, dependency, observation, access, feedback, verification, delegation, constraint and transition semantics compose the grounds through typed relations/control structures. Current evidence does not justify one generic peer `Governance/Control` ground.

## Verification limit

The hierarchy refinement is same-model adversarial synthesis with cross-lineage evidence, **not independent verification**. No external source independently validates this exact four-ground hierarchy.
