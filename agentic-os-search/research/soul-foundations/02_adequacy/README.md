# Four-Foundation Adequacy Inquiry

## Purpose

Test whether the four independently researched problem regions — Records, Work, Actors and Technical Work Environment — provide a sufficient and productive top-level analytical/problem-ground decomposition for later SOUL and Development OS reasoning.

This package does **not** assume the four-area hypothesis is true and does not define a SOUL ontology or architecture.

## Central question

> Do Records, Work, Actors and Technical Work Environment cover the first-order problems that SOUL and Development OS must reason about, either within the four areas or through genuinely relational structure among them, without hiding a distinct missing first-order problem region?

## Two adequacy levels

1. **Project adequacy:** sufficient for the current SOUL and Development OS intent and the failure/problem history already observed in SOUL, OS Architect and KEEL.
2. **Generality adequacy:** sufficiently general for SOUL's broader general-purpose claim when challenged outside the project lineage.

A positive answer at level 1 does not establish level 2.

## Method

The inquiry proceeds in this order:

1. extract a problem inventory from SOUL / Development OS / OS Architect / KEEL **without organising it under the four foundation labels**;
2. only then map the resulting problems against the four foundations and their relations;
3. identify forced fits, unresolved leftovers, reducibility and recurrent relation/control structure;
4. attack the four-area hypothesis with rival decompositions and candidate fifth regions;
5. use external/cross-lineage holdouts and literature to challenge project-lineage blind spots;
6. judge coverage, separability and productive decomposition separately for SOUL and Development OS.

## Success criteria

The four-area hypothesis survives only if:

- decision-critical problem families are naturally representable inside the four areas or as genuine relations among them;
- no important problem repeatedly requires an artificial fit;
- no area is merely a disguised special case of another at the intended analytical level;
- each area opens coherent lower-level research/design questions;
- no materially better top-level decomposition dominates;
- the answer remains defensible after cross-lineage challenge.

## Guardrails

- `category`, `module`, `primitive`, `entity` and `ontology class` are not assumed synonyms for these research regions.
- SOUL and Development OS may receive different adequacy judgments.
- Governance, verification, coordination, policy and similar recurrent concerns are not automatically promoted to a fifth region; first test whether they are cross-cutting relation/control structures.
- The archived broad operating-world Environment research may be recovered as a falsifier/candidate missing region, but it is not evidence for Technical Work Environment.
- Research findings are not architecture decisions.
