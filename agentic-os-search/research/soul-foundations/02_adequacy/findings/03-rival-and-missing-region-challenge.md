# Finding 03 — Rival Decomposition and Missing-Region Challenge

**Status:** current adversarial comparative finding  
**Purpose:** challenge the current four-area hypothesis rather than optimize it.

## Evaluation criteria

Each rival is judged against:

1. **Coverage** — can it express decision-critical problem families without forced fit?
2. **Separability** — do its top-level regions ask different questions?
3. **Productivity** — does each region open coherent deeper research?
4. **Non-residuality** — does one region become an `everything else` bucket?
5. **Representation neutrality** — does the analytical split avoid prematurely dictating modules/classes?
6. **Generality** — can it survive materially different domains?
7. **Economy** — does it add a region only when the distinction cannot be safely represented through relations or a broader validated ground?

---

# R0 — Current four unchanged

1. Records / persistent information
2. Work
3. Actors
4. Technical Work Environment

## Strengths

- all four are independently researched, separable and productive;
- most SOUL/Development OS internal problem families map naturally;
- strong external convergence exists with work-system/process/participant/information/technology traditions.

## Failure

- actual external/domain state has no natural home;
- nonpersistent observation/belief/context semantics exceed Records' current scope.

## Verdict

**REJECT as a proven complete top-level decomposition.**

Retain all four as strong problem regions; reject only the completeness claim.

---

# R1 — Current four + Operating World / Work Domain

1. Records / persistent information
2. Work
3. Actors
4. Technical Work Environment
5. Operating World / Work Domain / Domain Reality
6. typed relation/control fabric across them (not counted as a peer ground)

## What the fifth region would ask

- What relevant state of affairs exists independently of the working system's current representations?
- What domain processes/dynamics can change independently?
- What objects/entities/conditions are being acted upon, affected or reasoned about?
- Which world conditions constrain feasible Work or outcome success?
- What effects did Work actually produce in the domain?
- What was observed versus what was actually true?

## Strengths

- directly closes P13 without redefining the four completed verticals;
- preserves the important distinction between **means/support** (Technical Work Environment) and **subject/operating world** (Work Domain);
- supported independently by CWA/WDA, agent–environment models, STAMP controlled-process reasoning and Activity Theory's object of activity;
- gives physical, social, market, clinical, operational and other domain states a non-Record home.

## Weakness

- P6 remains only partly solved because `observation/belief/context/knowledge` may still exceed persistent Records.
- `Operating World`, `Work Domain` and `Domain Reality` are not yet terminologically resolved.

## Verdict

**STRONGEST MINIMUM CORRECTION currently.**

This is a research hypothesis, not an accepted five-module architecture.

# R2 — Replace/broaden Records with Information / Representation

Candidate top-level ground:

> Information / Representation / Epistemic State

with Records as the persistent-information branch.

## What this broader ground would ask

- observations and signals;
- persistent Records;
- transient context;
- beliefs/models/claims;
- provenance and source binding;
- knowledge/uncertainty;
- transformations between observations, beliefs, records and derived views.

## Strengths

- naturally covers P6;
- aligns with Work System Theory's broad `information` element;
- aligns with POMDP observation/state distinctions, STAMP process models and modern agent perception/memory separation;
- avoids pretending every decision-relevant representation must be persisted.

## Risk

The current Records research is deep specifically because persistence creates distinctive identity/history/retrieval/retention obligations. Broadening too early could dilute those hard-won semantics into an overly general `information` bucket.

It is not yet established whether:
- nonpersistent representation semantics require a full peer region;
- they are better treated as Actor/Work/environment-local state plus typed observation/belief relations;
- or Records should become one subregion of a broader Information ground.

## Verdict

**LIVE CANDIDATE / REQUIRES ITS OWN FOUNDATION-LEVEL TEST BEFORE PROMOTION.**

Do not silently rename Records yet.

# R3 — Preserve four by broadening `Environment`

1. Records or Information
2. Work
3. Actors
4. Environment, containing both:
   - Technical Work Environment;
   - Operating World / Work Domain.

## Attraction

This would preserve a four-label top level.

## Problem

The two environment phenomena play materially different roles:

- Technical Work Environment is the support/means context through which Work becomes technically possible.
- Operating World / Work Domain is the reality/subject/context whose state constrains or is changed by Work.

One can act **through** the first **upon/about** the second.

Merging them merely because both are called `environment` risks recreating the exact scope collision that caused the first broad Environment pass to answer the wrong question.

The umbrella also risks becoming residual: everything outside Actor/Work/Records could be called Environment.

## Verdict

**REJECT as current preferred correction.**

A future higher-level umbrella may exist, but current evidence says the two subproblems must remain explicitly distinguishable.

# R4 — Demote Technical Work Environment to an enabling layer

Candidate:

1. domain/world;
2. actors;
3. work;
4. information/representation;

with tools/resources/infrastructure treated merely as implementation/enabling mechanisms.

## Attraction

This resembles classical agent/control decompositions where the action-support substrate is hidden below the agent–environment interface.

## Failure

The Technical Work Environment vertical showed substantial first-order semantics that do not disappear merely because infrastructure is enabling:
- support boundary;
- actual versus declared technical state;
- capability/resource/interface exposure;
- access/isolation/capacity;
- readiness/availability;
- environment identity/equivalence;
- lifecycle/rebuild/restore/migrate;
- hidden load-bearing dependencies.

SOUL explicitly performs capability formation and may construct task-specific operating systems. Development OS failures also repeatedly arise from mechanisms that are declared but not actually available/wired.

## Verdict

**REJECT as a universal demotion.**

Technical Work Environment may not become a product module, but it remains a first-order analytical problem ground for systems that must form/control their own working support context.

# R5 — Relation/control as the fifth peer region

Candidate peer:
- Governance / Relations / Control / Coordination.

## Evidence in favour

Many important problems are inherently relational:
- authority over Work;
- provenance from Work/Actors to Records;
- access to environment resources;
- dependency and coordination;
- observation/feedback;
- verification/acceptance;
- delegation;
- transition/evolution control.

External PROV and STAMP traditions also make relation/control structure central.

## Why peer status is not yet justified

`Relation` is structurally different from the things/problem regions it connects.

A dependency without Work, authority without Actors/decision scope, provenance without entities/activities, or access without an Actor/resource has no independent subject matter.

Likewise `Control` often names:
- a relation;
- a constraint;
- a Work activity;
- an Actor authority position;
- a feedback structure;
- a technical mechanism.

Promoting it to a peer bucket could collapse these typed differences.

## Verdict

**REJECT as fifth peer foundation; ACCEPT as mandatory cross-foundation fabric.**

Later modelling must explicitly represent typed relations/control/transition semantics. The need for a fabric is a strong result; its exact representation is not decided.

---

# R6 — No stable problem regions; relational graph only

Candidate claim:

> Everything can be represented as arbitrary entities/states/relations; fixed foundation regions are unnecessary.

## Strength

- maximum representational flexibility;
- avoids category essentialism;
- compatible with relational results inside Actors and Technical Work Environment.

## Failure

Representation reducibility does not erase recurring analytical questions.

The vertical research repeatedly showed that even when a phenomenon can be represented compositionally/relationally, its own failure modes remain:
- Records persistence/retrieval/retention;
- Work selection/coordination/completion;
- Actors authority/delegation/accountability;
- Technical Work Environment readiness/equivalence/lifecycle;
- candidate Operating World state/observation/effects.

A relation-only meta-model gives insufficient guidance about which questions must be investigated and which distinctions must not be collapsed.

## Verdict

**REJECT as an adequate research decomposition; retain relational representation as a later implementation possibility.**

# Scenario stress test

## Software development / Development OS

Typical relevant reality:
- source/repository/decision/evidence state is largely persistable information;
- tools, GitHub, CI, models and local execution form technical support context;
- work and actors are explicit.

Result:
- current four cover most first-order problems naturally.
- a separate Operating World is less often forced because much of the work domain is informational/technical and can be represented through Records + Technical Work Environment.

**Implication:** current four may be boundedly adequate for Development OS even if they are not general-purpose complete.

## Clinical decision support

- patient physiological condition is not identical to the EHR Record;
- clinician/team/AI roles are Actors;
- diagnosis/treatment/monitoring are Work;
- EHR/lab reports are Records;
- clinical IT/tools are Technical Work Environment.

The patient's actual condition remains a distinct domain state.

**Implication:** R0 forced fit; R1 natural.

## Autonomous/robotic operation

- road/room/object state changes partly independently;
- sensors provide partial observations;
- robot/human/system roles act;
- mission/planning/execution is Work;
- logs/maps are Records;
- compute/tool stack is Technical Work Environment.

**Implication:** Operating World is unavoidable; observation/representation semantics are also first-order.

## Market/analytical decision support

- market/legal/customer state can change independently;
- retrieved quotes/reports are representations with freshness/coverage limits;
- analysis and decisions are Work;
- people/organizations/systems are Actors;
- APIs/data tooling form Technical Work Environment.

**Implication:** world/source state and representation cannot be safely collapsed.

## Scientific/experimental work

- phenomenon/sample/specimen state is the subject of work;
- measurements/observations may later become Records;
- instruments/lab compute form Technical Work Environment;
- experiments/analysis are Work;
- researchers/organizations are Actors.

**Implication:** R1 again gives the cleaner separation.

# Rival-round result

## Current four

**KEEP as independently established analytical grounds.**

## Operating World / Work Domain

**PROMOTE from weak hypothesis to strong candidate missing first-order problem region.**

The evidence is now cross-lineage and structurally different.

## Information / Representation beyond Records

**KEEP as unresolved candidate level correction.**

There is enough evidence that the problem exists, but not enough yet to decide whether it requires:
- a fifth/sixth peer region;
- a broader parent over Records;
- or typed observation/belief/context relations distributed across other grounds.

## Relation / control fabric

**ESTABLISH at research level as structurally necessary for composition, but not as a peer foundation category.**

## Interim decomposition hypothesis

The strongest current research frame is therefore:

```text
Strong problem grounds already established:
- Records / persistent information
- Work
- Actors
- Technical Work Environment

Strong missing-ground candidate:
- Operating World / Work Domain / Domain Reality

Mandatory composition structure:
- typed relations / control / transitions across grounds

Still unresolved:
- whether Information / Representation should become a broader ground above/around Records
```

This is not yet a SOUL or Development OS architecture.
