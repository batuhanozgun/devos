# 2. Specific falsifiers

## F1 — Environment is just `everything outside`

Rejected.

Irrelevant external facts do not become load-bearing Environment. Boundary alone is insufficient; material coupling/relevance to target/effect or execution conditions is required.

## F2 — Environment is just `everything influencing Work`

Rejected.

This swallows Actors, Work, Information and relation/control. Environmental participation does not transfer semantic ownership.

## F3 — Environment can be dissolved into Information

Rejected.

Actual conditions can differ from observations/models/specifications/Records and can change while representation remains unchanged.

## F4 — Environment can be dissolved into Work

Rejected.

A realized condition can exist/change independently of Work lifecycle and affect many Work units. Work completion does not imply actual effect.

## F5 — Environment can be dissolved into Actor capability

Rejected.

Same Actor capability can yield different situated actionability under changed conditions/resources/interfaces.

## F6 — TWE is just Domain/Operating World

Rejected.

NIST/ISO enabling systems can support lifecycle Work without belonging to the system-of-interest's operational environment.

## F7 — Domain reality and execution conditions must therefore be peer top-level grounds

Not supported as current best hierarchy.

They retain different lower questions but share enough parent problem grammar, role switching and level symmetry for one broad ground to be more economical/productive.

## F8 — third `ambient` branch required

Not supported by current holdouts.

Ambient conditions become analytically relevant for a reason that maps to Domain/Effect and/or Work Conditions.

## F9 — `support` is sufficient label for second branch

Weakened/rejected as universal phrase.

Physical hazards, scarcity, adverse conditions and prohibitions are constitutive execution conditions without being naturally positive support.

## F10 — Context is a better parent

Rejected.

Context is too broad/relevance/representation-oriented and can include Actor identity, activity, time, information and other semantics. Environment must preserve realized-condition side.

## F11 — Environment requires one intrinsic object type/module

Rejected.

The same concrete thing can occupy multiple roles, cross boundaries or become the Work target. A relational/compositional architecture remains viable.

# 7. Stop judgment

## Environment ground

**BOUNDEDLY SUFFICIENT FOR CURRENT GROUND-DEEPENING PURPOSE; READY FOR USER EVALUATION.**

## Why stop broad Environment search now

The information trajectory changed:
- prior evidence recovery showed two deep lower traditions;
- boundary testing preserved the parent;
- nontechnical-condition testing produced a material branch correction;
- third-branch and rival tests did not expose another region;
- later cross-domain holdouts mainly clarified dual roles and exclusions;
- topic-preservation check found no unexplained omission.

Further broad search without a new falsifier is now more likely to refine terminology/domain profiles than to change the first-order ground structure.

## Reopen Environment if

- a later domain exposes a realized condition that cannot fit Domain/Effect or Work Conditions without forced stretching;
- the Environment parent repeatedly steals Actor/Work/Information semantics;
- deeper Information research shows actual-vs-representation boundary is wrongly drawn;
- deeper Work/Actors research exposes a missing Environment-side variable;
- an independent review presents a materially stronger rival decomposition;
- a new source tradition materially overturns the support/condition or boundary result.

---

# Final audit statement

The current evidence supports closure of the Environment ground **for this research stage**, not a claim of universal ontology or architecture completeness.
