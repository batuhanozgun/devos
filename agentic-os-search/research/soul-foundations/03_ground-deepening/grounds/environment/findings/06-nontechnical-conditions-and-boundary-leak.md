# Environment Finding 06 — Nontechnical Conditions and Boundary-Leak Check

**Status:** current Environment-ground finding  
**Question:** does broadening beyond Technical Work Environment create a residual bucket or a missing nontechnical support family?

## Current finding

Nontechnical work conditions are real Environment concerns, but they do **not** currently force a third first-order Environment branch.

They strengthen the corrected second role:

> **Work Conditions / Enabling–Constraining Environment**

rather than a narrower `technical support` or positive `support` concept.

## Physical conditions

Human-factors/ergonomics and SEIPS repeatedly preserve physical environmental factors such as:
- lighting;
- temperature;
- noise;
- vibration;
- air quality;
- space/layout;
- physical access.

These can alter safety, effort, feasibility and performance without being the Work's target.

They therefore fit the execution-condition role when they matter because Work must be performed under them.

## Social and organizational conditions

O*NET uses `Work Context` for physical and social factors influencing work; broader ergonomics models include psychosocial interaction.

The project must not infer `social/organizational = Environment-owned`.

Preserve semantic ownership:
- person/organization identity, capability, role, authority, obligation → Actors;
- schedules, assignments, coordination/workflow → Work where appropriate;
- policies/rules/models/texts → Information/relations as appropriate;
- **realized situational effects** of these structures on the focal Work may participate in Environment as conditions.

Example:
- understaffing can create a realized capacity condition for focal Work;
- the identities, assignments and responsibilities of staff remain Actor/Work questions.

## Institutional/regulatory conditions

A law, contract, market rule or institutional regime can constrain feasible action.

Environment may capture the realized condition:
- permit is currently required;
- action is currently prohibited;
- market is closed;
- jurisdiction applies;
- facility access is suspended.

But Environment does not own:
- authoritative text/interpretation;
- issuer/enforcer authority;
- compliance Work;
- normative justification.

Thus no generic `institutional Environment object` class is needed.

## Economic/resource conditions

Budget, scarcity, supply, availability and capacity can shape execution. Their Environment role depends on realized condition and coupling.

Do not equate:
- accounting/financial representation with actual resource availability;
- strategic priority with environmental condition;
- Actor ownership with resource state.

## Tools and support personnel

Tools, services and people may all mediate/support Work, but their intrinsic first-order semantics remain elsewhere when relevant.

A field technician's presence can be a realized support condition while the technician remains an Actor.
A manual can be a support resource while its content/provenance remains Information.
A test harness can be a technical support condition while its own maintenance Work remains Work.

## Boundary-leak rule

The Environment ground may legitimately record that a realized condition is load-bearing without claiming the whole semantic identity of the thing producing that condition.

This is the key anti-leak rule:

> **environmental participation ≠ semantic ownership**

## Third-family test

Candidates tested:
- physical environment;
- social environment;
- institutional environment;
- organizational environment;
- economic/resource environment;
- ambient/background context.

None currently requires its own first-order sibling because each can be analyzed through:
- Domain/Effect role;
- Work-Condition role;
- or both;
while retaining non-environment semantics in other grounds.

## Result

Broad Environment remains coherent if the second branch is framed as **realized enabling–constraining work conditions**, not merely technical support.

The current evidence therefore suggests:

```text
Environment / Operating Reality
    ├─ Domain / Effect Reality
    └─ Work Conditions / Enabling–Constraining Environment
         └─ Technical Work Environment
```

without adding a third peer branch for physical/social/institutional conditions.
