# Environment Finding 05 — Actionability, Resources, Constraints and Effects

**Status:** current Environment-ground finding

## Central result

Environment matters because realized conditions change **what can happen**, but possibility/actionability is not an intrinsic Environment property.

Stronger rule:

> **Situated actionability is relational: it depends on Environment conditions together with Actor capability/authority, Work requirements/purpose and available interaction surfaces.**

Therefore Environment research should preserve the condition-side variables without claiming ownership of the full actionability relation.

## Affordance/actionability evidence

Ecological-psychology affordance traditions characterize action possibilities relationally between environmental properties and the perceiver/actor's capacities.

This independently supports the project's earlier distinction:

> Actor capability ≠ situated actionability.

A ladder's geometry, a network's connectivity, a machine's current state or a road's friction can alter what is possible, but only relative to an Actor/system and intended action.

## Environment-side primitives/dimensions

When material, distinguish:

### Conditions / constraints
Physical, technical, institutional or resource conditions that bound possible execution/outcomes.

### Resources / capacities
Load-bearing material, energy, compute, bandwidth, time-window, physical-space or service capacity whose current availability/sufficiency matters.

A universal resource ontology is not established; resourcehood is role-relative.

### Interfaces / coupling surfaces
Surfaces through which sensing, action, exchange, dependence or support occurs.

The interface may be physical, technical, procedural or institutionally mediated. Concrete interface semantics remain domain-specific.

### Disturbances / interference
Changes not generated by the focal Work that alter state, feasibility, risk or outcomes.

### Opportunities / affordances
Environment-side structures that participate in action possibilities. The final affordance/actionability judgment remains relational.

## Positive and negative environmental roles

The second Environment branch must cover both:
- enablers/support: available tools, services, workspace, access routes, infrastructure;
- constraints/degraders: heat, noise, congestion, resource scarcity, unavailable services, adverse weather, interference, regulatory prohibition in its realized-effect role.

This is another reason `Work-Support Environment` is too positively framed as a universal branch label.

## Effects and outcome grounding

A Work episode can be internally `complete` while the intended environmental effect is absent, partial, delayed or reversed.

Examples:
- treatment administered but patient physiology unchanged;
- deployment completed but production behavior wrong;
- maintenance procedure closed but machine remains unsafe;
- order submitted but market execution differs;
- experiment run but specimen compromised.

Therefore:

> **Work state/closure ≠ actual environmental effect/outcome.**

Environment must support reasoning about before/after/evolving domain or condition state independently of Work's internal control state.

## Causal caution

Not every correlated condition is an Environment cause.

The ground should distinguish at least:
- actual condition/state;
- known or hypothesized causal influence;
- interaction path/interface;
- observed outcome/effect.

Causal explanation may require domain-specific models and Information/evidence. The Environment ground preserves the relata and dynamics; it does not make every influence claim true.

## Failure families exposed

- assumed capability without required Environment condition;
- resource exists but is unreachable/unpermitted/unavailable/insufficient;
- hidden shared dependency or contention;
- constraint/hazard ignored;
- interface/coupling failure;
- disturbance not represented in feasibility model;
- Work completion mistaken for real-world success;
- environmental side effect/partial effect not tracked.

## Result

Environment's contribution to later SOUL/Development OS reasoning is not a list of things around Work. It is a structured account of **realized conditions/dynamics/couplings that participate in feasibility, risk and actual effects**, while actionability and causal judgments remain explicitly relational/evidential.
