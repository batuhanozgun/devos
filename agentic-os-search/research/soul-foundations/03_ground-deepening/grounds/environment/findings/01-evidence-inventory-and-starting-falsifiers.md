# Environment Finding 01 — Evidence Inventory and Starting Falsifiers

**Status:** current Environment-ground finding  
**Scope:** prior-evidence recovery and pre-search falsifier freeze; not final synthesis

## Purpose

Prevent the Environment pass from merely restating either the archived operating-world corpus or the Technical Work Environment corpus.

## Recovered project evidence

### Operating-world evidence

The archived operating-world research established, for its original question:
- system-of-interest and boundary are load-bearing;
- actual world/process state is distinct from observation, belief/model and persistent Record;
- world conditions/dynamics can change independently of Work state;
- actionability depends on both Actor-side and environmental conditions;
- tools/resources/infrastructure are boundary-relative roles rather than intrinsic Environment object classes;
- a first-order boundary-relative operating-world problem survives reduction.

### Technical Work Environment evidence

The completed TWE research established:
- a technical environment is a bounded realized support context, not a tool list;
- actual support surface depends on effective capabilities/resources/interfaces/constraints/current state;
- declared, discovered/observed and actual realized state are distinct;
- support closure, identity/equivalence, lifecycle, readiness, availability, capacity, sharedness/isolation, dynamic resolution and observability are productive first-order technical-support semantics;
- a manifest/profile is optional; realization is primary.

### Adequacy hierarchy evidence

The hierarchy refinement established the working hypothesis:

```text
Environment / Operating Reality
    ├─ Operating World / Work Domain
    └─ Work-Support Environment
         └─ Technical Work Environment
```

but explicitly left the parent semantics and nontechnical support incompletely researched.

## Starting falsifiers frozen before new synthesis

The current Environment ground must be revised if any of the following survives adversarial testing:

1. target/domain reality and work-support conditions cannot share a useful higher problem grammar without becoming residual;
2. a third first-order Environment region repeatedly appears outside both target/domain and execution/support roles;
3. nontechnical work conditions cannot be represented without absorbing Work, Actors or Information;
4. `Environment` can be fully reduced to Work + Information + Actors + relations without losing independent realized conditions;
5. boundary/reference-point dependence makes the parent too unstable to support reusable analysis;
6. technical-support semantics are so specialized that treating TWE as a deep branch hides a separate peer top-level problem;
7. the parent label causes systematic conflation of actual conditions with specified/represented context;
8. a materially stronger decomposition explains clinical, physical, digital, scientific, institutional and hybrid cases with less forced fit.

## Initial expected risk

The strongest risk is **not** that Environment disappears. Both prior corpora already show independently changing realized conditions that are not Work/Actor/Information facts.

The stronger risk is that the current branch name `Work-Support Environment` is too narrow: many load-bearing conditions constrain, disturb, degrade or endanger Work without naturally being described as support.

This risk is carried forward rather than resolved here.

## Guardrail

This inventory does not treat prior project syntheses as independent validation. The current pass must use external/cross-lineage evidence and new counterexamples before accepting, changing or closing the Environment hierarchy.
