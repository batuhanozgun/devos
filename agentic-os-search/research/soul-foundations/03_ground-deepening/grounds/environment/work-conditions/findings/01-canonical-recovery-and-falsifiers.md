# Work Conditions Finding 01 — Canonical Recovery and Frozen Falsifiers

**Status:** pre-search control finding
**Date:** 2026-09-03

## Inherited evidence

Current Environment research already established bounded support for two analytical roles:
- Domain Reality — what realized reality the Work is about / acts against / judges outcomes against;
- Work Conditions — what realized circumstances materially enable, constrain, degrade, endanger or otherwise shape focal Work execution.

The Environment parent also established:
- positive support is too narrow; adverse/degrading conditions matter equally;
- environment participation is role-relative and load-bearing, not an intrinsic object taxonomy;
- resource existence is not equivalent to availability/reachability/permission/sufficiency;
- situated actionability is relational across Environment, Actor and Work;
- the same concrete system may be Domain Reality for one Work and Work Condition for another;
- Technical Work Environment is deep prior evidence for technical support contexts, not automatically the universal Work-Conditions decomposition.

## Inherited hypotheses — not findings of this pass

The following enter as hypotheses to attack:
- `Work Conditions` is a coherent first-order Environment branch;
- `Enabling–Constraining Environment` is explanatory but may be too long as a permanent label;
- Technical Work Environment may be one specialization below Work Conditions;
- physical, technical, institutional, social and resource conditions may be types rather than universal children;
- availability/readiness/sufficiency/capacity may be condition states/dimensions rather than children.

## Frozen falsifiers

F1–F15 are preserved exactly in `../WORK-CONDITIONS-RESEARCH-PLAN.md` before external search.

Most decision-critical attacks:
1. reduce Work Conditions to Domain Reality facts + relation to Work;
2. promote a stable child split if one survives across materially different domains;
3. challenge Technical Work Environment's placement as immediate child versus specialization;
4. prevent Work, Actor and Information semantics from leaking into the node;
5. close as a bounded leaf if all candidate splits behave as dimensions/types/relations rather than stable recursive research branches.
