## Research question

What realized circumstances materially shape whether and how focal Work can be performed, and does `Work Conditions / Enabling–Constraining Environment` form a coherent first-order Environment branch with stable immediate children, or is a stronger rename/reduction/decomposition needed?

## Main framing attacks

1. Is `Work Conditions` a genuine research problem, or merely `Domain Reality + relation to Work` under another name?
2. Is the paired label `Work Conditions / Enabling–Constraining Environment` too broad or too awkward; is a shorter label justified?
3. Does the node concern only execution feasibility, or also quality, safety, risk, pace, reliability and permissible method?
4. Are positive enablers and negative constraints distinct children, or two directions of the same condition relation?
5. Are resources/capacity, access/interfaces, hazards/interference, physical conditions, institutional constraints and support services genuine first-order children or only condition types/dimensions?
6. Does `Technical Work Environment` deserve immediate-child status, or is it one domain specialization/profile among several?
7. Can social/organizational support conditions be represented without absorbing Actor identity/capability/authority semantics?
8. Can regulations/procedures/policies constrain Work without collapsing their textual/epistemic form into Information or their authority into Actors?
9. Are time windows, schedules, deadlines and timing constraints Work-local structure or Work Conditions, and under what reference rule?
10. How should current availability/readiness/sufficiency/capacity be distinguished from mere existence of a resource/service/tool/person?
11. When does an ambient fact become load-bearing Work Condition rather than irrelevant context?
12. How does the same concrete system switch between Domain Reality and Work-Condition roles across focal Work without duplication?
13. Do hidden dependencies, shared contention, degraded support and partial failure expose a distinct child or cross-cut several condition types?
14. Does the node generalize across software, physical maintenance, clinical work, field science, logistics, institutional/legal work and emergency response?

## Frozen falsifiers

Before external search, the current candidate must be reopened or changed if evidence shows any of the following:

F1. `Work Conditions` cannot be separated from Domain Reality except by arbitrary naming and adds no distinct first-order research question/failure surface.
F2. A stronger parent label than `Work Conditions` is needed to avoid implying only employment/job context or only static conditions.
F3. Enablers versus constraints form stable independent recursive branches across materially different domains.
F4. Resources/capacity form a stable child distinct from other enabling/constraining conditions.
F5. Access/interfaces/coupling form a stable child rather than a cross-cutting relation/dimension.
F6. Hazards/interference/degradation form a stable child rather than negative states of several condition families.
F7. Physical/technical/social/institutional/logistical categories form a stronger universal child decomposition than problem-role decomposition.
F8. Technical Work Environment is a necessary universal immediate child rather than a specialization profile.
F9. Availability/readiness/sufficiency/current capacity require their own first-order child.
F10. Timing/windows/schedules constitute a Work-Conditions-local child rather than Work structure or cross-cutting temporal constraint.
F11. Actor-provided support cannot be represented without leaking Actor semantics into Work Conditions.
F12. Institutional/regulatory constraints cannot be represented without leaking Information/Actor semantics into Work Conditions.
F13. Role switching between Domain Reality and Work Conditions breaks the node boundary.
F14. A materially different domain exposes a recurrent first-order region outside the candidate map.
F15. No stable immediate child survives; the node should close as a bounded leaf.

## Holdouts

At minimum test:
- software development with remote services, permissions, secrets, CI and partial outages;
- physical maintenance under heat/noise/hazard/access constraints;
- clinical treatment with equipment, staffing/service and facility constraints;
- field/laboratory science with instruments, weather, site access and sample-handling constraints;
- logistics/operations with capacity, route, timing and infrastructure constraints;
- institutional/legal work under filing windows, jurisdictional/procedural constraints and service access;
- emergency response with temporary resources, permissions, hazards and changing control boundaries;
- same concrete system as Domain Reality for one Work and Work Condition for another;
- resource exists but is unavailable/unreachable/unpermitted/insufficient;
- nontechnical work where no meaningful Technical Work Environment exists.

## Closure criteria

Close when:
- node coherence or stronger reduction/rename is explicit;
- immediate-child map or justified leaf is explicit;
- Technical Work Environment placement is explicitly tested;
- positive/negative, resource/access/hazard/type-based rivals are classified;
- Domain Reality, Work, Actors and Information boundaries survive;
- role switching and load-bearing relevance threshold are explained;
- diverse holdouts stop producing new first-order regions;
- load-bearing claims have recoverable source/view evidence;
- residuals and deferred relations are explicit;
- broad search shows declining structural information gain.

Do not research any discovered child recursively in this pass. Do not activate another node after closure without a later user transition decision.
