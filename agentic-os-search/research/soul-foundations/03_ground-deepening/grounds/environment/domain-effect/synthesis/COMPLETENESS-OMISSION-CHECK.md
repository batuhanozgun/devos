# 2. Specific falsifiers

## F1 — Domain and Effect are equal reality regions

Rejected.

Actual results are domain conditions/trajectories; causal effect is relational/counterfactual/evidential.

## F2 — Effect is required for Domain Reality

Rejected by observational/diagnostic Work.

## F3 — Domain Reality is every Work target

Rejected because Work can target Information/Actors/Work-local structures without transferring first-order semantics to Environment.

## F4 — Domain Reality must be representation-independent in every ontological sense

Rejected/weakened by social/institutional cases.

Current rule only requires separation from **particular representations/observations/Records**.

## F5 — State and Dynamics are merely descriptive dimensions and should not become children

Not supported as the strongest current research hierarchy.

They pass child-promotion because they define different questions, failures and later reasoning obligations across domains.

## F6 — Structure must be a third child

Not currently supported.

Current structural facts can be represented as realized configuration; structural constraints can cut across State/Dynamics; no holdout forced a third branch.

## F7 — Constraints/laws/invariants must be a third child

Not currently supported.

They constrain possible states and/or transitions; their represented forms can also belong partly to Information.

## F8 — type-based physical/digital/social/etc. children are required

Rejected as universal level because hybrid domains cross those types and the same State/Dynamics questions recur in each.

## F9 — Controlled Process / Target System is sufficient universal replacement

Rejected as too narrow for observational, scientific and broad institutional domains.

## F10 — relation-only framing eliminates Domain Reality

Rejected as research reduction. Relations can later encode the result but do not force explicit recovery of actual condition and evolution.

- Dynamics research exposes a missing co-equal region;
- a materially different domain cannot map to State/Dynamics plus explicit cross-cutting relations;
- deeper Information/Work/Actors research reveals boundary leakage;
