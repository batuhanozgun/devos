## Executive result

The node survives, but with a material rename and decomposition correction.

Starting hypothesis:

```text
Domain / Effect Reality
```

Strongest current bounded result of this pass:

```text
Domain Reality
    ├─ Domain State / Configuration
    └─ Domain Dynamics / Processes
```

`Effect / Outcome` is **not** supported as a separate immediate reality child. Actual results are realized domain conditions/trajectories; causal effect and outcome judgment are relations involving Work, alternative/intervening conditions, evidence and success criteria.

These children are research/problem branches, not ontology classes or exclusive kinds of things.

---

# 1. Why the node was renamed

The combined `Domain / Effect Reality` label joined two concepts that do not behave symmetrically.

## Domain Reality survives

A first-order problem remains around the realized reality whose condition and evolution are materially at issue for focal Work.

This includes, depending on the analysis:
- patient physiology/pathology;
- actual machine/system condition;
- physical terrain/traffic;
- specimen/phenomenon;
- actual deployed software/system behavior;
- market/business conditions;
- realized institutional/legal status;
- hybrid physical/digital/social systems.

The node is reference-relative and optional. It does not mean `everything Work is about`.

## Effect Reality does not survive as a sibling/child

Three evidence lines converge:

1. **state-transition traditions:** actions/events/disturbances produce changes in a domain/world/process state;
2. **causal inference:** causal effect is a contrast among outcomes under alternative interventions/exposures, not merely the observed later condition;
3. **observational Work:** meaningful inquiry can concern a real domain while intentionally producing no effect on it.

Therefore the stronger node label is simply:

> **Domain Reality**

---

# 2. Domain Reality boundary

Strongest bounded formulation:

> **Domain Reality is the reference-relative realized condition and evolving process-space that is materially at issue in the Environment role of focal Work, independently of any particular observation, model, assertion or Record of it.**

Important qualifications:
- the domain may be external or internal to a wider system boundary if it is the relevant realized target/domain for the focal Work;
- the same concrete thing can participate in Information, Work, Actors, Work Conditions and Domain Reality under different analytical roles;
- not every Work item requires a material Domain Reality target;
- some social/institutional realities may be constituted through collective rules/attitudes/representations while remaining non-identical to any particular representation of them.

Reject:
- Domain Reality = every target of Work;
- Domain Reality = everything outside the system;
- Domain Reality = representation-independent reality in every philosophical sense;
- Domain Reality = a fixed object class.

---

# 3. Immediate Child A — Domain State / Configuration

Primary question:

> **What realized domain condition/configuration obtains at the relevant time and scope?**

This branch covers, when material:
- constituents currently present;
- current properties/qualities/statuses;
- current relations/topology/configuration;
- current spatial arrangement;
- current institutional/social standing in its domain role;
- actual current condition relevant to outcome or subsequent evolution.

`State` is deliberately architecture-neutral. It does not require one state vector or database record.

## Failure surfaces

- wrong or stale current condition;
- omitted load-bearing constituent/relation;
- wrong actual topology/configuration;
- wrong state granularity;
- represented/observed state substituted for realized state;
- identity/continuity assumptions silently alter what is treated as the same domain entity/situation.

## Why Structure is not a third immediate child

Structure is important, but current evidence does not require a universal separate `Domain Structure` problem region at this level.

Realized constituents/relations/topology can be researched as part of current configuration. More invariant constraints can cut across state and dynamics. External structure taxonomies vary strongly by domain, and a separate universal structure child increases the risk of stealing Information/Actor semantics in digital and social/institutional cases.

This can be reopened during deeper State research if forced fit emerges.

---

# 4. Immediate Child B — Domain Dynamics / Processes

Primary question:

> **What realized processes, interactions, events and disturbances make Domain Reality persist, evolve or transition through time?**

This branch covers, when material:
- endogenous domain processes;
- interactions/flows/feedback;
- exogenous disturbances/events;
- transitions caused partly by focal Work actions;
- transitions caused independently of focal Work;
- temporal ordering/rates/delays;
- path dependence/hysteresis;
- recovery/degradation/evolution trajectories.

## Failure surfaces

- static snapshot used where trajectory matters;
- omitted disturbance;
- wrong process/transition mechanism;
- feedback/nonlinearity/path dependence ignored;
- temporal ordering/rate wrong;
- Work action assumed to be the only source of change;
- delayed or indirect consequences missed.

---

# 5. Why State and Dynamics are promoted rather than left as dimensions

The hierarchical method explicitly warned against promoting common dimensions merely because they recur.

The distinction passed the stronger child-promotion test.

## Distinct first-order question

- State / Configuration → **what is actually the case?**
- Dynamics / Processes → **how is it changing / what makes it change?**

## Distinct failure mode

Correct current state does not imply correct trajectory/process model. Correct dynamics knowledge does not imply the current realized condition is known.

## Cross-domain recurrence

The split remains useful in:
- clinical diagnosis/treatment;
- physical maintenance;
- scientific observation/experiment;
- markets/business;
- robotics;
- digital/software systems;
- institutional/legal domains.

## Cross-lineage recurrence

Control/state-space, situation-calculus/MDP, systems-thinking, systems-modeling and formal-ontology traditions all preserve some form of condition/entity versus behavior/process/change distinction even though they disagree on exact ontology and representation.

## Downstream consequence

SOUL/Development OS later needs to distinguish:
- current actual condition;
- possible/ongoing trajectory;
- hidden disturbances/processes;
- actual resulting condition;
- causal attribution of change.

Collapsing State and Dynamics loses these distinctions.

Thus they qualify as immediate **problem branches**, not because they form two exhaustive object classes.

---

# 6. Effect / Outcome correction

Keep at least these four concepts distinct:

```text
intended / desired outcome
actual resulting Domain State / trajectory
observed / represented result
causal effect / attribution
```

## Actual result

If Work changes reality, the resulting condition belongs to Domain State/trajectory.

## Intended outcome

Belongs to Work/Information/relations as later composition determines. It is not actual reality merely because it was intended.

## Observation/result representation

Belongs to Information/representation side. A measured result can be wrong/stale/partial.

## Causal effect

Requires a relation/evidential standard. Potential-outcomes traditions make the distinction especially sharp: causal effect concerns alternative outcomes under alternative interventions, only one of which is normally realized for a unit.

Therefore:

> **pre/post change ≠ causal effect by itself**

and:

> **Work completion ≠ actual Domain change ≠ causal attribution**

# 7. Cross-cutting dimensions not promoted to children

The following remain important but did not pass the immediate-child test:

## Boundary / reference frame

Determines what Domain Reality is at issue; applies to both State and Dynamics.

## Space / time

Indexes state/process scope; not separate universal child regions.

## Constraints / laws / invariants

May constrain admissible states and/or transitions. Their represented formulations can belong to Information. No third universal child is currently necessary.

## Observability / uncertainty

The Environment side supplies actual state/process; observation/inference/confidence belongs primarily to Information/composition.

## Causality

Connects actions/events/processes to differences in trajectories/outcomes; not reducible to two observed states.

## Outcome / success

Connects intended/accepted Work results to actual Domain State/trajectory.

## Identity / continuity

Matters across both branches and remains partly domain-specific.

## Actionability / controllability

Relates Domain condition/process to Actor capability, Work intent and Work Conditions.

---

# 8. Cross-ground boundary result

## Information

Actual Domain State/Dynamics are not the same as observations/models/specifications/Records.

## Work

Work can act upon or study Domain Reality, but Work lifecycle/action is not Domain Dynamics by identity.

## Actors

Actor actions can alter domain trajectories; Actor identity/capability/authority remain Actors semantics.

## Work Conditions

The same realized condition can be:
- Domain Reality for one Work;
- Work Condition for another;
- both roles for the same Work.

No sibling research was opened during this pass.

---

# 9. Important optionality — Domain Reality is not mandatory for every Work target

The phrase `what Work is about` was too broad.

Work may primarily target:
- Information/Records;
- Actor capability/authority;
- another Work process.

Those first-order semantics stay in their own grounds. A Domain Reality role exists only when a realized domain/environment condition/process is materially at issue.

This avoids making Environment the superclass of every possible object of Work.

---

# 10. Social / institutional reality correction

The parent rule `actual condition ≠ representation` survives, but it must not be overread as a metaphysical independence claim.

Some institutional facts may depend on collective acceptance, rules or representational practices.

The project needs only the narrower distinction:

> **an actual institutional/domain condition is not identical to any particular observation, model, assertion or Record of it.**

This permits institutional reality without collapsing it into Information or excluding it as unreal.
