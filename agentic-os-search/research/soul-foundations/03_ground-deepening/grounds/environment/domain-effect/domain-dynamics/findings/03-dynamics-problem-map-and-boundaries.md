# Domain Dynamics Finding 03 — Problem Map, Dimensions and Boundaries

**Status:** active-pass integrative finding  
**Stage:** D2 / D4

`Domain Dynamics` currently survives as a bounded leaf rather than a childless empty bucket. Its internal problem map remains explicit.

## Dynamics-local problem families — not immediate children

### P1 — Realized occurrence / interaction

What processes, activities, interactions, flows, couplings or disturbances actually occur over time?

Includes when material:
- ongoing activity;
- flows/rates/accumulation;
- interaction/coupling;
- feedback;
- initiation/continuation/cessation;
- disturbances and shocks.

### P2 — Realized change / transition / trajectory

How do Domain Structure and Condition actually persist or change through time?

Includes when material:
- state/structural/condition transitions;
- temporal ordering and sequence;
- gradual versus abrupt change;
- trajectory/evolution;
- regime/mode change;
- recovery or irreversible change.

P1 and P2 are analytically distinguishable but not promoted: a continuous process can itself be the trajectory/change, a transition can be a coarse-grained view of a process, and scale/formalism choices can move cases between descriptions.

### P3 — Temporal dependence / memory

Does future unfolding depend on history beyond the selected current-State description?

Includes:
- hysteresis;
- path dependence;
- lag/delay;
- persistence;
- accumulated exposure;
- hidden/internal state versus genuinely history-dependent evolution.

Do not assume Markov sufficiency. Conversely, do not call every omitted current variable 'history'.

### P4 — Dynamic pattern / regime

What persistent or recurrent pattern does the unfolding exhibit?

Examples:
- equilibrium/non-equilibrium;
- oscillation/limit cycle;
- stability/instability;
- attractor/basin behavior;
- regime and regime shift;
- synchronization/desynchronization;
- tipping/threshold behavior.

These are properties/patterns of Dynamics, not separate children.

### P5 — Multi-scale and nested Dynamics

Which process/transition boundary and timescale matter for the protected reasoning purpose?

A phenomenon may appear event-like at one scale and continuous at another; one subsystem's internal process may appear as another subsystem's disturbance.

## Strong boundaries

### Dynamics versus State

```text
what obtains at selected scope/time
≠
what actually unfolds across time
```

Dynamics can alter Structure or Condition, but a current State description is not itself the realized process/trajectory. A sufficiently rich State may support prediction under a chosen model; that does not erase the Dynamics question.

### Realized Dynamics versus possible/admissible Dynamics

```text
actual process / transition / trajectory
≠
transition rule / law / constraint / reachable-state relation / forecast
```

Possibility structures may constrain or model Dynamics. They do not become the actual unfolding merely because a realized path satisfies them.

### Dynamics versus Information

```text
actual process / transition / trajectory
≠
event log / measurement / model / simulation / forecast / digital twin
```

Representations may be synchronized with reality yet remain representations.

### Dynamics versus Work

```text
Domain process / transition
≠
Work lifecycle / execution / completion
```

Work is purpose/selection/commitment/execution/control-oriented. Domain Dynamics may continue autonomously, precede Work, outlast Work, or be only partly influenced by Work.

### Dynamics versus Work Conditions

The distinction is role/reference-relative, not object identity. The same evolving system can be:
- target Domain Dynamics for Work that acts on or studies it;
- enabling/constraining Work-Condition dynamics for different Work.

### Dynamics versus Actors

Actor actions may participate in domain processes and transitions. Actor identity, capability, authority, obligation and accountability remain Actor semantics.

### Change versus causal attribution

```text
A changed after X
≠
X caused the change
```

Causal effect requires contrast/intervention/evidential machinery beyond a realized temporal sequence.

## Identity / continuity result

Identity remains State–Dynamics cross-cutting. Dynamics research did not yield a universal identity criterion. Replacement, persistence and transformation must be evaluated relative to the domain and protected reasoning purpose; identity is not promoted as a Dynamics child.
