# Domain Dynamics Finding 06 — Falsifier Disposition and Bounded Leaf Result

**Status:** active-pass adversarial finding  
**Stage:** D7

The pre-research falsifiers were frozen before external search. This file records their disposition without rewriting the challenge set around the observed result.

## F1 — arbitrary bundle / stronger split or rename

**Partly confirmed.** The node survives, but `/ Processes` is too privileged. Rename to **Domain Dynamics**.

## F2 — Processes/Flows/Interactions versus Events/Transitions as children

**Not confirmed.** Distinction is useful but not invariant under hybrid systems, scale changes or occurrence ontologies. Retain as occurrence/change modes in the problem map.

## F3 — Trajectory/Evolution/History as child

**Not confirmed.** Trajectory is the realized path/pattern of Dynamics; history dependence is a property/dependency question whose status can change when State sufficiency changes.

## F4 — Endogenous versus exogenous children

**Rejected as universal children.** Classification is boundary/reference-relative and may switch with the system-of-interest.

## F5 — Feedback/Interaction/Mechanism as missing child

**Not confirmed.** Feedback/coupling can be load-bearing across many dynamics, but forms mechanisms/patterns within Dynamics. `Mechanism` also risks importing causal-explanation semantics.

## F6 — Identity/Continuity as Dynamics-local child

**Not confirmed.** Deeper State and Dynamics both require identity across change; no universal Dynamics-local identity criterion emerged.

## F7 — Path dependence/history requires distinct child

**Not confirmed.** Genuine hysteresis/path dependence exists, but it cuts across occurrence modes and interacts with selected State sufficiency.

## F8 — formalism-based distinctions are merely modeling dimensions

**Largely survives.** Continuous/discrete and deterministic/stochastic can expose different analytical methods but do not form stable universal reality children. Hybrid systems are a direct counterexample to forced continuous/discrete partitioning.

## F9 — Dynamics collapses into causal explanation

**Boundary survives.** Actual sequence/transition is insufficient for causal effect attribution; counterfactual/intervention evidence remains separate.

## F10 — Dynamics collapses into Work

**Boundary survives.** Autonomous processes, disturbances and post-/pre-Work evolution exist; Work can interact with Dynamics without defining it.

## F11 — social/institutional cases break actual/representation or Environment/Actors boundary

**Not fatal, but qualification required.** Social/institutional reality can be constituted through collective/rule-mediated practices. `Actual` therefore means realized institutional condition/change rather than metaphysical independence from all representation. Any particular Record/model and Actor authority remain distinct.

## F12 — materially different domain exposes missing child

**Not observed in current holdouts.** Software, physical, ecological, market, scientific, institutional/social and hybrid cases fit the explicit problem map without recurrent forced fit.

## F13 — no child split adds first-order value; bounded leaf

**Supported.** Additional decomposition currently tracks occurrence type, temporal scale, dynamic property, source/boundary or representation/model choice more than stable first-order problem regions.

## Strongest counterattack against the leaf

The best remaining rival is a two-part distinction:

```text
Occurrence / Interaction / Process
Change / Transition / Trajectory
```

Why it nearly qualifies:
- it can expose different practical questions: what is happening versus what changed/unfolded;
- process failure versus wrong transition outcome can produce different investigative paths.

Why it still fails child promotion:
- an ongoing occurrence is itself a realized trajectory/change through time;
- a transition can be a coarse-grained description of a process;
- discrete impact and continuous flow coexist in one hybrid Dynamics;
- scale/boundary can transform a process into an event/disturbance description;
- no holdout required independent recursive research packages for both to preserve later reasoning.

Therefore the distinction remains explicit in the leaf problem map but is not promoted.

## Bounded result

```text
Domain Reality
    ├─ Domain State
    │    ├─ Domain Structure / Composition
    │    └─ Domain Condition / Status
    └─ Domain Dynamics     ← bounded leaf at current foundation depth
```

`Domain Dynamics` is not 'simple'. Leaf means no stable immediate child split currently changes first-order research/failure reasoning enough to justify another hierarchical level.

## Reopen conditions

Reopen if later evidence shows:
- occurrence/process and transition/trajectory repeatedly require independent first-order recovery across domains;
- a dynamics-local identity or constraints region cannot remain cross-cutting;
- path dependence exposes a recurrent family not expressible through the current problem map;
- Work Conditions or Information research reveals systematic boundary leakage;
- a materially different domain forces a missing region;
- independent review produces a stronger decomposition.
