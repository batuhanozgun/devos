## Why no children

The strongest rival split was:

```text
Occurrence / Interaction / Process
Change / Transition / Trajectory
```

It remains useful but does not survive as a stable universal hierarchy because:
- process and trajectory/change overlap materially;
- transition can be a coarse-grained process;
- hybrid systems combine continuous flow and discrete jump;
- scale/reference can switch event/process/disturbance classification;
- diverse holdouts did not require independent recursive branches.

Other candidate splits—endogenous/exogenous, continuous/discrete, history/path dependence, stability/regime, mechanism/trajectory—behave primarily as dimensions, properties, boundary relations or representation/model choices.
