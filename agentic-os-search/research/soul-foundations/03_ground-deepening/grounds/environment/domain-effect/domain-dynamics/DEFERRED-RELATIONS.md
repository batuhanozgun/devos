# Domain Dynamics — DEFERRED RELATIONS

**Status:** cross-node/cross-ground observations captured during Dynamics research; not solved here

## Domain State ↔ Domain Dynamics

- Dynamics can change Structure/Composition and Condition/Status.
- A State description may or may not be sufficient to predict relevant Dynamics.
- Identity/continuity connects State across change.
- Stable/invariant constraints can apply over both admissible State and transitions.

Local rule:

> **State at a time ≠ realized unfolding across time.**

## Domain Dynamics ↔ Work

- Work actions can participate in domain transitions without defining all domain change.
- Work completion does not prove a resulting Domain transition.
- domain change can begin before Work, continue after Work, or occur without focal Work.
- Work may respond adaptively to evolving Domain Dynamics.

Local rule:

> **Work execution/closure ≠ Domain Dynamics/result.**

## Domain Dynamics ↔ Work Conditions

- the same changing concrete system may be Domain Dynamics for Work targeting it and a changing enabling/constraining condition for other Work;
- support degradation/recovery can alter Work feasibility/readiness while remaining one realized process.

Local rule:

> **target/support role is reference-relative; do not duplicate the same reality merely because its role changes.**

## Domain Dynamics ↔ Information

- observations, logs, traces, process models, forecasts, simulations and digital twins can represent Dynamics;
- provenance, uncertainty, sampling, lag and currentness belong to the representation/evidence side;
- a transition rule or model of possible evolution is not automatically the realized trajectory.

Local rule:

> **actual Dynamics ≠ any particular representation of Dynamics.**

## Domain Dynamics ↔ Actors

- Actor actions may initiate, perturb, maintain or terminate domain processes;
- Actor identity, capability, authority, obligation and accountability remain Actor semantics;
- institutional/social Dynamics may depend on collective practices while retaining separate Actor/Information roles.

## Causal / effect relations

Candidate later relation questions:

- changes / transitions-to;

Do not turn this list into a final relation taxonomy here.

## Possible / admissible Dynamics

Later composition must distinguish:
- actual realized process/trajectory;
- permitted/possible transitions;
- laws/rules/constraints/invariants;
- predicted or simulated futures;
- counterfactual trajectories.
