# Domain Reality Finding 01 — Evidence Inventory and Starting Falsifiers

**Status:** current active-node finding  
**Question:** what was actually established before opening the Domain/Effect node, and what must remain falsifiable?

## Strong prior evidence

Current Environment parent research already supports:
- realized condition/dynamics are distinct from specification/observation/model/Record;
- Environment participation is boundary/reference-relative;
- Work closure is not identical to actual environmental effect;
- Domain/Effect and Work-Condition roles are materially different at the parent level;
- one concrete system can switch roles across focal Work;
- environmental participation does not transfer all semantic ownership from Work, Information or Actors.

Archived operating-world evidence independently supports:
- world state can vary while Records/Actor belief/Work state remain fixed;
- state, observation, belief and Record must remain separable when hidden change/latency matters;
- actionability is relational;
- boundary choice is load-bearing.

These facts are inputs, not proof of the current child label or its immediate-child map.

## Inherited claims that remain under attack

The parent synthesis used `Domain / Effect Reality` as one child label. That does **not** establish:
- that `Domain` and `Effect` are two internal regions;
- that `effect` is a kind of reality object/state rather than a relation over changes/outcomes;
- that all Work has a Domain Reality target;
- that domain entities/processes/state/dynamics should become child branches;
- that physical/social/digital/institutional domain types are universal children;
- that system-of-interest is always the right reference rather than focal Work/situation.

## Highest-value falsifiers

1. **Effect-relational falsifier** — causal-effect traditions define effect through contrasts/interventions, while state-transition traditions encode action effects as changes between states. If this generalizes, `Effect Reality` should not be a child region.
2. **Observational-Work falsifier** — scientific/diagnostic Work may investigate a domain without intending to change it. Domain Reality must survive with no intended effect.
3. **Cross-ground-target falsifier** — focal Work can target an Information artifact, Actor capability or another Work unit. `what Work is about` is therefore too broad as a definition of Environment Domain Reality.
4. **Model-view falsifier** — structure/state/dynamics/object/process decompositions may be modeling/ontology views rather than project children.
5. **Institutional-reality falsifier** — some social/institutional facts depend constitutively on collective attitudes/rules/representations. `actual reality = representation-independent reality` is too strong if interpreted ontologically.
6. **Hybrid-domain falsifier** — physical/digital/social/institutional type branches may fail in mixed systems.

## Research criterion

The pass will prefer the smallest immediate-child map that preserves distinct first-order questions and failure modes without importing an ontology merely to deepen the tree.
