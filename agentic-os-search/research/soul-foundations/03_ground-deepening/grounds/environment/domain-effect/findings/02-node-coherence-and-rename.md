# Domain Reality Finding 02 — Node Coherence and Rename

**Status:** current active-node finding  
**Question:** should `Domain / Effect Reality` remain one node and label?

## Result

The underlying domain-reality problem survives, but the combined label is misleading.

Current stronger node label:

> **Domain Reality**

rather than `Domain / Effect Reality`.

## Why `Domain` survives

Across systems engineering, Work Domain Analysis, control/state-transition traditions and project evidence, a first-order problem remains around the **realized reality whose condition and evolution are materially at issue for focal Work**.

This reality can be:
- natural;
- physical/engineered;
- digital/operational;
- biological/clinical;
- market/economic;
- social/institutional;
- hybrid.

Its membership is reference-relative. A subsystem can be Domain Reality when it is the Work target even if it is internal to a wider engineered system.

## Why `Effect` weakens as part of the node name

Three discriminators converge:

1. state-transition/control traditions represent action/event effects as changes in the domain/process state or trajectory;
2. causal-inference traditions define causal effect through contrasts among potential outcomes/interventions rather than as a separately existing region of actual reality;
3. observational/diagnostic/scientific Work can have a material Domain Reality while intentionally producing no effect on it.

Therefore `Effect Reality` suggests a symmetry that the evidence does not support.

## Corrected primary question

> **What realized domain condition/configuration and evolving processes are materially at issue for focal Work, independently of how they are observed or represented?**

When Work intends to change that reality, actual results remain Domain Reality; whether the change is attributable to Work is a separate causal/relation question.

## Boundary correction — `what Work is about` is too broad

A Work item can directly target:
- a Record/representation;
- an Actor capability/authority arrangement;
- another Work process.

Those targets do not become Environment-owned merely because Work is about them.

Domain Reality therefore means the **realized environmental/domain role** of the target/subject matter, not every object of Work.

## Social/institutional correction

Do not define Domain Reality as universally `representation-independent` in an ontological sense.

Some institutional/social facts may be constituted or sustained by collective attitudes, rules or representational practices.

The safer project distinction is:

> **actual domain condition ≠ any particular observation, model, assertion or Record of that condition**

while allowing that some kinds of domain reality may themselves be socially/representationally constituted.

## Current node

```text
Environment / Operating Reality
    ├─ Domain Reality                         [stronger label]
    └─ Work Conditions / Enabling–Constraining Environment
```

This rename is a research correction, not an architecture naming decision.
