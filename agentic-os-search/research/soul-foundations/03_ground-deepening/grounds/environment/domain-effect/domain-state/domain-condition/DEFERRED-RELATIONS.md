# Domain Condition — DEFERRED RELATIONS

**Status:** captured during Condition research; not systematic composition  
**Date:** 2026-09-03

These observations preserve later relation/control work. They do not authorize another node.

## R1 — Characteristics ↔ standing

A criterion-relative standing can depend on one or more realized characteristics/values plus an applicable criterion.

```text
realized condition facts
+ applicable criterion
→ standing relation
```

This pass does not define a rule engine, derivation graph or evidence model.

## R2 — Condition ↔ Information

Information may represent:
- measurements/observations of characteristics;
- inferred/estimated values;
- health/status classifications;
- criteria, thresholds and requirements;
- certificates or registry assertions of standing.

None is actual Condition by identity.

Later composition must bind representation to the claimed reality fact and preserve currentness/provenance/uncertainty.

## R3 — Condition ↔ Structure

Structure can causally shape Condition, but current Condition does not establish Structure.

Examples:
- topology can affect load/latency;
- component composition can affect whole health;
- structural redundancy can preserve standing despite constituent degradation.

Do not infer root cause or structure from a condition label alone.

## R4 — Condition ↔ Dynamics

Dynamics changes current characteristics and standings over time.

Current mode/standing is a Condition snapshot; transition, trend, degradation trajectory and dwell/history belong to Dynamics when material.

## R5 — Condition ↔ constraints / criteria

A criterion/rule/threshold may define admissibility or expected function.

Current satisfaction/violation/compliance can be a criterion-relative standing.

The criterion's source, authority, version and applicability remain later relation/Information/Actor questions.

## R6 — Condition ↔ Actors

Actors may:
- establish or revoke institutional standing;
- define/apply criteria;
- inspect or certify condition;
- act because of current standing.

Actor identity, authority and responsibility remain Actors semantics even when the resulting standing is Domain Condition.

## R7 — Condition ↔ Work

Work may inspect, maintain or change Condition.

Work completion does not establish resulting Condition. Intended/accepted condition does not establish actual condition.

## R8 — Condition ↔ Work Conditions

The same realized condition fact can be:
- Domain Condition for Work targeting the domain;
- Work Condition for Work whose execution depends on it;
- both roles where both questions matter.

Role switching does not imply duplicated intrinsic types.

## R9 — constituent ↔ whole condition

Whole condition/standing can depend on constituent conditions through domain-specific aggregation, redundancy, criticality or emergence.

No universal aggregation relation is defined here.

## R10 — Condition ↔ causality / diagnosis

Condition tells what obtains. Diagnosis/causal reasoning asks why.

A degraded standing can result from:
- Structure;
- Dynamics;
- Work intervention;
- Actor action;
- Work Conditions;
- exogenous disturbance.

Do not turn status classification into root-cause explanation.

## Guardrail

Do not expand these notes into systematic relation ontology, health model, rule engine, causal graph or architecture during Condition closure.
