# Constituent Composition — DEFERRED RELATIONS

**Status:** recoverable cross-node / cross-ground relations encountered during child research

These relations are recorded for later synthesis/composition. They are not additional children and do not authorize another research ground.

## Composition ↔ Relational Organization

- constituent inclusion establishes the participant/part set for many topology questions;
- topology/relations can provide evidence for constituency in some domains;
- do not collapse the questions: correct set can coexist with wrong topology and vice versa.

## Composition ↔ Domain Condition

- included constituent can be healthy/degraded/ready/unavailable without ceasing to be a constituent;
- structural cardinality can remain Composition;
- concentration/fraction/abundance or other realized quantitative values can be Condition depending on protected question.

## Composition ↔ Domain Dynamics

- membership at selected time is Structure;
- add/remove/join/leave/replacement is Dynamics;
- history may be evidence for current membership without becoming current composition itself.

## Composition ↔ Information / Representation

Representations may include:
- BOM / SBOM;
- inventories / registries;
- architecture/configuration models;
- organizational rosters;
- endpoint/service-discovery objects;
- scans/observations.

Later Information research must preserve:

```text
claim/model/list of constituents
≠
actual constituent composition by identity
```

while allowing provenance/evidence/currentness relations between them.

## Composition ↔ Actors

A person or organization can be a structural constituent/member/participant while Actor semantics separately govern:
- identity;
- capability/competence;
- authority/permission;
- obligation;
- delegation/representation;
- accountability.

Do not duplicate Actor semantics into composition merely because membership has a role.

## Composition ↔ Work Conditions

The same concrete system can be:
- constituent of the selected Domain Structure for one focal Work;
- a Work Condition/support dependency for another.

Role switching is reference-relative and does not change concrete identity by itself.

## Composition ↔ criteria / requirements

Distinguish:
- actual current composition;
- required/allowed/intended composition;
- criterion-relative judgment that actual composition satisfies a requirement.

Later composition may connect these across Information and Domain Condition without collapsing them.

## Composition ↔ identity / continuity

Replacement creates a relation among:
- prior composition;
- subsequent composition;
- identity of constituents;
- persistence/identity of the higher whole.

Identity/continuity remains cross-cutting until later evidence supports a dedicated treatment.

## Composition ↔ boundary / scope

A constituent assertion is incomplete without a selected whole/reference scope. Later composition must support multiple/nested scopes and boundary changes without turning the whole world into one composition graph.
