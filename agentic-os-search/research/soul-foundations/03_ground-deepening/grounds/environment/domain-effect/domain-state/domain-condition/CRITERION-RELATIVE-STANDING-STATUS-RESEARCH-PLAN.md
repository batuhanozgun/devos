## Candidate decompositions to attack

Do not assume any candidate is correct:

1. **Functional / operational standing** — healthy/degraded/failed, ready/not-ready, operable/inoperable.
2. **Normative / regulatory standing** — compliant/non-compliant, conforming/non-conforming.
3. **Legal / institutional standing** — good standing, licensed/unlicensed, eligible/ineligible where constitutive rules apply.
4. **Validity / quality / acceptability standing** — valid/invalid, acceptable/unacceptable, quality grades.
5. **Binary versus graded / ordinal / severity standing**.
6. **Bearer-local versus relational/contextual standing**.
7. **Constituent versus whole / aggregate standing**.
8. **Directly determined versus inferred/modelled/adjudicated standing**.
9. **Current standing versus capability/readiness/availability/reliability/disposition**.
10. **domain-type branches** — software, physical asset, ecology, market/finance, institution, science, hybrid systems.

Promotion requires a stable first-order protected question, distinct failure/recovery surface and cross-domain recurrence. Criterion vocabulary or adjudication mechanism alone is insufficient.

## Boundary tests

### Realized Characteristics

Preserve the parent split unless falsified:

```text
current characteristic(s)
+ applicable criterion / rule / expected function
→ possible true criterion-relative standing
```

Examples to test:
- temperature = 85°C versus over-temperature/degraded relative to threshold;
- memory.available = x versus MemoryPressure relative to eviction threshold;
- measured defect dimensions versus conforming/non-conforming;
- entity facts versus good-standing/not-good-standing under constitutive legal rules.

The pass must test whether the standing is reducible to characteristics or has a distinct recovery question because criterion applicability and semantics matter.

### Information / representation

Strictly separate unless falsified:
- actual standing;
- criterion text/specification/standard/policy;
- assessment procedure;
- observation/evidence;
- model or score;
- API/status field;
- report/certificate/attestation;
- confidence/provenance.

A representation can establish, evidence, declare or communicate a standing without being the standing by identity.

### Actors / authority

For institutional/legal cases, distinguish:
- authority/rule-making/adjudication powers;
- the rule or criterion;
- the constitutive process or authoritative act;
- the current institutional standing that obtains.

If these cannot be separated without forced fit, parent/boundary reopen is required.

### Dynamics

Separate current standing from:
- transition into/out of standing;
- duration/dwell time;
- trend/severity trajectory;
- failure/recovery process;
- historical compliance or health record.

### Work Conditions

The same standing can matter as a Work Condition relative to focal Work. Role switching does not by itself create a new standing type.
