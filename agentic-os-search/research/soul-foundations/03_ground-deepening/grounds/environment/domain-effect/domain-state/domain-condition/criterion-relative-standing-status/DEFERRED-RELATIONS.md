# Criterion-Relative Standing — DEFERRED RELATIONS

**Status:** deferred cross-node/cross-ground relations; not current architecture  
**Date:** 2026-09-04

## Purpose

Capture relation questions exposed by the standing pass without turning the research hierarchy into a premature ontology/control model.

## R1 — Standing ↔ criterion

Later composition must bind a standing to the criterion/regime relative to which it obtains.

Potential dimensions:
- criterion identity/version;
- effective time;
- jurisdiction/scope;
- bearer applicability;
- exception/waiver;
- precedence/conflict.

The criterion is not the standing by identity.

## R2 — Standing ↔ Realized Characteristics

Underlying characteristics can support standing recovery without being identical to it.

Later work may need typed support/derivation/evidence relations while preserving cases where standing can be established without enumerating all non-load-bearing characteristics.

## R3 — Standing ↔ evidence / observation / assessment

Potential later relations:
- observed-by;
- measured-by;
- assessed-by;
- supported-by;
- contradicted-by;
- confidence/provenance;
- decision-rule-used.

These belong to later Information/composition work.

## R4 — Standing ↔ certificate / report / API status

A report, certificate, registry view, dashboard or status field may:
- state;
- certify;
- attest;
- communicate;
- lag;
- misrepresent

a current standing.

Do not encode representation and standing as the same thing merely for storage convenience.

## R5 — Standing ↔ authority / Actors

Institutional cases may require relations to:
- rule-maker;
- regulator;
- adjudicator;
- certifier;
- delegated authority;
- subject of authority.

Authority semantics remain for later Actors/composition work.

## R6 — Standing ↔ Dynamics

Later composition may relate standing to:
- transition into/out of standing;
- last transition;
- dwell duration;
- failure/recovery process;
- historical standing sequence;
- future projected standing.

Current standing remains distinct from these temporal relations.

## R7 — Standing ↔ Work Conditions

A domain standing can materially enable/constrain focal Work and therefore play a Work-Condition role. This is role switching, not a second standing fact by default.

## R8 — Standing ↔ policy/action

Standing may trigger or condition:
- approval;
- rejection;
- maintenance;
- remediation;
- suspension;
- escalation;
- workload routing.

The control/action rule is not the standing.

## R9 — Multiple criteria and conflicting standings

Later composition must support multiple simultaneously valid criterion-relative standings rather than one flattened `status` field.

## Guardrail

No relation here is an approved ontology edge, schema relation, module interface or implementation design.
