## F1 — inherited paired label already optimal

**Rejected.**

`Values` is useful explanatory vocabulary but too ambiguous between actual magnitude/category and representation-side measured/attributed/result value. `Realized Characteristics` is the stronger semantic label.

## F2 — `Values` should be dropped/demoted

**Supported.**

BIPM and W3C/OGC evidence shows strong need to distinguish actual characteristic from value expression/result/estimate.

## F3 — `Characteristics` is too broad

**Rejected under explicit protected-question boundaries.**

Structure, Dynamics and Standing remain separately recoverable by asking whether the protected question is structural organization, unfolding/change, or criterion-relative judgment.

## F4 — quantitative vs qualitative/categorical child split

**Rejected.**

VIM/IUPAC/SSN traditions all preserve quantitative and nominal/categorical forms without requiring a different first-order property-recovery problem.

## F5 — intrinsic vs relational child split

**Rejected.**

The distinction is not one stable universal binary and relation-valued characteristics remain coherent within the node.

## F6 — mode/regime child

**Rejected.**

Current mode is a state-like characteristic when snapshot semantics are protected; transition/history remains Dynamics.

## F7 — intensive vs extensive child

**Rejected.**

Legitimate physical quantity taxonomy; not universal or exhaustive across the node.

## F8 — whole/aggregate/emergent child

**Rejected.**

Changes bearer/scope and aggregation semantics, not the first-order protected question.

## F9 — observable vs latent/hidden child

**Rejected.**

Changes evidence access. SSN proxy/ultimate-feature patterns make the distinction explicit without redefining the underlying property family.

## F10 — scalar/vector/distribution/field/structured value is representation/value shape

**Supported as anti-child result.**

These forms do not reliably define independent recovery programs.

## F11 — capability/capacity/availability/reliability child

**Rejected.**

Current capacity magnitude can fit here, but capability/availability/reliability span conditional dispositions, probability, time and criterion semantics.

## F12 — relation-valued characteristics collapse into Relational Organization

**Rejected.**

Relation existence/organization and current magnitude/category borne by a relation can vary independently. GeoSPARQL topology-versus-distance is a useful external discriminator.

## F13 — structural cardinality collapses Composition into this node

**Rejected.**

Constituent multiplicity belongs to Composition when membership/constituency is protected; concentration/fraction/load are characteristic questions.

## F14 — mode/current value collapses into Dynamics

**Rejected.**

Snapshot fact and transition/history are distinct. Derived temporal statistics require explicit provenance but do not collapse the node.

## F15 — measured/modelled value identical to actual characteristic

**Rejected strongly.**

BIPM and SSN independently distinguish target property/measurand from observation/measurement result.

## F16 — uncertainty/confidence is automatically an actual characteristic

**Rejected.**

Epistemic uncertainty generally belongs to evidence/model state. Genuine stochastic domain properties remain possible but must be typed separately.

## F17 — criteria/ranges/requirements belong inside Realized Characteristics

**Rejected.**

Criteria can be needed to judge a characteristic without being the current characteristic itself.

## F18 — standing reduces to characteristics

**Rejected.**

Threshold/criterion applicability creates an additional semantic dependency. Current characteristic and current standing can fail/recover independently.

## F19 — cross-domain holdouts force missing Condition region / parent reopen

**Rejected under current evidence.**

Software, physical, ecological, market, institutional, scientific and hybrid cases fit without a recurrent third Condition-local problem.

## F20 — bounded leaf

**Supported.**

Late search mainly produced subtypes, scales, measurement/result forms, domain metrics, aggregate semantics and boundary cases rather than a stable new recovery region.
