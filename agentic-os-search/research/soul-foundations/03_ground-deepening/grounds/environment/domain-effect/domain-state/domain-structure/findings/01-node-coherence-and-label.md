# Domain Structure Finding 01 — Node Coherence and Label

**Status:** bounded finding  
**Date:** 2026-09-03

## Question

Does the inherited `Domain Structure / Composition` node survive deeper research, and which label best protects its full problem surface?

## Result

The node survives, but the strongest label is:

> **Domain Structure**

rather than `Domain Structure / Composition`.

## Why the node remains coherent

Across materially different traditions, a recurring problem remains that is neither current Condition nor Dynamics:

> Which constituents actually form/participate in the selected whole, and how are those constituents structurally organized at the selected scope/time?

SEBoK's current Structure glossary describes structure through system elements and relationships. NIST system-engineering material similarly treats a system as an arrangement of parts/elements and stresses both hierarchical constituent decomposition and non-hierarchical relations. These sources are not hierarchy authorities for this project, but they support the recurrence of a broad structure problem distinct from property/health/status questions.

## Why `Composition` moves below the parent label

`Composition` is an important part of the problem but too narrow as a co-primary parent synonym.

Counterexample:
- the correct components can all be present;
- their part/whole membership can be correctly known;
- yet connections, dependencies, topology, placement or interfaces can still be wrong.

That is a structural error without a composition/membership error.

Conversely:
- an expected constituent can be missing or an unexpected constituent present;
- the known relations among the remaining constituents can all be correct.

That is a composition/membership error without necessarily being a relational-topology error.

Therefore the broad node should be `Domain Structure`, with `Composition / Membership` as one immediate region.

## Rival labels

### `Composition`
Too narrow; under-protects non-part/whole structural relations and topology.

### `Organization`
Broad but risks importing process/governance semantics and can blur realized structural arrangement with organizing activity.

### `Topology`
Too narrow and strongly network/spatial/model flavored; does not naturally cover membership/part-whole composition.

### `Membership`
Too narrow; cannot carry arrangement/connectivity.

### `Configuration`
Rejected again because it can mean settings, baseline/specification or recorded configuration as well as realized structure.

### `Structure / Composition`
Acceptable explanatory phrase but less clean than treating `Structure` as the broad node and `Composition` as a child region.

## Falsifier judgment

- **F1 arbitrary bundle:** rejected after refinement; the node remains coherent.
- **F11 structure cannot be separated from representation:** rejected; source/view boundary survives.
- **F15 bounded leaf:** rejected; two stable immediate problem regions survive deeper testing.
