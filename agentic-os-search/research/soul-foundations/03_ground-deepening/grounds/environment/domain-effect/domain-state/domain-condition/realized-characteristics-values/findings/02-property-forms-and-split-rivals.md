# Realized Characteristics Finding 02 — Property Forms and Split Rivals

**Status:** bounded finding  
**Date:** 2026-09-04

## Question

Do quantitative/qualitative, intrinsic/relational, intensive/extensive or value-shape distinctions form stable immediate children?

## Result

**No tested property-form split currently deserves universal immediate-child promotion.**

## Quantitative versus qualitative/categorical

This is a legitimate distinction in value/property form but not a stable recovery split.

Independent counterframes:
- VIM distinguishes quantities (properties with magnitude) from nominal properties (properties without magnitude), while both remain properties that can have values;
- IUPAC `kind-of-property` explicitly spans qualitatively distinguished attributes and kinds of quantity;
- W3C/OGC SSN allows observation results to be numbers, quantities, categories, vectors, arrays, geometries and other forms while keeping one Property/Observation pattern.

Recovery question stays the same:

> what characteristic actually obtains?

The answer may be `85 °C`, `blue`, `Manual`, a vector, a sequence or another structured characteristic without requiring a different foundation child.

**F4 rejected.**

## Intrinsic versus relational/contextual

The distinction is useful in some analyses but does not survive as one clean universal partition. Philosophy counterframes show that intrinsic/extrinsic, relational/non-relational, local/non-local and interior/exterior distinctions do not collapse into one stable binary.

Project examples also cross the proposed line:
- distance to another object is relational but may be the protected current characteristic;
- pressure/temperature can be bearer-local;
- assigned/institutional characteristics can depend on external relations/rules;
- some whole-level characteristics depend on constituent organization.

Recovery still centers on what obtains, with relation/bearer/reference explicitly bound.

**F5 rejected.**

## Intensive versus extensive / amount-dependent

IUPAC treats these as real physical quantity distinctions:
- intensive magnitude independent of system extent;
- extensive magnitude additive across subsystems.

But the split is physical-quantity specific and does not cover categorical modes, software runtime characteristics, institutional properties or many relation-valued cases. It therefore cannot be a universal immediate-child basis.

**F7 rejected.**

## Scalar / vector / distribution / field / structured value

These are primarily value/result shapes or mathematical representations. VIM includes vector/tensor quantities under quantity, and SSN permits many observation-result forms without changing the semantic property question.

A distribution can additionally summarize uncertainty or temporal variation and therefore may belong partly to Information or Dynamics depending on what it represents.

**F10 supported as anti-child result.**

## Child-promotion judgment

None of these distinctions provides all of:
- universal recurrence;
- independent first-order failure/recovery question;
- stable boundary across materially different domains;
- semantic necessity independent of measurement/data representation.

They remain explicit dimensions/forms, not immediate children.
