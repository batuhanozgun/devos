## F1 — node incoherence

**Rejected.**

Across integral parts, groups, open SoS, ecological communities, networks and mixtures, one stable higher recovery question remains: determine which actual constituents count in the selected structural whole at the selected scope/time under domain-valid constituent semantics.

The node does not require one primitive relation.

## F2 — `Membership` overbreadth / poor parent label

**Partly supported.**

`Membership` remains important vocabulary but over-privileges group/set-style constituency. It is dropped as a co-primary node label and retained as an internal relation form.

Preferred label: `Constituent Composition`.

## F3 — `Composition` too narrow for open / non-physical cases

**Rejected under the refined definition.**

Systems engineering uses composition over heterogeneous elements including humans/processes/data; social/ecological/network traditions speak of constituents/composition; SoS participation can be handled without claiming integral parthood.

The `Constituent` qualifier plus explicit relation semantics prevents physical-only interpretation.

## F4 — immediate split: parthood/composition vs participation/membership

**Rejected as universal split.**

The relation distinction is real but the space is not stably binary. Material constitution, mixture composition, aggregate member-part, group membership and autonomous SoS constituency produce additional contested relation forms.

Promoting two children would import a disputed ontology choice.

## F5 — merge everything into Relational Organization

**Rejected.**

Membership can be established without all internal relations; ecological membership provides cases where interaction is neither necessary nor sufficient for membership. Wrong constituent set and wrong topology remain independently recoverable failures.

## F6 — nesting/hierarchy requires child

**Rejected.**

Nesting recursively reapplies constituent composition at a new selected whole/granularity. It is a structural form/reference-level issue, not a new first-order question.

## F7 — temporary/fuzzy/peripheral participation requires child

**Rejected.**

Temporary validity occurs across membership and parthood traditions. Fuzziness may reflect epistemic uncertainty, criterion-relative classification, Condition-like degree or genuine domain semantics. No stable Composition-local child survived.

## F8 — boundary/scope requires child

**Rejected.**

Boundary/reference governs Composition, Relational Organization, Condition and Dynamics. It remains cross-cutting.

## F9 — identity/continuity requires child

**Rejected.**

Identity through constituent replacement also governs Condition bearers and Dynamic continuity. Current composition answers who/what is in the whole now; persistence of the higher whole is cross-cutting.

## F10 — multiplicity/cardinality/amount requires child

**Rejected.**

Structural cardinality is a facet of actual constituent composition when number of member instances is the protected question. Concentration/fraction/abundance and other realized quantities may instead be Domain Condition characteristics. There is no one clean Composition-local quantity child.

## F11 — normative membership leakage

**Rejected.**

Required/allowed/intended composition belongs to specification/criterion surfaces; actual current composition remains reality-side. Compliance with a required composition can later become criterion-relative standing.

## F12 — topology leakage

**Rejected.**

Constituent relation to the selected whole is distinguished from connections/adjacency/interfaces among included constituents. Relation arity alone does not determine branch ownership.

## F13 — Condition leakage

**Rejected.**

Included-but-unhealthy/unavailable/inactive remains Condition rather than absence by default. Kubernetes EndpointSlice's represented endpoint set versus endpoint conditions is a useful operational counterframe.

## F14 — Dynamics leakage

**Rejected.**

Membership at time t is snapshot Structure; join/leave/add/remove/replacement transition is Dynamics.

## F15 — Actor leakage

**Rejected.**

Organizational membership/participation does not own authority, obligation, capability or accountability. W3C ORG's distinction between Membership and Role/Post is a representation-side counterframe supporting that semantic separation.

## F16 — representation collapse

**Rejected.**

BOM, SBOM, inventory, architecture/configuration model, registry and observation can represent or evidence composition but do not equal actual composition by identity. NASA CM explicitly depends on keeping product/configuration information consistent with the product/configuration.

## F17 — cross-domain breakage

**Rejected in current holdouts.**

Physical assembly, deployed software, SoS/open systems, ecology, mixtures/materials, institutions, supply/market networks, scientific setups and hybrid systems all fit without a recurrent new child.

## F18 — Domain Structure parent reopen

**Rejected.**

No evidence collapses Composition into Relational Organization and no third co-equal Domain Structure region appears. The parent split remains useful.

## F19 — leaf rival

**Supported.**

Late search increasingly produced relation subtypes, domain terminology, criteria/specification distinctions and cross-cutting dimensions rather than a stable new first-order recovery region.

## Late adversarial search

Late probes tested:
- functional versus material parts;
- component versus arbitrary portions;
- essential/mandatory/optional parts;
- core/peripheral/transient ecological members;
- logical versus physical architecture decomposition;
- type/token and multiplicity distinctions;
- set membership versus mereological parthood.

Result:
- functional/material/part taxonomies refine constituent-relation semantics;
- essential/mandatory/optional is largely normative/criterion-relative unless asking actual inclusion;
- core/peripheral/transient is scale/time/criterion-sensitive;
- logical/physical architecture is representation/design-side unless it describes realized constituency;
- type/token/multiplicity are granularity/cardinality dimensions;
- no probe created a stable universal immediate child.

Information gain therefore declined structurally.
