## 1. Why `Realized Characteristics`

`Realized` is load-bearing. It separates actual current condition from:
- characteristic kind/type;
- specification/requirement;
- observable/measurable property definition;
- measurement/model/report;
- intended or possible value.

`Characteristics` is deliberately broad enough to include:
- quantity/magnitude;
- nominal/qualitative/categorical characteristic;
- current mode/regime when protected as a snapshot fact;
- current magnitude/category borne by a relation;
- whole/aggregate/emergent characteristic.

The slash companion `/ Values` is dropped because `value` is materially ambiguous between reality-side magnitude/category and representation-side measured/attributed/result value.

This is a naming refinement, not removal of current magnitude/category semantics.

---

## 2. Why quantitative versus qualitative is not a child split

Metrology and analytical-chemistry traditions distinguish quantities from nominal properties, but both remain characteristic/property questions.

Independent evidence:
- VIM quantity = property with magnitude;
- VIM nominal property = property without magnitude but with a value expressible in words/codes;
- IUPAC kind-of-property spans qualitative and quantitative forms;
- W3C/OGC SSN observation results can be number, quantity, category, vector, array, geometry, etc.

The recovery question does not change merely because the answer form changes.

---

## 3. Why intrinsic/relational, intensive/extensive and mathematical value shape are not children

### Intrinsic / extrinsic-relational
The distinction is not one clean universal binary. Several related notions diverge, and relation-valued characteristics recur legitimately.

### Intensive / extensive
A real physical quantity distinction, but physical-domain specific and non-exhaustive.

### Scalar / vector / distribution / field / structured
Primarily mathematical or representational shape. A distribution may additionally encode epistemic uncertainty or temporal derivation, requiring Information/Dynamics boundary checks.

None produces a stable universal recovery program.

## 5. Whole / aggregate / emergent characteristic is a scope dimension

Whole-level characteristics can be real and not reducible to trivial constituent arithmetic. SEBoK explicitly recognizes emergent system-level properties.

This changes:
- bearer/scope;
- evidence requirements;
- aggregation/emergence semantics;
not the first-order question of what characteristic actually obtains.

No universal whole-versus-constituent child is justified.

## 8. Actual-versus-observed boundary

This boundary strengthened substantially in the pass.

BIPM/JCGM separates measurand, measurement result, measured value and true-value concepts. W3C/OGC SSN separates Feature of Interest/Property from Observation/Result.

Therefore:

```text
actual characteristic
≠ sensor reading
≠ measurement result
≠ estimate
≠ model value
≠ report/status field
```

An actual characteristic can exist while unknown or mis-estimated.

Observability/latency of access changes epistemic recovery, not the reality-side type of fact.

---

## 9. Uncertainty boundary

Measurement uncertainty, confidence and provenance usually qualify the evidence/result/model. They are not automatically characteristics of the underlying reality.

A genuinely stochastic domain property can be real, but must be distinguished from uncertainty about an unknown characteristic.

## 10. Criterion-relative standing remains a separate sibling

The parent split survives.

```text
current characteristic
+ materially applicable criterion
→ possible criterion-relative standing
```

Kubernetes pressure mechanics provide a clean counterframe: signals such as available memory are compared against configured thresholds to produce pressure conditions. NASA/NIST condition-management traditions similarly distinguish state/condition data from maintenance/health assessment.

Thus:
- criterion/rule/threshold is not this child by identity;
- standing such as healthy/ready/compliant is not reducible to characteristics merely because characteristics support it.

`Criterion-Relative Standing / Status` remains a promoted sibling requiring its own future pass.

## 11. Capability / availability / reliability do not produce a new child

Current capacity magnitude can fit here.

But:
- capability is ability under specified conditions;
- availability can be probabilistic and interval/asymptotic;
- reliability is probability of performing without failure over a time period under conditions.

These concepts span dispositions, temporal models, criteria and Work-relative semantics. They do not define one universal immediate child under Realized Characteristics.
