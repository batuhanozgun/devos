# Realized Characteristics Finding 05 — Cross-Domain Holdouts

**Status:** bounded holdout finding  
**Date:** 2026-09-04

## Purpose

Attempt to break the proposed `Realized Characteristics` node by requiring materially different domains to fit without manufacturing domain-type children or stealing neighbouring-ground semantics.

## Holdout 1 — Deployed software / services

Examples:
- current free memory;
- queue depth;
- request latency;
- current operating mode;
- current replica utilization.

Boundary checks:
- service/component membership → Constituent Composition;
- dependency/link existence → Relational Organization;
- latency/load/memory magnitude → Realized Characteristics;
- pressure/ready/healthy relative to thresholds/checks → possible Criterion-Relative Standing;
- transitions/restarts/traffic flow → Dynamics.

Kubernetes pressure mechanics independently demonstrate current signals versus threshold-relative condition reports.

**No new child.**

## Holdout 2 — Physical machine / process asset

Examples:
- temperature;
- pressure;
- vibration magnitude;
- wear/damage magnitude;
- power/standby mode.

NASA maintenance material explicitly lists functional-state data such as power, temperature, pressure and standby. NIST distinguishes condition awareness from diagnostics/health assessment.

Quantitative versus categorical/mode forms do not require separate first-order recovery programs.

**No new child.**

## Holdout 3 — Ecology / biology

Examples:
- nutrient concentration;
- species abundance at selected scope;
- dissolved oxygen;
- physical/chemical attributes;
- whole-system emergent characteristics.

EPA's ecological-condition umbrella also contains processes and structure-like topics, so the project must keep its sharper Structure/Dynamics boundaries rather than copy the external taxonomy.

Whole/aggregate bearer changes do not force a universal child.

**No new child.**

## Holdout 4 — Market / financial domain

Examples:
- current price;
- bid-ask spread;
- displayed depth;
- current inventory/exposure magnitude.

SEC market-structure materials use spreads, depth, volatility, turnover and distributions. Important boundary:
- instantaneous/current characteristic can fit here;
- volatility or distributions computed over a time window may be derived Information over Dynamics rather than primitive snapshot condition.

This is a derivation/time boundary, not a new child.

**No new child.**

## Holdout 5 — Institution / organization

Examples:
- current headcount;
- current operating mode/category where factually instantiated;
- current resource/capacity magnitude;
- relation-valued characteristic such as geographic separation between units.

Keep distinct:
- membership → Constituent Composition;
- reporting/authority/delegation relation → Structure or Actors depending protected semantics;
- good standing/compliance → Criterion-Relative Standing;
- records declaring the values → Information.

**No new child.**

## Holdout 6 — Scientific specimen / experiment target

Examples:
- concentration;
- mass fraction;
- temperature;
- colour/category;
- sequence/property identity.

BIPM/IUPAC source traditions deliberately span magnitude-bearing quantities and nominal/qualitative properties. Measurement/examination outputs remain evidence surfaces.

**No new child.**

## Holdout 7 — Hybrid cyber-physical system

Examples:
- actual physical temperature;
- actuator mode;
- network latency;
- battery state/charge magnitude;
- relation-valued distance between mobile components.

W3C/OGC SSN makes the observation/property/result separation explicit across physical and software sensors. Hybridity does not require a separate characteristic family.

**No new child.**

## Holdout 8 — Whole / constituent role switching

The same kind of characteristic can be borne by:
- constituent;
- relation;
- subsystem;
- selected whole.

Emergent whole characteristics can require whole-level evidence without changing the first-order protected question.

**Scope/bearer is a dimension, not a universal child.**

## Holdout 9 — Relation-valued characteristic

OGC spatial standards discriminate topological relation facts from distance computations. The project analog is:

```text
relation exists / organization form     → Relational Organization
current magnitude/category of relation  → Realized Characteristics candidate
```

**No collapse into Structure.**

## Holdout conclusion

No tested materially different domain exposed a recurrent third Domain Condition region or a stable immediate child under Realized Characteristics.

**F19 rejected under current bounded evidence.**
