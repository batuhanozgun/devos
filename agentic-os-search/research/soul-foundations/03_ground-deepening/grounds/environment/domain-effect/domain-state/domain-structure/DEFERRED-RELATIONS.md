# Domain Structure — DEFERRED RELATIONS

**Status:** captured during Structure research; not systematic composition

These observations are preserved for later relation/control research. They do not authorize another node.

## R1 — Composition ↔ Relational Organization

- constituent membership determines the bearer set over which many structural relations are evaluated;
- structural relations can help operationally determine whether a set of constituents forms a relevant whole;
- part-of is formally a relation, but in this project it belongs to Composition when it answers constitution/membership;
- the branches are analytically distinct but not ontologically disjoint.

No final relation taxonomy is defined here.

## R2 — Structure ↔ Condition

- structure can contribute causally to Condition;
- Condition can motivate reconfiguration;
- a relational fact can be Structure or Condition depending on semantic role;
- `present but unhealthy/unavailable` must not be collapsed into `absent from structure`.

## R3 — Structure ↔ Dynamics

- Dynamics can add/remove constituents and create/remove/rewire structural relations;
- before/after snapshots belong to Structure;
- interaction/flow/exchange through a standing relation is not the relation itself;
- structural change does not by itself establish why the change occurred.

## R4 — Structure ↔ Information

- inventories, BOMs, architecture diagrams, topology maps, Kubernetes objects, baselines, ontologies and digital twins can represent structure;
- they can be stale, wrong, partial, prospective or differently scoped;
- evidence/currentness/confidence/conflict belong to later Information/composition reasoning.

## R5 — Structure ↔ Actors

- realized institutional role relations can be Domain Structure when organization is the focal domain;
- Actor identity/capability/authority/responsibility remain Actors semantics when those are the protected questions;
- the same role relation may need cross-ground composition later without duplicate intrinsic object types.

## R6 — Structure ↔ Work

- Work can discover, verify, construct, repair or reconfigure Domain Structure;
- the procedure/workflow is not the resulting structure;
- task completion does not establish structural conformance without evidence of actual outcome.

## R7 — Structure ↔ constraints/invariants

- constraints can limit allowed membership and relations;
- current satisfaction/violation can yield Structure/Condition facts;
- the rule itself remains cross-cutting and may have Information representations.

## R8 — Structure ↔ identity/continuity

- membership comparison through time requires identity criteria;
- component replacement can alter composition while preserving or destroying whole identity;
- identity connects Structure to Dynamics and remains cross-cutting.

## R9 — Structure ↔ scale/scope

- selected scope determines constituent membership and external/internal relation classification;
- nested systems permit recursive structural analysis;
- different useful representations may choose different granularity without changing the represented reality by fiat.

## R10 — Structure ↔ causality

A structural error can be causal, contributory, correlated or incidental.

```text
observed structural mismatch ≠ established root cause
```

Later causal attribution must recover mechanism/evidence rather than infer causation from structural difference alone.

## Guardrail

Do not expand these notes into systematic cross-ground composition, architecture, ontology, graph design or control semantics during Structure closure.
