## Protected question

> Relative to the materially relevant and applicable criterion, expected function, rule or constitutive regime, what true current standing obtains for the selected bearer or whole?

This question is distinct from:
- what current characteristics obtain;
- what criterion exists;
- what evidence or assessment result is available;
- what document/report/status field says;
- who has authority;
- why the standing arose;
- how it changed through time.

## Parent decomposition retained

```text

    ├─ Realized Characteristics       ← bounded leaf / accepted
    └─ Criterion-Relative Standing    ← bounded leaf / awaiting user evaluation
```

The sibling distinction survives because standing recovery can fail even when underlying characteristics are recovered correctly:
- wrong criterion;
- wrong version;
- wrong scope/jurisdiction;
- wrong bearer binding;
- misunderstood expected function;
- ignored exception/waiver;

Conversely, a standing can often be recovered without reconstructing every irrelevant characteristic.

## Generic recovery structure

A cross-domain explanatory pattern is:

```text
materially relevant current facts / characteristics
+ materially applicable criterion / expected function / rule / regime
+ correct scope / binding
→ possible true current criterion-relative standing
```

This is not a proposed schema, inference engine or ontology relation. It describes why standing is a distinct research-recovery problem.

## Label refinement

### Why keep `Criterion-Relative`

Without criterion relativity, standing collapses into generic current state/characteristics.

Examples:

```text
memory.available = 500 MiB
→ Realized Characteristics

MemoryPressure = true relative to configured threshold
→ Criterion-Relative Standing
```

```text
capital ratio = x
→ Realized Characteristics

well-capitalized under applicable regulatory thresholds
→ Criterion-Relative Standing
```

### Why drop `Status`

`Status` is semantically overloaded:
- lifecycle/current-state label;
- API/database/report field;
- institutional/legal state;
- broad synonym for condition;
- synonym for standing.

Keeping it as a co-primary label invites source/representation collapse. Domain-native `status` vocabulary can still be preserved within specific cases.

## Standing-family split result

The pass explicitly challenged whether the node should split by criterion/standing family.

### Functional / operational

Examples:
- healthy/degraded/failed;
- Ready/not-ready;
- operable/inoperable.

These depend on expected function, readiness gates or functional criteria. They fit the node but do not require a universal child.

### Normative / regulatory

Examples:
- conforming/nonconforming;
- compliant/non-compliant.

ISO audit vocabulary is especially discriminating: the same audit-finding structure can be termed conformity/nonconformity generally and compliance/non-compliance when criteria are statutory/regulatory. Criterion provenance matters, but the first-order recovery question remains criterion fulfilment.

### Legal / institutional

Examples:
- good standing;
- active/suspended/revoked license;
- legally eligible/ineligible where defined by a regime.

These cases may be partly constituted by rules, filings and authorized acts. That increases the importance of Actors/authority and criterion-binding semantics, but does not produce a separate universal standing child.

### Validity / quality / acceptability

These words are too overloaded to support a universal child. `Validation` and `verification`, for example, can name conformity-assessment processes over claims/information rather than a reality-side standing of a domain object.

### Conclusion

Criterion family changes semantics, authority and evidence needs, but does not consistently change the protected standing-recovery problem enough to warrant universal immediate children.

## Result-scale split rejected

Standing may be:
- binary;
- ternary;
- ordinal;
- graded;
- severity-based;
- categorical.

FDIC capital categories and EPA good/fair/poor assessments show that graded standings are normal. Result scale is important but does not define a separate first-order research branch.

## Bearer / aggregation split rejected

Standing may apply to:
- a constituent;
- relation;
- subsystem;
- whole;
- institution;
- population/site/region.

Whole-level standing can require an aggregation rule. This is a scope/bearer and criterion-composition issue rather than a universal child.

## Access / assessment split rejected

Standing may be recovered through:
- direct checkpoint evaluation;
- measurement + decision rule;
- health/diagnostic model;
- audit/inspection;
- statistical assessment;
- authoritative registry;
- regulator/board/court adjudication.

These materially change evidence and verification. They do not create distinct reality-side standing types merely because access differs.

## Reality versus representation

The strongest anti-collapse result is:

```text
true current standing
≠ criterion / standard / policy by identity
≠ assessment process by identity
≠ evidence / measurement by identity
≠ model / score by identity
≠ API/status field / dashboard by identity
≠ certificate / report / attestation by identity
≠ Actor authority by identity
```

### NIST conformity counterexample

NIST decision-rule work distinguishes characteristics that truly conform from measurement results accepted under a decision rule. Measurement uncertainty can generate false acceptance or false rejection.

Therefore an assessment can say `accept/conforming` while the true conformity standing differs, and vice versa.

This is material evidence that criterion-relative standing cannot simply be identified with assessment output or Information state.

### Legal/institutional counterexample

Official corporate/licensing systems distinguish current status/standing from certificates or verification outputs that certify/report that standing.

Institutional facts may depend on rules and authority, but that does not make them identical to the document/database field through which we learn them.

## Authority boundary

Institutional standing may require:
- valid rule-making authority;
- jurisdiction;
- filings/payment;
- disciplinary acts;
- adjudication;
- delegated powers.

Those are material truth/binding mechanisms. The Actor's authority role is not itself the standing. Later composition must connect them without collapsing Actors into Domain Condition.

## Capability / readiness / availability / reliability

No new child survives:
- readiness can be current criterion-relative standing when gates/checkpoints are the protected question;
- capability is generally an ability/disposition under specified conditions;
- availability is often probabilistic and time-indexed;
- reliability is probability of successful function over a time period.

These terms require role-sensitive routing across Condition, Dynamics and Work Conditions rather than a universal standing child.

## Dynamics boundary

Keep:

```text
current standing = degraded
```

separate from:

```text
healthy → degraded transition
standing duration
recovery/failure process
standing trend/history
future projected standing
```

The latter require Dynamics/time semantics.

## Causal boundary

A standing such as `degraded`, `noncompliant`, `suspended` or `not-ready` does not itself establish:
- root cause;
- responsible actor;
- mechanism;
- causal attribution.

Those remain separate recovery questions.
