# Constituent Composition Finding 04 — Scope, Time, Identity and Nesting

**Status:** bounded child-pass finding  
**Date:** 2026-09-04

## Question

Do open-system boundaries, temporary/fuzzy participation, nesting, simultaneous multi-membership or identity through replacement require additional immediate children under `Constituent Composition`?

## Result

**No. They remain load-bearing dimensions/reopen triggers, but none survives as a universal immediate child at this level.**

## Boundary / scope

Composition is always relative to a selected whole/reference scope.

Questions such as:
- is an external service a constituent or merely interfacing Environment?
- does a supplier count inside the selected supply network?
- is a laboratory instrument part of the experimental system or a Work Condition?

cannot be answered by object type alone.

The boundary/reference frame determines which constituent relation is under evaluation. Because the same issue governs Relational Organization, Condition and Dynamics, `Boundary / Scope` remains cross-cutting rather than Composition-local child.

### F8
`boundary/scope` as immediate child → **rejected**.

## Temporary / intermittent participation

SoS evidence shows constituent participation need not be permanent. W3C ORG explicitly allows membership duration. OBO temporal semantics likewise shows even parthood among continuants can vary over time.

Therefore temporary participation is not unique to one membership ontology.

Current rule:

```text
who/what is a constituent at t
→ current Constituent Composition snapshot

joining/leaving/addition/removal/change of membership through time
→ Domain Dynamics

history/validity interval used as evidence about membership at t
→ Information/temporal evidence boundary as appropriate
```

### F7
open/temporary participation as separate child → **rejected**.

## Fuzzy / graded membership

Some domains use probabilistic, peripheral or threshold-based inclusion.

The pass does not assume all actual constituency is metaphysically binary. However:
- uncertainty about whether an entity is included can be epistemic/Information-side;
- a criterion that decides inclusion can be normative/model-side;
- a realized participation degree can be a Condition-like characteristic;
- the final structural inclusion decision remains scope/relation-relative.

No recurrent Composition-local recovery program distinct from those boundaries was exposed.

## Nesting / decomposition

Nested composition is recursive:

```text
whole
→ constituent subsystem
→ constituent component
→ lower constituent
```

The same composition question is re-applied at a new selected whole/granularity.

Nesting does not itself require a separate semantic child; it changes reference level and preserves parent/constituent relations.

### F6
nesting/hierarchy as immediate child → **rejected**.

## Simultaneous participation in several wholes

A constituent system may participate in multiple SoS; an organization/person may participate in several groups; a resource may be shared among several structural scopes.

This is not an anomaly. It demonstrates that constituency need not imply exclusive ownership or integral parthood.

Treat multiple current constituent relations separately, each bound to its selected whole/scope.

## Identity / replacement

Examples:
- one wheel is replaced while the vehicle persists;
- a team changes a member while the team persists;
- a Service backend Pod is replaced while the Service persists;
- a SoS swaps a constituent system while maintaining mission identity.

These cases make identity criteria load-bearing, but the criterion is not Composition-local: identity also governs the bearer of Condition and continuity through Dynamics.

Current composition answers **what is included now**. Whether the higher whole remains the same whole through replacement is a cross-cutting identity/continuity question.

### F9
identity/continuity as Composition-local immediate child → **rejected**.

## Resulting internal dimensions

Retain explicitly without child promotion:
- reference whole / scope / granularity;
- constituent-relation semantics;
- temporal validity at selected time;
- nesting / recursive decomposition;
- simultaneous multi-whole participation;
- identity criteria and replacement boundary;
- uncertain/graded inclusion boundary.
