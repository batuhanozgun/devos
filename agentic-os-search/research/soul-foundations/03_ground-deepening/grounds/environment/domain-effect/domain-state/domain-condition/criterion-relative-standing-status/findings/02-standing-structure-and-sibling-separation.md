# Finding 02 — Standing structure and sibling separation

**Status:** supported bounded finding

## Core result

The dedicated pass retains a material distinction between:

```text
Realized Characteristics
and
Criterion-Relative Standing
```

A useful generic recovery pattern is:

```text
materially relevant current facts/characteristics
+ applicable criterion / requirement / expected function / constitutive regime
+ correct scope/binding
→ true current criterion-relative standing
```

This is a recovery structure, not a proposed implementation formula or ontology rule.

## Why standing does not reduce to Realized Characteristics

Correct current characteristics are not enough when the criterion is wrong, inapplicable, obsolete, bound to a different jurisdiction/scope, or semantically misunderstood.

Examples:
- the same temperature can be acceptable for one component and over-temperature for another;
- the same market-value figure can satisfy one listing tier and violate another;
- the same corporate filing/tax facts can have different legal implications across jurisdictions;
- the same resource signal can produce or not produce a pressure condition depending on the configured threshold.

Recovery of the standing therefore requires more than recovery of the underlying characteristic.

## Conformity versus assessment result

NIST conformity/decision-rule work provides a strong anti-collapse case:
- the conformance zone concerns characteristics that truly conform to the specification;
- the acceptance zone concerns measurement results that will be accepted under a decision rule;
- measurement uncertainty can produce false acceptance or false rejection.

Therefore:

```text
true conformity/nonconformity
≠ acceptance/rejection decision based on evidence by identity
```

This makes it possible for a true criterion-relative standing to be distinct from our assessment result or confidence about it.

## Relational character does not eliminate the node

Standing is often relational in the broad sense that it is relative to a criterion/regime. That does not make it merely `Relational Organization` or an unworthy research region.

The protected question is not structural organization among constituents. It is whether a bearer currently occupies a criterion-relative condition such as conforming, ready, healthy or in good standing.

Likewise, the criterion relation can later be represented as an explicit typed relation without eliminating the distinct recovery problem.

- F16: **rejected** — do not merge standing into Realized Characteristics.
