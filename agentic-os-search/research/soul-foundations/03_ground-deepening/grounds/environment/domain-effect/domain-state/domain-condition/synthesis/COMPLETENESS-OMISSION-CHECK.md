## Rival-decomposition audit

### A — one unsplit `Condition / Status` leaf

**Weakened.**

Collapsing current characteristics and criterion-relative standing loses a material dependency: standing requires identifying/applying a relevant criterion, while characteristics do not necessarily do so.

### B — `Realized Properties / Quantities` + `Health / Quality`

**Refined rather than adopted literally.**

`Health` can name underlying condition or a health classification. `Quality` is explicitly requirement-relative in ISO vocabulary. Stronger semantic boundary is criterion-relativity, not the words health/quality.

### C — `Descriptive Condition` + `Evaluative / Normative Status`

**Closest rival; retained with terminology correction.**

`Evaluative` can sound subjective and `normative` is too narrow for functional health/readiness. The stronger child label is `Criterion-Relative Standing / Status`.

### D — continuous/quantitative + categorical mode/status

**Rejected.**

This is primarily a value/data-form split. A mode can be a categorical characteristic; standing can also be scalar/ordinal.

### E — constituent + whole/aggregate condition

**Rejected.**

Scope/aggregation cross-cuts both children.

### F — intrinsic + relational condition

**Rejected.**

Property-metaphysics distinction does not produce stable recovery boundaries and is not cleanly equivalent to relational/non-relational.

### G — Condition + Constraint / Compliance

**Rejected as third child.**

Constraints/rules are not current condition by identity. Current satisfaction/violation/compliance is criterion-relative standing.

### H — reduction into Structure + Dynamics + representation

**Rejected.**

A current non-structural reality question remains even if representation and change-process questions are removed.

### I — bounded leaf

**Rejected at this depth.**

Two stable immediate questions survive.
