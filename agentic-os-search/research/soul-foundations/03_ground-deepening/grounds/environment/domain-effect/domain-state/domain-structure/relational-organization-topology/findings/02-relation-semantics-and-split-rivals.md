# Relational Organization Finding 02 — Relation Semantics and Split Rivals

**Status:** bounded finding  
**Date:** 2026-09-04

## Question

Do connectivity, dependency, interface, spatial and higher-order relations form stable immediate children, or are they relation-semantic forms inside one coherent problem region?

## Result

**No tested relation-semantic split survives as a universal immediate child.**

The differences are real and must be preserved, but the stable higher-order protected question remains:

> which structurally material relation/arrangement facts actually obtain among the selected constituents at the selected scope/time?

## Connectivity versus dependency

A connection and a dependency are not identical:
- `A is physically/logically connected to B`;
- `A depends on B for service/resource/function`.

However, current software/runtime traditions commonly represent dependency as one typed relationship in a broader dependency/connectivity graph. The correction task is still to reconstruct the actual typed relation among constituents.

No recurrent cross-domain evidence forces a child hierarchy such as:

```text
Relational Organization
    ├─ Connectivity
    └─ Dependency
```

This would privilege relation vocabulary rather than a distinct first-order recovery program.

## Pairwise versus higher-order relations

Simple graphs encode pairwise relations, but higher-order network research shows real systems can contain irreducible group relations modeled by hypergraphs or simplicial structures.

This **does not** create a `pairwise vs higher-order` project child split.

Instead it establishes a guardrail:

> Relational Organization must not assume that every structurally relevant relation is dyadic or reducible to graph edges.

Arity is therefore a relation-form dimension unless later evidence shows a separate recovery program.

## Directed, weighted, signed and typed relations

Directionality, arity, weighting and sign can materially change the meaning of organization, but formal representational distinctions alone do not justify child promotion.

A directed supply dependency, an undirected physical contact relation and an n-ary coordination relation can all belong to the node while requiring different relation semantics.

## Local relations versus global organization patterns

Global structural patterns such as reachability, bottlenecks, hierarchy, redundancy or clustering may be derived from or summarize lower-level relation facts.

The project should not force a hierarchy of:

```text
local relation facts
vs
global topology
```

because:
- many global patterns are reducible to the selected relation structure at a chosen abstraction;
- some higher-order relations are not pairwise but remain direct structure facts;
- numeric summaries of those patterns may cross into `Domain Condition → Realized Characteristics / Values` when the value itself is the protected question.

## Falsifier impact

- F4 connectivity/dependency versus spatial split → not decided here; tested fully in Finding 03.
- F5 connection versus dependency child split → **rejected**.
- F17 relation-form taxonomy trap → **rejected as child-generating basis**.
- F18 derived-pattern child → **rejected as a universal child**.
