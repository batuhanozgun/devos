## Question

## Verdict

**No. `Realized Characteristics` is a bounded leaf at the current foundation depth.**

## Why the candidate children failed

### Quantitative vs qualitative/categorical
Legitimate value/property forms, but same first-order recovery question.

### Intrinsic vs relational
No stable universal binary; philosophical and project counterexamples expose several non-equivalent distinctions.

### Mode/regime
Current mode can be a characteristic; mode transition/history belongs to Dynamics. No independent universal recovery program.

### Intensive vs extensive
Real physical quantity distinction, but domain-specific and non-exhaustive.

### Whole/aggregate/emergent
Bearer/scope dimension. Whole-level characteristics can be real without a separate universal child.

### Observable vs latent/hidden
Evidence-access distinction; actual property remains the same kind of reality-side question.

### Scalar/vector/distribution/field/structured
Mathematical/result shape, often representation-specific.

### Capability/capacity/availability/reliability
Role-sensitive mixture of current magnitudes, dispositions, probabilities, stated conditions, criteria and temporal models. No stable universal child.
