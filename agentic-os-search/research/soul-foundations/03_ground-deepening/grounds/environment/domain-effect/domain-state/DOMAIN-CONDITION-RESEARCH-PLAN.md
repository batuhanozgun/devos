The pass must explicitly test rather than assume:

1. Is `Domain Condition / Status` one coherent node, or an arbitrary bundle of different phenomena?
2. Is `Condition` the stronger primary label, with `Status` explanatory only, or does another label better protect the intended problem?
3. Do descriptive properties/quantities and evaluative health/quality conditions require separate immediate children?
4. Do categorical operating modes/statuses/standings form a distinct child from scalar/continuous/qualitative properties?
5. Is `health` a realized condition, an inference/evaluation over conditions, or role-relative depending on use?
6. Is current capability/capacity/availability a Condition fact, a Work Condition, a Structure implication, or role-relative across these?
7. Does whole/system condition form a distinct first-order child from constituent condition, or is level/scope a cross-cutting reference dimension?
8. Do intrinsic versus relational properties form stable children, or merely property forms that recur within one Condition problem?
9. Does current constraint satisfaction/violation/compliance belong to Condition while constraints/rules themselves remain cross-cutting/Information-side?
10. Can institutional standing/status remain a Domain Condition fact without absorbing Actor authority or rule/Record semantics?
11. Where is the boundary between current mode/state and Dynamics, especially when mode is defined by transition history or persistence?
12. Where is the boundary between current condition and causal explanation/diagnosis/prognosis?
13. Can actual realized Condition remain distinct from observed/measured/inferred/reported status across partially observable systems?
14. Do physical, software, biological/ecological, market, institutional and hybrid domains share one project-level decomposition without type branches becoming the hierarchy?
15. Does deeper Condition research expose a missing co-equal Domain State region and reopen the parent result?
16. Does any further split add genuine first-order recovery/reasoning value, or only vocabulary/data-type/evaluation distinctions?

## Plan adversarial review before execution

The execution must guard against four plan-level biases:

1. **Parent-label anchoring:** the inherited list `properties / values / qualities / health / mode / status / standing` may itself overbundle or over-separate the phenomenon.
2. **Data-model bias:** scalar vs categorical, attribute vs relation, field vs enum are representation primitives and cannot become research children without independent problem-role evidence.
3. **Monitoring-domain bias:** SRE/PHM/condition-monitoring vocabularies may over-emphasize health and observability; they are challenge sources, not project taxonomies.
4. **Normative-status bias:** words such as healthy, compliant, valid or available may encode comparison against a criterion. The pass must distinguish the realized underlying condition, the realized institutional standing, the criterion/rule, and the assessment/report.

The strongest rival decompositions must be given real opportunity to win; the plan does not precommit to either a leaf or a multi-child result.
