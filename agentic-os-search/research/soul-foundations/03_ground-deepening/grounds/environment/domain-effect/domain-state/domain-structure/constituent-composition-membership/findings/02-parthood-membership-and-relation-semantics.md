# Constituent Composition Finding 02 — Parthood, Membership and Constituent-Relation Semantics

**Status:** bounded child-pass finding  
**Date:** 2026-09-04

## Question

Should `Constituent Composition` split immediately into something like:

```text
Constitutive Parthood / Composition
Participation / Membership
```

because physical/system parts and group/network participants have different semantics?

## Result

**No universal immediate-child split is currently justified.**

The difference is real and must be preserved, but the strongest evidence says it behaves as a **constituent-relation semantic dimension/family**, not as one stable two-way hierarchy.

## Why the distinction is real

Do not infer one relation from another.

Examples:
- a person's finger can be part of the person while the person is a member of a team; it does not follow that the finger is a team member;
- a constituent system can participate in several systems-of-systems while remaining an independent useful system;
- organizational membership can carry role and duration semantics without making the member an integral physical part;
- an ingredient/constituent of a mixture is not automatically governed by the same relation rules as a machine component.

Therefore the node must preserve a relation-type question such as:

> **In what sense does this entity count as a constituent here — integral part, member, participant, ingredient/material constituent, aggregate member, or another domain-valid constituent relation?**

## Why this does not become two immediate children

A binary split would be unstable and non-exhaustive.

The external counterframes include at least:
- strict/general parthood;
- material constitution;
- mixture/ingredient composition;
- group membership;
- aggregate `member-part` treatments;
- autonomous constituent-system participation;
- organizational affiliation/membership.

Traditions disagree on which of these reduce to parthood. Forcing a project child split between only `parthood` and `membership` would therefore import a contested ontology choice rather than discover a stable problem decomposition.

## Operationally, the higher recovery question remains shared

Across these relation forms the first recovery step is still:

1. select the structural whole/reference scope;
2. determine what relation qualifies something as a constituent at that scope;
3. determine which actual entities satisfy that relation now.

The exact relation semantics matter for inference, but do not yet create separate universal research programs.

## Same-object / nested counterexample

A SoS constituent may itself have integral components. One layer uses autonomous participation semantics; a lower layer uses stronger component/parthood semantics. The relation form changes with scope without creating a new top-level composition problem.

Likewise an organization may contain posts structurally and people may hold memberships/roles; exact role/authority semantics remain Actors/Information-side where appropriate.

## Falsifier judgment

- F4 `parthood/composition` versus `participation/membership` immediate split → **rejected as universal immediate split**.
- F6 nesting as separate child → **rejected**; nesting is recursive constituent composition with scope-level changes.
- F15 Actor leakage → **rejected**; membership does not absorb role/authority semantics.

## Retained internal dimension

`Constituent-relation semantics` remains explicit and decision-critical inside the leaf candidate.

Do not implement this as one generic transitive `partOf` relation merely because the research node is unified.
