2. **Descendant sufficiency:** every discovered unresearched Structure/Condition child is either sufficiently supported for the current ground purpose without its own pass, or its omission is non-material to later synthesis. Mere existence in a parent pass is insufficient.
3. **Method compliance:** closure respects `G3 Selective Deepening`, the child-promotion logic and the one-node/one-level method; planned-sequence completion is not used as a closure argument.
4. **Boundary integrity:** deeper results do not collapse Environment into Information, Work, Actors, causality/control or architecture.

6. **Cross-domain robustness:** materially different domains do not expose a recurrent Environment-local case outside the current map.

## Decision-critical question — descendant research debt

The strongest current threat to closure is not a new parent branch. It is the four stable children discovered by the Structure and Condition passes.

The Ground Deepening plan says selective deepening is required when a branch is materially under-supported, with triggers including:
- a load-bearing decision depends on it;
- it exposes a distinct failure mode;
- current evidence only names it but does not explain it;
- competing frames produce materially different implications;
- it is needed to distinguish the ground from another ground.

The review must test each discovered child against these criteria rather than treating all four as one undifferentiated debt.
