## 4. Accepted Environment boundary recovered

Current Environment ground owns actual realised operating/domain conditions and Work-support conditions.

Work therefore must not silently own:

- whether a resource/capability actually exists or is ready;
- actual runtime/domain state;
- actual external effect/state merely because Work intends or changes it;
- environmental constraints as if they were only item-local Work state.

Work may target, depend on, observe or alter these conditions; the conditions themselves remain Environment-side under the current four-ground frame.

## 5. Accepted Information boundary recovered

Current Information ground owns mediated informational states/representations and formation/derivation/selection-exposure transitions.

Work therefore must distinguish actual Work from representations such as:

- request/intent descriptions;
- plans/specifications;
- status reports;
- evidence/test outputs;
- context/working views;
- records/history;
- acceptance/review statements.

The existence or currentness of a Work representation does not by itself establish actual execution, outcome or Environment state.

## 6. Actors boundary recovered for audit use

The current Actors vertical separates:

- bearer identity;
- role/position;
- participation;
- capability/competence;
- permission/authority/obligation;
- delegation/representation;
- responsibility/accountability.

Work may contain decision/participation points, but who may perform, commit, waive, accept, terminate or reprioritise remains Actor/authority-side unless later composition establishes a different relation model.
