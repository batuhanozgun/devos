## 4. Strong boundary corrections retained

### Actors vs Information

```text
Actor standing
!= representation/evidence of Actor standing
```

Identity records, role files, tokens, delegation claims, logs and accountability statements are informational states. They may evidence Actors relations but are not identical to them.

### Actors vs Environment

```text
Actor capability/competence
!= Environment resource/access/availability
!= situated actionability
```

Likewise:

```text
Actor authority
!= technical effect power
!= runtime enforcement outcome
```

### Actors vs Work

```text
Actor participation/assignment
!= Work identity/admission/execution

Work requiredness
!= Actor obligation

Work acceptance state
!= authority to accept
```

### Operational vs normative attribution

```text
system produced an effect
!= system is the institutional/legal/moral bearer
!= system is accountable
```

## 5. Effective / transitive authority result

The strongest new agentic pressure did not create a new Actors family.

A path such as:

```text
A → B → privileged tool/effect
```

can mean A has effective technical reach without A having legitimate authority.

The later system must distinguish:

- direct authority;
- delegated authority;
- invocation/reachability;
- Environment capability/access;
- Work/orchestration path;
- policy legitimacy;
- attribution/accountability.

Therefore:

> **effective effect power is a high-priority cross-ground relation/control problem, not a synonym for authority and not a new Actor child.**

## 7. Non-performing parties result

The audit explicitly strengthens one orientation correction:

> `Actors` must not be interpreted as `only active doers`.

Beneficiaries, affected parties, rights holders, principals, recipients and accountability forums can be semantically relevant even when they do not perform a positive action.

This does not force a new `Parties/Stakeholders` ground. The same bearer can occupy different performer/non-performer positions across situations, and these positions remain typed relations around bearer/standing/participation/attribution.

## 8. Non-human agency result

Modern AI agents can exercise substantial operational discretion—tool choice, delegation, subagent spawning, adaptive planning and recovery.

This increases the importance of the prior distinction but does not break it:

```text
operational agency/discretion
!= legal/institutional attribution
!= authority/obligation
!= moral/personhood status
```

No separate non-human ground is justified.
