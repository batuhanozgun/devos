# Actors Ground Audit — A4 External Holdout Decision

**Status:** CONDITIONALLY SKIPPED / REOPENABLE  
**Date:** 2026-09-05

## Question

After A1 boundary testing, A2 omission testing and A3 newer candidate-study holdouts, is broad new external research justified by a live first-order Actors gap hypothesis?

## Decision

**No, not currently.**

A4 broad external expansion is conditionally skipped.

## Why

1. The prior Actors vertical already spans materially different evidence traditions:
   - W3C provenance;
   - NIST RBAC/ABAC;
   - delegated authorization / RFC 8693;
   - agency/apparent-authority law;
   - action/omission philosophy;
   - moral responsibility/accountability;
   - shared/collective/corporate agency;
   - automated-action attribution;
   - human-machine/joint-system analysis.
2. That prior corpus produced real falsifiers and frame changes rather than only confirming the starting Actor concept.
3. A1 resolved current boundary-theft hypotheses without exposing a missing Actors-local region.
4. A2 attacked effective authority, epistemic authority, non-performing parties, stewardship, dynamic collectives, multi-hop delegation, human gates and high-discretion AI; none survived as a new first-order family.
5. A3 introduced post-vertical agentic-system holdouts and they reinforced existing distinctions rather than breaking them.
6. No decision-critical structural question remains for which another broad source family would currently discriminate among live rival outcomes.

## Why this is not `no more research exists`

Actors literature is obviously much larger than the inspected corpus. Additional domains could refine:

- legal authority vocabulary;
- fiduciary/stewardship duties;
- consent/rights-holder structures;
- collective identity thresholds;
- competence/expertise;
- AI autonomy/personhood;
- accountability regimes.

But at the current Foundation depth these are increasingly likely to refine thresholds/vocabulary/domain rules rather than reveal another universal Actors-local first-order family.

## Reopen condition

Reopen A4 if A5 or later top-level adequacy produces a specific live falsifier such as:

- a stable non-performing-party region not expressible through bearer/stake relations;
- a form of effective/transitive authority requiring a genuinely new Actor-side semantic rather than cross-ground path composition;
- a non-human agency case that breaks operational-vs-normative attribution separation;
- a stable child decomposition requiring external discrimination;
- evidence that the Actors ground itself should be replaced or split.
