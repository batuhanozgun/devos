## Current bounded result

Actors / Agency–Participation–Authority remains a coherent, boundedly sufficient Foundation ground with no promoted universal immediate-child hierarchy at current depth.

## Residual Actors-local questions

1. **Bearer continuity:** what continuity rules should apply to organizations, offices, software/AI agents and agent instances across restart, replacement, merger or succession?
2. **Role / office vocabulary:** when must role, office, position, appointment and membership be separate in later synthesis?
3. **Competence/expertise:** which distinctions among skill, qualification, certification, demonstrated performance, domain expertise and model capability become load-bearing in actual SOUL use cases?
4. **Authority vocabulary:** when must permission, authority, power, mandate, entitlement and consent be modeled separately rather than preserved only as non-collapse distinctions?
5. **Effective authority / effect power:** can later relation/control composition calculate reachable effect power and legitimate delegated authority without conflating them?
6. **Affected/beneficiary/rights-holder positions:** do later SOUL domains repeatedly require a stable party/stake family beyond typed bearer relations?
7. **Collective continuity:** how should corporate/group identity and accountability survive changing membership, reorganization or dynamically formed agent teams?
8. **Omission representation:** how should possible/expected conduct and meaningful non-action be represented without duplicating Work or normative policy structure?
9. **Responsibility regimes:** which operational, governance, contractual, legal, safety, fiduciary or moral responsibility/accountability regimes are actually needed later?
10. **Non-human agency thresholds:** when does operational discretion require treating an AI/software system as an enduring bearer rather than a process/tool/role occupant, without overclaiming legal/moral status?
11. **Epistemic roles:** how should expertise, certification mandate, trust and source authority compose across Actors and Information?
12. **Standing responsibility:** how should long-lived stewardship/ownership/accountability relate to continuing Work while occupants and sessions change?

## Explicit reopen triggers

Reopen Actors substantive research if:

- top-level adequacy shows the current Actors boundary is misleading or incomplete;
- later composition cannot express authority, obligation, delegation, attribution or accountability without a missing Actors-local distinction;
- effective/transitive authority repeatedly requires a stable Actor-side semantic not captured by authority/delegation plus cross-ground path composition;
- affected/beneficiary/rights-holder relations become a stable independent problem region across materially different domains;
- a materially different non-human/collective case breaks the operational-vs-normative attribution separation;
- independent review identifies a first-order omission or incoherent boundary;
- a stable immediate-child decomposition emerges that preserves the current distinctions without false disjointness;
- a load-bearing prior Actors source claim is invalidated and removing it changes the synthesis.

Do not reopen merely because a domain offers more role names, authorization types, legal doctrines, agent topologies or responsibility labels.
