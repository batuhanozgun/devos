# Actors Ground Audit — Deferred Relations

**Status:** current deferred cross-ground relations after Actors audit  
**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Role:** preserve boundary/composition questions; not permission to solve them now

## 1. Actors ↔ Information

Later composition must preserve:

- actual bearer/role/authority/delegation/accountability standing vs informational representations asserting them;
- identity/role/delegation records vs current standing;
- action/decision logs vs actual participation/attribution;
- source authority/expertise/mandate vs claim provenance/reliability/evidence standing;
- context-visible permissions/roles vs actual authority;
- historical authority/delegation reconstruction through exact source/version/time evidence.

## 2. Actors ↔ Environment

Later composition must preserve:

- bearer capability/competence vs actual resource/tool/interface/network availability;
- authority/permission vs technical enforcement;
- credential possession vs legitimate authority;
- sandbox/network/tool restrictions vs normative standing;
- non-human operational participant vs process/runtime/credential/tool substrate;
- Actor capability + Environment affordance + policy/Work conditions → situated actionability.

## 3. Actors ↔ Work

Later composition must preserve:

- participant/performer vs Work identity;
- assignment vs Work admission;
- Work requiredness vs Actor obligation/duty;
- Work acceptance state vs authority to accept;
- performer vs requester/decider/authorizer/principal/accountable party;
- reassignment/delegation without loss of Work identity;
- continuing Work vs standing responsibility/ownership;
- omission/non-action vs expected/available Work conduct.

## 4. Effective / transitive authority and effect power

This is the highest-priority deferred relation exposed by newer agentic cases.

A later control/composition model may need to distinguish and relate:

- direct Actor authority;
- delegated authority;
- represented principal;
- technical capability;
- reachable Actor/tool/channel paths;
- Environment credentials/network/tool access;
- Work/orchestration transitions;
- policy/enforcement;
- actual effect;
- causal/institutional attribution;
- responsibility/accountability.

Critical rule:

```text
reachable effect power != legitimate authority
```

Do not infer authority from technical reachability or one local permission check.

## 5. Epistemic authority / evidence

Later synthesis may need a multi-anchor relation where:

- Actor holds competence/expertise or institutional certification mandate;
- Information carries claim/source/provenance/reliability/uncertainty;
- Work defines review/certification/verification responsibility;
- Environment supplies actual target/source access where relevant.

`trusted expert`, `authoritative source`, `authorized decider` and `verified claim` must not collapse.

## 6. Affected / beneficiary / rights-holder relations

Later composition should test typed relations for parties who may not perform Work:

- beneficiary;
- affected party;
- rights holder;
- recipient;
- principal;
- represented subject;
- forum/oversight body.

The Actors audit does not promote a separate Parties/Stakeholders ground. If later domains cannot express these cleanly as bearer/stake/participation relations, reopen adequacy rather than silently ignoring them.

## 7. Multi-agent / collective agency

Later composition may need to distinguish:

- collection of agents;
- coordinated/shared activity;
- organizational/corporate bearer;
- manager/coordinator role;
- delegation chains;
- group-level decision procedure;
- member-level participation;
- group vs individual attribution/accountability;
- dynamically changing membership/session/process identity.

Do not infer a corporate/group Actor merely from orchestration topology or shared task membership.

## 8. Human governance

Later work should avoid:

```text
human involved → therefore authorized
human approved → therefore technically verified
human owner → therefore performer/coordinator
```

Human status, authority, value judgment, technical expertise, verification role and accountability can diverge.

## 9. Non-human agency

Later synthesis should preserve:

- operational discretion/participation;
- persistent system identity if needed;
- delegated/standing authority;
- represented principal;
- provenance/causal contribution;
- legal/institutional attribution;
- accountability;
- moral/personhood questions where a domain genuinely requires them.

Do not infer normative personhood from operational autonomy.

## 10. Top-level adequacy revisit

After user evaluation of Actors, the ground-deepening stage requires a separate top-level adequacy rerun.

That later pass should test whether:

- `Actors / Agency–Participation–Authority` remains the best top-level label/boundary;
- a label such as `Agency / Participation / Standing` better fits the recovered region;
- affected/non-performing parties pressure the boundary;
- effective authority/control relations expose a missing top-level problem;
- the four-ground frame still remains boundedly sufficient before systematic composition begins.

## Guardrail

These are deliberately deferred relations. This Actors audit does not authorize top-level adequacy, systematic composition, architecture, ontology/data model or implementation.
