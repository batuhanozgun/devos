# Information / Representation State — Node Research Plan

**Status:** active plan for the authorised top-level Information node  
**Method:** `../../HIERARCHICAL-DEEPENING-METHOD.md`

## Objective

Determine whether `Information / Representation State` is a coherent top-level research ground and, if so, identify only its stable immediate first-order child problem regions.

Do not assume that prior adequacy findings, Records research, AI knowledge mechanisms or context/memory engineering classifications already determine the answer.

## Decision-critical rivals to keep alive

### Rival A — Broader Information/Representation ground

Information/Representation is a distinct top-level ground; Records/Persistent Information is a specialised persistent branch.

### Rival B — Reduction to other grounds/relations

No independent Information ground is needed; observations, beliefs/models, context and records can be represented without material loss as properties/relations of Environment, Work and Actors.

### Rival C — Candidate parent is too broad or mixed

A representation ground may be useful, but `Information / Representation State` may merge different analytical levels. Observation/data, epistemic/model state, context/view and persistence may require another parent/split.

### Rival D — Label/framing error

`Information`, `Representation`, `Epistemic State`, or another framing may select materially different boundaries. The current label is provisional.

## Node sequence

### N0 — Canonical/internal evidence recovery

Recover, without restarting completed work:

- current Records corpus state and integrated semantics;
- adequacy evidence that motivated the broader parent;
- relevant SOUL context evidence about context, state, evidence, assumptions and continuity;
- newer AI knowledge strategy and context/memory/harness research as domain/failure-surface evidence, not Foundation authority;
- relevant Environment boundary result where actual reality vs representation matters.

Output: `EVIDENCE-INVENTORY.md` with exact roles and unresolved tensions.

### N1 — Node coherence attack

Test:

- Information vs Representation vs Epistemic framing;
- split/merge/reduction rivals;
- whether the ground becomes an `all describable things` residual bucket;
- whether representation can be distinguished from represented reality without reifying every description into a separate entity.

### N2 — First-principles problem inventory

Derive Information-local failure/problem families independently of ready-made taxonomies.

Seed questions only, not accepted categories:

- referent/subject and scope;
- signal/observation/data;
- assertion/claim;
- interpretation/model/belief;
- inference/derivation;
- evidence and support relation;
- uncertainty/conflict/confidence;
- temporal validity/freshness;
- perspective/holder/shared state;
- context selection/view formation;
- persistence/recordhood boundary.

### N3 — Immediate-child discovery

Cluster only stable first-order problem regions. Do not force symmetry with Environment or Records.

### N4 — Boundary and anti-collapse tests

Stress-test against:

- Environment / actual world state;
- Work state, goals and commitments;
- Actor-local mental/role state;
- Records / persistence;
- same concrete representation serving multiple roles;
- shared vs actor-local representation;
- transient vs persistent representation;
- source vs derived view;
- claim vs evidence vs decision.

### N5 — External and cross-domain falsifiers

Use materially different traditions/domains. Candidate traditions include information theory only where semantically appropriate, epistemology/evidence, databases/data provenance, control/agent observation models, distributed systems/state estimation, records/preservation, HCI/situation awareness, scientific data/measurement and knowledge representation.

No single external taxonomy becomes the project taxonomy by import.

### N6 — Omission / reduction / rival-decomposition audit

Try to find:

- a missing first-order child;
- forced-fit cases;
- a reason to reduce the ground into others;
- a reason to split the parent differently;
- supposed children that are only cross-cutting dimensions.

Same-model audit is non-independent verification.

### N7 — Node closure and user evaluation

Close only when the immediate-child map is boundedly sufficient and broad probes show declining structural information gain.

Return to the user before activating any Information child or another top-level ground.

## Explicit non-goals

This pass does not choose:

- database/storage architecture;
- RAG, Knowledge Graph, memory or context stack;
- final ontology/data model;
- relation/control taxonomy;
- SOUL or Development OS architecture;
- implementation.
