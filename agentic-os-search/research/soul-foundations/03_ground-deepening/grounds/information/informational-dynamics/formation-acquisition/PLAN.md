## Authorization and question

The user accepted the integration/frontier result and explicitly requested continuation. That activates Formation/Acquisition, not the other two Information Dynamics children. The completed integration review is not repeated.

How does a phenomenon, source act, signal, assertion or source resource become an informational state at a specified locus, and what must be preserved to distinguish source encounter, representational realization, evidential support and later use?

The accepted parent identifies this question but does not predetermine a leaf, a child tree or a sensor/ETL/RAG pipeline. A new file, an external API call, an `Observation` label, or a successful parser is not a sufficient classification rule.

## Pass design

- **F0 — Recovery and falsifiers:** recover accepted State/Dynamics, the explicit integration handoff, relevant Composition boundaries and candidate metadata. Preserve exact input identities and current authority.
- **F1 — Origin and realization:** derive a source-relative account of formation, acquisition and representational novelty. Test assertion, measurement, endogenous generation and receiver-relative import.
- **F2 — Coverage and distinguishability:** separate target universe, reachable source opportunities, encountered signals and formed records. Bound what missing or indistinguishable records can establish.
- **F3 — Fidelity, mode, time and observer effects:** test source/claim fidelity, model-mediated acquisition, identity/version, time roles and acquisition that changes the source.
- **F4 — Rival decomposition:** seriously compare source-contact versus representational-realization children, observation/assertion/import children, lifecycle stages and collapse into other Dynamics questions. Independence of failures is necessary pressure, not by itself a sufficient child-promotion proof.
- **F5 — External/candidate pressure:** primary metrology, observation/provenance standards, survey methodology, statistical quality and telemetry sources; selected candidate metadata only unless a claim requires deeper recovery.
- **F6 — Synthesis and structural decision:** propose leaf/children or the minimum justified parent correction; preserve uncertainty and unresearched siblings.
- **F7 — Adversarial closure and publication:** challenge the strongest candidate with contrary cases, execute bounded constructive checks, distinguish semantic review from publication verification, and return for user evaluation.

These are research passes, not required runtime stages. Source discovery and argument revision can iterate. The plan was recorded after canonical recovery and initial source reconnaissance; it is not a preregistered empirical study. The check specification was fixed before executing the constructed checks.

## Falsifiers and success boundaries

Reopen the parent or revise this child if source-facing formation cannot be distinguished from semantic derivation and use-facing exposure; if a universal raw-data-first stage is needed to make the model work; if an origin mode or contact/realization split protects a recurring independent inquiry the common relation systematically obscures; or if non-measurement, copied, generated, qualitative or model-mediated cases become residual exceptions.

A bounded result must retain, where material: exact target and source roles, what was newly instantiated and where, procedure and interpretation assumptions, representational mode, coverage/detectability limits, time/version, source-fidelity versus truth, and actuality versus evidence. It must also retain occurrence-neutral accounting: a false or badly captured representation can still exist.

Do not import external ontologies or statistical thresholds. Do not demand every individual acquisition be exhaustive. A bounded sample can be appropriate if the claim and uncertainty fit its design.

## Return condition

Publish the dedicated result, evidence scope, strongest rival arguments, open conditions, reproducible constructive checks, current state and retrieval routes. Return for evaluation. Derivation/Transformation and Selection/Exposure remain unresearched/inactive; their order is reselected after this result rather than inferred from a universal pipeline. No new descendant is researched automatically.
