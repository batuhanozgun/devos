**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** targeted external challenge complete at the recorded scope

## 1. Why these traditions

The candidate must work beyond software ingestion. Metrology stresses intended versus realized target and model-mediated measurement. Human-source methodology stresses elicitation, source frames and missing responses. Provenance and observation standards stress origin, production and representational mode. Telemetry stresses occurrence versus receipt. The goal is hostile pressure on live distinctions, not a vote among external taxonomies.

## 3. What changed rather than merely survived

The simple source-to-raw-record picture did not survive unchanged. F1/F3 now permit model-mediated and interleaved formation/derivation, use locus-relative rather than absolute firstness, and distinguish generating a representation from observing its claimed referent.

The source-contact/realization rival became stronger under survey-quality pressure. F4 therefore gives it separate diagnostics, countercases, cost and reopen conditions rather than rejecting it because the branches overlap. The bounded leaf decision remains a project judgment, not a consensus result published by these sources.

## 4. Candidate reuse boundary

Catalog and relevant AI Knowledge Strategies / Context-Memory-Harness metadata directed attention to capture, parsing, context formation and source visibility. Those are discovery prompts. No candidate package runtime was rerun or upstream adoption evaluated. Primary sources and explicit counterexamples, not a presumed RAG pipeline, carry the new distinctions.

## 5. Remaining evidence requirements

The pass does not supply empirical detector sensitivity, sample-design validity, semantic-fidelity scores, hidden LLM uptake, cognitive acquisition mechanisms or a universal negative-evidence threshold. Later concrete claims must bind their target, procedure, source data, horizon and verification method. An access limit or untested deployment condition is not erased by the present conceptual closure.
