**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** bounded leaf proposed; evaluation pending  
**Role:** uncertainty and conditional reopening, not automatic execution authority

## Target-specific evidence conditions

1. What source universe and opportunity class are materially relevant to a real inquiry, including unknown or inaccessible sources?
2. What acquisition/detection model is justified, with what sensitivity, false positives, health, reporting loss and uncertainty? F2's exact finite result does not establish these values.
3. Which source distinctions and interpretation dependencies must be preserved to support a given use, especially multimodal, ambiguous or compressed records?
4. When a probe or elicitation changes the source, what correction or causal comparison supports the pre-probe versus under-probe claim?
5. How can an opaque producer's reported origin or mode be independently tested rather than accepted from its own metadata?
6. How should source-reference, production and receipt clocks be reconciled when their comparability is unknown?
7. When does imputation or model-assisted acquisition add warranted information rather than merely plausible unsupported completion?
8. What evidence distinguishes genuinely different origins from shared upstream reports and common-mode errors?

## Structural reopening

Reopen the leaf if a recurring Source Contact/Coverage or Representational Realization inquiry cannot be recovered from the common grammar without systematic loss; if source-relative formation collapses into Derivation/Exposure without losing a diagnosis; if the system/locus choice becomes arbitrarily outcome-driven; if an endogenous, gradual, qualitative or cognitive formation case requires a materially different protected question; or if independent research yields a stronger decomposition.

Overlapping cases are not by themselves evidence against children. Nor is the fact that a broad grammar can contain all examples evidence for a leaf. The test is preserved diagnostic meaning at a defensible scope.
