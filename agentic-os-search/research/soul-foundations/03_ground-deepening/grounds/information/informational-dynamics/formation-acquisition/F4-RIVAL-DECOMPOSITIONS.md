# F4 — Rival Decompositions and the Cost of a Leaf

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** strongest current structural judgment; challenged again in F7

## 1. Promotion criterion

The task is to find protected research questions, not disjoint event types. Overlap therefore does not by itself reject a child. Conversely, independent failure locations do not automatically require a separate child for every time, unit, identity or scope qualifier.

The canonical Hierarchical Ground Deepening Method asks whether separation is materially useful. A distinct first-order question, otherwise-unexplained failure, cross-tradition convergence, repeated loss on collapse, or distinct boundary behavior can support promotion; these are not a mandatory all-of checklist. It also warns against promoting generic observability, uncertainty, identity and reference-frame dimensions. A leaf must retain diagnostics explicitly; merely claiming that one broad category can contain everything is insufficient. This method was re-read during final structural review, before publication, to check that the child test had not silently become stricter than the project method.

## 2. Strongest rival: Source Contact/Coverage versus Representational Realization

### Positive case

This rival is substantial. A source can be reached appropriately but captured incorrectly. A perfectly copied record may come from an inadequate sample or the wrong source frame. The corrective work can differ: change source reach/design versus repair the mapping or capture. Metrology, surveys and imported documents all furnish relevant pressure. The four-way capture/selection fixture is not itself a full test of this rival, but the finite coverage and fidelity cases preserve its separate failure locations.

### Limitation of the split

Contact and coverage are not yet a stable independent Formation object separated from realization. What counts as having encountered a property depends on the procedure that represents it; nominal contact with a device or document does not settle that question. A coverage statement itself requires evidence about the source frame and capture path. For an assertion, a physically received signal may not be a captured assertion; for model-mediated measurement, the informative contact may already include interpretation.

The competing split also alternates between two different meanings of coverage: target-opportunity coverage and exposure of existing source resources. The latter already has a separate Dynamics inquiry. These meanings cannot be merged into a new contact child simply to make its scope broad enough.

This does not prove the rival impossible. At present its contribution is preserved as two mandatory diagnostic facets of one source-to-result relation: **reach/coverage of opportunities** and **realization/fidelity of particular results**. Their separate questions, oracles and remedies remain in F2/F3 and FINDINGS-MAP. No extra substantive inquiry has yet been demonstrated that those routes systematically obscure.

### Applying the actual promotion test

The rival has distinct diagnostic questions and different possible remedies; that is positive evidence, not denied. The remaining issue is first-order usefulness. Reach/coverage behaves here as an observability/scope dimension shared with other inquiries; realization/fidelity is assessed over the same source-result relation. The external sources distinguish errors but do not converge on this exact two-child arrangement. The naive single-success-flag collapse loses information; the explicit two-facet findings map does not show that loss in the tested cases. Boundary tests also leave contact crossing Environment/Work and existing-source Exposure. On balance, the present evidence supports retaining the facets without a new child boundary. A later contrary case can reverse this judgment.

### Cost and reopening condition

The leaf has a richer grammar and is easier to summarize badly. A generic quality score or a lone ingestion-success label is therefore not an acceptable summary of it. Reopen if a recurring cross-domain contact/coverage or realization problem requires its own protected method and boundary that cannot be recovered through these facets without material loss. Future evidence need not show mutually exclusive event classes to justify a split.

## 3. Observation/Measurement — Assertion/Elicitation — Copy/Import

These are useful origin profiles, not yet an immediate-child tree. A human can report a measurement; a virtual sensor can compute an estimate; a document can contain quotations, hypotheses and observed values; a copied dataset can mix them. Choosing the label by sender type would change a claim's evidential mode without changing its origin.

The decisive distinctions remain in the source episode, target, mode and realization relation. Separate physical, human and digital methodological specialties can be researched later without promoting them as universal Foundation siblings now. Their overlap is not the sole objection: the proposed division also lacks a stable answer about which protected inquiry an imported measurement report owns.

## 4. Target Definition — Sampling — Capture — Parsing — Validation

This pipeline makes operational failure points visible but asserts an unjustified sequence and modular boundary. Passive receipts may precede the current inquiry; acquisition can be adaptive; interpretation can be constitutive of measurement; validation can acquire new evidence and reopen prior capture; a partial stream may produce several provisional states.

Target definition also touches Work/State, while evaluation touches Standing and authorized acceptance. The pipeline can implement the inquiry in a particular system, but it cannot define the universal Formation decomposition.

## 5. Origination versus Reception

New information can originate at one locus and be received at another. These are real roles. However, the same act can produce a source statement for one party while instantiating an acquired record for another. Absolute firstness is not available as a universal boundary: a new assertion can repeat old content, and a new observation can have old payload bytes.

Keep occurrence, origin, carrier and receiver novelty separate inside the grammar. Reopen if an endogenous-formation inquiry cannot be represented without treating reception as its hidden universal model. The present research does not settle every theory of mental-state acquisition or perception.

## 6. Formation versus Validation

Existence, fidelity, truth and decision sufficiency are different claims, but they are not successive Formation children. A false or badly captured representation may exist. Validation may require additional acquisition, derivation, evidence evaluation or domain intervention. Calling validation a child would absorb those accepted boundaries rather than explain formation more precisely.

The opposite mistake is also rejected: removing validity questions from Formation entirely would leave it as nothing but a write event. Formation retains whether the claimed source-to-result relationship is actually supported; it does not claim universal truth-testing ownership.

## 7. Collapse into neighboring regions

- **State:** a constituted result does not explain its source contact, capture history or omissions.
- **Derivation:** modeling every prior signal as an informational input can be formally possible, but it need not preserve the empirical/source-interface question. The accepted distinctions are diagnostic, not claims of mutually exclusive mathematics.
- **Selection:** choosing an existing source does not establish correct realization of its representation; a faithful acquired representation can later be omitted.
- **Environment:** physical causation is not by itself informational mediation; the protected interpretation/source/result relation must be retained.
- **Work:** a deliberate collection task and its completed status do not establish that the intended information was acquired. Unplanned receipts can also form information.

These arguments retain the parent split without pretending every activity belongs to exactly one branch.

## 8. Bounded decision

**Retain Formation/Acquisition as one proposed bounded leaf at the present depth. No immediate child is promoted.** This is not the claim that the listed failure facets are trivial, interchangeable, uniquely organized, or fully solved for deployments.

The surviving object is a source-/reference-relative formation relation. F6 proposes narrow parent wording corrections: no absolute-firstness or raw-before-inference requirement; distinguish token/receiver/origin/content/evidence novelty; preserve occurrence separately from source fidelity and target truth. The accepted three-question Dynamics structure is not restructured.
