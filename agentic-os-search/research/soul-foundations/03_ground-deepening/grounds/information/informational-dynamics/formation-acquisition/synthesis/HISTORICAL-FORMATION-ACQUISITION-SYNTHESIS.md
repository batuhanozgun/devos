# Informational Formation / Acquisition — Integrated Synthesis

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** PROPOSED BOUNDED LEAF / USER EVALUATION PENDING  
**Research:** F0–F7 complete; no immediate child promoted

## 1. Result

Formation/Acquisition remains a coherent source-facing Information inquiry at the present Foundation depth. Its object is not simply a new file or a successful input operation. It is the relation by which an informational occurrence/result becomes instantiated from an origin at a specified locus, with enough source, procedure and scope information to determine what the result can support.

The protected question is:

> How did this representation become instantiated from its source/origin here, what source-to-result relationship is actually established, and which target alternatives can the available acquisition evidence distinguish?

The accepted three-question Dynamics parent remains supported. This child is proposed as a bounded leaf; Derivation/Transformation and Selection/Exposure still lack dedicated research passes. The parent is not recursively complete.

## 2. Target, source and representation are not interchangeable

The subject of an inquiry may be a deployment's actual state. Its immediate source may instead be an actor's claim about that deployment. The acquired result may be a transcript or status record. Accurate capture of the claim can establish what the actor said without establishing the deployment state.

The distinction also works in reverse: a miscaptured statement can happen to assert a true proposition while falsely attributing it to the source. Source fidelity, subject-matter truth and evidence adequacy are different judgments. Authority and operative use are further questions, not automatic consequences of capture.

A false, truncated or misattributed representation can still exist. The research therefore distinguishes formation occurrence from successful realization of the intended representation. An error record about a failed attempt may be valid information without reporting the remote target's condition.

## 3. Novelty must be named

A representation can be new as a token, at a receiving boundary, as an origin episode, as semantic content, or as evidence. A copy can be new locally without a new source event; repeated observations can carry identical payloads; a calculation can reveal a new consequence without adding another empirical observation.

```
new file/copy != new observation != new independent evidence
same payload != same originating episode
source available != acquired correctly != selected for use != used
```

These are distinct claims, not a required linear workflow. Formation, derivation and exposure may occur together or repeatedly within one process.

## 4. Acquisition is not necessarily raw, passive or prior to reasoning

The primary-source challenge required a correction to any simple raw-data-first interpretation of the parent. A source-to-result procedure may itself include models, interpretation and calculation. A generated prediction is a genuine artifact that can be acquired, but its existence is not an observation of its future referent.

This does not eliminate Derivation. It prevents chronological order, source location or an observation label from deciding the protected question. The origin/result relation and the validity of a computation can both be material inside one acquisition episode.

Likewise, measurement or elicitation may change what is encountered. A reading under a probe and a pre-probe target need not be identical. Such an acquisition can be valid for its intended purpose when those conditions are explicit; passivity is not a universal success criterion.

## 5. Complete processing is not complete contact with reality

The target universe, reachable source frame, encountered opportunities, emitted signals and captured records are different coverage surfaces. Processing every captured record cannot by itself certify the earlier surfaces. A partial sample may be appropriate, but its claim and uncertainty must fit its design.

A no-detection result can support absence when the observation path is sufficiently discriminating for the exact claim. The same empty record may support no such conclusion when coverage or detector function is unknown. Neither automatic absence nor universal skepticism about negative evidence is justified.

Missing fields, explicit nulls, unknown responses and recorded zero also need source-contract-sensitive interpretation. A derived imputation can be useful, but it must not silently become an acquired observation.

## 6. The exact record-only limitation

F2 states a bounded mathematical criterion. For a declared class of source situations X, record function h and target q, an exact decoder from the record alone exists if and only if q is constant among situations producing the same record. If two still-allowed situations produce identical records but require different answers, that record alone cannot uniquely distinguish them.

This is not a claim that reasoning cannot produce knowledge, nor that new measurement is always necessary. Additional justified assumptions can restrict the alternatives; other evidence can distinguish them; analysis can expose implications already carried by the record. The limit concerns exact discrimination between alternatives that remain compatible after those resources are specified.

The finite check verifies the proposition in its supplied four-world example, not the correctness of an unknown real observation model.

## 7. What a faithful acquisition must preserve

The minimum semantic grammar keeps the bounded locus, target/source/result roles, origin and representation identities, procedure and interpretation dependencies, mode, relevant coverage/detectability, temporal/version frame, and evidence/alternatives separate. It does not require one data structure or that every small acquisition carry every field.

Time is role-specific: reference time, production time and receiver-observation time can differ. Receipt order does not necessarily establish source currentness. A historically accurate record can be inappropriate for a present-state query; a forecast can correctly refer to a time after its production.

Byte equality and parser success are useful for limited claims but cannot alone certify semantic fidelity, appropriate target framing or world truth. The selected verification method must be sensitive to the actual error claimed to be excluded.

## 8. Why a leaf, and what that choice costs

Source Contact/Coverage versus Representational Realization was the strongest rival split. Those facets have genuine separate failures, evidence requirements and remedies. F4 does not reject them merely because they overlap.

At the present depth, the source-to-result grammar and explicit retrieval routes preserve their contribution without demonstrating an additional Formation-specific inquiry lost by that representation. Observation/assertion/import and pipeline-stage alternatives either change role by context or absorb neighboring questions. No immediate child is therefore promoted.

The cost is a richer leaf that can be summarized badly. Coverage and capture cannot be replaced by one success flag. A recurring separate inquiry that this organization hides is an explicit reason to reopen the decision. The result is bounded, not a uniquely proven universal taxonomy.
