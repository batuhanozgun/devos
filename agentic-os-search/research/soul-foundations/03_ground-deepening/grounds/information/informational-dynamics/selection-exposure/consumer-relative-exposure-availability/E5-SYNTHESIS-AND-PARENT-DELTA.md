# E5 — Exposure synthesis and exact parent refinements

**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Status:** PROPOSED BOUNDED LEAF / E5.1–E5.5 AWAIT USER EVALUATION
**Immediate descendants:** none proposed; no successor active through this document.

## 1. Protected question

> What specified information is available to which consumer, at which locus and time, in which required access or presentation mode; under which actionable conditions and limits; and what evidence supports that exact claim?

Propose Consumer-Relative Exposure / Availability as one bounded leaf at the current Foundation depth. Opportunity, presentation, supported representation and assurance remain explicit facets, not one generic success flag. The accepted Selection/Exposure parent remains two research questions; the accepted use-conditioned sibling/U5.1–U5.6 remains unchanged. E4 preserves the strongest opportunity/presentation rival and reduction tests.

This is not a universal connector, network protocol, actor procedure or storage architecture. The same operation can instantiate information, transform its representation, change access and support an inquiry while raising different research questions at those boundaries.

## 2. A qualified relation rather than a delivery ladder

An identifier can be exactly sufficient for a discovery endpoint. A viable permitted read path can meet an on-demand contract without eager copying. A particular invocation can instead require content already present. A source description, selected object and actual consumer view must not silently substitute for one another.

P1 supplies the concrete resource/host boundary; P4 and X06 separate metadata from data. These do not prescribe MCP or FAIR infrastructure. Their role is to prevent a positive claim about one resource or endpoint from being treated as evidence for another.

Consumer identity can be a person, account, process, session or invocation, depending on the claim. Availability to one does not by itself establish availability to another. Actual possession and legitimate authority must also remain separate: an unauthorized copy can still be physically present, while an authorized route can fail operationally. Correct access control is not identical to maximizing exposure, and relevance never grants permission.

## 3. Opportunity has time, resources and information requirements

X01 demonstrates why the union of previously available contacts is insufficient for timely access. Its ordered path succeeds without instantaneous full connectivity, while its reversed path fails. The guarantee depends on contact order, allowed waiting/custody and deadline. A stronger simultaneity requirement can be wrong just as an unqualified static path can be wrong.

The finite recurrence and separate time-expanded graph agree over 4096 schedules. This is a scoped computation and elementary equivalence argument, not a production-access guarantee. Real tools, permissions, representation dependencies and consumer schedules are not supplied by the graph automatically.

E2 §3 adds a separate analytical quantifier boundary: a successful path in every possible world need not yield one feasible policy for an actor unable to distinguish those worlds. Availability claims should not borrow an omniscient operator. Conversely, an evaluator's uncertainty does not prove that actual access is absent. Opportunity, executable strategy and evidence about them require their own stated scope.

## 4. Evidence cannot exceed its endpoint or horizon

X02 obtains the same accepted response with present and absent result content. The acknowledgment is real evidence of that acceptance, not of all downstream conditions. On the declared alternatives, availability remains unknown until a discriminating observation is obtained. A stronger receipt could support a stronger claim only with corresponding binding and production evidence.

The result read is still tied to its target, consumer and observation time. It does not certify another representation, unknown recipients, a later invocation or continuing availability. Likewise, all observed recipients matching does not establish that every applicable recipient was identified. No universal polling, repeated full read or infinite acknowledgment chain follows; the needed assurance is relative to the use and evidence already available.

## 5. Source change and consumer state can diverge

X03 distinguishes source v2 from a retained v1 view. The old view can remain correct for a historical request and wrong for a current request. Source correctness, replication state and the current use target must be evaluated separately. A cached or retained view is not inherently defective.

X04 separates denial of a new source read from deletion of a received file. Revocation, reliance withdrawal, replacement of the displayed view and destruction of copies can protect different outcomes. The observed local handler does not implement institutional authority or prove erasure of all copies. Records, Revision and Propagation retain those broader requirements; Exposure specifies the consumer-side availability that was actually established or withdrawn.

A claim about repeated or future access needs relevant dependencies and a horizon. An exact content hash does not certify current permission, decoder availability, host inclusion or later source validity. Preservation for future reuse and present working access remain different duties.

## 6. Supported representation is not demonstrated understanding

X05 preserves bytes while one decoding path fails and another succeeds. P5 supplies a separate human-facing constraint on supported presentation. Neither warrants equating content presence with successful meaning transfer, attention or comprehension. The same payload can be available under a byte-download contract and fail a contract requiring a decoded view.

Late P6 extends the boundary to communication: access to a message and evidence of shared understanding are different claims. Its historical communication analysis is not a current-model benchmark or a required SOUL dialogue protocol. E4 records the exact reading extent and its timing after the tests.

The useful research consequence is diagnostic: missing material, unavailable representation support, misunderstood meaning and unjustified reliance need not have the same remedy. The present work does not show that any particular actor will notice or correct these failures.

## 7. Five exact proposed parent refinements

| ID | Overextension to prevent | Proposed refinement | Evidence route |
|---|---|---|---|
| E5.1 | A source, locator or tool result means the actor has the required information | Bind the exact informational target, consumer, locus and required mode. Metadata, executable references and materialized invocation content can be different legitimate endpoints. Separate actual presence from authority. | E2 §§1,7; P1/P4; X06; E4 §§2,7 |
| E5.2 | Individually available pieces establish an actionable end-to-end opportunity | State timing/order, permitted waiting, dependency and resource conditions, horizon and relevant actor observations. A static union or omniscient path is not a general access guarantee; simultaneous connectivity is not always necessary. | E2 §§2–3; P3; X01; analytical two-location case |
| E5.3 | Acceptance or a successful observation certifies all recipients and future uses | Bind evidence to the claimed endpoint, target and time; retain UNKNOWN where observed alternatives remain indistinguishable. Known-scope coverage and future availability require their own support. | E2 §4; P2a; X02; inherited Propagation |
| E5.4 | Updating or restricting the source changes every retained consumer view | Distinguish current source, historical/retained views, future-read restriction, reliance withdrawal and copy removal. Revalidate the conditions material to the new use rather than treating prior read success as timeless. | E2 §5; P2b; X03/X04; Records/Revision |
| E5.5 | Correct bytes or presentation support establish understanding and coordinated use | Include required representation and interpretation-resource availability in the endpoint claim, while keeping semantic fidelity, attention, understanding, shared assurance, acceptance and action as separate claims. | E2 §6; P5; X05; E4 §§5–6/P6; Re-expression |

These refinements combine inherited distinctions and the present temporal/endpoint/source-pressure work. They are proposed additions to the accepted parent, not automatically accepted results or installed actor-control. They do not create a third Selection child or a fourth Dynamics peer.

## 8. Why no child is promoted

E4 takes the opportunity/presentation split seriously: its failure modes and operational remedies differ. At this level, however, they are alternative or combined modes of the qualified information-to-consumer relation. A retained copy can support future opportunity; a viable route need not have been exercised; each mode needs its own evidence. Direct facet routes preserve those differences without a mandatory pipeline.

The qualification/assurance split repeats a broader specification-versus-realization distinction. Reducing Exposure into Formation or Propagation loses the narrower standing access relation; expanding it to understanding would absorb neighboring questions. Human/machine, public/restricted, push/pull and transient/persistent cases change conditions rather than yield stable new immediate outcomes in the examined scope.

This is same-assistant bounded organizational judgment. Reopen for repeated loss of opportunity/presentation distinctions, a stable first-order access or presentation region, a forced byte-only human model, or a failed boundary with Formation/Re-expression/Propagation. More green tests alone would not settle that comparison.

## 9. SOUL / Development OS reuse and return

A later research worker may find the right result yet still lack a working source route, applicable version, required decoder or evidence that the next actor received it. Another actor may retain a withdrawn view after source access is closed. The findings map exposes these questions with exact evidence routes, allowing the result to serve as a prerequisite, exclusion, audit question or revalidation trigger.

No guarantee is made that all Foundation findings will be used, that every source must be preloaded, or that a new infrastructure should be installed. Concrete actor benefit, permission correctness, complete source/recipient coverage and ongoing guarantees need target-specific evidence.

Complete E6 publication and return this leaf and E5.1–E5.5 for evaluation. After acceptance and explicit continuation, a focused integration/completion-depth review of the now-researched Selection children and their Dynamics boundaries is a logical next candidate, not an automatically active stage or proof that the whole Foundation is complete.
