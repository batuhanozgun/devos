# U4 — Non-decision uses, rival structures and omission review

**Date:** 2026-09-15. **Standing:** same-assistant bounded structural assessment; user evaluation pending.
**Evidence:** preserved U1–U3/K01–K06; new P4–P6 and N01–N03 in U4-SOURCE-AND-EXECUTION-ADDENDUM.

## 1. The objection that must survive the mathematics

U2 gave a precise condition for a particular action-preservation contract. That does not make choosing an action the universal purpose of information. A design review may require a reason, a source trace, an exception, an unresolved question or a basis for checking a claim. Optimizing action payoff can hide those requirements even when the arithmetic is correct.

The user's observation that these findings touch an actor's reasoning is a useful application direction, not evidence of actual behavior. This pass examines informational prerequisites and distinctions. It does not install a reasoning procedure or verify that an actor notices, understands, follows or benefits from them.

## 2. Explanation is not an action label

N01 holds the stipulated action fixed at HOLD in two records while changing the recorded reason. The action-only view supports the action but cannot recover which reason the actual record supplied. This is a counterexample to action-preservation implying rationale-preservation, not a causal diagnosis of a release system.

P4 provides a different, formal explanation tradition: what explanatory information is needed can depend on the explanandum, epistemic background and model uncertainty. A cause description, an exact recorded rationale, a proof and an account of actual decision production are not interchangeable. We do not import P4's definition as a universal explanation test.

Selection must therefore bind the explanatory question: explain which result, in what sense, against which alternatives or background, at what evidential strength? Finding a plausible reason is not evidence that it caused an actor's decision. Truth/causal warrant and production-history fidelity remain their own requirements. The selected view can supply the relevant material without establishing the explanation itself.

## 3. One support is not the support structure

N02 separates three legitimate information requests: return the query value; supply one sufficient contributing witness; recover all minimal supporting witnesses within the supplied database. The selected subset {a} preserves the output and one witness, but loses the alternative {b}. That loss matters when a is later removed: the full database still yields the output while the selected-only database does not.

This is not a universal requirement to retrieve every possible support. The exact-output request legitimately needs less than an all-witness audit. It is a warning against treating a witness adequate for the first request as adequate for the second. P5's provenance questions supply primary-source pressure; the SQL fixture is our separate small construction. A database witness also does not establish independent empirical evidence or world causality.

The same research result can thus serve different later uses. An outcome may be enough for navigation; a contrary case or support alternative may be necessary for re-evaluation. The selected unit's adequacy is conditional on that use, not an intrinsic priority permanently attached to its file.

## 4. Refutation and certification ask different coverage questions

N03 enumerates a declared four-input Boolean domain. One observed counterexample refutes the universal all-zero claim. Several observed zeros leave it undecided when untested inputs can still vary freely. Full coverage can certify this bounded property. Direct checking agrees with enumeration of all compatible functions, preserving TRUE, FALSE and UNKNOWN rather than treating every unfinished check as failure or success.

The asymmetry is about the quantifiers and allowed alternatives. It does not prohibit an invariant, proof, trusted contract or justified statistical model from licensing a different conclusion with fewer observations. Nor does exhaustive testing of this tiny domain establish production coverage. The choice of which tests to retrieve remains distinct from the validity of the tests and from the argument that connects them to a claim.

A repeated positive example may add little discrimination of the remaining alternatives. The missing useful item can instead be a source-domain limit, a contradiction or a reason that the test family is not sensitive to the alleged error. This is an analytical diagnostic implication, not an observed defect in a real actor or test pipeline.

## 5. Exploration and adaptive selection are not the same refinement

K06 concerns a fixed known question: after seeing one report, choose which existing report to read next. P6 explicitly distinguishes such changing retrieval from evolution of the need itself. Exploration can change which question is worth asking, which sources matter and which contrasts count as informative. It cannot be fully validated by K06 or by success against yesterday's query.

For exploratory use, a defensible intermediate result can be a newly exposed distinction, an explicit alternative, a better-bounded question or a source path that can resolve it. That is not yet a sufficient answer to the final question. Record what changed, the evidence for the change and the remaining uncertainty; do not relabel an easier new goal as completion of the authorized original goal.

There is no universal exploration-complete predicate supplied here. Unknown alternatives limit a completeness claim. A bounded return may be justified by the actual research question, material residuals and evidence already obtained, rather than by either endless reading or a claim that all future needs were enumerated. These are use/return distinctions, not a new algorithm for autonomous goal formation.

## 6. Strongest rival: need/adequacy specification versus view construction

Positive case: a selector can perfectly satisfy the wrong requirement, or fail a well-grounded one. Revising the criterion and improving the search can be different remedies. Treating them as one success flag would hide a serious error. The rival satisfies the method's diagnostic-usefulness concern and is not rejected merely because the activities overlap.

The boundary cost is that specification can mean three different things: authorizing what Work should accomplish; representing the informational obligations of that use; or validating the truth/warrant of a proposed acceptance rule. The first and third cross Work/Standing/Warrant. The second is the use side of the same adequacy relation, repeatedly modified when candidates and constraints reveal infeasibility. A separate universal specification child risks either taking over neighboring authority or becoming only a field-description stage.

A rich leaf must not suppress that distinction. Its findings map must expose criterion fidelity separately from candidate/view adequacy: does the rule cover the protected use, and does this view satisfy the rule? The new rationale, witness and quantifier cases are reachable through these distinct questions without a residual Selection-local outcome becoming homeless. That is a positive adequacy check for this representation, not proof that no alternative tree can work.

Bounded judgment: retain these two diagnostic facets within one qualified use-to-view inquiry. The evidence does not currently warrant the added child boundary's cost. Reopen if recurring criterion-discovery work has a distinct Selection-local object/method that the facet route systematically hides. Mere overlap or absence of a universal external taxonomy is not a reason to resist such future evidence.

## 7. Rival: fixed-contract selection versus adaptive inquiry

K06 defeats fixed-bundle infeasibility as a verdict on every policy, but it does not change the protected question: does the allowed information-selection strategy serve the stated use? Static ranking, contingent reads and mixtures can be different admissible methods. A policy's possible-path union is not its actual per-path input.

Need evolution is the stronger split argument. Yet a dedicated exploration child would mix source encountering, hypothesis construction, goal negotiation, knowledge revision and selection. These questions already have distinct neighboring owners; their occurrence in one exploratory episode does not make them one additional Selection-local outcome. Selection retains how its current inquiry state conditions the next view, including a bounded exploratory return rather than an answer.

Bounded judgment: adaptation is a mode of selection; exploration is a use/profile with explicit boundary obligations. Neither is promoted as an immediate child here. Reopen if a later discovery case has a protected Selection-local outcome irreducible to these routes without loss. Do not insist every adaptive process has a known fixed need or a single utility function.

## 8. Rival: action, explanation, verification and reconstruction as children

These profiles require different information and tests, but the same source/view can participate in several simultaneously. The difference lies primarily in the use contract and permissible loss, not in separate populations of selection relations. N01–N03 show why one score is insufficient; they do not force four universal branches.

A request for one witness differs from all witnesses even within one profile. Subdividing by every quantifier, explanation kind or output format would keep branching without a stable new first-order region. Preserve the relevant requirement, source evidence and exact limitation directly instead. A later specialist research question can be valuable without becoming an immediate Foundation child now.

## 9. Why a leaf here does not undo the accepted parent split

The parent separates need-to-view adequacy from specified-information-to-consumer availability. The latter can vary without a new need or selection computation, for example when access to an existing resource changes. Here the proposed specification/construction split is instead about the two sides and validation of one adequacy relation. The new cases refine what that relation claims; none establishes consumer availability.

Formation retains source contact/capture; Re-expression retains intended meaning and loss; Warrant retains justification of a claim; Revision retains continuing-state change. Work/Actors/Control retain legitimate purpose, decisions and responsibility. Records retains future preservation/disposition. Exposure retains actual access; interpretation/use and causal benefit require additional evidence. Knowing an actor has appropriate material is not a behavioral or outcome guarantee.

Eligibility, identity, time, source standing, uncertainty, cost and baseline qualify the inquiry. Ranking, set cover, provenance queries and lookahead are mechanisms. They are not additional immediate children by name.

## 10. Decision, cost and reversal

Propose **Use-Conditioned Selection / Set Adequacy as one bounded leaf at this depth**, with no immediate descendant. Interpret the retained label as covering qualified views and permitted contingent reading, not only a frozen set. No folder rename or mandatory model follows.

The leaf's cost is a richer contract and a risk of lossy summaries. Mandatory in the research representation are separately retrievable questions about use/criterion, coverage/alternatives, constraints/method family, selected-view sufficiency and reuse limits. This is not a new runtime control instruction. U5 states exact proposed parent refinements; the maps provide evidence access.

The choice is same-assistant comparative judgment, not an independently measured superiority claim. Reopen for repeated facet loss, a stable Selection-local specification or inquiry child, a non-decision use forced into action payoff, or an Exposure boundary that cannot be maintained. Further probes examined here add use conditions, source obligations or cross-boundary work rather than another immediate outcome. This supports bounded closure for evaluation, not universal exhaustion of information-seeking theory.
