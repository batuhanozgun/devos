**Updated:** 2026-09-15. **Status:** accepted bounded leaf and U5.1–U5.6.
**Role:** intentionally lossy orientation, not evidence or actor-control instructions.

Which admissible view or contingent reading strategy preserves what the authorized informational use requires, under its actual scope, constraints and permitted loss? Adequacy is use-relative, not a permanent property of a document, query or score. No immediate descendant is promoted.

Preserve these distinctions:

- satisfying a query/criterion is not proof that the criterion expresses the protected use;
- preserving a decision is not preserving its exact rationale, explanatory basis or audit trail;
- a result, one sufficient witness and all alternative supports have different information requirements;
- finding a counterexample differs from certifying its absence under a declared coverage/model;
- individual information value can differ from joint and contextual value;
- no adequate fixed bundle does not establish no adequate contingent strategy;
- adapting reads for a fixed need differs from discovering a changed need;
- exploratory progress differs from a completed answer or authorized goal change;
- not selected now does not mean disposable, and selected material does not establish actor understanding or benefit.
