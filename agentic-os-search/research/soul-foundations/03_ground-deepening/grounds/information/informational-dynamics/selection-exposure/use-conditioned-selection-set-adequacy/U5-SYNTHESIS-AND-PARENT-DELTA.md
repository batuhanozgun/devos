## 1. Protected question and proposed result

Propose one bounded **Use-Conditioned Selection / Set Adequacy** leaf. No immediate descendant is promoted. The name remains a retrieval anchor; set adequacy is not restricted to a frozen bundle. ## 2. Sufficient for what?

A query, information-need description and adequacy criterion can each be incomplete representations of the authorized use. Optimizing them accurately does not by itself establish that the use was served. Relevant items are not necessarily a sufficient set; a high-scoring best effort is not necessarily feasible sufficiency. The candidate domain, eligibility, joint interpretation and cost/horizon must match the claim.

U2's finite criterion remains valid at its exact scope: each indistinguishability cell needs a common optimal action for deterministic view-only full-state-optimal choice. Complete state identification or identical optimal-action sets are unnecessary for that purpose. Loss tolerance, expected loss and worst-case regret are different contracts. None supplies the correct actual world model or utility function.

A view's adequacy therefore belongs to a use relation, not to its file once and for all. Changing the question, action alternatives, risk criterion or reference scope can change what information is required. A summary that preserves one decision may fail another, while retaining many irrelevant details would not repair the missing distinction.

## 3. Action preservation does not encompass explanation or audit

N01 keeps the action HOLD in two records but loses which recorded reason applies. P4 independently pressures the choice of explanatory target, background knowledge and model assumptions. Exact rationale, causal explanation, proof, source history and actual decision production must not be treated as the same requested output.

N02 obtains the same SQL result from the full data and one selected witness. The selected subset loses another minimal witness that preserves the result after the first is removed. A result, one support route and the family of alternative supports can thus require different views. P5 supplies the provenance distinction; N02 is only a small original SQLite construction.

A compact general lens is possible without reducing everything to action payoff. For a supplied finite state class and finite output space, let B_u(w) be the acceptable outputs for use u in state w. A deterministic view-only output valid throughout a view cell exists exactly when the intersection of B_u(w) over that cell is nonempty. Necessity follows because the same view receives one output; sufficiency follows by choosing a common member. U2's action condition is a special case. This elementary extension is an explicit argument, not a new stochastic information theorem or a general semantic oracle. The N01 case illustrates one instance, not exhaustive testing of all such relations.

The hard substantive task is determining B_u, the candidate states and what counts as evidence for them. Recording a desired explanation or finding a common string does not make it true. Combining outputs in a tuple can represent multiple obligations, but notation does not validate their adequacy or authorize the underlying Work.

## 4. Counterevidence and completeness have different burdens

N03 separates finding a counterexample from certifying that none exists in a declared finite domain. A green selected test set may leave compatible failing functions. A full check, or a separately justified stronger assumption/proof, can change that conclusion. Missing a counterexample is not the same observation as showing it cannot exist.

Selection must preserve the claim's quantifier and comparison universe when material. One witness can settle an existence claim; an all-support or no-counterexample claim may require a different search and assurance path. Actual coverage, test sensitivity and evidential legitimacy remain external facts to establish, not properties conferred by a completion label.

## 5. Useful selection can be joint, contextual and contingent

Original K04 shows complementary information whose individual gain is zero while the pair is worthwhile under stated costs. K05 shows information priority depending on an outside action alternative. A context-free item score or one-step stopping rule is not universally sufficient. Conversely, the examples do not justify unlimited lookahead, reading all sources or a particular utility/risk policy.

K06 shows a contingent two-read strategy succeeding where every fixed two-item bundle fails for a known target. Infeasibility must identify the permitted strategy family; failure of one bundle family does not certify failure of all adaptive policies. Constraints and actual per-path consumption still matter.

P6's evolving-search account supplies a stronger boundary: adapting retrieval to a fixed need differs from learning that the need itself should be reframed. Exploratory progress may be a new question or discriminating source route rather than a finished answer. Keep the change and its reason visible; do not count success against an easier substituted goal as completion. No complete unknown-need-discovery test or universal stopping rule has been established.

## 7. Research-to-SOUL reuse

A future reader may use a finding as a premise, exclusion, applicability check, contrary case or next question. The question-to-evidence map exposes those routes with their limits. A result can be useful because it prevents an unsupported conclusion, not only because it changes an action.

The present research supports evaluating informational prerequisites of actor reasoning. It does not show that a real actor will notice the relevant condition, choose the right strategy, use the selected content correctly or improve outcomes. Those need target-specific evidence during later authorized design/validation. Neither every finding's future use nor a complete future query family can be scheduled now. No maximal retention, full-context, graph, embedding, provenance architecture or universal schema follows.
