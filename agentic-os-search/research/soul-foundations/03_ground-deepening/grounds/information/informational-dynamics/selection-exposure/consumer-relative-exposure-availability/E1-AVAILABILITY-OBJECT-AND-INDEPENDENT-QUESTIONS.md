# E1 — Availability object and independent questions

**Date:** 2026-09-15. **Status:** initial analysis and live hypotheses, not a structural result.
**Timing:** before fresh external inquiry for this node; inherited Foundation and candidate boundaries are identified in PLAN.

## 1. What can change while the information remains the same?

An unchanged research passage can become available to one actor and remain unavailable to another. A newly opened route can change that relation without generating a new proposition or recomputing a ranking. Conversely, a correct passage can be chosen and copied somewhere without becoming available at the endpoint required by the current work.

The focal relation is between specified information and a consumer under a qualified mode of access. The target may be an identifier, a complete document, an exact historical passage, a representation with its interpretation resources, or material included in a particular working invocation. These are alternative contracts, not mandatory stages that every use must traverse.

Selection asks whether the designated material serves the use. Exposure asks what availability of that designated material is established. Formation still owns the source/result relationship of any new copy; Re-expression owns preserved meaning; Propagation owns actual cross-ground change; Work/Actors/Control own authorized purpose, action and responsibility. The distinctness of this Exposure question must be tested, not assumed from its existing name.

## 2. Possibility, realization and knowledge of realization

A source exists. A locator is known. An actor has permission. An effective route can be followed. The content is present at a locus. A representation is rendered. A particular invocation contains it. These predicates are not interchangeable.

A useful access contract may require only a viable on-demand route, not eager materialization. But viability can depend on credentials, tooling, expiry, source state, discovery information and a feasible time/cost budget. An unbounded existential route is weaker than an actionable route under the declared conditions. Correctly delivering an address fulfills an address request but not a claim that its contents are already in the model input.

Real availability and evidence about it differ. An acknowledgment observed by a producer may describe acceptance into a queue rather than presentation to a consumer. Absence of an acknowledgment may mean failure or merely missing observation. A verifier whose observations are identical in a success and failure world cannot distinguish those worlds without another premise or observation. This is a proposed finite observation argument, not a general impossibility claim for every protocol.

## 3. Consumer and target identities are material

The person, account, process, session, model call and role can designate different consumers. 'The agent has the file' can hide that one process has bytes while another invocation only has a path. A shared source address can return different versions or representations. A receipt for one recipient or request cannot silently certify another.

Exact bytes are sometimes the target, but they do not universally establish the required representation. A compressed payload can be present without a compatible decoder; a document can require a codebook, units, linked definitions or an assistive representation. Re-expression retains whether those resources suffice for faithful interpretation. Exposure must account for their availability when the target contract requires them, without claiming actual comprehension.

A dependency list is itself fallible: all named resources being present is not proof that all necessary resources were named. A closed test may stipulate this list; a real use must justify it. Not every consumer requires the same closure.

## 4. Access has a temporal and relational shape

Permission at one time, a route at another and a readable source at a third do not necessarily form a usable access opportunity. Combining facts from incompatible snapshots can create a route that was never usable as claimed. Conversely, a valid time-ordered transfer can exist without all edges being active simultaneously if intermediate custody/waiting is permitted. 'All pieces were available at some time' and 'an admissible route can complete by the deadline' are different statements.

A successful read at t0 does not guarantee a later read at t1 after the link, credentials, source, consumer or decoder changes. A source updated at t1 does not imply a consumer's already materialized view updated. A deliberately historical representation can still be correct. The required horizon may be one read, one invocation or continuing availability; none is universally mandatory.

These are prospective hypotheses for bounded tests. They are not findings of a production outage or a proposal to add locks, leases, polling or particular consistency machinery.

## 5. Removal has several targets

Removing future source access does not necessarily erase an already delivered copy. Withdrawing reliance on a claim does not destroy its historical record. Removing a locator does not prove the object no longer exists; destroying one object does not establish that every copy vanished. These inherited Records/Revision distinctions become consumer-relative when different actors retain different views.

The legitimate target may be preventing future disclosure, preventing use as current authority, withdrawing a published view, or disposing of particular copies. Those goals need different evidence. Exposure does not grant permission or prescribe universal revocation/retention policy. An intentionally restricted view can be correct for one authorized use and inadequate for another unauthorized one; improving availability is not automatically better.

## 6. Strongest rivals

**One rich availability relation:** target, consumer, access mode, conditions, time and evidence can be kept together with explicit facets. This avoids treating every implementation stage as a child.

**Access opportunity versus realized presentation:** this protects the difference between being able to obtain something and having it in the required working view. They can fail separately and need different observations. The rival is serious, but it could merely split two requested modes of the same relation. Test whether a distinct first-order inquiry survives beyond generic potential/actual separation.

**Qualification versus realization/assurance:** identifying the required target and endpoint can fail even when delivery implements its specification. This resembles the accepted adequacy child's criterion/construction rival. Do not promote or reject it solely for symmetry.

**Reduction:** Formation can explain new receiver copies, Re-expression interpretable representation and Propagation effective change. Does Exposure add a protected information-specific relation for unchanged shared access, or only repeat these established questions? The accepted parent split stays challengeable.

Push/pull, cache/live fetch, filesystem/network and human/model are mechanism or consumer profiles, not immediate children by name. Discovery may matter for actionable access while selecting the right evidence remains the sibling's question. Availability and understanding must not be collapsed in either direction.

## 7. Planned adverse cases and scope

Seek different source traditions before choosing a structure. Candidate probes: a queued receipt with and without final content; correct bytes in the wrong consumer view; a historical response that remains displayed after source refresh; time-varying access edges with no admissible end-to-end route; a valid delayed route with intermediate custody; decoder or representation dependency missing; source access revoked while a prior copy survives; a successful read whose evidence does not cover the next use.

Each case must state its target and what counts as success before execution. A local test can establish a local observation, not a live-agent benefit or universal taxonomy. E2 must distinguish genuinely new refinements from inherited boundaries; E4 still owes the structural comparison and human/non-network omission tests.
