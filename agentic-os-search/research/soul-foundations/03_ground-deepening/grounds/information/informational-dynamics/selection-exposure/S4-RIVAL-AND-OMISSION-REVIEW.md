# S4 — Rival structures, omissions and proposed child decision

**Date:** 2026-09-14. **Standing:** same-assistant bounded comparative judgment; user evaluation pending.
**Additional canonical check:** Formation F4 and Propagation KEY were read in full after the finite tests. Their accepted boundaries are not reopened.

## 1. Apply the actual promotion standard

The hierarchical method asks whether separate research protects a distinct first-order question, exposes otherwise obscured failure, or improves boundary reasoning. Independent failures are positive evidence, not an automatic child rule. Overlap is not disqualification. Conversely, writing every concern inside one large contract does not demonstrate adequate research organization. Derivation T4 and Formation F4 must be applied symmetrically, without preferring a leaf because recent nodes were leaves or a split because two names already exist.

## 2. Strongest rival — one qualified information-to-use leaf

Steelman: one relation can bind the information need, candidate domain, selected view, recipient, availability mode, time, eligibility and actual observation. Exact lookup, browsing, context assembly and publication can all use it. A rich findings map could separately expose the source universe, set adequacy and endpoint assurance. This avoids a mandatory retrieval-then-delivery pipeline and duplicated identity/permission/freshness work.

This is a viable organization, not refuted by E06 alone. Any system distinguishes a desired result from its realization. Turning that universal distinction into two children everywhere would contradict the accepted Formation and Revision reasoning. Our proposed exposure child therefore cannot merely mean execution of a previously chosen selection.

## 3. Two protected relations survive that objection

**Use-Conditioned Selection / Set Adequacy** concerns the relation between an informational need or inquiry state, an admissible candidate domain, and a selected/ordered view. Its result can be an adequate candidate view, explicitly unresolved alternatives or evidence that no sufficient view is presently available. It must investigate the changing need, omitted universe, complementary inputs and the criteria behind choosing a set. It does not establish that the designated content is available at a consumer endpoint.

**Consumer-Relative Exposure / Availability** concerns the relation between specified existing information and its availability to a particular consumer at a locus/time under the required access mode. Publishing to an audience, opening an existing shared view, rendering a selected fragment or including material in a working input can change that relation while source content remains intact. It has its own questions about what counts as available, for whom, through which path, at which representation/version and with what evidence. It does not choose the informational need or prove that the exposed view is adequate for it.

This is not solely proposal/application. A role can gain effective access to unchanged information without a new ranking computation or a newly formed document. Conversely, choosing an adequate evidence set does not answer whether an already-running consumer still has a different cached view. A source/resource can be available without a specific search or a useful ranking; a selection can be evaluated relative to its use without certifying a recipient's state. The resulting research contracts remain different even when one API call performs both.

## 4. Why promote here but not automatically in Formation or Revision?

Formation's strongest contact/realization rival concerns the reach and fidelity of one source-to-result occurrence; its nominal contact predicate can be too weak to specify a separate informational outcome. The present pair instead protects need-to-view fitness and resource-to-consumer availability after information already exists. Exposure is not a prerequisite sampling count and need not instantiate a new representation. Revision similarly protects an old-to-new maintained state, not the same relation as making an unchanged state available to another use.

The distinction is also not a claim that source selection in Formation or support selection in Revision disappears. Their local roles remain. It is a claim about which protected relation is focal. A source contact, faithful copy, warranted answer, revised record, selected evidence view and currently available consumer input answer different questions.

The leaf can retain both proposed contracts, just as the Derivation leaf rival could retain transfer, warrant and revision. The benefit of promotion here is making two materially different research duties explicit: how a use-fit view is formed from an incomplete/evolving candidate space, and how qualified informational availability is established beyond intermediate receipts. We prefer that diagnostic visibility over the cost of another level at the present Foundation scale. No measured universal retrieval advantage or uniquely correct taxonomy is claimed.

## 5. Mechanism-independent analytical holdouts

| Setting | Selection can fail despite availability | Availability can fail despite a suitable selection |
|---|---|---|
| Research evidence | Visible reports all repeat one favorable result and omit the needed contrary case | Correct source list reaches a reader, but required documents are unavailable at that reader's permitted endpoint |
| Development context | Complete delivery of an irrelevant or incompatible code/test set | Chosen correct source content is represented only by locators in the actual input |
| Publication/access | An accessible collection does not cover the audience's question | Correct approved material is present in one local store but not available in the designated audience view |
| Historical reconstruction | Every selected item is individually recent but the versions cannot be read together | A correct as-of view is computed but a different current representation is exposed |

These rows are analytical comparisons, not new executed systems. E01–E07 constrain small subclaims; P1–P7 provide different source pressures, not votes for this hierarchy.

## 6. Strong merge and boundary attacks

**Exposure into Formation:** receiver-side capture may occur, but effective access to an already-existing shared resource need not create a new token or new source evidence. Formation still asks whether any newly formed representation is faithful. Exposure asks whether the required existing representation is available to this consumer. Neither gives the other guarantee.

**Exposure into Propagation or Work:** Propagation retains realized cross-ground change and observation; Work retains pursuit and completion. They do not by themselves define the informational target's required availability mode, representation, recipient and use-facing visibility. Exposure is the narrower information-local inquiry, not ownership of every network or institutional effect. If dedicated research cannot preserve that narrower contract without merely repeating Propagation, merge/reduce this proposal.

**Selection into Re-expression:** selecting fragments can change meaning, which keeps transfer analysis material. But an undistorted presentation of the wrong evidence set still needs selection diagnosis. **Selection into Warrant:** selected evidence may warrant a conclusion conditionally while a decisive source is omitted. **Selection into Work:** need negotiation and goal authorization remain Work; the adequacy relation over available information is still a separate informational question.

## 7. Other child candidates

Eligibility/permission is material at both selection and exposure. It is not promoted as a third peer: legitimate authority comes from Actors/Control, and eligible-set membership alone neither supplies a use-fit set nor establishes availability. Discovery/indexing is a mechanism and source-domain coverage obligation within the selection inquiry; creating new source information remains Formation.

Relevance/ranking/diversity are policy and mechanism families, not three universal children. Freshness, identity, ordering, budget, uncertainty and granularity qualify both proposed relations. Push/pull, local/remote, human/model and public/private are profiles. No mechanism partition is promoted.

Need evolution and exploratory browsing defeat a universal static-query pipeline but do not create an independent third Selection outcome. P7 pressures the inquiry-state input and return loop. Attention, interpretation, acceptance and action are downstream or cross-boundary conditions; they are not proved by exposure and are not annexed as a third child.

## 8. Proposed result, cost and reversal

Propose Selection / Exposure as a bounded parent with two immediate research questions:
1. Use-Conditioned Selection / Set Adequacy.
2. Consumer-Relative Exposure / Availability.

Neither child is separately researched or active. The original Dynamics parent remains three questions; no fourth Dynamics peer is introduced. Its earlier N6 combined-label judgment is preserved as the broad parent-pass checkpoint, refined here at the dedicated node's own depth.

The cost is extra research and shared boundary material. Reopen the split if adequate direct facet routing proves equally diagnostic without hidden separate duties; if exposure reduces entirely to generic realization/Formation/Propagation; or if a materially different domain exposes a missing first-order relation. Do not keep the pair merely to preserve this author's decision. Dedicated child work may refine names or recommend merging. This is a bounded, falsifiable research-organization proposal, not architecture, universal partition or independent scientific verification.
