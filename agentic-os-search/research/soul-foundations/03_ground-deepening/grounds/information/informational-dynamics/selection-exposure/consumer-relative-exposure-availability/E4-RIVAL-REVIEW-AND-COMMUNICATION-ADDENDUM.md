# E4 — Rival structures, communication boundary and bounded decision

**Date:** 2026-09-15. **Standing:** same-assistant structural assessment; proposed result, not user acceptance.
**Input:** E1/E2, X01–X06 and the actual source/execution limits in E3/SOURCE-LEDGER.

## 1. Apply the promotion test symmetrically

The accepted hierarchical method does not require disjoint operations or an external consensus taxonomy. Separate first-order questions, otherwise obscured failures or materially distinct boundaries can justify children. Conversely, independent failure or different implementation machinery does not automatically create a child. A leaf must show how its important questions remain recoverable, not merely claim that one sufficiently long definition can contain everything.

Formation F4 and Re-expression R4 were freshly read in full during this comparison, after E3. Their contact/realization and conservation/interpretation rivals provide pressure against indiscriminately promoting every potential/actual distinction. The accepted Selection parent's adequacy/availability split is not exempt from this same challenge. No predecessor is rewritten or treated as newly researched.

## 2. Strongest rival: access opportunity versus realized presentation

**Positive case.** An actor can have a viable read route without presently holding the content; another can retain the content after the original route disappears. These are genuinely different states and can require different operational remedies. X04 makes one direction concrete; P1 separates resource operations from host context incorporation. A single statement that 'access works' would hide the distinction. Dedicated access-provisioning and delivery techniques may differ materially.

**Why that does not yet settle the tree.** Opportunity and presentation can be different requested forms of the same target's availability, not two universal successive outcomes. A reference-discovery service can end at the locator; on-demand reading can end at an executable route; an invocation contract can require actual included content. A stored local copy can be the implementation of a future access opportunity. Cutting the route can leave that copy available, while removing the copy may leave another viable route. Identity, timing, eligibility, dependencies and observation qualify both.

The substantive question is therefore not simply 'potential versus actual', but which availability relation is being claimed for this consumer. Keeping the requested mode explicit preserves the separate failure routes without requiring every use to perform both stages. The observed cases do not expose an additional information-local outcome lost by that organization. This is positive evidence for a qualified leaf at this depth, not proof that operational specialties do not exist.

**Cost and reversal.** The leaf risks lossy shorthand. Its map must separately expose actionable opportunity, current presentation, supported representation and the evidence actually obtained. Reopen if future work repeatedly hides a stable access-provisioning or presentation inquiry despite those direct routes, or if a split gives clearer boundaries with materially less diagnostic loss. Mere overlap is not grounds to reject such evidence.

## 3. Rival: target qualification versus realization/assurance

A transfer may perfectly implement the wrong target, recipient, version or endpoint. The right target may also be badly delivered or poorly observed. Correcting the contract, the mechanism and the evidence can be different work. This rival protects a real distinction and cannot be dismissed as cosmetic.

However, target qualification is constitutive of the availability claim, while assurance evaluates whether that exact claim is warranted. Similar distinctions occur in Formation, Re-expression, Revision and use-conditioned adequacy. Promoting them here as universal children would duplicate generic specification, Work and Standing responsibilities. Removing them from Exposure would be equally wrong: an unbound success response is not an adequate statement of availability.

Retain explicit questions: what target/mode is required, what opportunity or endpoint is actually established, and what observation supports that claim? X02 checks only a narrow mismatch between receipt and endpoint; it does not make 'verification' an independent Exposure child or certify arbitrary receipts. Material target and evidence errors remain directly retrievable in the findings map.

## 4. Actionability is not omniscient reachability

E2 §3's two-location example shows that a path in every world is weaker than a feasible actor policy under uncertainty. The concern is not settled by X01's fully known schedules. A discovery task can itself deliver an actionable locator; a fixed endpoint may need no discovery; an archive may genuinely be recoverable through a known procedure without being preloaded.

This is a strong objection to defining availability as an unqualified existential path. It does not require a new discovery child here. Selection retains which source or view fits the inquiry; Environment retains actual tools/capabilities; Work retains the permissible plan and resources; Exposure retains whether those facts support the particular consumer-relative access claim. A claimed executable route cannot silently assume information or capabilities the consumer lacks.

Nor does uncertainty imply nonavailability. The resource may be reachable even when an observer cannot certify it. Record unknown evidence as unknown instead of replacing a world fact with the evaluator's belief. The distinction is shared across the node and its modes, rather than another outcome that only one child can own.

## 5. Representation support and the boundary with interpretation

P5 prevents this inquiry from ending at transport or byte storage. X05 demonstrates a narrow format dependence, but availability of an appropriate representation for a human may depend on different presentation means. A paper notice, a screen-reader-compatible alternative and an API payload are not interchangeable endpoints. No single codec fixture establishes their adequacy.

Re-expression asks what meaning is preserved under the intended interpretation. Exposure asks whether the required representation and material interpretation resources are available here. A decoder that preserves meaning but is unavailable to this consumer leaves an exposure gap. A readily available decoder that assigns the wrong meaning leaves a semantic-transfer gap. The same operation can raise both questions; the research boundaries are not mutually exclusive event labels.

Actual attention, comprehension, agreement and use remain stronger outcomes. It would be a mistake either to annex them into Exposure or to define availability so weakly that an unusable representation always counts as success. The endpoint must be specified at the required support level without claiming cognition that was not observed.

## 6. Late P6 — human communication is not mere transmission

**Primary source:** Herbert H. Clark and Susan E. Brennan, Grounding in Communication (1991), author-hosted chapter:
https://web.stanford.edu/~clark/1990s/Clark,%20H.H.%20_%20Brennan,%20S.E.%20_Grounding%20in%20communication_%201991.pdf

Read the introduction, grounding criterion, presentation/acceptance and evidence discussion, then the medium-constraint and reception/understanding/start-up-cost sections. PDF pages 2, 7 and 8, zero-based, were visually inspected, including the media table. The author's publication-list page first returned a server error; the separately located author-hosted PDF succeeded. No full chapter audit, underlying experiment replication or modern-media generalization occurred. This reading followed X01–X06 and did not retroactively supply their specification.

The source distinguishes presenting a contribution from evidence of purpose-sufficient understanding; the medium affects what can be perceived, reviewed and acknowledged. This is pressure against treating communication as a content pipe, not a universal protocol prescription. Its conversational 'acceptance' is not the same as HTTP 202, approval of a research result, factual agreement or action authorization.

**Our boundary inference:** two actors can possess the same document without knowing that the other's view matches, and a recorded message can remain reviewable without being understood. Actual access, belief about access and coordination based on that belief require different claims. Exposure can supply one prerequisite of coordinated action; it does not absorb common-ground formation or prove group coordination. Conversely, requiring demonstrated mutual understanding for every archive-read contract would overstate that contract.

## 7. Reduction into neighboring research

**Formation:** a receiver copy can be a new informational occurrence, but access to an unchanged shared resource can change without creating a new document. Source/result fidelity does not establish current availability for every consumer. Formation retains the new-occurrence question; Exposure retains the existing information-to-consumer relation.

**Propagation:** establishing or withdrawing access can be a cross-ground transition. Propagation identifies exact realized change, scope and observation; it does not by itself specify what counts as the intended informational availability mode. Exposure can also evaluate a standing relation with no newly requested transfer. Do not rename every network effect 'Exposure' or reduce every availability question to an update event.

**Selection:** a viable route to the wrong source may satisfy its exposure contract and fail the information need. Choosing the correct evidence does not establish its availability. The accepted parent's two questions remain useful; they are not simply proposal and execution of the same target-selection judgment.

**Actors/Environment/Control:** these supply capability, eligibility, authority and operational constraints. Their correct configuration is not a substitute for observing the designated information endpoint. Exposure must not grant authority merely because a route physically works, or deny actual presence merely because possession was unauthorized.

**Records/Revision:** preserving a future basis and changing current reliance differ from present access. A restriction on new reads does not prove erasure, and a source correction does not prove every retained view updated. X03/X04/X06 retain those inherited distinctions without reopening their predecessor research.

## 8. Omitted-domain and adverse-view check

| Setting | Distinction that must remain visible | Status of evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Delayed handoff | Correct contact order can work without simultaneous full connectivity; reversed order can fail | X01 finite enumeration, P3 |
| Model context | Resource read and host incorporation are different endpoints | P1; candidate case, no live host |
| Archival research | Description can outlive the object; a historical view may still be correct | P4; X03/X06 local cases |
| Human presentation | Perceivable/reviewable form is not demonstrated understanding | P5/P6 source pressure, no human test |
| Restricted publication | Denied future reads do not establish copy removal or withdrawal of reliance | X04; inherited Records/Revision |
| Shared work | Matching material does not alone establish mutual awareness or successful coordination | Analytical P6 boundary inference |

These cases add target modes, temporal conditions or neighboring obligations; they do not yield a stable additional first-order Exposure outcome that the qualified relation cannot preserve. This is a bounded comparison, not an exhaustive survey. Human/machine, push/pull, transient/persistent, cache/live, local/remote and one/many recipients remain profiles. Time, identity, scope and uncertainty remain cross-cutting dimensions.

## 9. Proposed decision and remaining uncertainty

Propose **Consumer-Relative Exposure / Availability as one bounded leaf at the present depth**, with no immediate child. The protected question binds specified information to a consumer-relative availability mode, actionable conditions, scope/horizon and evidence. The rich-leaf cost is explicit complexity, not a license to hide the distinctions in one success flag.

The strongest opportunity/presentation rival remains reopenable. Further examined counterframes changed the qualification or evidence required rather than introducing another stable information-local relation. A later case may reverse that judgment, especially if actionable access and presentation cannot be kept distinct, if a human-supported representation is forced into a byte model, or if the boundary with Formation/Propagation loses explanatory value. No taxonomy uniqueness, live-system benefit or independent verification is claimed.

E5 must integrate precise parent refinements; E6 must publish and return the result before any parent integration review or new research node. The temporary E2/E3 open-E4 labels remain their checkpoint provenance, not the final current state after closure.
