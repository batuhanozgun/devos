## 2. Exact refinements submitted with the node

| Parent shorthand or possible overextension | Refinement |
|---|---|
| A valid inference establishes the target claim | Separate conditional consequence/calculation from premise truth, model applicability and target support. |
| No proof or justification was found | Distinguish incomplete search, a complete negative result, inconsistency and a supported negation; they are not interchangeable. |
| A confidence number expresses generic certainty | Preserve the exact conditional direction, guarantee, conditioning population, dependence and joint-claim scope. |
| A fitting or strongest explanation establishes the cause | Separate observational fit, candidate-relative ranking, causal identification and target applicability; remaining alternatives may require other evidence or assumptions. |
| A source has been defeated, therefore its claim is false | Separate rebuttal from attack on the support bridge; alternative support can survive. |
| More checks, reports or selected successes strengthen support automatically | Establish independence and account for evidence/test selection; their count alone is not the inferential basis. |
| A supported claim or good explanation determines the next action | Separate offered justification from production history, and epistemic support from goals/losses, authority, actual use and applied revision. |

The rows are scoped research distinctions, not operational rules installed in agent-control, a universal confidence policy, or permission to overwrite maintained project knowledge. Original R/T/N/F evidence and earlier proofs remain unchanged. New numerical examples instantiate familiar mathematical relations; no novel mathematics is claimed.

## 3. Why leaf rather than children

W4 seriously tests strict/defeasible, deduction/induction/abduction, validity/grounding and construction/assessment. Their differences matter, but under the present level they specify support regimes or dependencies for the same qualified conclusion. The current map gives them direct routes and different error-sensitive checks without hiding an additional independent informational outcome. The strongest rival remains explicit, and the judgment is neither unique nor independently proved.

The cost is a dense leaf vulnerable to overly short summaries. Reopen if a regime-specific inquiry repeatedly disappears, if independent review exposes a missing support region, or if the current routing cannot prevent inferential modes from borrowing one another's guarantees.
