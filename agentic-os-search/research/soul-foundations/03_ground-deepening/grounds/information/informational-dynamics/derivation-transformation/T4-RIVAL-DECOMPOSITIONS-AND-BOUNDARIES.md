# T4 — Rival Decompositions and Boundary Audit

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** three-question parent is the strongest bounded proposal; not yet user-accepted

## 1. Promotion criterion

The hierarchical method allows diagnostic children whose protected questions differ even when their events overlap. It does not require one shared vocabulary across traditions, mutually exclusive operation types, or separate implementation components. Equally, the ability to place any concern inside a sufficiently broad grammar does not prove that a leaf is adequate.

The initial no-split possibility is genuinely challenged here. The question is whether separate inquiries materially improve the research frontier, not whether one can write every process as output=f(inputs).

## 2. Rival A — one qualified transformation leaf

Strengths: a common relation can record exact inputs, method, assumptions, output, intended use, semantic change, lineage and uncertainty. It handles mixed operations without double classification. This is the strongest economy argument and resembles the successful leaf outcome in Formation.

Counterpressure: three independently evaluable obligations survive under the same bounded setting. An entailed summary can lose a required qualification; a faithful presentation of premises can accompany an unwarranted conclusion; and a faithful, sound inconsistency report can leave a continuing reliance state unrevised. Fixes respectively require a different preservation contract, a corrected inference basis, or an appropriate revision relation. Naming all three inside one contract is possible, but the contract must then select among these distinct protected outcomes rather than supply one adequate verdict.

Judgment: retain the shared parent kernel, but reject a leaf as the current best research organization. The candidate split adds explicit work that a generic transformation-quality pass would otherwise be liable to omit. This is not a proof that every project must use three children.

## 3. Rival B — preserving versus meaning-changing transformations

Strength: equivalence-preserving conversions appear different from information-changing operations.

Failure: useful re-expression can intentionally omit detail; inference can expose a consequence already implicit in the source; a revision may alter current reliance without changing the quoted payload. New content and byte difference do not define the boundary. Truth preservation also does not imply suitability for purpose.

Judgment: reject novelty/losslessness as a partition. Preserve the narrower meaning-transfer question without demanding literal losslessness.

## 4. Rival C — re-expression versus inference

Strength: separates source-relative preservation from warrant for a conclusion. C02/C03/C05 expose distinct failures.

Failure: maintaining an informational state under new evidence or constraints is not settled by either alone. A revision can require withdrawal of an old supported commitment, preserve an unresolved alternative, or select between multiple admissible new states. That requires the old state, admitted change basis, support/priority and the resulting continuing state. Inferring a proposal does not establish that the required change happened.

Judgment: under-splits the maintained-state question.

## 5. Rival D — construction versus revision

Strength: separates an output-building activity from changing a continuing body of information. It preserves the most important temporal asymmetry.

Failure: construction becomes a residual region that combines two very different guarantees: carrying intended information from the source and licensing a derived claim. A sound calculation from an unfit abstraction and an unwarranted claim over a faithful abstraction require different diagnosis. C02 and C03 survive within construction itself.

Judgment: useful comparison, but three protected questions are more diagnostic at this research depth.

## 6. Proposed three-child result

| Proposed child | Protected question | Decisive failure | Boundary / non-claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Representational Re-expression / Semantic Transfer | What meaning, structure and use-relevant distinctions are preserved, recoded, abstracted or lost in another representation? | valid-looking or true output drops a decisive qualifier; wrong unit/identity/multiplicity | not only exact copying; not all meaning must survive every legitimate abstraction |
| Inferential Construction / Claim Warrant | What conclusion, estimate or hypothesis is licensed by which premises, rules/models and assumptions, at which claim strength? | plausible or actually true conclusion is not supported as claimed; dependence ignored | not only deduction; a hypothesis need not be asserted as fact; no universal confidence calculus |
| Maintained-Information Revision / Reliance Change | How is a continuing informational state or reliance designation retained, withdrawn, corrected or replaced under a new basis? | conflicting or superseded basis remains operative; withdrawal is mistaken for falsity; competing revisions are silently resolved | no universal revision policy, normative authority, persistent-storage requirement or automatic propagation |

These are immediate research proposals, not completed child passes. Their names may be refined by those passes. They are questions, not three stages every transformation must execute.

## 7. Why they are not merely mechanism categories

Each question recurs across multiple mechanisms and survives role changes. A summary, table conversion, translation or view projection may raise semantic-transfer questions. Arithmetic, model estimation, deduction or explanation generation may raise warrant questions. A scientific correction, session belief revision, current-version designation or evidence withdrawal may raise maintained-state questions.

Conversely, a single imputation can create an estimate, re-express a dataset and revise a maintained record. A model-assisted acquisition can involve the first two. These overlaps are expected. The organizing distinction is the protected relation, not whether the tool is a model, database, person or sensor.

The strongest dimension-only objection remains that all three are selected contracts. Our reason for promotion is not their labels: their first-order success conditions cannot be inferred from one another, the failures recur in materially different primary traditions, and failure to name one leaves a distinct research obligation hidden. Lineage, time, uncertainty and input cardinality instead qualify all three and are not additional children.

## 8. Rejected fourth-child candidates

- Provenance/support lineage: indispensable, but occurs in transfer, inference and revision; source history is not a replacement guarantee for any of them.
- Uncertainty propagation: changes form across numeric, linguistic and revision settings; a cross-cutting obligation rather than one universal operation class.
- Aggregation, summarization, translation, normalization, imputation, reconciliation: mechanisms or mixed applications whose protected role changes with the contract.
- Truth/factuality validation: can require new Formation, valid inference, evidential Standing and Work; not solely an information-transform operation.
- Update propagation: realized spread and consumer visibility remain Propagation/Selection interfaces, not a duplicate fourth child here.

## 9. Ground and sibling attacks

Formation does not disappear because a computation emits a new artifact. Its source-facing capture/instantiation question is independent of a derivation's warrant. Selection remains unresearched: no guarantee about choosing or exposing all relevant inputs is obtained from correctly processing those supplied.

State defines what content, mode and identity exist; these children study their transfer, warranted construction or temporal revision. Work owns the intended pursuit and actual decision activities. Actors/Control own who may revise and how a governing requirement is enforced. Standing owns truth-relative adequacy, assessment and recognition. The revision child does not legislate which evidence must win or grant an agent permission to rewrite accepted knowledge. Causal analysis answers actual influence or benefit, not merely whether a conclusion is a consequence of premises.

## 10. Costs, uncertainty and reopening

Three children create additional research and overlap that a leaf avoids. Promotion is worthwhile only if dedicated passes retain distinct questions instead of devolving into translation, logic and database tutorials. Reopen or merge if later work shows a shared inquiry can preserve all material distinctions with less duplication, or if revision is only a cross-ground governance question with no independent informational transition content. No claim of uniqueness, exhaustive cognitive theory or independent replication is made.
