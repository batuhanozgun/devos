## 3. Why a parent rather than a leaf

A conclusion can be entailed but insufficient for the intended use because a necessary distinction was omitted. A result can be true in the actual world without being warranted by the premises offered. A faithful and logically valid account of new evidence can coexist with an unrevised or inadmissibly revised maintained information state.

The appropriate corrections differ: restore the preservation obligation, repair or qualify the inference, or establish the right old-to-new informational revision. A broad grammar can mention all three but does not supply one verdict that settles them. T4 therefore finds separate child research materially useful under the hierarchical method. This is bounded comparative judgment, not mathematical proof of a unique taxonomy.
