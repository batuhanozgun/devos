# W4 — Rival Structures and the Bounded Node Decision

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** proposed bounded leaf; same-model structural comparison.

## 1. What counts as a real split

The hierarchical method permits overlapping diagnostic children. It does not demand disjoint algorithms, consensus terminology or software modules. Equally, every independently failing condition is not automatically a child: time, interpretation, source quality and criterion adequacy recur across many inquiries. A broad formula that can encode everything does not establish a leaf either.

The question is whether a recurring first-order inquiry about inferential support becomes materially unrecoverable under one qualified claim–basis relation, after its modes, evidence conditions and failure routes are made explicit. The proposed result is one bounded leaf, but the strict-versus-defeasible alternative below is serious and retained as a reopening route.

## 2. Strongest rival — Strict Consequence versus Defeasible Support

Promotion argument: necessity under fixed premises differs sharply from support that can be weakened by additional information while the original observations stay true. Proof/countermodel methods, probabilistic evidence and interacting defeaters have genuinely different success conditions. C01/C02/C03 and C04–C07 expose errors that cannot be checked by one generic score. Pollock's explicit contrast gives this rival more substance than a familiar vocabulary list [S7].

Counterpressure: the proposed division describes the guarantee and update behavior of a claim-support relation, not two independently produced informational outcomes. A probabilistic result can be a strict mathematical consequence of a stipulated model while its application to the target is defeasible. A symbolic, nonnumeric rule can be defeasible. Merely calling a calculation Bayesian or a statement logical does not determine which guarantee is being asserted.

For one actual claim, the analyst must fix its mode, basis and applicable relation. An exact conditional posterior need not establish an unconditional event, and a defeasible diagnosis need not be asserted as a theorem. The model calculation and its application may both need inquiry; making two universal children can obscure that nested distinction by suggesting one operation or result belongs to only one regime. Overlap is not fatal, but here the proposed children do not supply a second transfer or maintained-state outcome beyond the same warranted claim.

Judgment: retain strict, probabilistic, explanatory and defeasible regimes as explicit, directly retrievable analytic routes, rather than promote two children at this depth. This is not because regimes are unimportant or because every process can be written as a function. W2 and W3 retain distinct oracles, countermodels, dependencies and remedies. A generic green warrant score would fail this organizational choice.

Cost and reversal: a rich leaf may hide the substantial depth of defeasible semantics or strict consequence. Reopen if recurring autonomous questions cannot be handled without repeatedly switching to a second research object, if omitted support/defeat semantics systematically change answers despite the explicit routes, or if independent review shows that separate children reduce material diagnostic loss. Specialist proof theory and nonmonotonic reasoning were not exhaustively researched here.

## 3. Rival — Deduction, Induction and Abduction

Promotion argument: drawing a necessary consequence, generalizing beyond observations and selecting an explanation appear to have different purposes and failures.

Counterpressure: these are valuable traditions and method profiles, but they do not partition claim warrant reliably. An explanatory model can generate deductive predictions; a population model can support probabilistic consequences; checking a hypothesis can combine all three. Generating a candidate is not the same claim as preferring it, assigning it a probability, or accepting its truth. A familiar three-word taxonomy would therefore hide important distinctions within each bucket while duplicating the basis/target/strength problem across them.

Judgment: keep the method vocabulary, but route by the qualified conclusion and claimed guarantee. Do not turn these names into a ready-made Foundation hierarchy. This judgment does not say the disciplines are interchangeable.

## 4. Rival — Formal Validity versus Empirical Grounding

Promotion argument: a perfectly executed calculation can rest on a false or inapplicable premise. Different evidence is required to inspect the computation and to validate its target bridge.

Counterpressure: both can be material to one support claim. A purely hypothetical theorem need not assert actual grounding at all; an empirical conclusion can incorporate several nested conditional models. Source acquisition and source-to-referent evidence already activate Formation and applicable ground/causal/Standing boundaries. Creating a universal grounding child here would tend to absorb all empirical validation, while a validity child would duplicate the chosen reasoning regime.

Judgment: maintain conditional validity, target applicability and evidential establishment as noncollapsed facets. This distinction must be visible in a result even though it is not a new child pair. A later recurring inference-local grounding question not explained by these routes would justify reconsideration.

## 5. Rival — Candidate Construction versus Warrant Assessment

Producing a candidate can be successful exploratory Work while its support remains unknown. Assessing a candidate can also be Work that generates a new assessment. However, discovery versus verification is not an exclusive pair of inferential outcomes: a proof can construct and justify a result together, while a previously guessed result can acquire a valid independent argument later.

The Warrant node protects what support is established, not the complete workflow of proposing, reviewing, approving and acting. Actual production history remains distinct from offered justification, as S6 pressures. Keep those distinctions without importing a planner–generator–critic architecture.

## 6. Other proposed children and boundary theft

- Causal explanation: already has exact causal targets and identification boundaries in Composition. An inference about that target still needs warrant, but a peer here would duplicate the target domain rather than resolve a new generic support relation.
- Statistical confidence/calibration: these are particular guarantees under specified distributions and conditions, not a universal peer encompassing all support.
- Evidence aggregation, joint/alternative support, circularity and defeaters: relation structure and challenge modes that can recur in many regimes. A source list cannot substitute for them, but they are not independent semantic outcomes by label.
- Source credibility/admissibility: can alter the inferential basis, yet its assessment may require Formation, Actors or Standing. Source authority does not establish truth.
- Maintaining beliefs after defeat: Warrant can assess the current argument; Revision must separately investigate changes to a continuing informational state, history and reliance. A support assessment is not an applied revision.
- Selection and actual use: not completed by warrant over the supplied basis, and not guaranteed by a valid conditional result.

## 7. Why this differs from the accepted Derivation three-child decision

The Derivation parent separates carrying an interpreted representation, licensing a conclusion and changing a continuing informational state. Passing one does not establish either of the other outcome relations. Inside Warrant, the tested regimes qualify which support relation is claimed for the conclusion; they do not establish an additional required representation-transfer or retained-state outcome.

This is a level-relative comparative judgment, not a general theorem that regimes never deserve child research. The separate logic/probability/defeat routes must remain accessible. If a later pass repeatedly cannot investigate one without an independent unresolved region, the leaf decision loses its practical justification.

## 8. Proposed closure

One qualified claim–basis support leaf. Preserve target/mode, premises and applicability, reasoning regime, admissible alternatives, dependence, contrary evidence/defeaters, evidence-selection conditions, verification scope and limits. No immediate child promoted. Revision and Selection remain actual dedicated research debt. No claim of a complete theory of reasoning, universal verifier, optimal confidence calculus or independently validated taxonomy follows.
