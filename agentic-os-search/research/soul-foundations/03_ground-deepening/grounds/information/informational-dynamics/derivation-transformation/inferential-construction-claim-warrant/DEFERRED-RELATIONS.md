# Claim Warrant — Preserved Cross-Node Interfaces

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Role:** interfaces, not automatic new work or a new ontology.

| Neighbor | Preserved distinction |
|---|---|
| Re-expression / Semantic Transfer | intended meaning and necessary distinctions surviving transfer do not license every new conclusion; a valid argument can still receive a purpose-inadequate representation |
| Formation / Acquisition | authentic/faithful capture does not establish source truth; model adequacy may require new discriminating acquisition, not more proof text |
| Maintained-Information Revision | current support can be defeated without an applied change to continuing information; withdrawal, conflict, alternatives, history and current designation remain its dedicated inquiry |
| Selection / Exposure | selected evidence can change inferential guarantees; actual visibility/coverage still needs its own pass |
| Informational State | conclusion mode, target, scope, source and uncertainty are part of what is claimed, not merely decoration on an identical sentence |
| Records | preserving an argument preserves its identity/history, not truth; prior and current support may require distinct temporal reconstruction |
| Binding | exact roles, common sources, joint/alternative dependencies and applicability are not generic links |
| Causal Effect | observation, prediction and intervention target are distinct; identification and estimation cannot borrow each other's guarantees |
| Standing | qualification, assessment and recognition differ; a support assessment is not automatic target fact or decision sufficiency |
| Work and Actors/Control | goals, losses, acceptance authority and actual action are not supplied by confidence or proof alone |
| Propagation | a newly supported conclusion or local support withdrawal is not universal downstream uptake |

No architecture, data structure, confidence service, theorem prover or argumentation framework is adopted. No new actor behavior or live quality benefit has been measured. Revision and Selection remain visible unfinished dedicated research rather than absorbed by this map.
