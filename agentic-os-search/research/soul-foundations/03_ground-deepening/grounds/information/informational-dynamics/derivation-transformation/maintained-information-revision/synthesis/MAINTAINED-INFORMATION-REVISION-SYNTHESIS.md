## What is being revised?

Revision concerns a continuing informational state or reliance designation under an admitted change basis. It is not synonymous with editing text, adding a timestamp, selecting a search result or obtaining a supported answer. A well-supported correction can coexist with an unchanged maintained state; a stored update can coexist with obsolete downstream use.

The focus may be content, source-support status, accepted commitments, scoped currentness or a policy-bearing epistemic state. Identify which is being changed. Capturing a new report is not automatically accepting its content as a fact; it may instead establish that a particular source made a report.

## Support and alternatives

Source withdrawal, proposition contraction and acceptance of a contrary proposition are distinct transitions. Another independent or joint support can survive withdrawal. Recycled consequences and unsupported positive cycles cannot manufacture grounding under the declared least-Horn semantics; negative/default dependencies may matter even when positive contributors are unchanged. These finite examples do not prescribe one logic or ban recursive reasoning.

Support maintenance and successor choice are separately diagnosable. Enumerating compatible alternatives does not decide which to adopt, and selecting one does not prove that all its supporting records are current. A skeptical result, scoped alternatives or an explicit policy-based choice may each be appropriate under different contracts. Do not silently merge incompatible alternatives into consensus.

## Policy is part of the question

Minimal change has no unique meaning without the object, admissibility condition and comparison order. Inclusion-maximal preservation can differ from preserving the most items. A condition allowed to remain possible is weaker than a condition required to follow. D02–D04 supply small explicit counterexamples.

Correction of an old account differs from tracking a changed world. Reference/effective time differs from recording time. Currentness can be role-, branch- or interval-relative. These distinctions preserve the accepted Records result without requiring event sourcing, bitemporal tables or complete history in every system.

## Preserving a continuation is stronger than preserving an answer

M5's fixed-operator example agrees on all fifteen nonempty one-step inputs in a four-world domain and still diverges after two revisions. A state representation can therefore preserve today's answer and a full one-step response profile while discarding distinctions needed for later updating.

When a representation is intended to preserve a continuing process, test observation preservation and transition compatibility over the relevant admitted inputs and reachable states. This is a scope-relative condition, not a universal requirement to retain every past token. A deliberate substantive revision or policy change may intentionally alter future behavior. Preservation through handoff must not be confused with a prohibition on such change.

## Proposed, installed, observed and propagated

An admissible candidate is not an installed revision. A matching focal version does not show that an authorization, source, interpretation rule or other material dependency is unchanged. Readback at one target is not complete propagation. Uncertainty about the target or effect must remain uncertainty rather than being rewritten as failure or success.

Likewise, restoring a historical checkpoint does not restore the external world or recreate present authority. Replaying the same event and admitting a separate occurrence with the same text can have different semantics. D05 tests this distinction under a declared formal operator; it does not treat repeated messages as independent evidence or establish distributed exactly-once behavior.

## SOUL / Development OS relevance, not adoption

As an analytical application, a future agent should not treat a fresh answer, an up-to-date evidence list and an actually updated shared state as interchangeable accomplishments. A handoff may need the reasons, unresolved alternatives, temporal scope and remaining effect uncertainty that later change can make decisive. Conversely, unnecessary historical detail can make retrieval and maintenance harder. The protected future use, not maximal document volume, determines the required preservation.

These are research implications, not implemented behavior, mandatory storage fields, runtime modules or modifications to agent-control. Neither system's live efficacy was tested here.
