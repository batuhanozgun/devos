## 1. Result and protected question

Warrant is best retained, at this Foundation depth, as one qualified claim–basis support inquiry. Which conclusion is licensed by which premises, rules/models and assumptions, for which target and scope, and what evidence establishes that support? Strict consequence, probabilistic estimates and defeasible explanations are not interchangeable, but currently remain distinct regimes of that inquiry rather than newly promoted children.

This is not an inference-engine design or a universal confidence test. Re-expression and its R6 refinements are accepted by the current user continuation; this new leaf and W6 refinements separately await evaluation. Maintained-Information Revision and Selection/Exposure remain without dedicated passes.

## 2. The decisive distinction: a valid conditional result need not be an established target fact

An argument can correctly show what follows from a premise while leaving that premise unverified. A probabilistic calculation can be correct under a stipulated model without establishing that model's applicability. A causal effect can be well-defined and exactly calculated in one model while not identified by the evidence shared with another model.

The remedy depends on the missing support. Repeating a proof or adding more narrative may not validate a premise, independence assumption or source-to-target bridge. Conversely, uncertainty need not prohibit a useful answer: one can report a conditional result, a supported range, a tentative hypothesis or a conclusion stable across material alternatives. These modes must not silently become unconditional certainty.

The node therefore separates candidate production, actual input use, offered justification, conditional validity, target support, factual truth, decision sufficiency and authority. A valid offered explanation need not be a faithful record of discovery; a faithful trace can contain an invalid argument. The historical S6 study provides bounded pressure on that distinction, not a present-day model verdict.

## 3. Unknown, inconsistent and disproved are different

A budget-limited proof search may not yet find a consequence that exists. C03 demonstrates that with a two-step rule chain. Calling the uncompleted search a proof of negation would be a stronger unsupported claim.

C02 demonstrates a different issue: inconsistent classical premises have no satisfying model, so the universal entailment test succeeds vacuously for both a proposition and its negation. The code preserves that formal definition; it does not treat the result as sound factual evidence. The appropriate report must identify the basis problem or a different declared logic, not certify arbitrary facts.

A conclusion unsupported by the offered argument is not thereby false or impossible to justify by another argument. This keeps local evidence limits from turning into unjustified global impossibility claims.

## 4. Confidence needs its exact meaning and reference conditions

The alert example supplies a sensitivity of 9/10, but with the stated low prevalence and false-positive rate the probability of H after an alert is 1/12. The difference is not bad arithmetic; it is the direction and basis of the probability. A different prior changes the result. Such calculations illustrate why a score cannot be interpreted without its event, conditioning, population and assumptions.

Repeated delivery of the same alert retains that posterior; a pair specified to be conditionally independent gives another posterior. Multiple reports do not manufacture independence. Similarly, three distinct claims each with probability 9/10 can have different joint probabilities under different dependence structures.

The calibration construction goes further: the same predictor can match an overall 4/5 confidence condition while two subgroups have accuracies 1 and 3/5. This is an exact artificial population, not a measured model. It blocks the inference from a population-level guarantee to every context or individual.

## 5. A fitting explanation may not distinguish the causal alternatives

Two finite structural models in C06 yield exactly the same observed X,Y pairs. Both support perfect observational prediction. One says intervening on X changes Y; the other says the observed relation comes from their shared U and changing X alone does not change Y. The intervention effects are 1 and 0.

The point is not universal skepticism about causality. The shared observation does not identify that target among these remaining models. More justified assumptions, a suitable intervention or other discriminating evidence can change the problem. The accepted causal Foundation boundaries remain intact: target definition, identification, estimation and decision sufficiency are different claims.

Likewise, strongest among current candidate explanations is not uniquely true. The candidate set, comparison criterion and possibility of omitted alternatives matter. The next useful question may concern the support bridge or an alternative, not merely more repetitions of the favored narrative.

## 6. A defeated reason is not the opposite conclusion

Pollock's rebuttal/undercutting distinction supplies a concrete challenge to treating every objection alike. In our stipulated alert example, evidence of a faulty sensor path can remove the alert's support for H without proving not-H. A separate valid route can still support H. Contrary evidence directly supporting not-H is a different case.

A material defeater needs evidence too; merely imagining an exception is not sufficient to erase every conclusion. Competing arguments may leave the result unsettled or contested rather than licensing arbitrary certainty. The pass does not adopt one universal defeat calculus.

This sharpens the next Revision problem without completing it. Assessing the current support of a claim is not the same as deciding and applying what should now be kept, withdrawn or replaced throughout a continuing information state.

## 7. Selecting an encouraging result changes the evidential question

The four-test construction gives each preselected null test a 1/20 crossing probability. At least one crossing in four independent opportunities occurs with probability 29679/160000. Reporting the selected success with only the individual test's guarantee drops a material part of the procedure.

This is not a blanket objection to multiple testing, sequential analysis or adaptive inquiry. Those procedures can carry appropriate guarantees. It is a warning against borrowing a guarantee from a different selection/stopping protocol. Selection/Exposure owns the visible evidence-set question; Warrant concerns what that selected evidence can license. The former still needs its dedicated pass.

## 8. Supported belief and authorized action remain distinct

Holding the posterior fixed while changing losses can change the preferred action, as C12 shows. Choosing a goal, loss criterion or acceptable risk is not equivalent to establishing an epistemic probability; permission to act is another question. A high confidence score does not supply either automatically.

For later SOUL reasoning, this suggests a diagnostic distinction before asking for more information: is the missing element a fact, a premise, an applicable model, a dependence relation, an alternative explanation, a credible defeater, a selection condition, or a decision/authority basis? These are prospective uses of the research, not implemented actor behavior or measured work-quality improvements.

## 9. Why one leaf, and the strongest contrary case

Strict consequence versus defeasible support is the strongest rival. The success conditions and checking methods genuinely differ. We do not dismiss the distinction because the operations overlap. However, at this level both qualify the same conclusion–basis support relation; numerical and symbolic methods can each have strict or defeasible roles, often nested in one inference episode. W2/W3 preserve direct regime-specific routes and non-interchangeable checks.

Unlike the parent distinction between transferring a representation, licensing a conclusion and revising a continuing state, the tested regime splits do not currently establish an additional independent informational outcome. A generic score would still be inadequate. The leaf must remain rich enough to expose each basis, scope and defeat condition.

This is a bounded organizational judgment with costs: future specialist or independent research may show that strict or defeasible inquiry is systematically hidden and deserves its own child. W4 records that reversal condition. No complete theory of logic, probability, explanation or rational belief is claimed.
