# Re-expression — Cross-Node Boundaries

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Role:** semantic re-entry, not a relation enum or automatic work queue.

| Other region | Relationship to this leaf | Protected distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Informational State | constituted content, form, mode, scope, identity and qualifications | defining meaning is not observing a transfer |
| Formation / Acquisition | source-facing capture and instantiation | acquired representation is not already source-true or correctly transformed |
| Inferential Construction | additional conclusions, disambiguation and assumptions | carried claim is not a warranted new conclusion |
| Maintained-Information Revision | changing current information or reliance | a new rendering is not by itself an installed revision |
| Selection / Exposure | recipient-visible source/derivative and retrieval scope | recoverable source is not necessarily exposed or used |
| Records / persistence | source/decoder survival and historical interpretation | a future-use obligation is not proven by today's successful decode |
| Binding | exact anchors, roles, relations and version/time | preserved tokens are not preserved associations |
| Standing | purpose-relative fidelity assessment and its evidence | assessment result is not the property or authorization |
| Actors / Work | intended use, capability, participation and actual understanding/action | faithful expression is not successful consumer behavior |
| Environment | available libraries, decoders and operating constraints | possible interpretation is not currently available capability |
| Control / authority | permissible redaction, disclosure and reliance | semantic sufficiency does not grant permission or prove privacy |
| Propagation / causality | actual incorporation, spread and effect | correct local re-expression is not universal uptake or benefit |

Several relations may be material in one episode without requiring separate runtime components. Source/derivative role, exact interpretation and purpose determine the question. Original accepted results remain in their own packages; this pass does not revalidate all of them.
