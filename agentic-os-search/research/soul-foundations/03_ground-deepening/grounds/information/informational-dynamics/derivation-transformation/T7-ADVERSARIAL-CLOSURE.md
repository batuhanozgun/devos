## 3. Verification limits

The author chose the fixtures, contracts and semantic interpretation. C02 uses a second calculation path but not a second researcher. The revision fixture is not a verified implementation of a general AGM or Delgrande/Schaub operator. No LLM generation, source retriever, human belief intervention, production database, scientific experiment or actual SOUL runtime was executed. Numerical examples do not estimate empirical effect sizes, natural-language fidelity or model confidence.

The eight primary sources are independent authorship for component-level pressure at unequal documented depths. They do not validate this exact hierarchy. Source reading is not source endorsement, and no inaccessible classic was represented as read. The proposed split remains a revisable same-model synthesis.

## 4. Closure judgment

The parent is coherent. Transfer/warrant/revision survive the strongest one-leaf and two-child rivals; source lineage, uncertainty, temporal scope and propagation are retained without manufacturing a fourth child. Later dedicated passes may merge, refine or reject the proposed children. This is the bounded return point, not recursive research completion.

OPEN-QUESTIONS records unknown semantic oracles, opaque actual-use evidence, changing question families, revision-policy dependence and deployment-specific verification. User acceptance cannot resolve them by decree.
