## 2. What the parent investigates

The parent asks how existing information participates in producing or changing representations, conclusions or a continuing informational state, and what obligations that change satisfies. Inputs and outputs need not be distinct files. A changed encoding can preserve meaning; an unchanged statement can lose its current evidential standing; a computation can expose a new consequence without adding an observation.

Production history, actual input contribution, warrant, world truth and decision sufficiency are distinct. Knowing a result's source does not prove that its inference is valid. A detailed trace is not automatically an epistemic justification. Conversely, an output's actual truth does not establish that the offered premises licensed it.

## 3. Re-expression: what survives?

A summary can contain only statements that follow from its source and still omit a restriction needed by the next Work. C02 makes that precise: p-and-q entails p, but the projected record cannot answer q across the allowed source states. This is not necessarily hallucination; it is a failure of purpose-relative preservation.

The requirement is not to preserve everything. Abstraction, translation, recoding and deliberate omission can be useful. The obligation is to name the protected meaning and permitted loss and to avoid treating an output as sufficient for questions it no longer answers. Exact byte preservation, semantic equivalence and practical usability are different.

T2 reuses Formation's fibre/decoder criterion for a derivative representation and a family of questions. Its finite enumeration is a logical illustration, not a general natural-language evaluator. Choosing the relevant question family remains a substantive, use-specific task.

## 4. Inference: what is warranted?

A proposition p may happen to be true while the premise p-or-q does not justify selecting it. A conditional can support a conclusion only with the required assumption or another adequate basis. A model-generated estimate, explanation or hypothesis can be valuable, but must retain the mode and assumptions that make its use defensible.

This is not a truth-preservation-only theory of transformation. A factual summary, candidate hypothesis, statistical estimate and correction have different contracts. Inference can reveal consequences already implicit in evidence; it need not be a new independent observation to be informative. Nor does a newly generated artifact create independent empirical support merely by existing.

Dependence matters when evidence is combined. In the exact ±1 example, averaging two copies retains variance 1 while averaging independent values produces variance 1/2. No general confidence formula for language or agents follows. The point is that warranted uncertainty reduction requires a basis beyond the count of reports.

## 5. Revision: what is now retained or withdrawn?

Producing a sound new analysis does not settle how a continuing information state should change. New evidence may require correcting a representation, withdrawing reliance, preserving alternatives or changing a current designation while retaining history. These are not all the same operation as adding a sentence.

A withdrawn source does not prove its claim false. Another support route may survive. When two admissible revisions remain, the input alone may not select one; a priority or preference is an additional basis. The finite maximal-preservation example leaves two tied revisions rather than an invented consensus.

Revision is informational here. Actors and Control retain authority, Work retains actual decision activity, Standing retains qualification and Propagation retains spread. A computed proposed state is not an installed one; a locally installed correction is not a system-wide current view. A revision can occur within a session and does not universally require persistent storage.

## 6. Why separate children, not just a checklist?

A shared contract grammar is still useful. The decisive question is what one completed check leaves unestablished. Source-transfer adequacy does not establish inferential warrant; inferential validity does not establish that the output retains all task-required information; neither establishes an appropriate old-to-new maintained-state revision.

The one-leaf rival can accommodate this only by selecting among the same three protected outcomes inside its contract. Under the project's diagnostic child method, making these explicit is materially useful: they have different failure conditions, evidence demands and corrective questions, and recur in distinct primary traditions. T4 therefore prefers three immediate inquiries.

This is not a universal proof that all other organizations are wrong. The cost is additional child work and unavoidable overlap. A correction or imputation may involve all three. Dedicated passes may expose a stronger merge or refined boundary. Lineage, uncertainty, time, cardinality and mechanisms remain cross-cutting rather than becoming further peers.

## 7. Composition can fail without bad local arithmetic

A correct unit conversion can feed a consumer expecting a different unit. Individually true statements can refer to nonoverlapping times. Native SQL can execute perfectly while counting the wrong semantic units: two tasks totaling 20 become 30 after a repeated-log join; DISTINCT on equal cost values produces 10, not the desired repair.

A query result can also change when a formerly absent blocker appears, even though every positive input row remains unchanged. Currentness therefore cannot always be reduced to watching the named positive contributors. These constructions show why exact interpretation and dependency scope matter. They are not a prescription for one database, dependency engine or pipeline architecture.

## 8. Implications for later SOUL reasoning

As a prospective evaluation lens, an actor may need to distinguish three failures before asking for more information: the source was summarized badly; the conclusion was unsupported by what was available; or the managed knowledge state was not correctly revised after new evidence. More retrieval alone need not repair the first or second, and a correct new answer alone need not repair the third.

This is a research implication, not a new instruction installed in SOUL or agent-control. The current pass does not establish real actor behavior, work-quality gains, propagation reliability or causal benefit.
