# M3 — Primary pressure and rival structures

**Date:** 2026-09-13. **Standing:** leading bounded-leaf hypothesis; same-model comparison.

Seven primary documents were read at different, explicitly recorded depths. Their convergence is on qualified revision questions, not on this exact Foundation tree. S1 adds assumption-qualified support and the scope of a maintenance component's received clauses. The direct formal sources are not read as universal claims about human cognition or LLM internals. The older source's 'current technology' claims are historical and unused. ## 2. Strongest rival: support maintenance versus successor selection

Promotion argument: maintaining which conclusions follow under changing assumptions differs from choosing among competing revisions. A component can correctly retract one support while a surrounding system adopts an inappropriate replacement. Truth-maintenance and belief-revision traditions emphasize different information and algorithms. This is real pressure, not superficial terminology.

Counterpressure: the selected node protects a continuing informational transition under an admitted basis. For one target, support semantics determine which successors are admissible; priority or retention semantics may then leave one or several admissible outcomes. A deterministic maintenance policy is not outside that choice space merely because the selection is implicit. Conversely, a plausibility-based revision still needs a declared basis and an output state. The distinction can be routed directly without claiming that a maintained source graph and an independently accepted belief are the same thing.

At this Foundation depth, the leading judgment is to keep both as separately retrievable obligations of the common old-to-new relation. Warrant already owns the support assessment itself; Work/Control owns adjudication and authority where those become the focal outcome. A split here would be useful only if it exposes an additional Revision-local inquiry rather than recreating those neighbors or assigning algorithms to buckets. M1 §§2–5 and M2 §2 retain separate failure, evidence and remedy paths.

Cost and reversal: a rich leaf may hide a substantial support-maintenance program. If later cases repeatedly require a separately scoped continuing support system whose update cannot be recovered through this relation without circular reuse of Warrant or a hidden adjudication problem, reopen and promote rather than enlarging a generic checklist indefinitely. The current test does not prove that every possible organization should be one leaf.

## 3. Rival: correction of knowledge versus tracking world change

The different outputs in C04 make this split attractive. They have distinct temporal targets and different assumptions. However, S3 supplies substantive counterpressure against treating them as universally disjoint processes. A new observation can trigger both correction of an earlier model and tracking an event. Initial conditions and enduring invariants can switch their roles across cases.

Retain the two modes and their required temporal binding. Do not require a universal pair of child populations or import the postulates of one formal tradition as Foundation authority. Reopen if separate investigation proves materially more diagnostic than the explicit target/mode routes.

## 4. Rival: content revision versus reliance/status revision

A unchanged document can lose an accepted designation; an edited document can retain one. This is an important independence claim. But what counts as payload versus qualification varies with the informational focus: a retraction notice's content represents the status of another document. The accepted State and Records work already handles that distinction.

The revised focus must identify its content, support and designation effects, without equating them with world truth or action permission. A new child named institutional reliance would tend to absorb Actors/Control/Standing rather than expose a new generic informational transition. This does not make actual belief change identical to a recorded editorial label.

## 5. Rival: admissible proposal versus application

A stale proposal can be correctly derived and inappropriately applied. Nevertheless, candidate versus realized change is a general Work/Transition distinction rather than a Revision-only taxonomy. The revision relation must bind the encountered base and resulting local state. Control remains responsible for governing effect and authority; Propagation remains responsible for actual spread. C08/C09 show why these boundaries need checks rather than being considered automatically resolved by a correct algorithm.

## 6. Other rivals and boundaries

History versus current state is a use/horizon distinction and a Records profile when persistence matters, not a requirement for two universal subtrees. Merge, correction, contraction, reinstatement and supersession are operation/effect profiles. A protocol, event store, database, graph, LLM or human editor is a possible mechanism, not an immediate Foundation child. Conflict visibility remains Selection/State; conflict resolution is not completed by that visibility. Revision-induced new evidence can re-enter Formation or Warrant without collapsing those questions.

A proposed Revision leaf must not erase the accepted Derivation siblings: faithful transfer does not settle what to retain after a new basis; warranted inference does not install or maintain its consequences. Likewise a revised source does not establish that relevant users see it. Selection is still an actual missing dedicated pass, not covered by these boundary examples.

## 7. Leading result and required challenge

One qualified continuing-information revision relation appears sufficient at the present depth. It must retain the precise change target, support and alternatives, relevant future-revision state, admitted basis and retention policy, temporal scope, actual application and evidence limitations. No descendant is proposed at this checkpoint.

This choice is a bounded comparative judgment, not a theorem of completeness. M5/M6 must still test whether current evidence justifies closure, preserve the strongest rival and untested regimes, and return the result rather than silently starting Selection.
