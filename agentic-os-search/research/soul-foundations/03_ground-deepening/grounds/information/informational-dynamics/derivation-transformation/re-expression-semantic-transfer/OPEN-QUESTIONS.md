# Re-expression / Semantic Transfer — Open Questions

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Role:** bounded residuals and reversal conditions; not an execution queue.

## Structural reopen triggers

1. A recurring representational-accessibility or interpretation inquiry proves transfer-local and cannot be preserved as target semantics plus neighboring exposure/capability questions.
2. Semantic Conservation versus Interpretive Realization materially reduces diagnostic loss across new cases without duplicating the parent or other grounds.
3. A multimodal, social, sign-language or non-propositional case repeatedly defeats the current interpreted source-to-derivative formulation.
4. Independent review identifies an omitted first-order question, a forced fit or an overbroad loss/meaning frame.

## Target-specific evidence not established

The actual source/target semantic mapping can be uncertain, community-specific or opaque. This pass provides no universal natural-language equality test. Required question families, future use horizons and recipient resources can be partly unknown. Finite fixtures cannot certify their completeness.

Approximation and decision margin require justified target quantities, thresholds, tolerances and uncertainty. No numeric error tolerance is universally sufficient. External source links may or may not remain recoverable under a future operating environment; no live persistence guarantee was tested.

Round-trip and multiple-evaluator agreement can have shared errors. This pass does not establish independent human/model evaluation, probabilistic error bounds, rare-event sensitivity or empirical generalization.
