# R4 — Rival Structures and Boundary Audit

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** proposed bounded leaf after same-model comparison; not user-accepted.

## 1. Test used

The question is whether an immediate child would protect a recurring first-order inquiry that is materially lost under a single bounded transfer relation with explicit facets. Different errors, different tools or independent failure are serious pressure, not automatic proof of a universal hierarchy. Equally, overlap does not disqualify diagnostic children: the accepted Derivation split is itself overlapping.

Do not justify a leaf merely because a sufficiently large checklist can contain everything. The proposed leaf must retain a source-to-target protected outcome, concrete failure routes, discriminating evidence and reversal conditions. A new child is warranted if those routes systematically hide a separate inquiry rather than qualify the transfer outcome.

## 2. Strongest rival — Semantic Conservation versus Interpretive Realization

The first proposed child would ask what source content, uncertainty and required distinctions survive. The second would ask how a target code/form is interpreted by the intended recipient. Their failures are genuinely separable: an invertible code may be unavailable to its recipient; a readable paraphrase may omit a blocker; a reversible mislabeling can express the wrong literal meaning.

Why not promote them now? Intended interpretation is part of what the derivative expresses, not only a downstream optional success. A transfer does not first preserve a context-free meaning and then independently assign its meaning to a consumer. The source-to-target obligation must already bind the applicable interpretation. Where the residual question is actual delivery, decoder availability, usability or decision behavior, it crosses Selection, Environment, Actors and Work; promoting it here without a narrower outcome would absorb those boundaries.

Formal recoverability, intended expression and actual use remain separate claim scopes in R1/R2 and the findings map. A user can investigate each directly. Current evidence does not expose a further Re-expression-local protected outcome that disappears when those scopes remain facets of the interpreted transfer relation.

This is a bounded organizational judgment, not a deduction from the word interpretation. A future body of cases requiring a recurring autonomous representational-accessibility inquiry, not explainable as source/target semantics or neighboring capability/exposure, would reopen the split.

## 3. Rival — Preservation-Contract Formation versus Transfer Realization

This split would separate deciding what must survive from executing a re-expression. Its strength is real: a perfect implementation can meet a badly chosen question family; a good requirement can be implemented badly. C13's omitted blocker question illustrates the risk.

However, choosing a legitimate task criterion and satisfying it is a general Work/Standing distinction across Formation, Warrant and other research nodes. Promoting it here alone would duplicate goal/criterion validation rather than identify a new kind of representational change. The child must still preserve explicit permitted loss, source-required relations and uncertainty; it cannot validate itself by selecting only questions its output answers.

Keep contract justification visible, but do not create a universal planning-versus-execution hierarchy inside this node. Reopen if a specific recurring abstraction-boundary problem becomes unrecoverable through the current routes.

## 4. Rival — Lossless Recoding versus Lossy Abstraction

This distinction is useful in engineering. Its weakness as a Foundation split is that loss is relative to a specified distinction and interpretation. The same identity-stripping view can preserve a count exactly while losing identity; NFC and NFKC preserve different equivalences; a logical weakening can preserve one purpose and defeat another.

The mechanisms and evidence demands differ, but both evaluate what the derivative carries under the declared transfer obligation. Approximate, selective, exact and reversible profiles must be distinguished; treating them as clean universal populations would hide purpose relativity. No required lossless-first processing stage follows.

## 5. Rival — Content versus Structure versus Pragmatic Qualification

A value, its actor role, temporal reference, condition, modality and representational stance often jointly constitute the claim. Content/structure separation can be convenient for testing but the boundary changes with the format: a table header supplies meaning; word order supplies scope; a quotation marker changes the claim from P to someone asserted P.

These concerns are already explicit in the accepted State/Binding constitution and are transferred here. Promoting each as a universal child would recreate a rejected State hierarchy and duplicate transfer reasoning across inseparable aspects. C03/C04 keep relational and order-sensitive failures directly retrievable instead of reducing fidelity to text overlap.

## 6. Mechanism and medium rivals

Translation, summarization, compression, normalization, projection, redaction and serialization are rich specialist profiles. A single operation can alter content, structure, scope and recipient interpretation; the same protected question recurs across all of them. Text, table, image, graph and code are also not first-order inquiry outcomes by themselves.

No examined primary tradition shares or validates this exact proposed Foundation organization. Their differences provide pressure and prevent importing one task-specific taxonomy. The absence of a new label in seven sources is not a universal absence proof.

## 7. Why retain the Derivation siblings?

Re-expression asks what an interpreted derivative carries from a source. Inferential Construction asks whether a conclusion is warranted by premises, rules and assumptions. Revision asks how a continuing information state changes under a new basis. A source-faithful presentation may coexist with an unsupported conclusion or an unrevised current state. These are different protected transition purposes, not just different aspects of interpreting one derivative.

The new distinction between a possible decoder and the intended interpretation refines transfer evidence; it does not collapse Warrant or Revision into Re-expression. A justified disambiguation or correction may involve those siblings. Their dedicated passes remain unperformed and cannot borrow closure from the boundary probes here.

## 8. Other boundaries

Formation retains source-facing instantiation and capture. State retains what the representation means/is. Selection retains which existing information reaches a use, and may coexist with a newly constructed excerpt. Records retains continued interpretability/persistence obligations. Binding retains exact roles and anchors; Standing the relevant qualification and evidence judgment; Actors/Control permission and governing authority; Propagation actual spread; causal inquiry actual effects and benefit.

Representational recoverability does not imply that a human or model actually used the information correctly. Source fidelity does not prove source truth. Purpose-specific abstraction does not establish authorized disclosure or privacy. These distinctions constrain the node instead of becoming additional immediate children.

## 9. Decision, costs and reversal

Propose one bounded Re-expression / Semantic Transfer leaf with explicit source/target interpretation, conservation/loss, ambiguity/mode, structure/binding, approximate decision margin, chain compatibility, recipient/horizon and evidence routes. The cost is a richer leaf vulnerable to impoverished summaries. A generic fidelity score cannot replace the map.

Reopen if repeated cases expose a separate first-order inquiry systematically omitted by this organization; if interpreter/accessibility semantics cannot be retained without stealing neighboring grounds; if a split lowers material diagnostic loss without duplicating common contract validation; or if independent review supplies a stronger organization. Current breadth is bounded and same-model, not a uniquely proved theory of meaning.
