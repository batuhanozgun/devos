## 1. Bounded result

Re-expression concerns which source meanings, relations, alternatives and use-relevant distinctions survive a different representation under the intended source and target interpretations. Legitimate abstraction, recoding, translation and summarization need not preserve every feature. Their adequacy nevertheless cannot be inferred from fluent output, unchanged keywords, successful parsing or a general similarity score alone.

At the present Foundation depth this is best retained as one interpreted source-to-derivative inquiry. The strongest Semantic Conservation versus Interpretive Realization split remains explicit in R4. Its separate failure routes are preserved, but current evidence does not force another immediate child beyond this common protected transfer outcome and its neighboring capability/exposure boundaries.

This is a bounded same-model structural judgment, not a unique universal taxonomy or a runtime design. Derivation's parent and three immediate questions are accepted by the current user turn; this new child's leaf result and R6 refinements separately await evaluation.

## 2. The most important refinement: recoverable is not necessarily correctly expressed

The inherited fibre/decoder criterion answers whether some decoder can recover a required answer from a representation. That remains valid. It is not by itself proof that the intended recipient's interpretation yields that answer.

A reversible swap of the literal labels blocked and ready contains enough information to reconstruct the source if the swap is known. Under the fixed intended target labels, however, the intermediate representation says the opposite. Applying the inverse swap returns the original label, so a round-trip test passes while the intermediate meaning is wrong. C11 demonstrates this exact construction, not a human or model translation experiment.

Three claims must therefore stay separate: information is recoverable in principle; the derivative carries the intended meaning under the declared interpretation; the actual recipient can access, understand and use it. A codebook, unit convention, language context or linked source can legitimately supply interpretation. Such a dependency must not be silently replaced by an oracle that simply supplies the omitted answer.

## 3. Fidelity can fail by omission or by unjustified certainty

An output can state only what follows from its source and still omit a blocker necessary for the downstream Work. The error is not necessarily fabrication. Conversely, a source that leaves two alternatives open can be misrepresented as deciding one. Retaining uncertainty, disagreement, attribution and conditionality can be part of faithful transfer.

A set of words does not preserve its relations automatically. C03 retains every actor and role token while swapping who is reviewer and approver. C04 keeps two operations but changes their stipulated order-dependent meaning. Format validity and lexical similarity do not detect those particular errors. Not every order or formatting difference is material; the protected interpretation decides.

Faithfully reporting a false source claim remains different from showing that claim true. A justified correction can depart from the source under a different task, but then its extra warrant and revision basis cannot be credited to faithful carriage alone.

## 4. Useful loss and changing purpose

A public count can legitimately omit actor identities. That derivative can be exact for the count and insufficient for an identity question. The later question does not retroactively make the earlier scoped result wrong, but it does invalidate an unqualified claim of sufficiency for all future uses.

The protected question family must not be chosen merely from what the output happened to retain. Source-task requirements and consequential alternatives supply the basis. Unknown future uses remain an uncertainty, not something a finite test set silently covers. A source-linked orientation can defer detail, provided the source remains recoverable under the actual use conditions; this is not preservation in the standalone summary.

Decision preservation also needs a scope. Two accounts can produce the same current action while differing in explanation, attribution or audit meaning. Same action is a narrower success claim than full semantic preservation.

## 5. Small error can cross a consequential boundary

The constructed conversion of 49.6 to 50 has error below 0.5, yet changes a decision defined by >=50. The numerical approximation meets one requirement and fails another. A stronger exact condition works: when the source value is farther than the maximum error from the threshold, the threshold result is preserved. R2 states the elementary proof; C09 checks 960 eligible rational-grid pairs.

This does not prescribe any operational threshold, acceptable risk or universal confidence metric. It shows why permitted loss must be judged against the intended question. Near a boundary, an uncertainty interval or undecided result may preserve what is warranted better than a rounded point assertion.

## 6. Structured formats supply real executable pressure

Native Python and Node examples exposed the same issue outside prose. Adjacent large integer literals remain distinct in Python's default integer interpretation and collapse in actual Node JSON.parse. A JSON object with duplicate names parses under Python's default mapping while losing an earlier value; a different hook preserves the member occurrences for inspection.

Unicode normalization provides another contrast. Canonically equivalent accented spellings converge under NFC, while compatibility normalization can merge a superscript form and an ordinary identifier under the example's distinct interpretation. Individually normalized text fragments also need not concatenate into a normalized result. These are real library executions with synthetic inputs, not proof of a full standard implementation or a prescription for SOUL datatypes.

## 7. Good local steps need an aligned end-to-end obligation

One step can preserve a needed distinction, while the next correctly meets its own narrower contract and discards it. C12 demonstrates both a failing and an aligned successful chain. Composition is not inherently unreliable; the intermediate interpretation and preservation obligation must be compatible.

The same principle applies to codebook versions, source links and future-use conditions. Changed recipient resources can require re-evaluation even with identical output bytes. Records, Selection, Environment, Work and Actors retain the persistence, exposure, capability and actual-use questions; this transfer leaf does not absorb them.

## 8. A verifier can check everything it sees and miss what disappeared

C13 generates checks only from the surviving output fields. They all pass after a source blocker is dropped. A separately specified source/task blocker question fails. This is a stipulated blind spot, not an empirical failure rate of a named evaluator.

Source-to-output coverage and output-to-source support are different directions. The primary summarization and translation sources provide concrete evaluation approaches and error distinctions, but none establishes universal completeness of the question set, reference or judge. Round-trip, similarity, parsing and learned question-answer checks remain useful for bounded claims, not interchangeable certificates of meaning.

## 9. Why no immediate child, and what remains

The serious split separates conservation from interpretive realization. Both deserve direct diagnosis. Yet intended interpretation already helps define what was conserved, while actual access and successful use partly belong to other accepted grounds and Selection. The current map retains those scopes without a demonstrated new Re-expression-local outcome that needs a separate branch. Preservation-versus-abstraction, content-versus-structure and medium-specific splits are also examined in R4.

The cost is a rich leaf that can itself be summarized badly. Reopen the structure if independent or recurring cases show a distinct inquiry systematically hidden by this organization, especially around accessibility, multimodal meaning or interpretation across unfamiliar communities. Those domains were not comprehensively surveyed.
