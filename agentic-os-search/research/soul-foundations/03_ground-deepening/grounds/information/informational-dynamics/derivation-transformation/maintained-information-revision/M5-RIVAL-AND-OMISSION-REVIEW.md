# M5 — Rival structure and omission review

**Date:** 2026-09-14. **Status:** bounded same-assistant structural judgment for evaluation.
**Inputs:** original M1–M4; accepted Derivation T4/T6; hierarchical method; fresh P1–P3 and D01–D05 in [addendum](M5-SOURCE-AND-EXECUTION-ADDENDUM.md).

## 1. Apply the same promotion criterion

The accepted Derivation parent separates interpreted transfer, offered warrant and maintained-state revision because they protect different relations. Overlap of events does not disqualify children. Conversely, independently failing conditions do not by themselves make every condition a first-order child. Neither one common function notation nor a neighboring Work/Warrant/Control label settles this comparison.

This review therefore strengthens, rather than merely repeats, M3's leading leaf argument. A useful child should add a stable Revision-local protected inquiry whose research obligation is lost or persistently obscured by a fully specified parent relation and direct facet routes. A leaf must preserve separately diagnosable obligations; a single update-success flag fails the test.

## 2. Strongest rival: support maintenance / successor selection

Steelman:

- Support maintenance asks which assumptions, rules, alternatives and consequences remain operative after a basis changes. Removing one reason need not remove every route to a claim; stale negative/default dependencies can make apparently unchanged support unusable.
- Successor selection asks which continuing commitments or candidate states to retain under constraints and preference rules. Correctly enumerating two viable alternatives does not select one; a favored choice does not establish that its dependency records are current.

These failures and remedies are genuinely distinct. P1's reasoner/CMS interface and P3's extensions/selection distinction make the rival substantial. D02 explicitly leaves two alternatives after the fixed constraints are processed. Rejecting the split merely because the questions interact would contradict the accepted T4 method.

## 3. Why the bounded leaf remains preferable here

The distinction is retained, but the proposed pair is not presently the stronger immediate hierarchy.

First, specify the focal maintained object. A context-indexed map of conditional consequences, an endorsed belief set, a source-support designation, and a ranked epistemic state are different constituted objects. A reasoner can maintain several simultaneously. The Revision question for each is which old-to-new change preserves its declared commitments and admits its declared change basis. Maintaining a map is not equivalent to selecting one context from it; changing the endorsed context is a second focal revision, not proof that every Revision needs a two-stage architecture.

Second, distinguish a support relation from changing that relation. The validity of a fixed premise-to-claim relation remains Warrant-facing. Its continuing applicability, withdrawal target, alternative dependencies and historical meaning are Revision-facing and cannot be delegated away. But those transition obligations are the same kinds of obligations as when the maintained focus is a commitment set or a revision-policy state. The new protected relation is old-to-new maintained information, not an additional static support theory.

Third, make the rival labels precise. If successor selection means every admissible old-to-new informational result, it encompasses support-map revision and largely renames the parent. If it means choosing one preferred commitment from otherwise admissible alternatives, it is an important policy/outcome mode: unresolved alternatives, skeptical results and context-indexed states remain legitimate outcomes. The proposed split would need to move the shared constraint, priority, epistemic-state and temporal obligations between these meanings. This is a boundary/level cost, not an objection that some operations overlap.

Fourth, source mechanisms do not settle the hierarchy. P1 computes conditional support relative to what was communicated; it does not provide an all-purpose commitment policy. P3 jointly determines preserved equivalences and admissible extensions, with choice or skeptical treatment afterward. Neither organization establishes a stable universal Revision partition. The finite checks expose mandatory distinctions, not an empirical advantage of one document tree.

Compared with Derivation's transfer/warrant/revision split, the present pair mainly factors the admissibility, basis and selection of one continuing-state transition. The inspected cases do not expose another Revision-local protected relation left unaddressed after those obligations are explicitly specified. At this depth, direct facet routes preserve the distinction with less duplicated policy/identity/time inquiry.

**Judgment:** propose one bounded leaf, not because two children are impossible, but because their additional diagnostic benefit has not outweighed this boundary and duplication cost. This is a qualified organizational comparison, not a mathematical uniqueness result or an independently measured retrieval advantage.

## 4. Fixed-frame holdouts and correction paths

| Analytical holdout | What can fail independently? | Required inquiry retained in the leaf |
|---|---|---|
| A withdrawn direct report and an alternative sufficient derivation | Current conclusion is right but support designation is stale | Identify the precise withdrawn reason, surviving routes and changed dependency conditions; not automatic negation |
| Two incompatible yet admissible commitment repairs | Candidate enumeration is right but an unstated preference picks one | State admissibility, selection rule and unresolved alternatives separately; do not manufacture consensus |
| A maintained query after blocker insertion | Positive inputs unchanged, negative dependency invalidated | Scope the dependency contract beyond named positive contributors; original T3/C09/M2 limits apply |
| Unchanged current beliefs with changed rank gaps | Current response matches but later admitted inputs distinguish states | Preserve future-relevant disposition when continuation is a protected use; D01 |
| Changed policy, unchanged source content | Old proposal ceases to fit the governing comparison | Treat policy/version/applicability as material basis, not as a timeless update rule |
| Restored checkpoint after uncertain external action | Historical state reproduces, present effect/permission unresolved | Reconcile actual effect and current authority; do not turn memory restoration into world restoration |

The last example reuses the harness Note10 as analytical pressure only. These rows are not six newly executed tests.

## 5. Other rivals and omissions

**Correction versus world-change tracking:** M2/C04 preserve their different input interpretation and admissible transitions. Their difference cannot be erased by one overwrite operator. They are retained revision regimes under an explicit reference frame; no claim that the two use interchangeable postulates is made. A source report about an event is not the event itself.

**Content versus reliance:** a quoted payload may remain while its use designation changes. The protected focus and scope must specify which changed. Promoting these as disjoint children would lose cases where uncertainty, policy or source attribution is itself represented content; preserve the effect, not a universal content/metadata partition.

**Proposal versus installation:** candidate construction, actual informational change and downstream exposure have independent success conditions. Work, Control and Propagation supply additional questions, but do not remove the need to identify the actual resulting informational state. This is not a mandatory two-phase implementation or a third Revision child.

**History versus currentness:** history can be too sparse or unnecessarily exhaustive. Records retains use-relative reconstruction. The new behavioral-equivalence pressure does not require preserving all raw history or freezing behavior across an intentionally substantive revision.

**Formal versus natural-language / database versus memory:** these are representational and mechanism profiles. Our formal examples do not establish natural-language update quality. The unmeasured semantic oracle is an evidence limit, not evidence for a new medium-specific Foundation child.

**Consistency versus requirement satisfaction:** D03 requires an explicit constraint mode; mere compatibility does not establish a required consequence. **Minimality:** D04 makes the comparison relation explicit. **Repeated inputs:** D05 separates an admitted update sequence from duplicate delivery. All three are material refinements of the same revision contract.

## 6. Direct-route obligation and reversal conditions

The findings map must separately expose: withdrawal/support; alternatives and selection; state/disposition preservation; input/constraint/policy semantics; time/currentness/history; applicability/realization/replay. A leaf result is inadequate if these become an undifferentiated success score.

Reopen the structural decision if a materially different domain yields a stable Revision-local outcome that these routes cannot preserve without forced remapping; if the two-question organization demonstrably removes recurring diagnostic omissions despite adequate facet routing; or if deeper study shows that policy revision or support maintenance has a separate first-order contract rather than being an instance/qualification of continuing information change. Different terminology, another algorithm or the mere size of a specialist literature is not enough.

The strongest split is retained, not declared disproved. Current evidence supports bounded closure of this node's research at the stated depth, with no automatic child activation or acceptance of the proposed result.
