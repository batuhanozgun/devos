> What is this representation about, under what scope, what does it express, how is it represented, in what mode, and when is it materially the same as or different from another state?

## Why no deeper hierarchy was promoted

The candidate regions repeatedly constrain one another:

- referent/scope changes content meaning;
- form/schema changes interpretation and equivalence;
- mode/stance can change the meaning/use of identical words;
- identity/equivalence depends on scope, content, form, provenance and protected use;
- uncertainty/currentness/source can be content in one domain and qualification in another;
- evidence, authority and truth depend partly on relations outside one State.

Materially different external traditions factor these concerns differently rather than converging on one universal State sub-tree.

## Important anti-collapse rules

- representation exists != represented claim is true;
- authentic/current source != claim is true;
- source authority != universal truth;
- same bytes != same informational state;
- different bytes != necessarily different informational content;
- same text != same assertion/stance;
- same referent != same scope/time;
- persistence != currentness;
- source existence != current visibility/context;
- disagreement != genuine contradiction until scope/time/mode/granularity are aligned.
