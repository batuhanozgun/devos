## 3. Completed Records evidence reused

The Records vertical is a deep persistence specialization and provides strong holdouts for State identity.

Current Records findings distinguish when protected use can tell them apart:

- broader resource identity;
- contextual record role;
- version;
- representation/copy;
- derivation;
- aggregation.

It also establishes that bytes may survive while identity, interpretation, context, relations or future use fail.

State-level implications:

1. identity cannot be reduced to file/byte identity;
2. exact version/representation/aggregate binding can be decision-critical;
3. effective/applicable time and known/recorded time may differ;
4. retrieval hit does not establish exact/current/authoritative/truth;
5. persistent preservation can faithfully preserve a semantically wrong state.

These are State falsifiers, not evidence that persistence is a State child.
