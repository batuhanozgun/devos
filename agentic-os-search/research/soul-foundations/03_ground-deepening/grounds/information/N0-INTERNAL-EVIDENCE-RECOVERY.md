## 1. Why N0 was needed

The active Information node did not start from an empty literature question. The repository already contained:

- a completed Records / Persistent Information vertical;
- a focused adequacy finding proposing a broader Information / Representation parent;
- a completed Environment ground with an explicit actual-condition vs representation boundary;
- completed Work and Actors verticals defining neighbouring problem-region boundaries;
- newer AI knowledge and Context/Memory/Harness research that exposes technical representation, provenance, freshness and context failure surfaces.

The purpose of N0 was to recover these without treating any one of them as the answer.

## 2. Strong retained internal findings

### 2.1 Persistent information is a real specialised problem region

The completed Records vertical supports a bounded claim that persistence introduces distinctive problems around:

- resource/record/version/representation identity;
- preservation/interpretation closure;
- change, history, standing and time;
- provenance/context and derivation;
- organisation and retrieval contracts;
- requirement derivation;
- retention/disposition.

It also explicitly rejects `record = anything persisted`. `Record` is narrower than a working umbrella such as `persistent information resource`.

### 2.2 Persistence does not establish epistemic correctness

Records research explicitly leaves outside its own semantics:

- factual truth;
- evidential sufficiency for claims/decisions;
- reasoning correctness;
- who may designate an authoritative/current source;
- Actor authority and Work decision semantics.

This is important: a broader Information pass cannot merely rename Records and claim the epistemic problem is solved.

### 2.3 Environment has already protected actual reality from representation

The completed Environment ground established the local anti-collapse rule:

> actual condition != specification / observation / model / Record

Environment therefore owns the actual operating/domain condition side. Observation, model, uncertainty/freshness/conflict, evidence about actual conditions and selected context were explicitly deferred to later Information work.

### 2.4 Work cannot be reduced to represented Work state

The completed Work vertical treats Work as a temporally evolving problem space of purpose/outcome, selection/commitment, execution, coordination, evaluation, failure/recovery and related distinctions.

It explicitly leaves persistent evidence and Actor authority outside Work-local semantics. Therefore a plan, commitment or acceptance state may be represented as information, but Work itself is not exhausted by that representation.

### 2.5 Actors cannot be reduced to information about Actors

The completed Actors vertical preserves distinctions among bearer identity, role, participation, capability, authority/obligation, delegation, attribution/accountability and collective/nonhuman agency.

Representations about an Actor may be Information-ground objects/problems, but Actor identity/authority/participation cannot be collapsed into those representations without loss.

## 3. Strong predecessor hypothesis, still challengeable

The adequacy stage proposed:

> Information / Representation State is a broader top-level problem region; Records / Persistent Information is a specialised persistence branch.

That predecessor finding is supported by multiple traditions distinguishing actual world state from observations/models/beliefs and by the Records scope boundary.

However it explicitly left open:

- whether the whole parent can be reduced to relations/properties of Environment, Work and Actors;
- whether the parent becomes an undifferentiated `all information` residual bucket;
- whether Records nests cleanly;
- whether the current parent label is correct;
- whether observation, belief/model, context, evidence and persistence are children, roles or cross-cutting dimensions.

N0 therefore retains the parent as an **accepted working research hypothesis**, not as the result of this pass.

## 4. Newer technical evidence — useful but non-authoritative for the Foundation tree

### AI Knowledge Strategies

Recent technical research repeatedly exposed:

- source authority;
- provenance;
- freshness / temporal validity;
- epistemic status;
- access/permission;
- verification;
- update/invalidation/deletion

as problems not automatically solved by RAG, Knowledge Graph, CAG, Long Context or another mechanism.

This supplies contemporary failure cases for Information research, not a semantic decomposition.

### Context / Memory / Harness Engineering

Recent research distinguishes:

- current model-visible working-set formation (Context Engineering);
- cross-time memory lifecycle;
- model-external runtime/control environment.

Memory read becomes context, while persistent memory does not become authoritative merely by being remembered.

Again, these are engineering problem/failure-surface distinctions, not candidate Foundation children by default.

## 5. Neighbour-boundary consequence

The active Information node has a usable provisional boundary test:

- **Environment** asks about the actual condition/state/dynamics and Work conditions themselves.
- **Information** is candidate territory for informational access to, representation of, assertion about, derivation from and selective view of subjects/referents, including representations about Environment, Work and Actors.
- **Work** remains the pursuit/execution/coordination/evaluation phenomenon, not merely its description.
- **Actors** remain bearers/roles/participation/capability/authority/agency phenomena, not merely descriptions of them.

This does not prove that Information must remain a top-level independent ground. It only makes the reduction test precise enough for N1.

## 6. Decision-critical tensions entering N1/N2

### T1 — Representation vs information

`Information` may be too broad because facts or structures can be called information without a distinct representation object/state. `Representation` may be too narrow if signals/data or relational informational states matter before a conventional representation is formed.

### T2 — Content/state vs relation

Observation, evidence, context and authority are heavily relational. The parent must not reify every relation as a standalone information object merely to preserve an Information ground.

### T3 — Epistemic status vs informational content

Claim, belief, hypothesis, evidence and knowledge may share content while differing in source, support, holder, confidence or standing. It is unresolved whether those differences define first-order branches or typed relations/status dimensions.

### T4 — Context as child vs view relation

Current working context is likely selected relative to Work/Actor purpose. It may therefore be a cross-cutting view/selection relation rather than a stable child.

### T5 — Persistence as child vs lifecycle condition

Records is clearly a specialised problem region, but persistence may be a lifecycle condition crossing several representation types rather than the parent’s clean immediate child.

No internal contradiction currently forces reopening Environment, Work, Actors or Records.
