# Information / Representation — Deferred Relations

**Status:** bounded ledger after top-level closure; relation capture only, not composition research or execution queue  
**Date:** 2026-09-04

These cross-ground relations were encountered while defining the Information boundary. They remain deferred because systematic typed relation/control composition is a later phase.

## Environment ↔ Information

- actual state / event / condition ↔ observation or representation about it;
- measurement/observation may be partial, delayed, noisy or wrong while actual reality remains distinct;
- freshness/as-of binds representation to temporal claims about reality;
- forecasts/models can change without corresponding actual-state change and vice versa;
- evidence that a condition existed is not the same thing as the realised condition itself.

## Work ↔ Information

- Work may consume, produce, transform, select or preserve representations;
- goal/decision/commitment semantics must not be reduced to information merely because they can be represented;
- current context is often selected relative to current Work;
- Work execution/acceptance can diverge from its status/dashboard representation;
- information transformation can be an Information dynamic while the deliberately controlled pursuit belongs to Work.

## Actors ↔ Information

- Actors may observe, assert, believe, infer, hold, share, authorize or dispute representations;
- Actor-local belief/model state and represented informational content must remain distinguishable;
- source, holder, transmitter, principal and authorizer can differ;
- authority over an assertion/source is not factual truth;
- shared/system-level information need not reduce to one Actor's private state.

## Information ↔ Records / persistence

The completed top-level pass refines this boundary:

> Records / Persistent Information is currently a **deep persistence specialization/profile across Information**, not a clean universal immediate peer child.

Deferred composition questions include:

- how persistence obligations attach across Informational State and Informational Dynamics without duplication;
- when mere persistence becomes managed memory lifecycle or recordhood;
- when derived views/context/provenance states gain independent Records obligations;
- how retention/disposition authority and Work/Actor controls bind to Records semantics.

Persistence still introduces additional identity, preservation, history, retrieval and disposition obligations; it does not establish authority, truth or evidential sufficiency.

## Evidence / claim / decision / authority relations

Later relation/control research must preserve at least the non-collapse pattern:

```text
representation exists
!= representation is true
!= representation is evidence for target claim
!= source is authoritative for role
!= evidence is sufficient for decision
!= decision is authorised/committed
```

The Information pass does not define the final typed relation model for these distinctions.

## Information ↔ context / memory / technical mechanisms

- retrieval, graph representation, context selection and memory lifecycle are mechanisms/engineering concerns acting on informational states/dynamics;
- eligible source state, context-formation process and resulting working context are distinct failure surfaces;
- persistence, managed memory and current model-visible context are not synonyms;
- mechanism classifications from candidate studies must not be imported as Foundation hierarchy.
