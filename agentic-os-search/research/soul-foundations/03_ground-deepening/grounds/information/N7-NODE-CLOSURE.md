## Child 1 — Informational State / Representational Constitution

First-order protected question:

> What informational state/result/representation exists, what is it about, what content/form/identity does it carry, and what makes its interpretation materially distinct from another informational state?

Primary inherited problem regions:

- referent / subject / scope;
- content / form / interpretation / assertion semantics;
- informational identity / granularity / equivalence.

Important dimensions/relations may include source, perspective, confidence, epistemic standing, freshness, conflict, accessibility and persistence status, but these are **not accepted children** merely because they recur.

## Child 2 — Informational Dynamics / Formation–Transformation

First-order protected question:

> How do informational states become available, get generated, selected, transformed, revised, communicated, invalidated or otherwise change?

Primary inherited problem regions:

- observation / acquisition formation;
- derivation / transformation / lineage;
- selection / view / context formation.

Dynamic aspects also include correction, retraction, belief/standing revision, conflict handling, retrieval/exposure and persistence lifecycle operations.

---

# 4. Why these two are children rather than generic dimensions

The split survived the child-promotion test because:

1. the two branches ask distinct first-order questions;
2. they expose distinct failure families;
3. collapsing them repeatedly confuses procedure with result and derivation/change with current state;
4. materially different external traditions independently preserve analogous distinctions;
5. later SOUL/Development OS reasoning can materially depend on whether a failure lies in the informational state or in its formation/change process.

External convergence includes:

- metrology: measurement process vs measurement result;
- OGC/ISO observation models: observation procedure/act vs result;
- W3C PROV: entity vs activity/generation/derivation;
- belief revision: belief state/base vs operations of change;
- database provenance: output vs source/transformation lineage.

This is structural convergence, not vocabulary import.

# 8. Boundary result

The node remains distinct from neighbouring grounds:

```text
Environment → actual realised conditions / operating reality
Work        → pursuit/commitment/execution/evaluation phenomena
Actors      → bearer/role/capability/authority/participation phenomena
Information → mediated informational states + their formation/change dynamics
```

Boundary anti-collapse rules:

- actual condition ≠ observation/model/record;
- Work status representation ≠ actual Work execution/acceptance;
- Actor identity/authority ≠ information about that Actor/authority;
- source authority ≠ truth;
- message delivery ≠ shared understanding;
- persistence correctness ≠ factual correctness;
- context visibility ≠ source existence.
