# Ground Deepening — Research Plan

## Objective

Bring each accepted top-level research ground to sufficient internal scope/depth for later SOUL and Development OS synthesis, while reusing completed research and avoiding premature cross-ground composition or architecture.

Accepted working grounds:

```text
Environment / Operating Reality
Information / Representation State
Work
Actors / Agency–Participation–Authority
```

## Core planning principles

### 1. Do not force one shared layer count

The project should standardize the *quality gates* applied to each ground, not the number or names of its semantic layers.

A ground may close after a short coverage audit if prior research already supplies the needed depth. Another may require multiple new first-principles passes and several internal subregions.

### 2. One active ground at a time

At most **one ground may be under substantive deepening research at any time**.

When a ground is active:
- the other three remain inactive;
- they may be referenced only when needed for boundary discrimination, counterexamples or relation capture;
- cross-ground observations are recorded rather than pursued by opening another ground in parallel;
- a switch to another ground requires an explicit transition decision.

This rule exists to prevent the research from becoming a mixed synthesis before each ground has its own sufficiently clear semantics.

## Common quality gates for every ground

These are gates, not semantic layers.

### G0 — Evidence recovery
Recover all decision-relevant existing project evidence before opening new search.

Output:
- current evidence inventory;
- historical evidence that is valid but non-authoritative;
- known corrections/falsifiers;
- unresolved ground-local questions.

### G1 — Level and boundary test
Ask whether the accepted top-level ground is itself coherent at the intended level.

Test:
- what belongs to the problem ground;
- what must remain in another ground;
- whether the ground is a residual bucket;
- whether a known subregion is incorrectly promoted/demoted;
- whether the same concrete thing can participate under several analytical roles without breaking the ground boundary.

### G2 — Ground-specific decomposition
Derive the internal research map from evidence rather than imposing a shared taxonomy.

Output:
- first-order subproblem families;
- candidate parent/child relations;
- explicit anti-collapse distinctions;
- open rival decompositions.

### G3 — Selective deepening
Research only the branches that are materially under-supported.

A branch deserves a new pass when at least one is true:
- a load-bearing decision depends on it;
- it exposes a distinct failure mode;
- current evidence only names it but does not explain it;
- competing frames produce materially different implications;
- it is needed to distinguish the ground from another ground.

### G4 — Cross-ground relation capture
Record relations encountered during ground research, but do not solve the full composition model.

Examples:
- world ↔ representation;
- Work ↔ target/effect;
- Actor ↔ authority/participation;
- Work ↔ support dependency;
- representation ↔ provenance/evidence;
- Actor ↔ environment access/actionability.

Only relations required to define the local ground boundary may become load-bearing in the ground synthesis.

G4 never authorizes a second simultaneous ground pass.

### G5 — Adversarial completion check
Attack the current decomposition with:
- missing-region probes;
- reduction attempts;
- rival decompositions;
- materially different domain cases;
- source/view recovery for load-bearing claims.

Same-model review must be labeled as such and not presented as independent verification.

### G6 — Ground closure and user evaluation
A ground closes only when:
- its top-level boundary is usable and non-residual;
- material first-order subregions are identified;
- known deep prior corpora are correctly integrated;
- additional broad search has declining information value;
- residual questions are explicit;
- cross-ground deferrals are recoverable;
- the user evaluates the result.

# Ground A — Environment / Operating Reality

## Starting evidence

Reuse:
- `01_verticals/work-environment/` — deep Technical Work Environment evidence;
- `history/superseded/operating-world-environment/` — valid historical operating-world evidence, not current authority;
- `02_adequacy/findings/08-environment-parent-hierarchy.md`;
- `02_adequacy/findings/09-environment-parent-boundary-leak-check.md`;
- `02_adequacy/findings/10-agent-control-counterframe.md`;
- hierarchy source ledger and refinement audit.

## Main unresolved research problem

The parent `Environment / Operating Reality` is currently an evidence-backed working hierarchy, but its parent semantics and branch completeness have not been independently researched to the same depth as Technical Work Environment.

## Initial questions to test

- What makes a condition part of the Environment ground rather than Work, Information or Actors?
- Is `Operating World / Work Domain` the right target/domain branch boundary?
- Is `Work-Support Environment` broad enough and bounded enough?
- Does nontechnical support form part of the support branch or force a different split?
- How should system-of-interest and boundary selection work across nested systems?
- Which states/dynamics can vary independently of Work and representations?
- How do target/domain conditions and support conditions interact without collapsing?
- What distinguishes actual conditions from observation/representation of them?
- What makes a support condition load-bearing rather than merely ambient?
- When does the same concrete system switch target/support roles across different Work?

## Expected research intensity

**High.**

Environment likely requires genuine new parent-level research rather than only a coverage audit.

## Environment closure criterion

Do not close merely because both existing environment corpora can be placed under one parent. Close only if the parent remains non-residual under adversarial cases and its major lower branches are sufficiently explained to guide later composition research.

# Ground B — Information / Representation State

## Starting evidence

Reuse:
- `01_verticals/records-artifacts/` — deep persistent-information evidence;
- `02_adequacy/findings/05-information-representation-boundary.md`;
- internal SOUL/Development OS context evidence concerning context, belief, evidence, state and continuity;
- external sources already recovered in adequacy where load-bearing.

## Main unresolved research problem

The project has deep Records research but not a comparably deep first-principles account of the broader Information / Representation ground.

## Initial questions to test

- What counts as information/representation for this research purpose?
- How should actual world state be separated from observation, data, claim, belief/model and inference?
- What is the relation among signal, observation, representation, assertion, model, knowledge and evidence?
- What is transient/selective context, and is it a representation state or merely a relation/view?
- How should uncertainty, conflict, confidence, source and perspective be preserved?
- What makes information shared/system-level versus Actor-local?
- What is the derivation/inference/provenance structure before persistence?
- When does information acquire persistence obligations and become a Records problem?
- Are all persistent representations Records? If not, what distinguishes recordhood?
- Can Information remain bounded without swallowing goals, Work state, Actor mental state or the underlying world?

## Expected research intensity

**High.**

Information likely requires a substantial new foundation pass above the existing Records branch.

## Information closure criterion

The parent should explain nonpersistent and persistent representation states without collapsing world state into representation or treating every mental/social/operational fact as Information merely because it can be described.

# Ground C — Work

## Starting evidence

Reuse the completed `01_verticals/work/` corpus in full progressive-retrieval form.

It already covers purpose/outcome, units/levels, selection/commitment, situated execution, control regimes, dependency/coordination, multidimensional state, completion/verification/acceptance, failure/recovery and bounded/continuing Work.

## Main unresolved research problem

Determine whether that corpus is already sufficient for Work **as one of the accepted four top-level grounds**, rather than assuming that an older independent research closure automatically settles the new hierarchy.

## First action: coverage/gap audit

Test whether the existing Work corpus can cleanly support:
- Work targeting/affecting Environment without absorbing Environment state;
- Work consuming/producing Information without reducing Work to records/process traces;
- Actor participation/authority without reducing Work to assignment;
- support dependencies without treating them as Work-local state;
- goals/outcomes without creating a separate unexplained goal ground;
- multi-scale Work across Development OS and general-purpose SOUL cases.

## Reopen rule

Only open new substantive Work research if a material Work-local dimension is missing. Cross-ground relation questions alone are not a reason to reopen Work.

## Expected research intensity

**Low to medium initially.**

Most likely result is selective correction/extension, not a full restart.

# Ground D — Actors / Agency–Participation–Authority

## Starting evidence

Reuse the completed `01_verticals/actors/` corpus in full progressive-retrieval form.

It already covers bearer identity, role/position, participation, capability/competence, authority/obligation, delegation/representation, attribution/accountability, omission and collective/nonhuman agency.

## Main unresolved research problem

Determine whether that corpus is sufficient for Actors **as one of the accepted four top-level grounds** under the broader Environment/Information hierarchy.

## First action: coverage/gap audit

Test whether the existing Actors corpus cleanly handles:
- Actor state versus Information/representation about an Actor;
- capability versus situated actionability under Environment conditions;
- authority/obligation versus cross-ground control relations;
- participation in Work without collapsing Actor into role/task assignment;
- human, organizational and nonhuman agency across SOUL domains;
- delegation/representation when information and authority diverge;
- collective identity and responsibility across changing Work/environment conditions.

## Reopen rule

Only open new substantive Actors research if a material Actors-local dimension is missing. Relation/control taxonomy questions alone are deferred to composition.

## Expected research intensity

**Low to medium initially.**

Most likely result is a gap-confirmation pass plus targeted deepening if needed.

# Recommended order

## Recommendation

1. **Environment**
2. **Information**
3. **Work**
4. **Actors**

## Why

Environment and Information contain the clearest **top-level level-alignment gaps** discovered by `02_adequacy`. They therefore have the highest expected information gain and the highest chance of changing the accepted hierarchy.

Work and Actors already have deep top-level research and should be tested after the newly broadened grounds are better understood; otherwise their coverage audit may depend on unstable boundaries.

## Order is not a common-template assumption

This sequence does not imply equal research depth or a dependency that forbids revisiting earlier grounds. Material falsifiers can reopen adequacy at any point.

Each numbered ground is researched in a separate active pass; the sequence never means parallel research.

# Stage exit condition

The ground-deepening stage is complete only after all four accepted top-level grounds have been individually evaluated under this program and any material hierarchy falsifier has been handled.

At that point:

1. rerun top-level adequacy against the deeper four grounds;
2. decide whether systematic typed relation/control/composition research should begin;
3. do **not** jump directly to architecture unless that later gate explicitly supports it.
