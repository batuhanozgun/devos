## Agreed meaning

Foundation is the current, revisable research basis for asking and reasoning about the informational, actor, environmental, work and cross-ground needs of a target working system. It is not the sole knowledge source or a ready-made architecture. Later Development OS and SOUL work combines applicable Foundation findings with actual goals/constraints, selectively recovered studies, explicitly provisional explorations, additional specialist inquiry when needed and evidence from the target environment. Requirements and mechanisms are selected and tested for that target; no branch-to-component or term-to-file mapping follows automatically.

Foundation is not an empty checklist: its substantive distinctions, counterexamples, results and limits can directly support or constrain a decision. Neither Foundation nor the current candidate library contains all knowledge required for unknown future implementations.

## Qualifications retained from the explanation

**Structural coverage is not engineering sufficiency.** A concern having a route in the four-ground/Composition frame does not supply its actual target requirements, mechanism, feasibility or realized outcome. The frame remains bounded and reopenable, not a theorem about every conceivable structure.

**The grounds are not four independent boxes.** Binding, standing, control, effects, causation and feedback/adaptation protect material interactions. Individually satisfactory grounds do not automatically establish a valid composed result.

**Technology-noncommitted is not technology-blind.** Existing products or AI terms should not dictate the problem before it is understood. Technical evidence can nevertheless change a requirement, reveal a new option or expose a Foundation omission. Reuse is feedback-capable, not a one-way lookup.

**Specialization is not exhaustive form filling.** Determine what applies, what it requires here and what remains inapplicable or unresolved. Do not create a component/file/requirement for every leaf or place the full research history in every context.

**Actors are not synonymous with AI agents.** Performing and non-performing parties, owners, recipients and accountability roles may matter. Needed responsibility and legitimate authority precede mechanism or agent-count choices. An AI agent also uses information, performs work and operates under environmental conditions.

**Research-to-design is not a dictionary.** An instruction file can be a candidate means of conveying a role or task constraint; its existence does not establish real capability, current authority, actual loading or enforcement. No AGENTS.md, RAG, graph, memory store or agent topology was selected by this discussion.

**Studies and explorations have different standing.** Studies supply source-grounded evidence and mechanism knowledge at their actual scope. Explorations preserve conceptual trials and provisional models that need not be empirically verified. Both can be useful; neither is automatically adopted, and neither closes the space of future inquiry.

## Completion and later use

Foundation completion concerns whether a useful research basis has been developed, not whether all AI engineering or domain knowledge has been collected. A later target-specific process may clarify needs, recover relevant evidence, compare methods including not adding a mechanism, make authorized decisions, validate the target and revise the requirements or research. This is an explanatory account, not an installed universal workflow.
