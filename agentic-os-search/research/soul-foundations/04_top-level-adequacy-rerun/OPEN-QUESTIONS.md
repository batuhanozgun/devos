## Residual adequacy questions

1. **Relation/control sufficiency:** can later composition express all load-bearing typed relations and derived standings without exposing a fifth semantic locus?
2. **Goal/value directedness:** can purpose, outcome, criteria, value and acceptance be composed cleanly across Work, Environment, Information and Actors?
3. **Governance/control semantics:** can normative authority, policy representation, governance Work and realized enforcement remain separated while still forming a usable control model?
4. **Effective/transitive authority:** can reachable technical effect power, delegated authority, environment reachability and accountability be composed without creating a new Actor/control ground?
5. **Actors naming:** does `Actors / Agency–Participation–Authority` remain the best label after non-performing party/standing relations are systematically composed, or should `Standing`/`Parties` become more explicit in the name?
6. **Environment parent stability:** do Domain Reality and Work Conditions remain defensible children under one top-level realized-situational ground across later domains and composition models?
7. **Information parent stability:** do State/Dynamics plus persistence specialization remain coherent once observation, evidence, context, authority and Records are composed with other grounds?
8. **Work outcome anchoring:** can Work-directed outcome semantics remain a relation/composition family without requiring a separate Goal/Outcome ground?
9. **Time/evolution:** can continuity, feedback and evolution remain transition patterns over the four grounds rather than forming a separate top-level problem region?
10. **Risk/value/economics:** do future domains require a stable peer ground, or do these remain criteria/relations distributed across Actors, Work, Information and Environment?
11. **Non-human/collective agency:** can later composition preserve dynamic agent-team identity, authority and accountability without breaking Actors or introducing a new top-level locus?
12. **Independent replication:** would a materially independent second researcher/model produce the same top-level result or identify a missing/rival ground?

## Explicit reopen triggers

Reopen top-level adequacy if:

- systematic composition cannot express a recurring relation without inventing a stable fifth semantic locus;
- a later domain repeatedly forces semantic boundary theft between two grounds;
- Goal/Value, Governance/Control, Parties/Stakeholders, Actionability or Evolution becomes stable as an independent peer across materially different domains;
- Environment's parent semantics become residual/vacuous or fail to contain Domain Reality + Work Conditions without loss;
- Information's State/Dynamics/persistence account breaks under relation composition;
- Actors' umbrella treatment of non-performing parties/standing becomes incoherent;
- Work cannot preserve purpose/outcome without a missing ground;
- a materially stronger rival decomposition survives future tests;
- independent review identifies a first-order omission;
- a load-bearing accepted ground result is invalidated.

## Non-reopen conditions

Do **not** reopen top-level adequacy merely because:

- a domain has more role names, lifecycle states or policy categories;
- a relation taxonomy becomes large;
- architecture needs additional modules/components;
- a database/ontology representation uses more than four entity classes;
- a domain uses a different vocabulary such as activity, stakeholder, resource or context;
- an implementation mechanism spans several grounds.

The top-level frame is a research/problem map, not a component or entity-count constraint.
