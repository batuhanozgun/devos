## 3. What changed from the 2026-09-02 adequacy result

The **ground count did not change**, but the evidence basis and interpretation became substantially stronger.

### Environment is no longer a provisional parent sketch

It is now recursively researched into Domain Reality and Work Conditions, with deeper State/Structure/Condition/Dynamics distinctions and Technical Work Environment retained as a deep support profile.

### Information is no longer merely a proposed broadening above Records

It is now independently researched as Information / Representation with State and Dynamics, including Formation/Acquisition, Derivation/Transformation and Selection/Exposure; Records remains a deep persistence specialization/profile.

### Work was re-audited under the current boundaries

It remains a distinct pursuit-organization/evolution ground and cannot be reduced to Actor participation, Information traces or Environment state.

### Actors was re-audited under the current boundaries

It remains a distinct bearer/standing/participation/authority/attribution ground and now explicitly separates legitimate authority from technical effect power and includes semantically relevant non-performing parties/positions.

## 6. Q3 — boundary-interaction result

The strongest pairwise and multi-ground cases did **not** expose a missing locus.

They instead reinforced these separations:

```text
actual reality != representation
Work state != representation of Work
Actor standing != representation of standing
Actor capability != Environment actionability
Work admission != Actor obligation/authority
Work completion != Environment outcome
technical effect power != legitimate authority
verification evidence != authorized acceptance
```

Important composition cases remain:

- Actor ↔ Work assignment/participation/obligation/authority;
- Actor ↔ Environment capability/access/actionability/effective power;
- Actor ↔ Information identity/standing evidence/epistemic authority;
- Work ↔ Environment target/effect/readiness;
- Work ↔ Information plan/status/evidence/context;
- Information ↔ Environment observation/referent/freshness;
- verification/validation/acceptance;
- effective/transitive authority;

These are next-phase relation/control questions, not unresolved ground omissions.
