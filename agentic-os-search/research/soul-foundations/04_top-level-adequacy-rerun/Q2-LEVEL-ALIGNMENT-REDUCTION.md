# Top-Level Adequacy Rerun — Q2 Level-Alignment / Reduction Test

**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Date:** 2026-09-05

## 1. Question

Even if no fifth ground is missing, are the current four at the wrong analytical level?

Attack each ground by trying to:

- reduce it into the other three;
- merge it with a neighbor;
- split it into peer top-level grounds;
- demote it into relation/control fabric.

The test concerns **problem-ground level alignment**, not entity containment or architecture modules.

## 2. Environment / Operating Reality

### Reduction attack

Candidate reduction:

> Environment is only the collection of states/capabilities relevant to Actors and Work and therefore does not need an independent top-level ground.

### Why reduction fails

Actual realized reality can vary while Work, Actors and their informational representations remain fixed.

Examples already established by the accepted ground:

- actual target/domain condition can change without a Work-state change;
- actual support/resource/interface condition can change without Actor capability changing;
- the same representation can remain stale while reality changes;
- Work can complete while the intended Environment effect fails to materialize.

Environment therefore protects an irreducible question:

> What actual realized domain and Work-condition reality obtains, independently of how it is represented, pursued or attributed?

### Split attack — Domain Reality and Work Conditions as peer grounds

This was the major earlier hierarchy challenge.

The accepted Environment deepening now provides a stronger result than the 2026-09-02 parent hypothesis:

- `Domain Reality` and `Work Conditions` remain semantically distinct;
- both are realized, boundary/reference-relative conditions that materially shape focal Work;
- both can role-switch as focal Work changes;
- neither requires Environment to own Actor/Work/Information semantics;
- Technical Work Environment remains a deep support profile rather than a top-level peer.

Returning them to peer top-level status would restore level asymmetry rather than add explanatory power.

### Merge attack — Environment + Work

Fails because actual condition/effect is not pursuit/execution state.

```text
Work complete != target Environment condition achieved
Environment ready != Work admitted/active
```

### Q2 Environment verdict

**RETAIN at top level.**

No current split/merge/reduction is stronger than the accepted parent + explicit lower distinctions.

## 3. Information / Representation

### Reduction attack

Candidate reduction:

> Information is merely description of Environment, Work and Actors; the three referent grounds are enough.

### Why reduction fails

Representations have their own identity, content, form, scope, provenance, uncertainty, currentness and transition history and can diverge from the represented referent.

The following cases are impossible to explain by referent facts alone:

- two conflicting representations of the same reality;
- a stale but persistent representation after reality changes;
- a false claim that never corresponded to actual reality;
- a derived summary/model with its own lineage;
- a selected context that exposes only part of an available source universe;
- an informational state about hypothetical/non-realized Work or future conditions.

Information therefore protects an irreducible question:

> What mediated informational state exists, what does it represent, and how is it formed, derived or exposed independently of the represented reality?

### Split attack — Informational State and Dynamics as peer top-level grounds

Fails level alignment.

State and Dynamics are two immediate problem regions **inside informational mediation**. Dynamics forms/transforms/exposes informational states. Elevating them beside Environment/Work/Actors would split one ground by state/process form while leaving the others unsplit despite equivalent state/dynamic distinctions.

### Demote Records to separate peer

Rejected by current research. Persistence adds strong specialized obligations but crosses State and Dynamics rather than forming a clean sibling partition.

### Merge attack — Information + Environment

Fails the core referent/representation distinction.

### Merge attack — Information + Work

Fails because information transformation can occur without deliberate Work, and Work can occur without its representation being accurate or current.

### Q2 Information verdict

**RETAIN at top level.**

## 4. Work

### Reduction attack

Candidate reduction:

> Work is only Actors acting in Environment while consuming/producing Information.

### Strong counterexample structure

Hold fixed:

- the same Actors and authority configuration;
- the same Environment conditions/resources;
- the same informational source set.

Different Work can still be:

- selected or rejected;
- admitted or not admitted;
- decomposed differently;
- coordinated under different dependencies;
- executed under different methods/control regimes;
- paused, recovered or terminated;
- judged complete at different boundaries;
- continuing versus bounded.

Those differences are not recoverable from Actor, Environment or Information state alone.

Work protects an irreducible question:

> Which pursuits toward outcomes are being selected, structured, admitted, enacted, coordinated, evaluated, adapted, concluded or recovered over time?

### Merge attack — Work + Actors

Fails because participation/assignment is not Work identity and Work admission is not Actor obligation/authorization.

### Merge attack — Work + Environment

Fails because pursuit/execution is not realized target/support condition.

### Merge attack — Work + Information

Fails because plan/status/evidence representation is not actual Work state.

### Split attack

The Work audit already attacked intuitive top-level/internal decompositions:

- Plan / Execute / Evaluate;
- Candidate / Active / Closed;
- Bounded / Continuing;
- Purpose / Coordination / Execution / Evaluation / Recovery.

No stable universal immediate-child split survived without false disjointness.

### Q2 Work verdict

**RETAIN at top level.**

## 5. Actors / Agency–Participation–Authority

### Reduction attack

Candidate reduction:

> Actor identity is Information, capability is Environment, and participation is Work; therefore Actors is residual.

### Why reduction fails

Hold fixed:

- the same Work;
- the same Environment;
- the same identity/role/authority representations.

Actual Actor-side standing can still change:

- authority can be revoked;
- a different bearer can occupy the same office;
- obligation can attach or expire;
- delegation/principal relation can change;
- an accountability forum can change;
- the same operational action can be institutionally attributed differently.

Representations about these facts are Information, but the represented standing is not.

Environment capability/access also cannot establish legitimate authority, and Work participation cannot establish role/obligation/accountability.

Actors protects an irreducible question:

> Which bearers/positions/parties are implicated and how are identity, standing, participation, authority/obligation, representation/delegation and attribution/responsibility distributed across them?

### Naming attack

`Actors` alone is too easy to read as active performers. The accepted paired label and ground formulation already correct this by including non-performing relevant parties/positions.

A naming refinement remains possible, but naming pressure is not reduction evidence.

### Split attack

The Actors audit tested:

- Standing / Participation;
- Individual / Collective / Non-human;
- Operational Agency / Normative Standing;
- Bearer & Identity / Authority & Representation / Participation & Agency / Attribution & Responsibility.

All lost cross-cutting relations such as delegation, omission, accountability and collective agency.

### Q2 Actors verdict

**RETAIN at top level.**

## 6. Pairwise merge matrix

| Proposed merge | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Environment + Information | referent/representation divergence disappears |
| Environment + Work | actual condition/effect collapses into pursuit/execution |
| Environment + Actors | realized affordance/access collapses into bearer capability/authority |
| Information + Work | plan/status/evidence collapses into actual pursuit state |
| Information + Actors | representation of identity/standing collapses into actual standing |
| Work + Actors | pursuit/admission collapses into participation/obligation/authority |

No pairwise merge reduces complexity without destroying a load-bearing non-collapse distinction already supported by multiple cases.

## 7. Top-level split matrix

### Environment → Domain Reality + Work Conditions peers

Not preferred. Deepening supports distinct children under a common realized situational substrate and avoids earlier level asymmetry.

### Information → State + Dynamics peers

Not preferred. They are internal informational problem forms and depend on the parent semantics.

### Work → lifecycle/function peers

Not preferred. Tested decompositions are overlapping projections rather than stable sibling populations.

### Actors → standing/agency/normative peers

Not preferred. Delegation, omission, collective agency and accountability cut across the splits.

## 8. Cross-ground level symmetry

The four grounds now answer comparably broad but distinct questions:

```text
Environment → what actual realized reality/conditions obtain?
Information → what mediated informational states/representations obtain and change?
Work        → what pursuits are organized/evolving toward outcomes?
Actors      → which bearers/positions/parties have what participation/standing/authority/attribution?
```

This is stronger level alignment than the original independent vertical labels and the interim five-peer map.

The grounds need not have equal child depth. Equal abstraction does not require equal tree shape.

## 9. Q2 verdict

> **All four accepted grounds survive reduction, merge and top-level split attacks at the current Foundation depth.**

No ground is currently shown to be a residual bucket, duplicate, specialized peer at the wrong level, or safely reducible to the other three.

Q2 therefore retains the four-ground count and current level placement.

Open pressure moves to Q3:

> Do the actual interactions among the four grounds expose hidden boundary theft or a missing locus when multiple grounds participate at once?
