## 1. Result

No new child or fourth Dynamics peer is proposed by this review. Their conjunction, however, must preserve a shared target/use/consumer contract rather than combine unrelated positive verdicts.

This discharges that named research debt. It does not itself close every evidence limitation or declare whole Foundation complete. COMPLETION-DEPTH separates those claims and recommends a final Foundation-wide sufficiency/reuse assessment as the next bounded responsibility, subject to this review's evaluation and explicit continuation.

## 2. Two useful answers can fail to answer the combined question

[S5] separated which view serves an informational use from whether specified information is available to a consumer. [U5] refined the first relation; [E5] refined the second. Neither should be weakened to a single Boolean label attached permanently to a document.

Let A be the set of views adequate for a fixed declared use, and E the set available under a fixed consumer, mode and horizon. Separate facts `A is nonempty` and `E is nonempty` do not establish a view belonging to both. A complete current report can be adequate but unavailable, while an accessible obsolete report fails the current inquiry. J1 enumerates all sixteen pairs of masks over two candidate views: seven pairs have a common witness; two satisfy the separate-existence shortcut without a common witness.

This is elementary conjunction/witness reasoning, not a new general theorem. Its contribution here is to make the accepted child contracts' integration obligation explicit and test a named false inference. Even a common witness establishes only the scoped feasibility claim supplied to J1. It does not prove that an actor actually received, interpreted or used the view. An on-demand contract can legitimately end at an executable opportunity; a current invocation-content contract instead needs evidence at that endpoint.

Qualifications must also agree. A verdict for a historical reconstruction cannot silently support today's decision. Evidence for an account cannot silently certify an invocation. A permitted locator is not automatically the requested underlying content. A positive local result can remain correct while the proposed combined use fails because it changes one of these bindings.

## 3. The relevant view belongs to a consumer and an actual or permitted history

J2 assigns one bit to each of two consumers while the stipulated output is their XOR. Neither consumer's observation alone supports the output across the four possible worlds. A genuinely pooled pair does. The system-level union of possessed information is therefore not automatically the evidence of either consumer. This does not prohibit distributed computation: an authorized communication/computation protocol could create a sufficient local signal or joint outcome. Such a protocol and its realization were not supplied by the fixture and cannot be inferred from the existence of the parts.

The same boundary applies to contingent selection. A policy's possible-path union need not be any one execution's observations. [U5] already distinguishes fixed bundles, contingent strategies and changing needs; [E5] distinguishes time-respecting opportunity from an observation-feasible strategy. This review brings those constraints together rather than inventing a mandatory delivery sequence. Costs, deadlines, known capabilities and legitimate sharing conditions remain part of the claim when material.

J4 sharpens the other direction: a newly supplied bit is inadequate alone but adequate together with the consumer's already available complementary bit. The relevant informational input can include established side information. Do not require every new handoff to reproduce everything the recipient already has; equally, do not assume unobserved model knowledge, a forgotten earlier session or another worker's private state as that side information. J2 and J4 share a deliberately simple fixture and are not independent evidence families.

## 4. Correct composition does not require identical bytes everywhere

J3 applies U5's finite acceptable-output criterion to different consumer-visible partitions. For a supplied finite world class, output space and use contract B(w), a deterministic view-only valid output exists exactly when each observation cell has a nonempty intersection of acceptable outputs. Necessity follows because indistinguishable worlds receive the same output; sufficiency follows by choosing a common allowed output for each cell.

The program compares that criterion with exhaustive enumeration of binary output functions for fifteen partitions and eighty-one nonempty-output specifications per partition. All 1215 comparisons agree. A differently labelled parity representation still preserves the stipulated XOR use; constant content does not. These are supplied finite semantics, not an oracle for natural-language adequacy or actual cognition. J3 does not prove that arbitrary summaries preserve arbitrary tasks.

[Re-expression R6] already distinguishes exact recovery, intended meaning and actual use. A suitable substitute can therefore satisfy the combined use without reproducing every byte of an initially selected object. But a request for an exact quotation, a source identity, a rationale or an audit witness may impose stronger preservation requirements. The substitution is qualified by the protected use, not licensed merely because an encoding is reversible or a decoder exists somewhere.

The horizon matters as well. [Revision M5] shows why preserved present answers need not preserve a continuing state's future-update behavior. A current-answer view must not be silently promoted to a faithful replacement for future revisable knowledge. The present tests do not rerun that earlier result or supply a generic memory-compression mechanism.

## 5. Identity, time and current standing remain separate

The targeted [W3C source] describes resource states used to recover a temporally and representationally appropriate target. It also warns that request headers alone may not determine the representation returned. This provides source pressure for the endpoint-binding question; it does not prescribe Web Annotation for SOUL.

The targeted [PostgreSQL source] distinguishes per-command and transaction-relative snapshots, including own-write visibility and remaining isolation limitations. Our inference is narrower than a database recommendation: individually legitimate reads can require an additional joint-consistency argument for a particular use. Neither the database nor its concurrency behavior was executed here.

These sources support inspecting the actual observation contract instead of assuming address equality, individually current fragments or a successful read imply the intended joint view. They do not impose a universal newest-version or globally simultaneous-snapshot requirement. A historical view may be exactly right for its use; a task requiring current authority has a different obligation.

The candidate memory assessment adds an analytical historical-approval/current-authority example. The RPD assessment adds agent-relative prerequisites and compatibility of shared conditions. They contribute questions and countercases, not independent validation of our hierarchy, verified source media, adopted behavior or target implementation. SOURCE-LEDGER preserves their retrieval and original-source limitations.

## 6. Why this does not absorb neighboring research

The integrated judgment needs several distinct answers without owning them all. Formation concerns the source-to-occurrence relation; Re-expression concerns protected meaning; Warrant concerns the support actually offered; Revision concerns continuing state and reliance; Exposure concerns consumer-relative availability. Work, Actors and Control preserve purpose, authority, responsibility and actual activity. Records preserves future-use/disposition obligations; Propagation preserves the scope of realized change and its evidence.

A faithfully captured false assertion, a faithful but inadequate summary, a sufficient premise set with an invalid inference, a valid correction not present in the consumer's view, and a suitable available view that the actor ignores remain different failures. This review establishes neither actual source truth nor actual actor benefit. It does not require all these checks for every task; which distinctions are material depends on the task and warranted claim.

The join failure is not evidence for a third universal Selection child called Integration or Verification. It is a compatibility obligation among existing protected relations. RIVAL-AND-BOUNDARY-REVIEW retains the strongest objection: a rich single information-to-use relation could express the same factors. The present preference is diagnostic and organizational, not a proof of unique taxonomy or measured implementation superiority.

## 7. Five exact proposed refinements

| ID | Overextension prevented | Proposed integrated refinement | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | An adequate view exists and some view is available, so the use is supported | Require a common, compatibly qualified witness or permitted strategy for the combined claim. Separate feasibility from actual endpoint realization. | S5/U5/E5; J1 |
| I2 | All pieces are somewhere in the system or somewhere in a policy's possible paths | Evaluate the designated consumer's actual or warranted obtainable observations/history. Do not pool consumers, branches, sessions or times without the corresponding conditions and evidence. | U5.5; E5.2–E5.3; J2 |
| I3 | A fresh item has intrinsic adequacy, or every correct handoff must copy the selected bytes | Evaluate the use-relevant view together with established side information and supported interpretation. Permit justified substitutions while retaining exactness and future-state preservation when the use requires them. | U5.1–U5.2; R6; M5.4; J3/J4 |
| I4 | Successful current reads or an updated source establish a coherent, currently authorized consumer basis | Bind material representation, joint compatibility, reference/effective time, consumer scope and reliance/authority separately. A historical success need not be wrong, but does not automatically govern a changed use. | S5.4; E5.4; M5.5–M5.7; P1/P2; candidate cases |
| I5 | Research accepted at a parent or named frontier closed means all knowledge and performance are validated | Carry separate status for dedicated research, descendant depth, acceptance, publication, target evidence and independent verification. Close the specific missing-pass debt without laundering unresolved limits. | original B2/B4; current child STATEs; COMPLETION-DEPTH |

These are proposed integration refinements of accepted research. Many constituent distinctions were already discovered; this review's contribution is their joint qualification, targeted countercases, explicit debt reconciliation and next-work decision. It is not a claim of five wholly new scientific discoveries or five installed controls.
