# Rival structures and boundary audit

**Date:** 2026-09-16. Same-assistant comparative assessment; not independent verification.
**Question:** do the completed Selection children deliver the distinct diagnostic duties that justified their promotion, or should the organization change?

## 1. Strongest rival: one rich information-to-use leaf

The rival can bind use, candidate set, recipient, time, representation, available history and evidence in one qualified relation. It can express the same common-witness test. It avoids duplicated context and an extra hierarchy level. None of J1–J4 refutes this representation, and the tests must not be presented as a taxonomy oracle.

The positive reason to retain the accepted pair is narrower. Adequacy can be investigated without a transfer event: a fully accessible corpus can still lack the required exception, alternative support or explanatory distinction. Availability can change without a new adequacy decision: a previously qualified document can become inaccessible, remain in an old consumer view, or become available through a different supported mode. A remedy to one does not establish the other. The dedicated U and E arguments retained those questions rather than replacing them with a generic proposal/execution distinction.

The common-witness requirement strengthens the seam; it does not erase the separate relations being joined. At this research scale explicit duties are still useful. The cost is repeated qualification and possible verbose navigation. Reopen if a genuinely tested single-facet organization retains the same discoverability with materially less loss/duplication, or if the separated routes repeatedly cause consumers to miss their shared contract. Do not defend the hierarchy merely because files already exist.

## 2. Strongest child rivals are not disqualified by overlap

For U, criterion discovery/specification can fail even when selection satisfies the chosen criterion. For E, access opportunity and actual presentation can fail independently. Distinct remedies are real. But the accepted detailed comparisons preserve these as explicit facets of their respective qualified relations, without making every use perform both kinds of endpoint or splitting generic specification/verification out of every node.

J4 does not promote a new Context child: side information changes the adequacy relation and actual exposure boundary, while its constitution, interpretation and persistence retain existing questions. J3 does not prove that a repairing decoder is the actual consumer's semantics. Each successful finite case has stipulated meanings and observations. A recurring hidden first-order criterion-discovery or access-provisioning inquiry would still justify reopening the present leaf decisions.

## 3. Integration or verification as a third child

There is a genuine joint duty: independently successful results must match the same use, target, consumer and horizon. Could that itself be a new first-order Selection region?

At this scope the new countercases are explained by incompatibility of existing relations' arguments, scope or evidence. No additional information-local outcome survives after those are made explicit. Binding, Standing and Work already preserve relational identification, qualification and verification responsibilities. Adding a universal Integration child here would risk duplicating those responsibilities rather than resolving a missing inquiry.

This conclusion is conditional. Reopen if a repeated case has an independent protected informational outcome that remains unrepresented even with the qualified join, rather than merely a failed match or an unperformed evaluation. The present reasoning is not a prohibition against later specialist integration research.

## 4. Dynamics-level placement versus within-node depth

Original Dynamics N6 declined to make Selection and Exposure separate immediate Dynamics peers. Dedicated S4 later investigated the inside of the existing Selection region and promoted its two protected questions. These are different levels of decomposition; neither result implies the other was an unperformed task or a contradiction. Similarly, retaining Revision under Derivation at the Dynamics level is compatible with the later dedicated Revision child inside Derivation.

However, depth must not become an excuse for arbitrary placement. The test remains diagnostic loss: does the parent protect the child's common question, and are relevant failures recoverable without stealing neighboring meaning? U4/E4, T6, R6 and M5 provide positive boundaries at the present depth. No fresh result makes Revision a fourth Dynamics peer or requires moving Exposure into Formation.

## 5. Communication and distributed work

A communication episode can create a receiver occurrence, change presentation, preserve or distort meaning, support or fail a claim, alter reliance and affect coordination. That complexity is a serious challenge to a byte-delivery model. The E4 communication analysis already preserved understanding and shared assurance as stronger claims than presence.

J2 adds a narrow distributed-information boundary. Two consumers' observations can jointly distinguish a target while neither consumer can do so alone. This does not show that all recipients must receive all raw data, nor that no distributed protocol can solve the problem. A sufficient permitted message or joint procedure may change the available observation. Its possibility, authorization, realization and efficacy are separate evidence needs.

No new universal Communication child is forced by the examined case: it spans the existing source/transfer/exposure, Work and Actor relations. Reopen if a stable communication-local outcome cannot be recovered through those routes without annexing cognition into Exposure or making actor understanding equivalent to transport success.

## 7. Verdict, limitations and reversal

Retain the accepted research tree; propose I1–I5 in SYNTHESIS as integration refinements. The reviewed later evidence makes qualification and actual consumer scope more explicit rather than exposing another universal immediate region. The five false rules have counterexamples, but none of that independently validates our organizational judgment.

The strongest unresolved risk is that a rich contract becomes too easy to summarize badly. Current question-to-evidence routes should expose the separate duty and shared conditions, not merely show green statuses. A later Foundation-wide review should test this with integrated cases beyond Information, not re-run the same four fixtures and call it independent confirmation. User acceptance, same-author tests and fresh reading of two primary pages do not remove shared framing or source limitations.
