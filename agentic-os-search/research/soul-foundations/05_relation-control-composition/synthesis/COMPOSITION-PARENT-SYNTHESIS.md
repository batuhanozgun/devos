# Cross-Ground Composition — Parent Synthesis

**Status:** bounded synthesis / awaiting user evaluation  
**Date:** 2026-09-05

## Executive conclusion

After the four Foundation grounds were accepted and post-deepening adequacy survived, systematic composition was researched as a parent problem rather than as a flat relation list.

Strongest current structure:

```text
Cross-Ground Composition
├─ Relational Binding
├─ Composite Standing / Qualification
├─ Control / Constraint
└─ Transition / Effect / Feedback
```

These are diagnostic research regions, not mutually exclusive entity/event classes and not architecture modules.

## Why the parent is necessary

Recurring SOUL/Development OS questions depend on several grounds simultaneously:
- goal/outcome attainment;
- verification/validation/acceptance;
- actionability/effective power;
- governance/policy/enforcement;
- provenance/attribution/accountability;
- target/effect/causation;
- coordination/dependency/support;
- feedback/learning/evolution.

No one ground can own these without stealing Environment, Information, Work or Actors semantics.

## Why pairwise relations are insufficient

All six ground pairs expose real bindings, but high-value results such as `actionable`, `verified`, `accepted`, `goal attained` or `authorized for this transition` require additional criteria/anchors. Pairwise-only models either hide those anchors in attributes/application logic or project composite status onto one endpoint.

## Four immediate regions

### Relational Binding
Typed connection among accepted-ground anchors with explicit role, direction, scope/reference and time.

### Composite Standing / Qualification
Criterion-/target-/use-/authority-/time-relative standing composed from multiple anchors/relations.

### Control / Constraint
Semantics of allowed/required/blocked/gated/escalated transitions, preserving policy, authority, decision, obligation, enforcement and compliance differences.

### Transition / Effect / Feedback
Temporal/change semantics across grounds and composite standings, including effects, observation formation, propagation, causation pressure and feedback loops.

## Cross-cutting families rather than children

Not promoted as peers:
- provenance/evidence/attribution;
- causation;
- goal/outcome/value;
- verification/validation/acceptance;
- governance/policy;
- actionability/effective power;
- dependency/coordination;
- risk/value/priority;
- time/continuity.

They remain mandatory patterns/dimensions/specializations across the four regions.

## External pressure

W3C PROV, NIST ABAC and NASA systems verification/validation were used as materially different authoritative holdouts. Selected Anthropic, multi-agent and context/memory/harness studies provided current operational falsifiers.

They did not validate the exact project taxonomy, but none forced a fifth composition region or stronger organizing frame.

## Rival result

Pairwise-only, flat graph, generic State/Relation/Transition, descriptive-vs-normative, semantic-family, control-loop-centered and goal/commitment-centered frames all lost material distinctions or introduced weaker research stop rules.

## Sequencing result

If accepted, **Relational Binding should be researched first** because later standing/control/transition semantics depend on precise anchors and role/binding distinctions.

Do not pre-commit the remaining child order. Re-select after Binding closes.

## Verification boundary

C7 is same-model adversarial review. The exact four-region synthesis has not received independent second-researcher/model replication, and no external standard validates the project's exact decomposition.

## Closure recommendation

Accept the parent composition structure for current Foundation sequencing, then open Relational Binding as a separate child pass. Do not begin architecture, ontology/data model or implementation.
