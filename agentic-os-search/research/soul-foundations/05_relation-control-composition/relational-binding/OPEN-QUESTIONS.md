# Relational Binding — Open Questions / Reopen Triggers

**Status:** bounded residuals after child closure  
**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Role:** residual uncertainty; NOT an automatic execution queue

## Current bounded result

Relational Binding is a bounded leaf at current Foundation depth with no promoted immediate child.

## Residual questions

1. **Binding-label precision:** is `Relational Binding` the best durable label, or would `Semantic Binding / Association` better distinguish this region from generic graph relations?
2. **Exact-anchor equivalence:** when can two versions/copies/projections be treated as equivalent for a binding without importing domain-specific identity rules?
3. **Reference-frame inheritance:** when may scope/reference frame safely be inherited from enclosing Work/context rather than repeated on each binding?
4. **Temporal alignment:** what universal minimum is needed to preserve effective time, reference/as-of time and relation validity without turning Binding into event/history modeling?
5. **N-ary threshold:** what decision rule should later formalization use for when binary decomposition remains semantically safe versus when one qualified relation instance must be preserved?
6. **Inference profile:** which relation families require explicit direct/indirect, inverse, symmetry or transitivity rules, and how should those be validated without making Foundation an ontology language?
7. **Actual-vs-asserted binding:** what evidence standards later Composite Standing should require to move from `representation says binding B` to `binding B is sufficiently established for decision D`?
8. **Provenance boundary:** how should lineage relations remain Binding profiles while generation/invalidation/derivation events are handled by Transition?
9. **Participation boundary:** how should assignment, delegation and accountability bindings connect to Control and Actor standing without duplicating those semantics?
10. **Dependency boundary:** when does `depends-on` remain a direct Binding versus become a derived standing over resource availability/path/method?
11. **Causal bindings:** when should `contributes-to` / `caused-by` remain a descriptive resulting relation versus require dedicated Transition/causal analysis?
12. **Profile completeness:** do materially different domains expose a stable relation-family profile not currently represented by Reference, Participation, Dependency, Provenance, Situation/Access or Traceability?
13. **Independent replication:** would an independent researcher/model retain bounded-leaf status or promote one of the family profiles as a child?

## Explicit reopen triggers

Reopen Relational Binding if:
- a relation-family profile develops a stable protected question/failure family across domains without mainly duplicating ground semantics;
- qualified/n-ary cases cannot be handled by the shared signature without a distinct problem region;
- Binding repeatedly needs a residual `other relation` bucket that the signature cannot diagnose;
- later Composite Standing/Control/Transition research requires a missing Binding distinction;
- a materially different domain breaks exact-anchor/typed-position/applicability grammar;
- independent review identifies a stronger decomposition;
- an upstream accepted ground changes in a way that invalidates load-bearing Binding assumptions.

## Non-reopen conditions

Do not reopen merely because:
- a domain defines many relation names;
- a later ontology/database uses explicit relation objects;
- RDF/graph representation uses reification, RDF-star, edge properties or n-ary nodes;
- a domain needs fine-grained role vocabularies;
- one profile becomes large in a specific application;
- implementation needs indexes, constraints or relation tables.
