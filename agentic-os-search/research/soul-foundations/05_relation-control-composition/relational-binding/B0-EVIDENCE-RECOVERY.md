## 5. Initial minimum-semantic-signature hypothesis

A decision-usable binding may require more than `relation-type + endpoint A + endpoint B`.

Candidate signature dimensions:

### Likely universal/core
- **anchor identity / exact target** — which things are actually linked;
- **anchor role/position** — what each anchor is doing in this binding;
- **relation semantics/type** — what connection claim is being made;
- **scope/reference frame** — relative to what domain, Work, claim, use or analytical boundary the binding applies.

### Likely conditionally load-bearing
- direction/order / inverse role;
- arity/cardinality/participation multiplicity;
- effective time / validity interval / as-of;
- qualifier/context/operation/method/stage;
- version/identity constraints on anchors.

### Boundary-sensitive rather than automatically Binding-local
- whether the binding claim is asserted/inferred/planned/hypothetical versus actual;
- provenance/evidence supporting the relation claim;
- normative validity/authorization;
- resulting composite standing;
- transition/change that established or removed the binding.

B1 must determine which of these belong inside Binding semantics versus neighbouring composition children or Information/Transition.

## 6. Live falsifiers for B1–B7

### F1 — `type + A + B` is sufficient

If all additional dimensions can be safely represented outside the relation without changing semantic identity, the proposed signature is overbuilt.

### F2 — role and direction are redundant

If inverse relation naming or endpoint ordering always recovers role semantics without loss, role may be presentation rather than Foundation structure.

### F3 — scope/reference frame is not relation-local

If scope always belongs solely to anchors/Information/Work, Binding need not carry it.

### F4 — time is not relation-local

If temporal validity can always be derived from endpoint state/history without ambiguity, time is only cross-cutting metadata.

### F5 — qualified/n-ary bindings are modeling artifacts

If any multi-anchor case can be decomposed into binary bindings without semantic loss, arity/qualification need not be first-order Binding structure.

### F6 — semantic families form stable children

Reference, participation, dependency, provenance or another family may have distinct protected questions/failure modes sufficient for promotion.

### F7 — no family forms stable children

All families may instead share one binding grammar and overlap too strongly; Binding would then be a bounded leaf with internal signature/family maps.

### F8 — actual binding vs represented binding is mishandled

If child research silently treats an asserted edge/record as the real relation, Source–View Fidelity is broken.

### F9 — Binding absorbs Standing, Control or Transition

If `authorized`, `verified`, `actionable`, `blocked`, `caused`, or `changed` are treated as simple binding types without preserving their additional semantics, the child boundary is wrong.

### F10 — upstream falsifier

If correct binding semantics require a stable semantic locus outside the accepted grounds/composition parent, reopen minimum upstream research.
