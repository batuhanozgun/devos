## Pre-external falsifiers and decision criteria

- A representation-only or fixed-rule boundary excludes a genuine retained response change.
- A persistence criterion classifies every fleeting state change as adaptation or demands literal eternity.
- A test removes the alleged carrier/mediator and calls the failure absence of adaptation.
- Change at all future inputs is demanded although only a relevant subset/distribution changes.
- Fixed code is treated as proof of no learning or changed parameters as proof of learning.
- Local/actor-instance change is generalized across sessions, membership, routes or versions.
- Failure of expression is treated as erased acquisition, or historical acquisition as current retention.
- Repeated training/usage is mistaken for retention without declaring ongoing support.
- Causal history, achieved benefit, transfer and authorization are conflated.
- A proposed child protects an independently recurring question/failure family that one grammar cannot preserve without systematic loss.
- Evolution or ground-local dynamics exposes a scope error, or intelligibility requires an adopted implementation.
