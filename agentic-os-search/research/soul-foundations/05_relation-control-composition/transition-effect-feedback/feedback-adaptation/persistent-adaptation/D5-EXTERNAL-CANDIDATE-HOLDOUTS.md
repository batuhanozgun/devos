## Negative and limiting source pressure

The outside evidence is not uniformly 'learning works'. It also points toward dependence on evaluator quality, interference, latent-state ambiguity, measurement reactivity, decay/transfer limits and unsupported extrapolation. These pressures changed the initial shorthand rather than being dismissed to protect the parent.

Optional NASA, biological and team-memory searches were not treated as fully recovered sources. Broad claims requiring their unobserved full methods were removed from the closure target. The Evolution result is a bounded cross-ground scope judgment, not a general reduction of evolutionary science.
