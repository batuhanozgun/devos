## 2. Strongest findings

- Fixed code/weights can coexist with changed effective response through a retained carrier; changed parameters can also be behaviorally inert.
- Storage, acquisition, current retention, access/expression, transfer, benefit and authority are not interchangeable.
- A latent changed disposition can be evidenced before natural recurrence, but neither archival existence nor self-report proves it.
- Persistence is qualified by bearer, opportunity class, horizon, lifecycle boundary and ongoing support; it does not mean eternity.
- A test that removes the carrier asks a different question from supported continuity; a test that trains the target can contaminate pre-test attribution.
- Same visible response can mask changed distribution, a rare-case difference or a changed response to future experience.
- Multi-actor/path realization and membership continuity require exact scope rather than local-to-global promotion.

These are bounded original semantic conclusions under the documented primary pressure and constructions. A source may support a component distinction without proving this organizational choice.

## 3. Exact proposed parent wording delta

## 4. Why the parent survives

Returned Influence asks whether realized-condition information returned into an operative later response process at a specified claim level. Persistent Adaptation asks whether relevant history changed and retained the later response disposition. Feedback can operate while that disposition remains unchanged; a retained change can persist after an earlier episode without continuous recurrence.

The questions are distinct and non-coextensive, not necessarily logically independent historical predicates or disjoint event classes. This preserves the already accepted Returned Influence correction.

## 5. Why no immediate child

Acquisition/Retention/Expression and Formation/Continuity are the strongest rivals. They preserve important failure locations, but at current depth those locations remain qualified aspects of the same history-linked response claim. Their generic forms are already addressed by Information, Transition, Control, causal inquiry and Standing. D4 records the cost and reopen conditions rather than dismissing the distinctions as mere stages.
