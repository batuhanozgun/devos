# Persistent Adaptation — D4 Rival Decompositions

**Status:** COMPLETE / proposed bounded-leaf decision  
**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Method authority:** Composition PLAN; diagnostic questions, not architecture modules.

## 1. Decision standard

A useful distinction is not automatically an immediate child. Promotion requires an independently recurring protected question and failure family whose treatment as an internal facet causes material loss, while preserving accepted ground/sibling boundaries and avoiding residual buckets.

Neither a neat three-stage vocabulary nor a single compact grammar proves the right structure. Each candidate is evaluated for what would be lost if it were not promoted. Children need not be mutually exclusive; overlap alone is not grounds for rejection.

## 2. Strongest rival: Acquisition / Retention / Expression

This rival has real force. Acquisition failure differs from forgetting; a retained disposition can fail to be expressed; the three can require different evidence. A hierarchy would make these distinctions easy to find and assign separate research attention.

However, the exact adaptation claim binds all three to the same history, bearer, response class and horizon. Unqualified acquisition is only a transition; retention of arbitrary bytes belongs to persistence semantics; expression of an unchanged disposition is ordinary response. Their adaptation-specific meaning comes from the common history-linked future-response relation.

At the present Foundation depth, a three-child tree would distribute the same identity/comparator/applicability core across children and risk mistaking claim stages for three different populations. The alternative used here is not omission: D2 and FINDINGS-MAP explicitly provide separate acquisition, retention, access/expression and evidence routes.

**Decision:** do not promote now. **Cost:** the leaf is richer than a slogan and requires a maintained facet map. **Reopen:** if future domain work repeatedly needs an independent retention or expression inquiry that cannot be recovered from this grammar without systematic duplication or loss.

## 3. Formation / Continuity maintenance

A second strong rival separates how history creates change from how a changed regime survives time, replacement and interference. Formation can succeed while continuity fails. This is a genuine diagnostic separation.

But continuity here is always continuity of the specified history-linked response disposition. Generic preservation, propagation and current qualification already have homes in Information, Transition and Standing. Creating a universal separate child now would need a sharper adaptation-specific question beyond those intersections. D2/D3 preserve maintenance/support and lifecycle failure explicitly without asserting they are unimportant.

**Decision:** internal temporal/realization facets at this depth; not rejected forever.

## 5. Collapse rivals

### Into Information

The carrier may be a representation, but availability or persistence of that representation does not establish operative response change. A broader system can adapt without changing the particular document being inspected. Information is necessary in many cases, not an exhaustive replacement.

### Into Work

An improvement task can finish while the later response path remains unchanged. Conversely, a relevant experience can produce retained change without a formally registered improvement task. Work completion is not the occurrence criterion.

### Into Transition / Propagation

Transition establishes what changed where and how it propagated. Adaptation additionally binds that change to experience and retained conditional response. A behaviorally equivalent refactor and a history-responsive policy can have similar local change traces but different adaptation claims.

### Into causal research

Causal methods help establish the history relation or benefits, but do not replace the adaptation object's identity: what disposition, bearer, horizon and response opportunity are under inquiry? A generic causal graph without those meanings loses the protected question.

### Into Control

Some retained changes govern behavior; others concern estimation, representation use or skill. Control explains applicable governing semantics, not by itself how experience changed and retained the response organization.

## 6. Proposed result

```
Persistent Adaptation / Future-Response Change
    = one bounded history-linked retained-response-disposition inquiry
    = acquisition / retention / access-expression / scope / causal-evidence facets
    = no immediate child promoted at current depth
```

The shared grammar remains meaningful without choosing storage, controller, model, schema or orchestration architecture. It preserves actual failure locations in the findings map. That is a bounded method-based preference, not proof that every future research organization must use one leaf.

## 7. Nontrivial revisions rather than defensive closure

The investigation does not simply defend the parent's wording. It corrects fixed code versus effective regime, strengthens latent and trajectory comparisons, qualifies support-relative persistence, and rejects universal Evolution reduction. These corrections are part of the proposed result, not grounds to pretend no revision was needed.

No currently demonstrated falsifier requires reopening a ground, causal leaf or Composition parent. The narrow parent delta and remaining hard questions are made explicit in D6 and OPEN-QUESTIONS.
