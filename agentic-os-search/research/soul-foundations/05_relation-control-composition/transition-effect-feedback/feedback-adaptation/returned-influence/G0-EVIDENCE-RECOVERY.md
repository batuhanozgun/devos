## Accepted boundaries recovered

- Binding: exact source, target, semantic role, time/version and scope must stay connected to the same relation instance. A reported connection is not automatically an actual one.
- Information: signal production, transformation, provenance and exposure do not by themselves establish operative response influence.
- Work: the evaluation/response pursuit and its completion do not establish the intended environmental effect.
- Actors/Control: source credibility, decision authority and technical capability are not interchangeable.
- Transition: decision, attempted action, realized change and its propagation remain separate.
- Actual Causation: define the exact effect of interest; preserve joint/redundant contributors and competing explanations. A single deletion test is not universal.
- Contrastive Effect/Standing: intervention benefit and adequacy of its evidence are distinct from the existence of feedback.
- Adaptation: a fixed response rule can operate on feedback without being changed by history.
