## 1. Judgment

Returned Influence remains a coherent diagnostic research leaf. Its internal distinctions are deeper than the parent's short description, but no new immediate child is justified at the current Foundation depth.

The most important result is not a new name. It is that **operative feedback does not require a visible action change after every input**, and that the existence of a return path, active use, exact episode contribution and achieved external effect are different claims.

## 2. What the question now means

For a bounded system or continuing pursuit, information about realized condition/performance returns into a relevant response process. The inquiry establishes what actually happened at the claim level of interest: was the path present, was coupling active, did this signal or configuration contribute to a response, and did the downstream effect occur?

The system/pursuit boundary is not optional when omitting it would turn every downstream information dependency into 'feedback'. A new requirement, an external reference and a performance readback can each influence Work while playing different semantic roles.

Neither a shared actor nor continuous recurrence is necessary. A later human/team/session can consume an earlier result. But a single return episode does not prove the continuing reliability of the whole loop.

## 3. The no-change counterexample

Consider a controller that uses a current reading and chooses to hold because the condition is within its applicable tolerance. The signal was not ignored merely because no mutation occurred. Conversely, a disconnected controller can show the same hold output.

Therefore:

```text
unchanged output != absence of feedback
unchanged output != proof of feedback use
```

Evidence must discriminate active rule use from blindness, override or coincidence. Primary autoscaling and organizational-assurance sources supplied pressure; seven constructed checks in G7 demonstrate limited logical counterexamples. No real controller or agent was deployed.

For SOUL, the prospective implication is important: a critique should not force revision just to display responsiveness. Preserving a conclusion, stopping an action or seeking further information can be the appropriate response. Whether it is appropriate requires evidence and governing criteria, not a preference for either change or consistency.

## 4. Four claim scopes

| Claim scope | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Structural | A relevant feedback path/coupling is present |
| Operative | Returned information enters the applicable response process here |
| Episode-specific | A particular signal/configuration contributes to a specified response |
| End-to-end | Response is realized and affects the claimed outcome |

These are not four children. They make it possible to say exactly what a diagram, delivery receipt, execution trace or causal experiment supports.

A loop can be operative without every sample being individually decisive. The accepted Actual Causation research supplies joint/redundant-contribution semantics when an episode-level causal claim is needed.

## 5. The partial-return case

An observation can inform a decision even if permission, execution or propagation prevents the resulting action. An action can execute while the intended target remains unchanged. These are not interchangeable failures.

```text
signal produced/delivered/read
!= operative use established
!= response executed
!= target effect achieved
```

Name the response locus: triage, state estimation, decision, inhibition, scheduling, action or another material process output. Pure archival storage does not become operative feedback merely because a record changed.

Likewise, rejecting an irrelevant signal can be an informed triage response without being evidence that the signal influenced a protected operational decision. Exact scope prevents both false positives and false negatives.

## 6. Minimum grammar

```text
Returned-influence inquiry
├─ bounded system/process/continuing pursuit
├─ realized source condition/performance/event/artifact
├─ source-bound signal or assessment
├─ relevant return path and response consumer
├─ applicability: role, target, time, version, context
├─ explicit claim scope
├─ operative response locus and mode
├─ downstream realization boundary when an effect is claimed
└─ evidence, serious alternatives and uncertainty
```

Optional details become required when omitting them changes the claim. The grammar does not select a graph model, schema, controller, message bus, agent role or verification tool.

A scalar error, numerical goal, fixed setpoint, literal intervention and persistent rule update are not universal requirements. Physical feedback examples remain ground-local when no cross-ground semantic question is being asked.

## 7. Timing and multiple loops

Occurrence time, arrival time and use time may differ. A late old observation can arrive after a new one and reverse the apparent current condition. Several consumers can act on a signal differently; earlier responses can return as delayed observations that trigger additional responses.

These are applicability, topology and dynamic-effect issues. They do not currently require a new child. They do require restraint in claims of currentness, convergence or end-to-end closure.

## 8. Feedback truth, authority and evidence lineage

A false or spoofed message may genuinely affect behavior. That does not establish the reality it reports, valid source standing or permission to make a change.

A claim repeated through several derivative summaries may also change behavior without acquiring independent corroboration. Further reasoning over existing evidence can be valuable, but its value should be credited to the discriminating analysis rather than fabricated source independence.

Thus an operational causal loop can be real while its evidential support remains circular. Internal agreement is not a substitute for contact with the relevant operating reality.

Historical same-model refinement research supplies both positive and negative pressure under different tasks and evaluation conditions. It does not justify a universal conclusion about all self-feedback or current model capability.

## 9. Coverage is not the number of handled messages

```text
all received signals handled
!= all observed signals delivered
!= all material outcomes observed
```

An improvement in local or reported metrics can arise from shifted load, changed reporting or excluded outcomes. Neither message-handling success nor internal consistency settles broader user-goal success.

This is a framing guardrail within the leaf. Information Selection/Exposure and source acquisition remain relevant; the causal truth of the underlying condition is not replaced by what entered context.

## 10. Why no immediate children

G4 tested channel/uptake/response, structural/operative/episode, corrective/confirmatory/inhibitory, positive/negative/stability, local/network, human/automated/internal/external, evidence-assurance and observation/evaluation/learning splits.

Each either specialized the common return relation, introduced claim/evidence levels, duplicated accepted ground semantics or imported the separate Adaptation question. Different failure locations remain visible in the findings map; a leaf is not a license to omit them.

An alternative research organization is possible. The bounded claim is that the current project method does not yet justify further immediate child promotion without adding duplication or losing the common relation.

## 11. Parent correction and epistemic status

The two-child Feedback/Adaptation structure remains accepted and supported. The child proposes narrow current-wording corrections:

1. Replace visible-change shorthand with operative returned influence, allowing hold/continuation.
2. State the return reference explicitly.
3. Separate decision-side influence from downstream execution/effect.
4. Describe feedback and adaptation as distinct, non-coextensive questions rather than claiming that all their historical truth conditions are logically independent.

These refinements are part of the present result awaiting user evaluation. Original F0–F7 are retained as historical pass artifacts, not silently rewritten to conceal the development of the model.
