## What actually changed because of source pressure

The source work did not merely confirm the parent. It forced more precise language about feedback that maintains an output and about the difference between an operating loop and one causally decisive message.

A second refinement is the return reference: information must be related to the system/pursuit's condition or performance and relevant response, not merely occur earlier in a chain. These refinements appear in G1/G2 and the current parent delta.

## Lexical counter-test

Everyday or disciplinary speech may call a teacher's comment, survey answer or generated critique 'feedback' before uptake. The Foundation distinction must not pretend to invalidate that usage. 'Feedback signal supplied' and 'operative returned influence established' are different claims we need to track.

The decision to require operative-use evidence for the latter is this project's operational distinction, not a universal dictionary claim. Abstract-only educational/management searches were not used to manufacture full-text authority for it.
