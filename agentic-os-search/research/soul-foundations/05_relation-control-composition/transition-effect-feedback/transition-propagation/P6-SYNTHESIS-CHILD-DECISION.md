## 5. Propagation episode grammar

A high-value structural result is that propagation often consists of **multiple exact transition instances** rather than one monolithic transition:

```text
Transition A
  --typed dependency/propagation relation-->
Transition B
  --typed relation-->
Transition C
```

A higher-level propagation episode can group these transitions for one protected question.

This preserves:
- different local owners;
- different effective times;
- partial failure;
- path-specific semantics;
- heterogeneous downstream outcomes.

No graph/event architecture is selected.

## 6. Expected/applicable scope versus realized scope

When material, preserve:

```text
applicable / intended propagation scope
!= realized propagation scope
```

The applicable scope can derive from Binding/Control/Work/criteria. Transition owns where the relevant change actually became effective.

A completeness claim such as `fully propagated` is normally a Composite Standing over those transition facts and the applicable-scope criterion.
