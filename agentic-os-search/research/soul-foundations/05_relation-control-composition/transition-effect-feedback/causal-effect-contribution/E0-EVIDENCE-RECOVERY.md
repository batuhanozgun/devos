# Cross-Ground Causal Effect / Contribution — E0 Evidence Recovery

**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## 1. Objective

Freeze the current causal problem before constructing a Foundation synthesis.

The active child must answer a narrower and harder question than chronology, attribution, governance or responsibility:

> Given an exact realized cross-ground effect, what actually contributed causally to that effect, at what depth, under what evidence, and with what unresolved alternatives?

The pass must not assume:
- one universal root-cause method;
- one root cause per event;
- one causal vocabulary shared by every domain;
- that temporal order or association establishes causation;
- that a contributing Actor is therefore responsible/accountable;
- that a present/complied-with Control was therefore effective;
- or that every useful causal claim requires a fully identified counterfactual statistical estimand.

## 2. Accepted prerequisite — exact effect first

Accepted `Cross-Ground Transition / Propagation` now supplies the prerequisite causal target:

```text
exact realized transition / effect
+ exact scope / time / affected focus
+ propagation episode where relevant
```

This eliminates a major causal-analysis error:

```text
trying to explain an effect whose identity/scope is still ambiguous
```

Accepted prerequisite rule:

```text
transition/dependency sequence
!= causal proof
```

A causal claim must therefore bind to an exact realized effect, not merely to a requested, intended, recorded or later-observed event.

## 3. Accepted sibling/ground boundaries

### Relational Binding
Owns exact anchors, typed positions and applicability relations.

Causal Effect may consume Binding but must not turn every causal relation into generic connectivity.

### Composite Standing
Owns qualifications such as whether evidence is sufficient, a control is effective enough under a criterion, or a causal conclusion is sufficiently supported for a decision.

Therefore:

```text
causal contribution exists
!= evidence sufficient to establish it
!= causal claim accepted for a decision
```

### Control / Constraint
Owns governing semantics.

Accepted non-collapse:

```text
actual compliance
!= control effectiveness
```

NIST's current definition of control effectiveness explicitly asks whether a control contributes to risk reduction. This is causal pressure: presence/implementation/compliance alone is insufficient.

Causal Effect therefore owns the question:

> Did the control materially change the probability/trajectory/severity of the protected effect?

while Standing owns whether the evidence justifies a current effectiveness qualification.

### Actors
Actors owns authority, attribution, responsibility and accountability semantics.

Required boundary:

```text
Actor causal contribution
!= Actor responsibility
!= Actor accountability
!= blame
```

A person/tool/agent may be causally involved without being normatively responsible; conversely a responsible/accountable party may have no direct physical causal role in the immediate effect.

## 4. Project causal-depth protocol

The active R06 protocol requires the child to preserve:
- symptom versus cause;
- proximate versus systemic cause;
- trigger versus enabling conditions;
- prevention/detection control failure;
- multiple contributing causes;
- intervention relevance;
- recurrence risk;
- and a bounded stopping rule.

The protocol's strongest Foundation pressure is:

> A correct proximate cause is not necessarily a sufficient causal explanation.

Depth should stop when further causal decomposition no longer materially changes intervention, recurrence risk, control design, responsibility boundary or decision outcome.

## 5. Authoritative external pressure

### NASA mishap investigation

NASA's mishap-investigation material separates:
- events and conditions;
- timelines;
- cause-and-effect relationships;
- barriers and controls;
- Event and Causal Factor Trees;
- proximate/root cause questions.

NASA also explicitly notes that not every investigation finds a root cause and that contributing factors can themselves have root causes.

Foundation pressure:

```text
timeline != causal model
one contributing factor != complete explanation
root-cause existence cannot be forced
```

### FAA anomaly/root-cause guidance

FAA material states that root-cause analysis should eliminate potential causes unsupported by data and warns that stopping at the proximate cause increases recurrence risk.

FAA safety material also recognizes organizational, design, operational, environmental, human and process factors as potentially contributing together.

Foundation pressure:

```text
proximate correction
!= recurrence-risk closure
```

and:

```text
multiple causal-factor families may coexist
```

### NTSB investigative practice

NTSB's public investigative process separates fact gathering from analysis/probable-cause determination, then links findings to safety recommendations.

Historical NTSB causal-factor guidance explicitly permits multiple causes/contributing factors without forcing a primary cause when several actionable cause-effect relations matter.

Foundation pressure:

```text
one accident
!= one mandatory root cause
```

### CDC field epidemiology

CDC explicitly distinguishes statistical association from cause-and-effect and requires consideration of:
- chance;
- selection bias;
- information bias;
- confounding;
- investigator error;
- temporality;
- plausibility;
- consistency;
- other causal criteria.

Foundation pressure:

```text
association != causation
statistical significance != causal proof
```

and:

```text
alternative explanations must remain live until evidence discriminates them
```

### MIT CAST/STPA tradition

MIT's CAST material explicitly critiques “root cause seduction and oversimplification of causality,” hindsight bias, unrealistic human-error models and blame-oriented analysis.

Foundation pressure:

```text
single-root-cause framing can itself be a causal-analysis failure
```

### WHO patient-safety systems view

WHO emphasizes that patient harm commonly has multiple interrelated factors and that individual error should not eclipse system/process conditions.

Foundation pressure:

```text
human action observed
!= sufficient system-level causal explanation
```

### Causal-inference tradition

CDC and modern causal-inference traditions pressure a distinction among:
- association;
- intervention/change in exposure;
- counterfactual contrasts;
- confounding/selection mechanisms;
- causal effects at individual versus population levels.

No single formalism is promoted at Foundation level. The child must be able to consume mechanistic, interventionist, statistical, investigative and counterfactual evidence without declaring any one universal method.

## 7. Frozen live falsifiers

The following claims must be attacked before closure.

### F1 — Generic cause relation is enough

Falsifier test:
- does `A caused B` preserve enough information to distinguish trigger, enabling condition, prevention failure, direct mechanism and systemic contributor?

### F2 — One root cause should exist

Falsifier test:
- do cross-domain cases require multiple sufficient/contributing causes or no justified single root?

### F3 — Proximate / contributing / prevention / detection / systemic should become universal children

Falsifier test:
- are these stable sibling populations or overlapping analytic roles that can apply to the same contributor?

### F4 — Counterfactual reasoning is universally required

Falsifier test:
- can strong causal decisions sometimes be supported by mechanism/intervention/physical constraints or investigative evidence without an explicit counterfactual model?

### F5 — Counterfactual reasoning is optional everywhere

Falsifier test:
- do confounding/selection/competing-explanation cases require a contrast about what would have happened otherwise?

### F6 — Omission/failure-to-prevent is just Control

Falsifier test:
- can absence/failure of an expected barrier be a causal contributor while obligation/authority remains a separate Control/Actors question?

### F7 — Causal contribution implies responsibility

Falsifier test:
- can non-human conditions, infrastructure and innocent Actor actions causally contribute without normative responsibility?

### F8 — Control effectiveness is owned wholly by Control

Falsifier test:
- can a control be correctly specified/enforced/complied-with yet causally irrelevant to the observed outcome?

### F9 — Causal confidence is generic epistemology only

Falsifier test:
- does causal decision-making require preserving competing causal explanations and discriminating evidence beyond ordinary factual uncertainty?

### F10 — Causation should be a fifth composition sibling

Falsifier test:
- can causal analysis remain dependent on exact realized transition/effect identity such that it stays bounded under Transition / Effect / Feedback?

## 8. Initial structural hypothesis — not accepted

The strongest current minimum is not a taxonomy but a causal claim grammar:

```text
Causal Effect / Contribution
├─ exact realized effect being explained
├─ candidate contributor(s)
├─ typed causal role(s) where material
├─ relevant scope/context/time
├─ causal contrast / mechanism / intervention basis where material
├─ evidence basis
├─ competing explanations / confounders where material
├─ confidence / unresolved alternatives
└─ depth sufficient for the decision/intervention at hand
```

Potential analysis roles:
- proximate mechanism;
- enabling/contributing condition;
- prevention failure;
- detection/containment failure;
- systemic/framing condition.

These are probes, not accepted children.
