## Critical routing boundary

Environment already owns accepted ground-local `Criterion-Relative Standing`.

Therefore:

```text
ground-local standing
!= cross-ground Composite Standing
```

A standing belongs here only when multiple accepted-ground semantics are part of what the qualification **means or what constitutes it**.

Cross-ground evidence, assessment Work, reporting or access alone does **not** make a ground-local standing Composite.

Useful counterfactual test:

> If another-ground anchor were removed from the standing's definition while preserving our ability to observe/know the local bearer, would the standing's meaning change?

