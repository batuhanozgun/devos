## Explicit reopen triggers

Reopen Composite Standing if:
- later work repeatedly misroutes ground-local Criterion-Relative Standing into this child;
- the constitutive-vs-evidence boundary cannot be applied without arbitrary case-by-case exceptions;
- a standing profile develops a stable protected question/failure family across domains without mainly duplicating accepted ground or sibling semantics;
- Control research cannot preserve authorization/eligibility/acceptance distinctions without a missing Standing distinction;
- Transition research cannot preserve standing currentness/invalidation boundaries without a missing Standing distinction;
- a materially different domain requires a result/uncertainty/applicability semantic not expressible by the shared grammar;
- an upstream accepted ground changes in a way that invalidates the routing boundary;
- independent review finds a stronger decomposition.

## Non-reopen conditions

Do not reopen merely because:
- a domain defines many statuses or qualification labels;
- a later system stores standings as explicit status objects/fields;
- implementation uses scoring, rules, policy engines, caches or materialized views;
- one profile becomes large in a specific application;
- a standing has a non-boolean result domain;
- evaluation uses multiple evidence sources or methods;
- architecture wants a dedicated assurance/risk/readiness component.
