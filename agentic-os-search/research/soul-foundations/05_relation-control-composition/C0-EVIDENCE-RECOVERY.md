## 6. Initial composition candidates entering C1

These are hypotheses, not children.

### H1 — direct/binding relation semantics

Some semantics may be fundamentally about typed connections among accepted-ground anchors:
- represents;
- targets;
- participates in;
- depends on;
- uses/produces;
- delegates/acts-on-behalf-of;
- observes/measures;
- supports/constrains.

### H2 — derived standing / qualification

Some load-bearing statuses may not belong intrinsically to one ground, e.g.:
- actionable;
- authorized-for-this-transition;
- verified;
- validated/fit-for-use;
- accepted;
- satisfied/attained;
- compliant;
- current/relevant-for-use.

### H3 — control / constraint semantics

Normative or technical structures may govern which transitions are permitted, required, blocked, gated or escalated.

### H4 — transition / effect / feedback semantics

Some composition questions concern change across ground states rather than static relations:
- Work causes/attempts Environment effect;
- observation creates Information;
- decision/delegation changes Actor standing;
- evidence changes Work/decision standing;
- failure feedback creates new Work/control.

### H5 — evidence/provenance/causation may be separate immediate regions

Alternative possibility: provenance/evidence or causation may be too distinct to remain dimensions within H1–H4.

### H6 — pairwise matrix may be sufficient

Attack this directly: perhaps all multi-ground cases can be decomposed losslessly into pairwise relations.

### H7 — composition may reveal a fifth ground

Adequacy remains reopenable if recurring composition requires a stable semantic locus outside the four grounds plus relations/controls/transitions.
