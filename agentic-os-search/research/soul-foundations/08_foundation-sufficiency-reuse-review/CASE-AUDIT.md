# Six integrated analytical case families

**Date:** 2026-09-16. **Class:** predeclared family-level analytical stress cases; same-assistant assessment, not six empirical experiments.

PLAN fixed the six families before fresh external inquiry. The particular contrasts below are constructed during assessment, not preregistered datasets. The argument routes are canonical research, not claims that the examples occurred in SOUL. A named source role is not enough: each case asks what fact or assumption changes the legitimate conclusion. Abbreviations F6/R6/M5/U5/E5/I1–I5 refer to exact routes in SOURCE-LEDGER and REUSE.

## C1 — A faithful evidence chain can support the wrong stronger claim

**Case.** A release dossier contains twelve accurate summaries of one test run. The run checked a specified component/version under a limited input class. A reviewer receives all twelve summaries and a correct success receipt. The proposed conclusion is that the whole deployed system is safe and ready for authorized release.

**Matched contrast.** Keep the twelve summaries and their text unchanged. In one use the question is whether that exact component passed that exact test; in the other it is whether all relevant deployment hazards have been ruled out. The record can answer the first while leaving the second unsupported. Multiplying copies does not increase independent observation count. Adding genuinely independent, relevant evidence could alter the second verdict, but the number of documents alone cannot do that.

**Questions recovered.** Formation F6 separates token/receiver novelty, source episode and independent evidence. U5 separates one witness from wider support/coverage obligations. E5 identifies the consumer endpoint actually established. Warrant's accepted result separates conditional support, applicability and factual establishment. Work and Standing distinguish verification, validation and authorized acceptance. Environment and Propagation retain the actual deployed state/effect.

**Decision consequence.** Preserve the narrow positive result while declining the stronger unsupported claim. The missing work may be coverage/target evidence, not another summary, another copy or a new Foundation Evidence branch. A receipt for the dossier does not show an actor read, understood or accepted it.

**Residual.** Real hazard scope, test sensitivity, independent observations, acceptance authority and actual deployment evidence are target-specific. This example supplies none. No claim that an actual release failed, or that all tests require exhaustive coverage, is made.

**Structural result.** No first-order omission exposed. The distinction changes the admissible claim and next evidence request rather than merely giving the twelve documents an Information label.

## C2 — Accurate optimization does not settle a contested purpose

**Case.** Two communities use a shared service. A plan maximizes total throughput, and its calculations are correct. One community values predictable access at a particular time; some affected people never operate the service. An analyst treats the throughput metric as the universally agreed goal.

**Matched contrast.** Keep the same predicted throughput and plan. In one authorized project, throughput is the accepted criterion with an explicit service floor. In another, equitable access and that time window are binding requirements that the plan violates. The same numerical optimum need not be an acceptable option under both contracts. A further factual correction and a legitimate change of criteria are also different transitions.

**Questions recovered.** Work synthesis §§1,4–8 preserves purpose/method, plural pursuits, adaptive scope and value-relative selection. Actors synthesis §§2,4,7 includes affected non-performers, decision positions and standing responsibility. Information distinguishes a query/criterion representation from the protected use. Binding/Standing/Control connect the actual criterion, authority, target and scope. The source counterframes P1/P3 challenge single-customer or single-center shorthand; they are not acceptance policies for this case.

**Decision consequence.** Expose the conflicting requirements and the authority needed to resolve them. Neither the assistant's preferred allocation nor the operator's technical power settles legitimate priority. A feasible bounded fact-finding step may proceed even while the final value decision is unresolved.

**Residual.** The actual parties, rights, preferences, resource effects and decision procedure need situated inquiry. The example does not establish a universal social-welfare metric, legal rule or correct compromise.

**Structural result.** A Value/Governance fifth-ground proposal remains conceivable, but no new protected object survives here after criteria, consequences, parties, pursuits and governing relations are made explicit. The frame would fail if it forced these parties into one objective or treated an external analyst as omniscient/fully authorized.

## C3 — Available capabilities are not an authorized actionable route

**Case.** Participant A can request work from B. B can change a protected resource. A possesses one needed fragment, B another. A directory describes both capabilities as available, and an approval record exists.

**Matched contrasts.** First, hold capabilities and records fixed but change the approval's scope: it authorizes B's independent task, not A's requested effect. Second, hold the legitimate purpose fixed but change the relevant time/resource conditions: the two necessary opportunities do not overlap in a usable order, or both require the same exhausted resource. Third, information exists across the participants but no permitted working procedure brings the required distinctions to the actual decision point.

**Questions recovered.** Actors' authority/delegation differs from technical effect reach. Environment Work Conditions distinguishes existence, access, capacity and sufficiency. E5 preserves temporal/actionable opportunity and actor observations; I1/I2 preserve a common qualified witness and actual consumer/history scope. Control STATE makes protected-effect coverage different from one monitored or blocked path. Work retains dependencies, method choice and completion.

**Decision consequence.** Diagnose separately an authority gap, a capacity/time gap and a usable-information gap. More permission text will not repair unavailable capacity; more technical reach will not supply legitimate authority. An authorized alternative route may be evaluated, but no permission bypass is implied. The RPD assessment contributes a method-relative prerequisite/agent-relative readiness question, not an installed planner or evidence of actual performance.

**Residual.** Real credentials, alternative paths, noncooperative behavior, scheduling uncertainty and tool effects require specific evidence. No live access-control or distributed protocol was exercised.

**Structural result.** No new universal Capability or Coordination ground required by these contrasts. A valid composed judgment requires compatible grounds/relations rather than individually green entries.

## C4 — Preserving history is not preserving current authority or adaptation

**Case.** A worker resumes after an interruption. Its archive faithfully records an earlier approval and a useful procedure. The new request changes the protected resource, purpose or decision authority. A correction and a lesson are stored in the repository, but the resumed worker's view may still be old.

**Matched contrasts.** For historical reconstruction, the earlier approval is the right evidence of why work proceeded then. For current execution it may be inapplicable. A short view may preserve today's answer while losing state required for future revision. Finally, changing the lesson file does not by itself establish operative feedback or a retained change in future responses; an unchanged output does not by itself establish that no learning or use occurred.

**Questions recovered.** Revision M5 separates admitted input, support withdrawal, reference/effective time, future-update state and current authorization/replay. R6 distinguishes intended semantic preservation from invertibility. U5/I3 qualify current use versus future continuity; E5 distinguishes source updates from retained consumer views. Work preserves suspension/recovery and the unfinished inquiry, while Actors preserves continuing responsibility. The accepted Feedback/Adaptation questions and 06 B1 distinguish return structure, operative use, retained disposition and benefit.

**Decision consequence.** Preserve history without granting it timeless operational authority. Recover or obtain the exact current basis where material; do not delete valid history solely because it no longer governs this task. Measure the claimed behavioral/effect property rather than infer it from a changed file. Carbon Layer's memory assessment supplies a comparable analytical status/time example, not fresh video or agent evidence.

**Residual.** Actual retained state, needed history horizon, source custody, runtime use and causal benefit remain unverified. This pass does not rerun M5 or the Feedback/Adaptation experiments.

**Structural result.** Memory and Learning remain useful specialist inquiries across existing distinctions, not automatically additional Foundation peers or mandatory services.

## C5 — Non-software operating reality defeats a record-only completion

**Case.** A field team has an approved service plan, a qualified operator and an accurate inventory listing one generator. The intended service needs electrical power at a remote site during a given window.

**Matched contrast.** Keep the item count, plan and operator certification fixed. In one situation the generator is connected, compatible, fueled and available at the site. In another it is inaccessible across a blocked route or unavailable due to a conflicting use. A valid inventory statement does not distinguish all conditions needed for this service. Conversely, a correct plan can be completed as a planning deliverable even when service execution remains infeasible.

**Questions recovered.** Environment's domain/work-condition roles retain location, actual configuration, condition, dynamics, access and resource contention. Information Formation/State/U5 qualify what the inventory says and what it omits. Work separates planning completion, readiness, enactment and service outcome. Actors separates certification, assigned participation, authority and responsibility. Standing qualifies readiness; Propagation concerns realized effect; causal attribution is a further question.

**Decision consequence.** Locate the actual missing condition instead of treating a complete plan or stock count as service delivery. An additional logistical step or changed method may be required; the frame does not choose the engineering solution. No assumption that a technical runtime is the universal Environment is needed.

**Residual.** Actual operating safety, compatibility, capacity, route and effect evidence require competent domain-specific work. This is not equipment-operating advice or a field experiment.

**Structural result.** The physical case preserves meaningful contrasts without adding a software-derived child or turning Environment into everything relevant. The same generator can be a target of repair in one pursuit and a Work condition in another.

## C6 — Exploration can succeed before a fixed final answer exists

**Case.** A writer or research team begins with an open question and uncertain means. An early draft or experiment exposes a better distinction and changes the next inquiry. The initial final-output specification was intentionally incomplete.

**Matched contrast.** An authorized exploratory phase can legitimately return a sharpened question, contrary case or assessed draft. The same artifact is insufficient under a different contract requiring a finished publication or established conclusion. Explicit purpose development differs from silently replacing a difficult authorized goal with an easier one. Fictional content is not a failed factual assertion merely because it is invented; its representational mode and protected use matter.

**Questions recovered.** Work synthesis §§5–6 covers sensemaking, discovery-driven and meta-work, with progressive plans and bounded/continuing forms. Information State preserves mode/stance; U5 distinguishes adaptive selection for a known need from evolution of the need itself. Actors/Control preserve legitimate changes of scope, authorship and decision roles. Environment supplies actual expressive/experimental means and constraints. P2 provides a process-first counterframe to a compulsory linear lifecycle.

**Decision consequence.** Do not reject valid exploration for lacking a fully known final criterion, or label the final pursuit complete merely because exploration progressed. Select the bounded current deliverable and evidence appropriate to it. No requirement for a multi-agent system, a single utility score or complete initial decomposition follows.

**Residual.** Artistic merit, scientific truth, useful novelty and sensible stopping need target-specific judgment/evidence. The frame is not a creativity generator or universal exploration-complete oracle.

**Structural result.** No new Creativity/Discovery ground forced at this depth. The frame survives by preserving changing pursuits and informational modes, not by reclassifying every work episode as execution of a fixed known task.

## Cross-case assessment

The contrasts preserve different positive, negative and unresolved judgments. C1/C4 resist source-success and record-success inflation; C2/C6 resist fixed-goal/single-criterion assumptions; C3/C5 resist capability/stock/plan success as end-to-end feasibility. None proves the tree uniquely optimal. Each would instead reopen it if the decision-critical difference repeatedly disappeared even after its stated semantic qualifications were applied.

These cases are a bounded challenge to the confirmed purpose, not a representative sample of all future domains. They support current-purpose closure with explicit residuals; they do not remove the need for specialist knowledge, legitimate decisions or actual target validation.
