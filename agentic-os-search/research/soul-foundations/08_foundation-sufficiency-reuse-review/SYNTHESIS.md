## 3. The integrated result is more useful than four filled boxes

The decisive property is preservation of different claims and their decision consequences. A source can be accurately captured, suitably selected and available while its assertion remains false. A plan can be correctly executed while its stated metric misses the protected purpose. A capable participant can lack authority; a valid authorization can coexist with an infeasible route. A historical approval can be accurately remembered without authorizing today's different work. A lesson can be stored without becoming operative feedback or retained adaptation.

Those are not six names for a single failure. The cases retain different reasons to obtain evidence, revise an interpretation, renegotiate an objective, change a method, decline an action or investigate an actual effect. The analysis also preserves positive cases: incomplete knowledge need not block a bounded investigation; a historical record can be exactly right for reconstruction; an accessible on-demand route can meet its contract without preloading; a creative intermediate result can satisfy a legitimate phase without completing the whole pursuit.

This is analytical diagnostic value, not an observed improvement in a live worker. The present pass does not establish that a future actor will select the relevant distinction, apply it correctly or obtain a better outcome.

## 4. Purpose and authority cannot be silently centralized

One refinement to the global reading is especially important: a working system need not have one uncontested goal, one shared information state or one actor entitled to settle every trade-off. Different pursuits can interact; different parties can bear consequences without performing work; different scopes of authority can coexist. The analyst's focal Work is a declared perspective, not proof that all participants share it.

The Work and Actors arguments already support this interpretation. The fresh institutional counterframe in SOURCE-LEDGER made it a direct challenge rather than a background assumption. The frame survives only if plurality remains explicit. More evidence may resolve a factual disagreement; it need not determine whose values should prevail. Discovering a missing decision is not permission to make it on the owner's behalf.

This does not create a fifth Value or Governance ground. The examined problems remain expressible through actual consequences, represented claims/criteria, plural pursuits, affected/authorized parties and governing relations. It also does not prove that every normative or institutional problem has been solved. A recurrent protected question that those relations cannot preserve would reopen the relevant result.

## 5. A research basis is not a mandatory reading or execution order

Starting from the four grounds means retaining their substantive questions where relevant; it does not require every task to begin by mechanically filling four forms. A concrete investigation may enter through a desired outcome, disputed evidence, an incident, an institutional action situation or an exploratory prototype. The research frame then helps recover missing distinctions and boundaries.

A generic entity/event/relation model could represent the same cases if supplied with the needed meanings. A goal/process-first view may be a better task-facing entry. Neither observation refutes the current research organization or proves it uniquely superior. RIVALS-AND-REUSE retains these alternatives as complementary views and identifies what would justify replacing or reorganizing the tree. Expressive capacity, discovery usefulness, retrieval cost and empirical superiority are different claims.

Likewise, a real operation can instantiate several questions. An agent is not confined to the Actors box: it uses Information, performs Work, operates under Environment conditions and participates in Composition. A source fetch can involve selection, formation, transfer and exposure. Multiple applicable routes are acceptable; forcing each event into exactly one branch would lose the intended semantics.
