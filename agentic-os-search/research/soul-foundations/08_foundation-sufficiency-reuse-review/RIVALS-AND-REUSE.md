# Rival frames, omission and research-reuse review

**Date:** 2026-09-16. Same-assistant comparative assessment; no measured superiority or independent replication.

## 1. Do not let a broad taxonomy win by definition

Putting every troublesome fact somewhere under Environment or Information is not evidence of sufficiency. A useful research basis must retain the material question, discriminate a plausible wrong conclusion, identify what information/authority/condition could change it and expose a route beyond the label. CASE-AUDIT applies this test to each predeclared family. The full ground and Composition syntheses, exact Information refinements and current authority were recovered for those claims.

The tree is also not a formal factorization into mutually independent variables. Actor authority can be institutionally realized and represented; a resource can play several roles. Diagnostic non-collapse does not prove disjoint populations or causal independence. An all-purpose ontology proof is neither established nor needed for the agreed purpose.

## 2. Goal/process-first rival

A pursuit-first approach starts with the intended outcome, participants, constraints and possible methods. It may be a better practical entry than asking a worker to traverse a research tree. The Design Council's published account explicitly permits discovery, reframing and iteration rather than an invariably linear design sequence (P2). This challenges rigid phase readings but does not by itself determine a universal foundational decomposition.

**Comparison.** C2 and C6 work under a goal/process-first entry if it preserves uncertain/plural purposes, represented versus actual conditions, different actor powers and feedback. It is not refuted. Used alone as a fixed plan→execute→done list, it loses those distinctions. The four-ground/Composition corpus provides reusable questions beneath or alongside this entry, not a competing obligatory runtime sequence.

**Disposition.** Complementary view. Replace/reorganize the research routes if a tested pursuit-first organization preserves the same evidence and distinctions with materially less retrieval loss. No such comparative retrieval experiment was performed here.

## 3. Generic entity/event/relation rival

A sufficiently expressive typed graph can encode every presented example. It would be wrong to claim the current tree is necessary for representation. But the graph's expressiveness does not specify which roles, scopes, evidence distinctions or omissions an analyst will actually discover. Equally, the current tree does not automatically ensure discovery merely by naming its branches.

**Comparison.** C1 requires distinguishing source-copy provenance, offered warrant and acceptance, not just three edges. C3 requires legitimate authority versus effect reach, not just graph reachability. C4 needs historical designation versus current authorization. Either organization can preserve those meanings if it carries the relevant research. Their storage syntax is not the present research verdict.

**Disposition.** Valid possible representation, not a demonstrated stronger replacement for the research question library. No graph/database architecture is selected.

## 4. Institutional action-situation counterframe

P3's institutional-archive draft distinguishes broad frameworks from theories and more assumption-specific models. Its action-situation frame brings actors, positions, information, control and outcomes into a setting shaped by material conditions, community and rules. It challenges reliance on a single external designer or a uniquely centralized organizing objective. The retrieved text is a revised draft, not verified as the final lecture/article; no empirical claims are replicated or quoted here.

**Comparison.** C2/C3 must keep several participants' purposes, authority and information separate and allow institutional conditions themselves to become objects of legitimate change. Work's plural pursuits, Actors' non-performing parties/standing responsibility and Composition's authority/criterion bindings supply substantive routes. If all of that is collapsed into one agent's plan, the resulting use of Foundation fails this test.

**Disposition.** Useful alternative entry and boundary pressure, not independent validation of Foundation or an adopted governance model. Neither a central orchestrator nor a polycentric architecture is prescribed. The analysis must determine the actual target's decision structure rather than inherit one from terminology.

## 5. Fifth-ground proposals

**Goal / Value.** Importance does not settle placement. C2 retains criterion formation, different consequences and affected/authorized parties; more facts need not determine a unique legitimate choice. A fifth Value ground might improve specialist retrieval, but no additional first-order outcome is lost in the tested existing routes. A universal utility calculus is explicitly absent.

**Governance.** C3 requires current authority, applicable rules, decisions, enforcement and protected effects. Control and Actors do not reduce these to a policy file. The existing composition treatment preserves the distinction between a locally correct mechanism and coverage of the intended effect. A stable omitted authority or rule-formation question would reopen it; labels alone do not justify promotion.

**Communication / Cognition.** U/E and the earlier communication comparison preserve meaning, consumer availability and stronger claims about understanding/shared assurance. C3's distributed pieces are not automatically pooled. This does not amount to a completed theory of human cognition or every communication setting. For this working-system research purpose, no tested case exposes a missing universal peer; specialist cognitive/communication evidence remains needed when a target claim depends on it.

**Memory / Learning / Creativity.** C4/C6 retain preservation, modes of representation, revision, changing pursuits and feedback/adaptation without requiring a storage or agent mechanism. Importance of these subjects in AI engineering is not evidence that each is a foundational peer. Their full engineering theories have not been absorbed into Foundation.

## 6. Exact reuse inspection and narrow corrections

The inspected routes were question→current STATE/INDEX→substantive synthesis/refinement→residual/source scope. They supplied actual arguments for C1–C6 rather than only category names. REUSE.md records these entry paths and the specialist-library boundary.

|---|---|---|
| Environment FINDINGS-MAP | Its current hierarchy still marked Structure/Condition discovered/inactive despite accepted descendant STATE/INDEX | Preserve that early map as history, expose current accepted hierarchy and remove the old global queue |
| Composition FINDINGS-MAP | CURRENT_L1/pending header and Binding-next text could be mistaken for current work | Label the numbered parent-pass map historical; direct present acceptance/depth to STATE/INDEX |
| Feedback/Adaptation STATE | Its current continuation still showed the executed 06/Formation transition | Replace only global continuation with parent/root delegation; preserve accepted F/G/D semantics |

These are observed representation/continuity mismatches, not a diagnosis of why the earlier authors produced them. Their existence narrows any previous no-defect assessment; it does not mean completed research must be restarted. Corresponding current identity bindings are refreshed. Historical manifest checkpoints remain explicitly historical rather than all being recertified.

This is manual targeted retrieval and identity review. It is not an exhaustive anchor scanner, independent user study or autonomous-agent retrieval benchmark. A root-to-source route's success here does not prove all future questions can be answered or all missing external sources recovered.

## 7. Residual challenge and stop

The strongest remaining risk is not a demonstrated fifth ground; it is lossy application of a rich framework, particularly silently centralizing goals/authority, confusing stored representations with realized effects or treating a routed requirement as technically solved. The corpus gives distinctions to challenge these errors, while actual actor efficacy requires target validation.

Reopen a bounded research result for a recurring unrepresented protected question, a wrong-level distinction that changes decisions, an invalidated load-bearing source, or demonstrated retrieval/diagnostic loss under a materially better organizing frame. Do not treat every open design choice as proof of foundational incompleteness. On the present cases and source scope, a bounded current-purpose completion proposal is justified; another identically scoped review is not the recommended next task.
