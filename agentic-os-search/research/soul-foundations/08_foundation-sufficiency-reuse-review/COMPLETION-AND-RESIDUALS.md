## Five kinds of remaining responsibility

1. **Conditional Foundation reopening:** a stable missing protected question, boundary theft, forced-fit domain or materially better organization demonstrated under comparable demands. None established here; not all active at once.
2. **Target-specific needs and evidence:** actual purposes, parties, requirements, models, sources, permissible loss, capacity, authority, temporal scope and outcomes. They cannot be completed for an unspecified future system by this review.
3. **Specialist research and engineering:** domain knowledge and mechanisms, build/integrate choices, runtime/tool behavior, implementation and operational validation. Use studies/explorations/new inquiry without making their existence adoption.
4. **Independent validation and efficacy:** different-assumption/source or researcher replication, human/LLM understanding, real behavioral benefit and production reliability. This same-assistant review does not close them.
5. **Preservation and maintenance:** source recovery, stale links, old manifests, reproduction environments and future-version relevance. Three material reached routers are corrected here; the entire historical repository is not certified.

These categories distinguish why further work may be needed. They are not a mandatory future workflow or an excuse to bury a specific unresearched accepted child.

## Reopening threshold

A useful reopening request names the failed claim or lost distinction, a concrete materially different case/evidence and why an applicable existing route cannot preserve it. It may invalidate only one leaf, relation, source premise or reuse path. Global reopening is justified only when that failure is global.

Examples: an affected-party relation with a distinct recurring inquiry lost by the current Actors map; a communication case requiring a stable new protected outcome rather than a new protocol; a source error that reverses an accepted inference; or a consumer repeatedly losing qualifications despite following the recorded current retrieval route. More terminology, a new product, or another successful toy test is not alone a structural trigger.
