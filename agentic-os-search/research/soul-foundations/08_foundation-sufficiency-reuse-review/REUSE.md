# Reusing Foundation without turning it into a recipe

**Date:** 2026-09-16. Research retrieval aid accompanying the proposed bounded completion. Not an installed agent procedure or adopted architecture.

## Question-first entry

Start with the target's actual purpose, situation and current authority, then use the relevant research at the depth needed for the claim. The table is selective navigation, not a requirement to run every row or create a component for every branch. ## Research-to-target reasoning

A Foundation result may supply a necessary distinction, a premise with applicability conditions, a counterexample that rules out an overclaim, an evidence requirement or an unanswered question. It need not become a field, service, prompt or agent. A target may need only a small selection of findings; unselected research is not thereby disposable.

When the question becomes how to meet a situated requirement, use [studies CATALOG](../../studies/CATALOG.md) → selected META → actual finding/source, with freshness checking for material technical facts. The library can supply mechanisms, constraints, contrary cases or reasons not to build a mechanism. It is not the boundary of possible knowledge; a new specialist inquiry may be needed.

[Explorations](../../../explorations/INDEX.md) preserve provisional models and conceptual trials. A useful example can inspire a candidate without becoming verified evidence or accepted architecture. This review used the library-role distinction; it did not reread the complete exploration dialogue or validate a proposed model.

A role need does not imply an AI agent, and an instruction need does not imply that a particular file enforces it. A memory need does not automatically imply a vector database. Conversely, a simpler arrangement is not adequate merely because it is simple. Compare real options against the actual needs and evidence.

## A minimal use record, when material

For a load-bearing application, retain enough to recover: the target question; the exact research claim/path/version; its applicability and contrary case; the situated requirement or choice it informed; and what target evidence is still missing. This is a suggestion for preserving reasoning products, not a compulsory schema, hidden chain-of-thought record or new governance layer. Existing target records may already suffice.

A future finding may change a requirement or reveal that the frame is inadequate. Route that concrete discrepancy to the affected research result rather than force a fit, rebuild the entire library or treat every design unknown as an unresearched Foundation child.
