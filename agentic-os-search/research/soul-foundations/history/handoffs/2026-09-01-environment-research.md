## 2. Established foundation results carried forward

The completed verticals remain independent bounded research results:

- **Records / persistent information:** persistent information cannot safely be reduced to files/content; identity, history, standing, provenance/context, retrieval, preservation and disposition semantics can matter depending on protected use.
- **Work:** Work cannot safely be reduced to a single task/work-item plus scalar lifecycle; purpose/action/method, selection/commitment, situated execution, coordination/flow, multidimensional state/closure, recovery and continuing work can vary independently.
- **Actors:** Actor cannot safely be treated as one self-sufficient primitive carrying identity, role, capability, authority, obligation, delegation, attribution and accountability; the region is relational and multi-anchor.

These results constrain later reasoning but do not themselves establish final SOUL components or data structures.

## 3. Cross-vertical inferences from the discussion

The following are **cross-vertical inferences / working hypotheses**, not established vertical research findings:

### H1 — Recurrent system dimensions

Records, Work and Actors may be better understood as recurrent foundational system dimensions rather than mutually exclusive object classes or three fixed SOUL modules.

The same dimensions appear relevant at multiple levels:

- the current SOUL research/development operating arrangement;
- Development OS;
- SOUL itself;
- task-specific working systems that SOUL may later construct.

The concrete Records, Work and Actor instances differ at each level; the hypothesis concerns recurrence of the problem dimensions, not reuse of one fixed schema.

### H2 — Relations are load-bearing

The completed verticals repeatedly terminate at cross-boundary relations: provenance, authority, commitment, acceptance, delegation, evidence, responsibility, capability/resource access, work realization and externally visible effects.

This suggests that a future coherent system account may require an explicit **relation / control / coordination fabric** (working label) that gives typed meaning, validity conditions, temporal scope, authority/control effects and state-transition consequences to relations among foundational dimensions.

This is not yet a fifth foundation leg and not an accepted architecture layer.

### H3 — Environment is a serious fourth-leg candidate

Environment may be a distinct foundational region concerning the conditions, resources, accessible world, tools/capabilities, external systems and observable/changeable state within which Actors perform Work and Records acquire/use contextual meaning.

Current evidence is insufficient to establish this as a fourth foundation leg. It is therefore the next independent research candidate rather than an accepted addition to the decomposition.

### H4 — Same grammar may recur across meta-levels

A stronger but still unverified hypothesis is that Development OS, SOUL and task-specific systems may each instantiate the same high-level system grammar:

- Records;
- Work;
- Actors;
- possibly Environment;
- plus typed relations/control among them.

If this survives research, system quality at each level may depend materially on the quality and interaction of these dimensions. This remains a research hypothesis, not architecture truth.

### H5 — Records may function as an enabling substrate for Actor↔Work continuity

The current research operating arrangement exposed a sharper cross-vertical interpretation: many persistent control surfaces exist because a fresh or bounded Actor must be able to perform current Work with the correct purpose, authority, context, prior state, evidence and stop conditions without relying on latent conversation/model memory.

In that sense, some Records are not merely passive historical storage; they may be part of the enabling substrate through which Actors can orient to, perform, verify, hand off and continue Work across discontinuous executions.

This must **not** be collapsed into `Records exist only so Actors can work better`. Records research also protects independent persistence concerns such as evidential/accountability use, historical reconstructability, retention/disposition, provenance and future uses that may outlive a particular Actor or Work episode. The working hypothesis is therefore relational: **the quality and binding of Records can materially constrain Actor effectiveness and Work continuity without making Records reducible to Actor support.**

A timing refinement is also important. Saying that an Actor needs the "right Record at the right time" should not silently assign temporal control to Records or Environment. Work research already treats planning, sequencing, readiness, waiting, coordination, event/situation-driven control and actual situated execution as Work-relevant distinctions. A stronger working interpretation is therefore: **Work carries the semantics of when action is planned, eligible, ready or triggered; Environment may supply changing conditions/events/resource availability that make Work ready or blocked; Records preserve the plans, observations, state and evidence needed to know those conditions; Actors act within those bindings.** This is a cross-vertical working hypothesis, not an accepted ownership model.

The current agent-control files (`AGENT.md`, protocols), project state, handoffs and research routers are practical examples of this relation in the present research operating arrangement. They are operational observations, not proof that the same relation exhausts Records in SOUL or other systems.

## 4. Why Environment research comes next

Environment should be researched before a broad cross-vertical composition/relations study because:

1. Records, Work and Actors have already received independent foundation treatment; Environment has not.
2. Work explicitly leaves resources/capacity and externally visible effects open between Work, Actor capability, external environment and other model concerns.
3. Actors explicitly leaves the relationship among non-human systems, tools, Work units and environments unresolved.
4. Starting relation/composition synthesis before testing Environment would risk designing a three-leg relation model and later retrofitting a fourth first-order dimension.

Therefore the immediate research goal is **not** to prove Environment is the fourth leg. It is to try to determine whether Environment exposes independent first-order distinctions that cannot be preserved adequately as mere properties/relations inside Records, Work and Actors.

## 5. Environment research boundary

The Environment foundation pass should begin from a deliberately weak framing and test alternatives rather than assume the fourth-leg hypothesis.

Candidate questions include:

- What counts as a system's environment at different analytical levels?
- Which distinctions exist among world/domain state, runtime/execution environment, resources/capacity, tools/capabilities, interfaces/external systems, constraints and context?
- When is something an Actor capability versus an environmental affordance/resource?
- When is a tool itself an Actor, a resource, an environmental object or a relation-dependent role?
- How should externally visible effects and observations relate to Work state and persistent Records?
- Can environment/context be represented entirely as qualifiers/relations over the three existing verticals without first-order information loss?
- Does the same Environment problem recur for Development OS, SOUL and task-specific systems?

The research must actively search for rival framings and falsifiers. `Environment` is a working research label, not a committed ontology term.
