## 4. Epistemic status discipline

Keep separate:

1. **Established vertical result** — bounded finding established inside one completed research corpus.
2. **Cross-vertical inference** — a new conclusion produced by comparing two or more current vertical results.
3. **Working hypothesis** — a framing worth testing, including relation/control/coordination fabric or cross-level recurrence.
4. **Open question / missing bridge** — not settled by current corpora.
5. **Candidate decision** — a possible later research/architecture direction, not yet accepted.
6. **Accepted architecture decision** — none is created by this discussion.

The user’s views are not evidence. Preserve alternative framings and falsifiers.
