## Epistemic stance for the discussion

Keep these categories separate throughout the conversation:

1. **Established within a completed vertical** — a bounded research result supported by that vertical's corpus.
2. **Cross-vertical inference** — a new inference formed by comparing completed verticals; not yet an established research result.
3. **Working hypothesis** — a possible bridge, framing or research direction worth testing.
4. **Open question / missing bridge** — something the current corpus does not settle.
5. **Candidate decision** — something that might later become a SOUL or Development OS decision, but is not a decision merely because it sounds coherent.

When a claim becomes decision-critical, descend to the relevant evidence/source path rather than relying on a compressed summary.

## Questions the next conversation is authorised to explore

The conversation may examine, among other things:

- What do the three verticals actually constrain, and what do they leave open?
- Where do important relations cut across the current vertical boundaries?
- Which deferred relations appear repeatedly from more than one vertical?
- Are Records, Work and Actors still useful as research regions after independent investigation, or do they suggest different organising dimensions?
- What concepts or mechanisms seem to sit **between** the three foundation corpora and a coherent SOUL system?
- Which questions belong to SOUL's product/domain model, which to Development OS, and which require further independent research before either can be designed responsibly?
- What must Development OS preserve or enforce so that research findings do not silently become architecture truth?
- Which uncertainties are harmless refinements, and which are decision-critical blockers for later synthesis?
- What research sequence would reduce the largest uncertainty or architectural risk next?

These are prompts for inquiry, not a predetermined agenda or implied conclusions.

## Boundaries

The next conversation is explicitly authorised to:

- compare and discuss the three completed vertical results;
- recover existing repository evidence as needed;
- discuss SOUL and Development OS implications at a conceptual level;
- identify tensions, missing bridges, decision-critical assumptions and candidate next research questions;
- produce a proposed research agenda or decision map for user evaluation.

It is **not** automatically authorised to:

- declare Records, Work and Actors the final SOUL ontology;
- turn cross-vertical observations into settled architecture;
- design the final SOUL data model or implementation;
- silently promote old `exploratory/` material into current truth;
- launch a broad new external research pass merely because a possible next layer can be imagined.

If the discussion exposes a question that requires new evidence, identify it clearly as a candidate research task and let the user decide whether to authorize that research.

## Desired result of the discussion

A good outcome is not a finished architecture. It is a clearer map of the territory between the three foundation corpora and later SOUL / Development OS work, including:

- the strongest cross-vertical tensions or bridges;
- what is already supported versus newly inferred;
- unresolved decision-critical questions;
- plausible research directions and their dependencies;
- a reasoned proposal for what should be researched or decided next.

The user remains the evaluation gate before a new substantive research phase or architecture phase begins.
