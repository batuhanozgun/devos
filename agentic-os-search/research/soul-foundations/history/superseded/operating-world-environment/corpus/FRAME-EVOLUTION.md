# Environment — Frame Evolution

**Status:** research-history / audit surface  
**Vertical revision:** `environment-foundation-2026-09-01-r1`

## 1. Initial candidate frame

The parent handoff opened `Environment` as a possible fourth foundation region covering conditions, resources, accessible world, tools/capabilities, external systems and observable/changeable state.

The starting rule was deliberately weak: test whether this survives independently rather than prove a four-leg model.

## 2. First correction — Environment is boundary-relative

Systems-engineering and agent/environment traditions made the system-of-interest boundary load-bearing.

Revision:
- reject Environment as an intrinsic object category;
- require explicit analytical/operational boundary before `inside/outside` has meaning.

## 3. Second correction — Context is not Environment

Dey/Dourish context evidence showed that contextual relevance can be selective and interaction/activity dependent.

Revision:
- separate live world/operating conditions from which facts are currently contextually relevant and from how those facts are represented.

## 4. Third correction — Capability does not absorb actionability

Affordance, W3C, access-control and runtime evidence showed that stable Actor capability does not determine situated possibility.

Revision:
- preserve environmental conditions/resources/interfaces separately from Actor capability;
- treat actionability/affordance as relational rather than an intrinsic Environment attribute.

## 5. Fourth correction — world state is not Records

POMDP/control evidence plus Records/Work findings separated actual state/dynamics from observations, beliefs, persistent assertions and Work lifecycle/effect projections.

Revision:
- move live state/dynamics and observation interface to the center of the candidate region;
- reject `Environment = known context data`.

## 6. Fifth correction — tool/runtime/infrastructure are boundary roles

Runtime provides a strong concrete environmental case, but infrastructure/extended-system alternatives show tool/resource classifications can move across the boundary.

Revision:
- stop treating tools/infrastructure as a first-order Environment family;
- treat them as concrete entities playing relation- and boundary-dependent roles.

## 7. Direct null-hypothesis test

The strongest alternative allowed all Environment-like facts to reduce to Records + Work + Actors + typed relations.

Result:
- succeeds in showing no fourth object bucket/module is required by the evidence;
- fails to eliminate the underlying problem if external/current states/conditions/resources/interfaces are removed entirely, because those can vary independently and alter feasibility/outcomes.

## 8. Final bounded frame

The current research result is therefore:

> **boundary-relative operating-world state/dynamics + sensing/action/interface/resource coupling, with context/actionability represented relationally where appropriate.**

This is not accepted terminology or architecture. It is the strongest current research framing after falsifiers.

## 9. Epistemic outcome

The research did not confirm its starting frame unchanged. It:
- weakened the fourth-leg/object interpretation;
- strengthened a narrower independent problem-region interpretation;
- left architecture representation and relation/control ownership open.
