# B1 — Child-to-Parent Integration and Boundary Review

**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Class:** same-model semantic cross-check of accepted repository results.

## 1. What is integrated

The Composition parent protects four diagnostic regions: Binding, Composite Standing, Control and Transition/Effect/Feedback. The last contains Propagation, Causal Effect/Contribution and Feedback/Adaptation; its causal and feedback parents each have two distinct inquiries. The Persistent Adaptation acceptance completes the currently promoted Composition child-pass frontier, not every Foundation descendant.

The regions are not disjoint populations of events and not a prescribed process sequence. A single incident can involve them together; a different question may need only a subset. No runtime, entity table or orchestration pipeline follows from the tree.

## 2. Exact protected questions

| Region | Question retained after child deepening | Inference not licensed |
|---|---|---|
| Binding | Which anchors occupy which semantic roles under which scope/time? | Generic connectivity proves a valid relation. |
| Composite Standing | What cross-ground qualification actually holds under a criterion and frame? | An assessment, acceptance or record is the qualification itself. |
| Control | How is an exact behavior/effect governed, and where is that governance realized? | One enforcement point controls every route to the effect. |
| Propagation | Which exact changes became real, where and when? | Commitment, local realization or a successful retry proves full propagation. |
| Actual causation | Which contributors/configurations made this exact effect occur? | Provenance, chronology or a missing control proves causation/responsibility. |
| Contrastive effect | How would an exact target outcome differ under X versus X', in which context? | Before/after improvement proves causal benefit or transportability. |
| Returned Influence | Did relevant realized-condition information return into an operative response process, at which claim level? | Delivery, visible change or unchanged output proves operative use. |
| Persistent Adaptation | Did relevant history change and retain the response disposition over an explicit opportunity/horizon/support frame? | Stored lessons, new bytes, current expression or test success proves learning, benefit or authority. |

The wording above is an integrated retrieval view. The accepted leaf keys and original passes remain the evidence routes.

## 3. Changes to the original parent shorthand

**Composition:** the original parent treated causation as a cross-cutting pressure, not a fifth immediate Composition peer. Later Transition research gives causation a dedicated nested parent and two leaves. This is depth-specific refinement, not a contradiction: 'not a peer here' never means 'cannot be a descendant anywhere.'

**Standing:** not every qualification is composite, and neither evidence nor an authorized recognition is constitutive of every standing. The accepted child's semantic-removal test preserves Environment-local criterion standing. The older parent description must not silently turn Standing into assessment-only semantics.

**Control:** realized governing-effect coverage is not exhausted by the monitored or enforced path. The original policy/authority/decision/enforcement distinction is strengthened, not replaced.

**Transition:** a source event, its representations, local effects, causal contribution and later response are different claims; a propagation episode can comprise several exact transitions. Temporal order is not causal proof.

**Feedback:** the accepted G-pass replaces visible-change shorthand with operative returned influence. Holding, inhibiting or continuing can be a response. Return reference and exact response/effect locus are required where material. Feedback and Adaptation are non-coextensive questions, not universally independent historical predicates.

**Adaptation:** the accepted D6 refinements distinguish fixed code from fixed effective regime; allow conditional response distributions and continuation sequences; permit evidenced latent dispositions before natural recurrence; require meaningful horizon/support/lifecycle scope; separate historical acquisition, current retention and current expression; and reject a universal reduction of all Evolution to this leaf.

## 4. Cross-ground countercases

1. **Correct record, wrong world.** A report can be accurately copied and retrieved while reporting a false source condition. Information fidelity and Binding can hold while the Environment claim fails. Returned influence can still occur. Do not infer truth or legitimate authority from causal influence.
2. **Accepted task, unrealized outcome.** Work may be completed and formally accepted while the intended target effect fails. The action, report, recognition and actual state are not one status.
3. **Local block, indirect execution.** One control prevents the direct action while another actor/tool path reaches the protected effect. A local enforcement success does not support all-path control.
4. **Use without revision.** A consumer examines relevant feedback and preserves its decision. The unchanged output proves neither use nor non-use; the claim requires discriminating evidence.
5. **Stored correction, unchanged path.** An incident lesson exists but is not selected or used. Recording and even changing bytes do not establish retained response change.
6. **Same code, changed future response.** An external history-derived carrier changes a later response under the same interpreter/model. The adaptation bearer must include the relevant carrier and access path; the model alone is not thereby shown to have changed.
7. **Criterion changes, facts do not.** Qualification can change under a new criterion without a change in the underlying domain characteristic. That is not proof that the world improved or a controller learned.
8. **Probe teaches the target.** A later successful response can reflect the test's intervention, not the disposition before testing. Pre/post target identity and causal history are necessary.
9. **Actor replacement with continuity.** A system-level disposition may survive a worker replacement if relevant carriers and operative relations survive. This does not establish that every constituent learned or that all pathways are covered.
10. **Physical drift observed by an agent.** Cross-ground observation/evidence does not make all underlying domain dynamics Composition-local. Another ground must be constitutive of the question, not merely of our access to it.
11. **Two derivative reports, one origin.** More messages can change behavior without supplying independent corroboration. The number of sources seen by the consumer is not the number of independent observations.
12. **Changed distribution with unchanged mean.** A retained response regime can differ without a mean or one current output changing. Exact outcome/dimension and opportunity scope cannot be omitted.

These are source-derived or constructed diagnostic cases, not new observations of SOUL. No live experiment was run in this integration pass.

## 5. Bounded judgment

The recovered child findings preserve the protected questions and ground boundaries. No specific contradiction or additional immediate Composition/Feedback child is established by this review. This supports present coherence, not unique optimality or exhaustive adequacy across all domains. The unfinished Information passes can still produce a future boundary falsifier; composition is reusable with that limitation visible.
