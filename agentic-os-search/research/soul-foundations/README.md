## Preserved distinctions

Actual reality differs from its representation. Work completion differs from outcome attainment and authorized acceptance. Capability, legitimate authority, access and effective reach differ. Qualification differs from assessment or a recorded label. Policy, decision and enforcement differ. Change is not causal proof; stored lessons or changed code do not establish operative feedback, retained adaptation or benefit.

Formation separates capture, source coverage, copies, evidence and reference time. Derivation separates intended transfer, offered warrant and continuing-state revision. Revision retains support/withdrawal, alternatives/policy, future-update state, temporal meaning, realization and replay/current authority.

Selection separates need-to-view adequacy from information-to-consumer availability. Relevant items can form an insufficient set; a miss has claim-relative meaning; best effort is not sufficiency. Exact fragments and jointly appropriate versions can matter; planned and actual views can differ. U5 preserves action/rationale/audit and witness/quantifier distinctions, joint/contextual value and contingent reading versus evolving need.

E5 qualifies target/consumer/mode, actionable temporal access, observations, retained views and supported representation without claiming understanding. I1–I5 require a common compatible witness, actual consumer/history scope, legitimate substitutes and established side information. Different actors' information or authority cannot be silently pooled.
