# Early research-method implications — quarantined exploratory note

**Status:** LOCAL / PROVISIONAL / NOT PROMOTED  
**Origin:** extracted from the earlier mixed `research-to-architecture-boundary.md` note.

## Why this file exists

The original boundary note mixed two different epistemic roles:

1. source-derived context about `research ≠ architecture decision`,
2. early proposals about how SOUL Research packages, stopping and next questions might work.

The first role now lives in `../evidence/research-architecture-boundary.md`. This file preserves the second role without allowing it to masquerade as old-repo evidence.

## Provisional output-shape idea

An early working hypothesis was that a future decision-oriented research package might contain items such as:

- exact question / decision dimension informed,
- materiality of the uncertainty,
- observations vs inferences vs assumptions,
- primary/authoritative sources and versions,
- conflicting evidence / alternatives,
- source quality and known omissions,
- what the evidence supports,
- what it explicitly does not prove,
- capability/failure/control-property hypotheses,
- credible alternative frames/layers,
- strongest falsifiers / disconfirming evidence,
- where causal necessity is and is not identified,
- remaining uncertainty and what additional evidence could change a downstream decision,
- decision relevance without an embedded architecture verdict.

This was **not discovered as a canonical SOUL Research schema**.

## Provisional stopping idea

An early working rule was:

> stop a research line when its decision-critical uncertainty has been reduced enough that plausible additional evidence is unlikely to change the authorised next-work/design decision at the required risk level, or when the remaining uncertainty is explicitly bounded and needs another method/phase.

This was an exploratory inference, not a project-wide stopping contract.

## Open questions that were generated early

The exploratory line asked questions such as:

- what minimum research dimensions/evidence classes might be needed before SOUL definition/capability/failure architecture work,
- how research breadth could remain open enough to find missing problem classes without becoming unbounded,
- how selective-context omission could be represented,
- where causal necessity would require experiment/ablation rather than triangulated evidence,
- what handoff/promotion boundary should separate research from product decisions.

These questions may later be useful, wrong, incomplete or framed at the wrong level.

## Non-authority rule

Nothing in this file should be treated as a default when the project explicitly begins deciding **how research should be conducted**.

At that point the method must be re-derived from the then-current authorised objective, the agreed SOUL starting frame and external evidence. This file can be used as prior thinking or a challenge set only.
