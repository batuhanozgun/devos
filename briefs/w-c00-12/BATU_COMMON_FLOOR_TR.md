## Builder's assessment (English)

**Four principles.**
1. A common high floor plus real specialisation. Specialist abilities differ by role; general intellectual abilities (reasoning, problem understanding, system awareness, evidence and uncertainty discipline, contradiction detection, collaboration awareness) stay high in every role.
2. A narrow terminal goal is not a narrow view. The role keeps one goal but understands why its piece exists, what it serves, what it relates to, what it rests on and what its result could change. It does not take over other roles' work.
3. Intellectual stamina: the standard holds over the whole work, not only in the first turn or on hard problems.
4. A rating on the card is not the ability. The working environment (information, history, protocols, current state, other roles' results) has to make the ability usable.

**What the plan already has (checked, not assumed).**
- DevOS already carries principles 1 and 2. `plan/Ek_A_Rol_Sozlesmeleri.md` §2 "Ortak taban" and `plan/Ek_D_Dusunme_Protokolleri.md` §2 list the common floor every role carries, and item 3 is literally "Dar görev, geniş görüş" (narrow task, wide view). Role exams measure "the common floor's form in this role type" (Ek A §3.4). Ek A §3.2–3.3 gives each role a knowledge map, methods, known failure classes and continuity records, which is principle 4.
- The tension between a wide view and an independent check is already handled: D4 item 2 lists "review without seeing the producer's explanation" as a way to make a check independent.

**Gaps (searched in `plan/`; absence means "not found by grep and reading the named sections").**
- *The builder does not carry the common floor.* `plan/Builder_Operating_Model.md` never refers to the Ek A or Ek D common floor; its only mention of Appendix D is that `CLAUDE.md` is replaced by it at C05. The builder's own roles (runs, reviewers, probes, the session that triggered runs) get their standard only from whatever their first message says. Observed consequence: the run in L-033 was locally right (it recorded a status) and wrong for the system (it judged its own change "status-only" and skipped independent review), the exact failure the text describes.
- *Stamina is covered only as loss of state, not as loss of quality.* The builder model hands over at 50% context and writes intent ahead (§3.4); D7 keeps the work's state across sessions. Nothing addresses the standard decaying inside a long session while the state is intact. Observed in this builder session, which is long: timestamps estimated instead of taken from `date -u`, and another session's claim recorded without reading its transcript (B1). Both were caught afterwards, not prevented.

**Challenges and limits.**
1. *For LLM actors the floor depends mainly on the environment, not on the model.* All builder roles run on the same model, so their raw "reasoning" rating is the same. What differs is what each one is given: a narrow prompt, no purpose chain, a full context. A role becomes "reasoning 4/10" mostly by being starved, not by being weak. So the answer to Batu's second question is mainly "context, protocols and checks"; model choice matters more when DevOS later uses cheaper models for some roles.
2. *A wide view has a cost in context and in independence.* Too much material dilutes attention; the producer's own reasoning anchors a verifier. A wide view should mean a short purpose chain plus pointers to sources (the router or pyramid idea, principle 12), and not the producer's argument.
3. *A declared floor is not an existing floor.* Writing "be rigorous" in a prompt does not create it. It has to be tested. Candidate test: give a role a narrow task with a planted problem outside its specialty (a contradiction with the purpose, a stale source) and check that it notices and reports it without taking over.
4. *Not every actor needs the floor.* A deterministic script (the allow-list hook, `check_dispatcher_pr.sh`) has no reasoning at all and is still a good actor, because it does not interpret. The floor applies to actors that interpret or decide. Mechanical checks are the deliberate exception, and they should stay mechanical.
5. *Stamina for LLMs probably means fresh sessions rather than endurance.* A long session cannot be trained to stay sharp. The likely mechanisms are shorter units of work, hand-over before quality drops (not only before compaction), re-reading the floor at checkpoints, and a fresh reviewer for late-session work. Which of these works is an open question for W-C00-12; the research library should be consulted before choosing (principle 12).
