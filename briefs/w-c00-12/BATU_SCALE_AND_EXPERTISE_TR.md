## Builder's assessment (English)

**Three principles.**
1. Depth and effort scale with the work; the thinking standard does not.
2. An expert is an intellectual environment, not just an actor: knowledge, experience, methods, tools, standards and past mistakes.
3. Not every capability is used in every piece of work, but the system must notice when one is needed and call it ("the toolbox is ready").

**Builder installation (observed).**
- *"No architect needed" was decided by the producer.* The dispatcher-started run judged its own promotion of the operating model to binding as "status-only, so not high-impact" and skipped independent review (L-033). That is the doghouse question: the producer decided, without the relevant view having seen the problem.
- *No expert environment.* Every session starts empty. The builder's past mistakes are written in the log, but no session sees them at boot. The FND-001 class (stating something as verified or done before checking it) recurred at least five times. The architect's "remembered mistakes" do not exist for the builder, because no mechanism puts them in front of it.
- *The plan already has the idea for DevOS.* Appendix A's role packages carry a knowledge map, methods, an exam and limits: the actor as an environment. That idea is not yet applied to the builder itself.

**Limits.**
1. Asking the expert first must be cheap. A short triage from the relevant expertise's point of view, which may conclude "no deeper involvement needed". Otherwise every small item spawns a review session, the bloat the text warns against.
2. Process can grow by itself. The process-overhead check at stage closure (§4.5) becomes more important under this principle.

**Consequence for W-C00-12.**
- Acceptance (i), proportionality: depth and effort scale with the work, the thinking standard does not. Whether an item is small, and how much expertise it needs, is decided by a short triage from the relevant expertise's view, not by the producer.
- Acceptance (j), role environment: each builder role is defined with its environment (purpose, sources, methods, known failure patterns). Sessions see the known failure patterns at boot.
