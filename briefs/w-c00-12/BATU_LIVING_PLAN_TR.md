## Builder's assessment (English)

**What is new compared with the novel analogy.** `BATU_NOVEL_ANALOGY_TR.md` established living work and that understanding and execution are intertwined; acceptance (a) and (a2) already carry that. This text adds the shape of the plan:
1. *Zoom.* The whole stays visible at a coarse level and the active branch is detailed. Detail is uneven on purpose. Outside practice calls this rolling-wave planning: near work is detailed, far work stays at a coarse level until information arrives.
2. *Open notes on future branches.* A discovery about later work is attached to the branch where it will be needed, not to a general memory.
3. *Composition.* All children green does not make the parent done; an explicit synthesis step is needed.
4. *Planned, in progress and actually done are different states.* A task existing on the plan is not the task done.
5. *Controlled change.* Anyone may propose a plan change with its reason; the role that owns the plan decides.
6. *What an actor sees with a task.* The goal, the parent, the siblings and what the result affects downstream.

**What the DevOS plan already has (checked).**
- *Zoom.* The stage list C00–C12 is the horizontal view. Each stage's items, in the state file for C00, are the vertical view.
- *Composition.* `plan/Ek_G_Isleyis_Kurallari.md` G4.6: "part tests and link checks do not verify the effect of a large product." G4.1 keeps design, working and delivered states of a composite apart. `plan/Ek_B_Veri_Modeli.md` §3.10 has the matching `Assembly` record.
- *Planned versus done.* §3.3 separates `execution` from `acceptance`: "finished" never becomes "accepted" automatically.
- *Purpose links.* The `part_of` relation and the purpose audit in plan K-5 ("open works' link to the main goal is audited regularly") cover the link from a work item to its goal.
- *Gaps.* What the DevOS plan does not state explicitly (by grep): open notes attached to future work, and a task's context package showing parent and siblings. The second is close to Ek A §3.5, which gives every subagent "purpose and the decision it serves".

**The builder's own plan (observed).**
- *The composition failure happened.* W-C00-05 met each of its acceptance conditions one by one and was marked done. Batu's review then showed the whole was weak: "the design was built by patching review findings one at a time" (state file, Stage row; L-034). That is "all children green, parent not good".
- *Open notes sit in one place, not on their branches.* OI-011 holds 24 items in one long cell. Several of them concern later stages, not W-C00-12:
  - the Postgres documentation connector, for C02;
  - Batu's ten questions for memory products, for C02 and C04;
  - the library pyramid assessment, which also bears on C04.
  When the C02 session starts, nothing puts these in front of it (`BATU_STORED_IS_NOT_USED_TR.md`).
- *The work list is flat.* C00 items have no parent and child structure and no dependencies (already noted under (a2)). W-C00-12 itself has no decomposition yet. Under this text that is correct: it is the active branch and will be detailed when its work starts, not before.

**Challenges and limits.**
1. *A tree is not enough.* Real work has cross-dependencies between branches, for example a character that forces a chapter change. The plan needs a tree for zoom and dependency links for impact. The DevOS data model has both (`part_of` and `depends_on`); the builder's list has neither.
2. *Open notes rot as well.* A note attached to a branch that is later restructured can be orphaned. Restructuring a branch must carry its notes, which is the change-impact problem again (`BATU_MEMORY_LIFECYCLE_TR.md`).
3. *Showing parent and siblings costs context.* A short purpose chain plus pointers, not the full branch (principle 12 and the common-floor assessment).
4. *Who owns the builder's plan?* Today the producer edits the state file directly. Under this text, plan changes are candidates decided by the plan's owner. For the builder, the plan's owner and its main producer are the same session. How to separate them proportionately is a W-C00-12 design question, under acceptance (g).

**Consequence for W-C00-12.** No new acceptance condition. (a2) is extended, before the work starts, with:
- zoom: whole visible, active branch detailed;
- open notes attached to the branch they concern, with the OI-011 items that concern later stages moved or linked to those stages;
- a parent counts as done only after an explicit composition check, not from its children's states;
- plan changes recorded as candidates with a reason and decided by the plan's owner;
- every task given to a builder role carries its goal, parent, siblings and downstream effect.
