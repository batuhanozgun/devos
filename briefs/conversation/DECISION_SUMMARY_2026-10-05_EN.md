## A. Diagnosis
1. (K) The plan described in detail what is to be done (C00–C12) and DevOS's own way of working. For the installation work it had a sequencing principle, a ledger and stage close; but it had no continuity, hand-over or session start ("Kurucu Claude Code oturumu: Belgenin tamamını ve bütün ekleri okur, C00'dan başlar" (Builder Claude Code session: reads the whole document and all the appendices, starts from C00); opening the review sessions with Batu's step-by-step help). The builder filled this gap with a session chain of its own design; as the chain hit platform limits, it added mechanisms, and the plan's actual work was suspended.
2. (B) Batu left the decisions to the builder; the builder kept making the work bigger and bigger. Growth is not the problem; the problem is drifting off topic. What matters is being able to close the branch that was opened, after it has grown, and continue. What is really bad is the opened branch leading away from the main line of work.

## B. Batu's principles (the working session and every subagent follow them)
3. (B) "Kendi kendine ilerlemek" (moving forward on its own) = staying faithful to the plan, getting all the way to the last step and setting up DevOS; working without asking "şu dosyaya yazayım mı, bunu okuyabilir miyim" (shall I write to this file, may I read this) while working.
4. (B) The two foundations of the installation: (a) moving the work forward by the planned steps; (b) moving forward without breaking away from the plan, without drift, without forgetting where it left off.
5. (B) Batu does not make technical decisions. The builder makes technical decisions, based on research and evidence. Batu's words are not a technical specification; for example, when he said "ayrı oturum" (separate session) or "o işi yapmamış bir oturum" (a session that has not done that work), he did not mean a technically separate session.
6. (B) Reasoning principle: anomalies (e.g., the session dying; "olursa yapacak bir şey yok" (if it happens, there is nothing to be done)) are not mixed up with the standard flow; mixing the two undermines decisions. Things that will happen regularly in the standard flow (e.g., the usage limit), on the other hand, are designed as part of the standard flow.
7. (B) Reasoning principle: every side branch closes, and the work returns to the main line of work (item 2).
8. (B) Communication with Batu: short answers; step by step, together; not derailing the topic; not derailing the topic by asking questions.
9. (B) First, how the working session will work is decided ("kurulumun kurulması" (setting up the installation)), then it is set up; there is no going back to C00 before that. What is meant by "kurmak" (to set up) may become clearer as we talk; that is why this summary may change.

## C. Structure
10. (K) For continuity and non-binding checks, the installation runs in a single working session; the work is distributed to subagents and workflows. The chain that opens a new session at every step is dropped. Rationale (evidence): a session in Accept edits cannot open a new session and cannot set up a reminder (observed on 5 October, confirmed by the platform documentation); independence of thinking is provided by a subagent with a fresh context (the plan and the library say the same thing); the "where did I leave off" knowledge is carried not by the session but by the record (main branch, plan, ledger).
11. (K) Its limit: the single session holds fully up to C03. The separate sessions or environments that the plan requires remain for the following: the one-time independent review of the transition (item 29), the session and routines observed in C01, the audit environment from C03 on, C04's secret search question set, the exams (C05, C07, C10). When the need to start these arises, it comes to Batu as a single, narrow question (the first time in C01).
12. (B) Batu opens the working session once, from the app, and pastes the first message that the builder prepared. At opening, the model, effort and ultracode settings are checked. [Assumption: in a session opened from the app these settings will be visible; in the sessions the builder opened, the ultracode setting never showed, which points to invisibility, not absence. The repository setting fixes effort at xhigh.]
13. (B) Batu talks directly to the working session; this conversation session closes after the hand-over. Decisions and tasks that belong to Batu are still collected in the single GitHub issue, and the status is still kept current in DURUM.md.
14. (K) Each stage runs with a /goal objective and PC-01's three stop conditions (stage finished; Batu's decision or action is needed; an obstacle that cannot be overcome). PC-01 does not change.

## D. Roles (taken as the basis, not yet accepted as correct)
15. (B+K) There will be predefined roles for the installation (the idea and the content are Batu's: authority, reasoning procedure, timing, prohibition). The roles were derived from the plan's steps.
16. (K) Main session = executor (not a role): it reads the plan itself, picks the next step in plan order, splits the work, writes tasks for subagents, is the only one that writes to the main branch, opens and closes side branches, and brings Batu only his own decisions.
17. (K) Subagent roles: Producer (Üretici) (writes), Researcher (Araştırmacı) (reads, does not decide), Tester (Sınayıcı) (watches the platform, runs tests written in advance), Test designer (Test tasarımcısı) (writes the acceptance conditions and tests before the result), Auditor (Denetçi) (fresh eyes; evaluates, does not fix; also checks "have we broken away from the plan?" when a side branch is opened, when it is closed, and at the end of a stage), Counter-designer (Karşı tasarımcı) (designs without seeing the plan; only C00 step 5; not seeing the plan is ensured by a tool restriction, and if that cannot be ensured, the independence level is recorded as low).
18. (K) What the role file defines: authority (tool list, model, effort), procedure (the role text), prohibitions (tool list; the guard's general rules), limit (number of steps). The executor sets the timing; the workflow is a helper.
19. (K) In the transition, only the roles that C00 and C01 require are written (Producer, Researcher, Tester, Auditor, Counter-designer); Test designer in C02. These are the builder's helpers, not the DevOS roles of Appendix A; in C05, when DevOS's role set is set up, they are removed or handed over.

## E. Way of working (harness)
20. (B+K) Two layers: (a) running the plan, mechanical: the work list (acceptance conditions written in advance), where I left off, the next step in plan order, merging into the main branch, writing to the ledger, a hook that makes the session re-read the plan and the ledger after summarization, stop conditions; (b) high-level reasoning, inside a step: splitting the work, choosing the role, interpreting the result, making the technical decision, noticing a gap in the plan, questioning the frame when stuck.
21. (K) Limit: reasoning may propose a side branch; the branch's rationale is tied to the current plan step, its return point is written down, and it has a limit; there is no moving on to the next plan step before the branch closes.
22. (K) The way to change the plan is the plan's own section 14: old text, new text, rationale, affected stages; an acceptance condition is not loosened after the result has been seen; text labeled [Batu kararı] (Batu's decision) changes only by Batu's decision.
23. (K) Reasoning layer: Batu's principles (section B) + the thinking disciplines and the session opening/closing rules in the plan's Appendix D + the suitable parts of Appendix G (G4, G8, G10). These were written for DevOS; they are borrowed for the installation, adapted, and do not take the place of C05's setup. In place of Appendix D's rule "uzun oturumun özetlemesine güvenme, devirle geç" (do not trust a long session's summarization; move on by hand-over), this summary's decision applies (single session + re-read hook).

## F. Transition ("kurulumun kurulması" (setting up the installation))
24. (B) This session (the builder) prepares the environment and writes the files, then hands over. Rationale (Batu): a single session's reasoning cannot carry all of these at the same time; giving the summary to a new session loses many subtleties. That is why the work is done not in a single turn but with subagents.
25. (K) To preserve the subtleties: this summary is written to the main branch as the single source; the subagents' tasks are written by the executor that knows the whole conversation (this session), and it also checks every output against the conversation.
26. (K) Steps: 0 this decision summary (Batu approves its intent) → 1 inventory: subagents scan the repo, the executor says "keep, remove, change" for everything → 2 new files (Producer subagents): short rule text (new CLAUDE.md), 5 role files, re-read hook, guard change, plan change record → 3 an Auditor check on every file ("has anything been added that is not in the decision?") → 4 one independent review of the whole transition → 5 merging into the main branch and hand-over (first message; the working session starts from C00).
27. (K) Order of merging into the main branch: the outputs of 0 and 1 are merged on their own. The outputs of 2 and 3 (rule text, .claude/ files, guard) are high-impact; they are merged only after 4's approval, together.
28. (K) The old way is not left alongside; the inventory decides what will be removed. Definitely kept: the plan (binding), the guard's core (written-reason allow/deny; connector bans; protection against editing .claude/ files inside a session), the merge gate (its condition changes: instead of a separate session's verdict, the Auditor's verdict, with its independence level; this preserves the assurance Batu was given in D-008), the ledger history, the evidence.
29. (K) Why a separate review once: the transition must be approved under the old rules; otherwise the new way would have approved itself. The old rule requires a separate review session's verdict for high-impact changes. This needs Batu's decision (item 34).
30. (K) Closing the open ends: W-C00-01, 02 and 04 are not redone; they are submitted for acceptance. W-C00-05 (the old way of working) and W-C00-12 (the redesign and its sub-items) are closed with a cancellation/closure recorded with its rationale (the acceptance condition is not loosened); this lifts the C00 suspension. PR #86 is closed. D-009 is withdrawn; the need to start sessions comes back in C01 as a narrower question (item 11).
