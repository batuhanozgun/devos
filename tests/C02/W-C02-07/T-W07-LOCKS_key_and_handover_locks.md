## Cases (numbers fixed before any run)
How an interleaving is forced: a holder connection takes one session advisory lock of this test's own key space (classid 20702), the barrier. The first connection makes its call inside a transaction and then waits on the barrier, uncommitted. Only when it is seen waiting there does the second connection start; once the second is seen waiting inside its call (or has ended, at most 15 s), the barrier is released and the first commits (or rolls back). Each connection prints the server's clock just before its call (`t0`), after its call (`t1`), after taking the barrier (`tb`, just before its commit) and after its commit (`t2`). "Made while the first was uncommitted" means: the second's `t0` is earlier than the first's `tb`.
