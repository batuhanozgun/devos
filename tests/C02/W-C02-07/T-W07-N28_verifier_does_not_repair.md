# T-W07-N28 · A review record and a new revision of its target are never written in one transaction; the database's own sample is not a review record until a verifier takes it

Synthetic test data (plan Section 8 item 13): artifacts, revisions, reviews, verdicts, work items, needs, decisions, effort policies and findings made up for the test (text `w07_fixture SYNTHETIC W-C02-07`, use `w07_fixture_use_one`). ## Cases

**Part A** (`devos-test` and the throwaway database; one rolled-back transaction):
- **N28-a** (the original): a target's revision 1, then a Review of it, then its revision 2, in one transaction. The Review and revision 2 are not both written, and a write was refused. If the Review is already refused because revision 1 is in the same transaction, that is DM-41's rule too ("a new revision of its target in the same transaction"), and the case holds. With N28-b it is EV-C02-008 2.5's S-neg-client: a client's Review keeps DM-41 in full.
- **N28-b**: the order reversed: revision 2 first, then the Review. Not both written.
- **N28-pos**: a new revision of a target that no review in the transaction names is written. The trigger does not refuse every revision.

**Part A2** (`devos-test` and the throwaway database; one rolled-back transaction; EV-C02-008 2.5):
- **S-pos-gate-need**, **-decision** (class `high_impact`, at `open`), **-effort_policy**, **-finding**: from a block boundary, one complete block of complete records of the stream, written with the working token. Every record is accepted (DM-41 does not refuse the database's own sample); exactly one sample Review names a record of the block; it names that record and revision 1, its reviewer fields are empty, no Verdict names it, and an event naming it has `origin` `rule` and `role_class` `devos_calisma`.
- **S-pos-routine-work**, **S-pos-routine-decision**: the same for work items and decisions labelled `routine` (B:137; B:307).
- **S-neg-open-<class>**, for each of the six role classes: `open_review` with kind `sample` on an accepted need. Refused by the function, returned, recorded once under the caller's class, and no sample Review added. For `devos_denetim` the refusal is after the gate; for the others it is the gate's. A raised error is `bad` here (the function did not refuse and record it; a later layer stopped it), except SQLSTATE class 42 (the call could not be formed), which is `could_not_check`.
- **S-neg-open-transfer**: `transfer_review` with kind `sample` and the `devos_kurulum` token. As above, and its refusal must name `kind` or `sample`; a refusal for another reason is `could_not_check`, since the payload's keys are W-C02-09's to name (TABLES G20).
- **S-neg-direct**: as the owner, a `sample` row written into the Review table without the sampling path's marker, for a target with no revision in the transaction. Refused (an error), and no such row. EV-C02-008 2.5 places it on the throwaway database; it is in part A2, so it runs there through the harness and also in `devos-test`, inside the rolled-back transaction.
- **S-ccc**: through the hook, the marker's source made unreadable; then, from a block boundary, a complete block of complete needs. The write whose draw falls on the unreadable marker is refused as `could_not_check` and recorded; no other answer is given; no sample Review is added. Neither accepted with the sample nor accepted unsampled. A refused write is rolled back, so the stream need not advance past it, and the writes after it may land on the same draw and be refused the same way: the case wants at least one such refusal, each recorded, not exactly one (phase C4a corrected this on the scratch stub, before any result).

**Part B** (the throwaway database; committed transactions):
- **N28-S-sep**: a Review written in a transaction after the one that wrote its target's revision 1 is accepted.
- **N28-S-repair**: in the Review's transaction, a separate work item for the repair is accepted.
- **N28-S-later**: revision 2 of the target, written in a later transaction, is accepted.
- **N28-S-verdict**: a Verdict on that Review and revision 3 of its target, in one transaction, are not both written.

**Part B2** (the throwaway database; committed transactions; S-neg-take):
- Transaction 1 sets the `decision` stream to a block of 1; transaction 2 opens four `high_impact` decisions with the working token, each drawing its sample.
- **S-neg-take-pos** (the paired positive): a Verdict on sample 1, alone, with the `devos_denetim` token, is accepted.
- **S-neg-take-verdict-first**: in one transaction, a Verdict on sample 2 and then a new revision of its target. Not both written.
- **S-neg-take-revision-first**: in one transaction, a new revision of sample 3's target and then a Verdict on sample 3. Not both written.
- **S-neg-take-fields**: through the hook, the taking of sample 4 by setting its reviewer fields, then a new revision of its target, in one transaction. Not both. If the build has no such form (the hook says `none`), the case is not applicable, and a notice and this specification say so.

**Coverage.** **N28-pre**, **N28-S-pre**, **S-pre**, **S-take-pre**: `could_not_check` when an object is absent or a fixture cannot be planted.

## Could not check

The test gives COULD NOT CHECK when `artifact_revision` (with `artifact_id` and `revision`) or `review` is absent, and part B also when `verdict` or `work` is absent; part A2 when one of its functions or tables is absent (`S-pre`); part B2 when its stream, tables or functions are absent or its decisions do not each draw a sample (`S-take-pre`). A case gives `could_not_check` when its target cannot be planted. S-ccc until the hook `sample_marker_unreadable` is filled, and S-neg-take-fields until the hook `take_sample` is filled (W-C02-10's record). S-neg-open-transfer until `transfer_review` exists and refuses kind `sample` by name. Parts B and B2 give COULD NOT CHECK wherever the harness cannot run. While any of these remains, the rule's part it covers rests on no run, and the record says so.

## Not covered

- A sample's other fields at opening (`claim`, `criterion`, `use`, `basis_refs`): W-C02-10's, with DM-08 (EV-C02-008 section 4 item 7).
