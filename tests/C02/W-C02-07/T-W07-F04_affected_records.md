## Could not check
The test gives COULD NOT CHECK when any of these is absent: `affected_entities`, `explain_paths`, `relation`, `eval_run`, `work_revision` or `issue_env_token` (F04-pre). F04-m4-kur gives `could_not_check` until the hook `installation_mission` is filled (G21), and each F04-m4 case gives it when its control call is not accepted, or when `relate` is absent or cannot be called in interface v1's form. The same holds when a fixture cannot be planted, or when a call cannot be formed or is refused for its token: the case gives `could_not_check` with the reason, and a refusal is never read as a pass. Before the build exists, the test gives COULD NOT CHECK.
