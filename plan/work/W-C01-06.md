Row 1's result is recorded in an evidence file under `evidence/C01/` with the common evidence envelope (plan Section 8 item 9: the source commit, the deployment configuration, the criterion version, the input, the actual observation, the raw evidence ID and the independence level); its deployment configuration names the probe environment, the routine, the repositories attached and the Claude Code version, and its raw evidence is the probe sessions' own records on their `claude/probe-c01-…` branches.

Success condition (plan C01 row 1, verbatim):

> A cloud session started by a routine can run more than one work item from the queue; how long it runs, whether the sandbox is paused, and that the next session continues correctly through a structured hand-over are observed (probe: P2 in `devos-probe-a`, with P3's queue standing in for the work queue; two runs in a row, the second continuing from the first one's hand-over record on its probe branch)

It counts as met only when every part of it is observed and recorded; the record shows at least:

- two runs in a row of the `devos-probe-a` routine, the first running more than one work item from P3's queue;
- how long each session ran, and whether its sandbox was paused;
- that the second session continued correctly from the first one's structured hand-over record on its probe branch: it took up the work the hand-over names as next, without redoing a finished item;
- the hook events that fired at each session's start, and at a resume or a compaction if one occurred, or that none occurred (N-063 D18, U2).

Fail path (plan C01 row 1, verbatim):

> The number of working sessions is increased from the reserve budget; if needed, the options go to a decision, with their cost

If the success condition is not met, a plan-change decision is taken and recorded that applies the fail path above: a plan-change record `plan/decisions/PC-<nn>.md` (plan Section 14; working order section 6) and, where the fail path sends a choice to a decision that is Batu's (working order section 8), its decision record as well.

Whether or not the condition is met, plan 6.4's starting budget is kept, or changed with its reason through plan Section 14, from this measurement (6.4).

A part that could not be observed is recorded as "could not check" (plan Section 8 item 14), never as met: it is handled as a failed part, or, where its components cannot be stood in for, it moves to the stage where they exist (plan C01, the paragraph after the probe setup), and the record names that stage and the work its fail path comes before.

A fresh-context checker that did not produce the result (same session, fresh-context subagent; declared, Ek A 5.3; PC-06) judges the record against this condition and plan C01 row 1, at the head that records it, and states its independence level.
