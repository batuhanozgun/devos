# W-C02-23 · PR-A2: the six role packages in .claude/roles/, their check and its merge-gate call (SB-COR 2.3)

**What it is.** SB-COR step 2.3 (`plan/SB-COR_Correction_Plan.md`): one package per role, `.claude/roles/<role>.md`, for the five roles and the executor, each within a byte limit that a static check enforces at every change that can break it. One class-high pull request (PR-A2) writes the six packages, copied by tool from the texts the roles gave in EV-C02-028 part 9, the check `tools/check_role_packages.py`, and one call of it in `tools/merge_gate.py`. The approach was agreed after two team rounds (EV-C02-028 parts 2 to 10; version 2 in part 10.1, pasted below). Delivery of the packages at a role's start is W-C02-25's, not this item's.
