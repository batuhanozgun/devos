# W-C01-25 · Phase-A probe (PC-16)

Plan C01, "Phase-A probe" (PC-16; CHK-C00-046 condition 5): "A C01 task, not a row", which probes the behaviour of the C07 measures that need no C02–C06 component (measure 1's real-task part and measures 2, 3, 6, 7, 8 and 9; the first table under plan Section 9's ordering principle). "It needs no probe setup, so it does not wait for Batu's; it is done before C02 starts." Its criteria, budget and checker are fixed in the plan and copied below unchanged. Its result is input to C02, not a gate (PC-16 place 9), and it is not C07's result: N-101 on `plan/work/C07.md` keeps the probe's question and result out of C07's own test. It reads the library directly, so its public records carry only DevOS's own synthesis and source identifiers (plan Section 0.5).

## Acceptance

<!-- acceptance -->
The phase-A probe runs as plan C01 fixes it:

- **Question:** one real SOUL development question, picked by a subagent whose task is discovery, from SOUL's purpose (Section 1.1) and the library, not by the builder or Batu (Section 1.2).
- **Run:** subagents of the working session, each with its role stated in its task, read the library directly and produce a sourced result; a fresh subagent that sees only that result and its records then continues the work from it. What goes into `devos` is DevOS's own synthesis and source identifiers only (Section 0.5).
- **Success criteria**, fixed here, before the run: (a) the result names its material gaps and wrong assumptions, each with its source, or says that none was found and how it searched (measure 1); (b) every prerequisite it adds answers "which decision or action would be wrong without this?" (K-1 item 3; measure 2); (c) at least one decision in it is changed, limited or justified by library content it cites by passage (measures 3 and 8); (d) the fresh subagent continues from the result alone, without redoing its research; (e) an error, if one occurs, is followed to its failure class or to a capability gap candidate (measure 6), and a squeeze, if one occurs, leads first to a question about the frame (measure 9); if neither occurs, that is recorded; (f) for every decision in it that Appendix B 3.17's class rule would make `high_impact`, the library or current outside sources were researched and the decision reflects it, or the result records that no such decision arose (measure 7).
- **Budget:** at most 12 subagent runs, the checker's not counted. When it is spent, the probe stops, and what it reached is recorded as partial, never as met.
- **Checker:** a fresh-context checker that took no part judges the result against (a) to (f) and states its independence level (Section 8 item 7).
- **Use:** the result, met or not, enters C02's work list, which asks again each record family's first need (the ordering principle); it is not C07's result, and C07 runs as planned.

Its question, its result and the probe checker's verdict on it are recorded in an evidence file under `evidence/C01/` with the common evidence envelope (plan Section 8 item 9: the source commit, the deployment configuration, the criterion version, the input, the actual observation, the raw evidence ID and the independence level), before C02 starts. Every subagent run is recorded (its role as stated in its task, its task and its agent reference), so the budget count can be checked; the discovery subagent's choice of the question is recorded with its reasons. The probe checker's verdict states, for each of (a) to (f), met, not met or not shown; a probe stopped by its budget is recorded as partial, never as met. The result, met or not, is entered in a note on `plan/work/C02.md`, which C02's work list reads. What the probe writes to `devos` is DevOS's own synthesis and source identifiers only, and passes the guard's leak check. This item asks that the probe ran, was judged and reached C02, not that its criteria were met (PC-16 place 9). A fresh-context checker that is not the probe's checker and took no part in the probe (same session, fresh-context subagent; declared, Ek A 5.3; PC-06) judges the record against this condition and plan C01's "Phase-A probe", at the head that records it, and states its independence level.
