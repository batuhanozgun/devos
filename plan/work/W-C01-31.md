# W-C01-31 · Re-plan of C01's work list under FR-05 and PC-21

FR-05 "What follows" (2) (L-179) and PC-21 (L-180) change how C01 establishes the probe setup and rows 1 to 5, 9, 12, 13 and 17 and N-109. The work list that W-C01-01 wrote (CHK-C01-003 to CHK-C01-006) was built on the earlier probe setup. This item disposes of every C01 item under the new frame, and carries the obligations CHK-C01-022 left for it (L-180, "Carried to the re-plan").

Under D-017's standing constraint, the item works from the work list in `plan/work/C01.md` (each item's title, the plan text or note it serves, its `depends_on`) and from PC-21, not from a detailed reading of the earlier probe material; an item kept whose acceptance block names something PC-21 removes is flagged, and disposed of when that item starts, one item at a time.

## Acceptance

<!-- acceptance -->
1. **Disposition.** Every C01 item from W-C01-03 to W-C01-30 is disposed of against PC-21 (plan Section 9, C01, the PC-21 note) and FR-05, as one of: kept; kept with its `depends_on` changed; or cancelled (`execution: cancelled`), with its reason in its file and its acceptance block unchanged. Each disposition is listed in the work list of `plan/work/C01.md` with a one-line reason. A kept item whose acceptance block may name a component PC-21 removes (P1, P3, the probe-only guard rule, a second probe environment, an active trial across environments) is flagged there, to be disposed of when it starts.
2. **Replacement.** Where a cancelled item's work is still needed under PC-21, a new item replaces it. Each new item has an acceptance condition derived from PC-21's item for its row and from the plan row's success condition and fail path as PC-21 takes them; it names the plan text and the notes that the cancelled item served (from the work list's "Plan text or note served" column), so that no obligation is dropped; and it states whether it needs Batu's routine (PC-21 item 1).
3. **Pre-C02 obligations.** Two named items carry the obligations PC-21 puts before C02, each with its deadline written as "before C02 opens DevOS's environments": DevOS's own tests showing that the guard denies a tool not on its list, never answers "ask", and denies when it fails or runs past its time limit (PC-21 item 5); and row 17's account-change option, prepared as Batu's decision under Appendix E section 3 (PC-21 item 7).
4. **Record alignments** (CHK-C01-022 findings 5, 9 and 10). D-019's alternative (b) and PC-21's `reopen_if` cite N-109's "Neither" rule and `plan/work/C00.md`'s rule for the non-acceptance case; D-017's closing paragraph matches its `superseded_in_part_by`; D-019's clause "the risk exists in the same way for all his use of the platform" is narrowed to what the evidence carries. A change to D-019's text after it was posted is stated in the log entry, and if it changes what Batu is asked, he is told in one line on issue #6.
5. **Order.** The work list's order and critical path are rewritten for the new items (plan C01's purpose: rows 1 to 4 and 17 first); `python3 tools/records.py render` shows the frontier consistent with them.
6. **No loosening.** No acceptance block of an existing item is changed or removed; no condition is loosened; C01's Acceptance line and its copy stand as written.
7. **Check.** A fresh-context checker judges the result against points 1 to 6 at a fixed head and states its independence level (same session, fresh-context subagent; declared, Ek A 5.3; PC-06), working at the level of need (D-017).
