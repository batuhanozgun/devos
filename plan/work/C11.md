**C01 row 16's moved parts: the second model's limits and the key's tier, observed when the key exists** (W-C01-23; `evidence/C01/EV-C01-014_W-C01-23_row16_free_tier.md`; 2026-10-11). C01 also could not check the free tier's numbers: the provider publishes none (its rate-limits page, updated 2026-10-09, says they "can be viewed in Google AI Studio", apply per project and "are not guaranteed"), and AI Studio's view needs a signed-in account (EV-C01-014 point 2). When Batu creates the Gemini key, before the first request: (1) the builder reads the provider's terms, rate-limits, pricing and billing pages again, with their dates (plan 0.3 item 12), and gives Batu, with his step, the conditions he accepts on his account: age 18 or older; use "for professional or business purposes, not for consumer use"; an available region; content and responses used to improve Google's products, with human review; no sensitive, confidential or personal information sent; (2) the key's project is on the Free tier with no billing account linked, since the API is a paid service only through a project with an active billing account, and creating the key asked for no payment method, fee or billing account; (3) the models that plan 5.8's two jobs use are named from the free-tier list read then (on 2026-10-09 the newest Pro model was not on it), and the key project's limits for each (requests per minute and per day, tokens per minute) are read in AI Studio with their date and recorded. **Then:** if (2) does not hold, if (1)'s reading shows a use of the content sent beyond the one B2 weighed, or if (3)'s limits are lower than plan 5.8 assumes ("a few dozen requests a day on some models"), row 16's fail path applies, "To a decision, with its cost", before any work relies on the second model; a cost or a change of B2 is Batu's (working order section 8). If a condition in (1) does not hold for him, B2 returns to him as his decision before the key is used. Owner: the executor. Deadline: when the key is created (plan Section 12 item 5), before C11 tasks 3 to 6 rely on the second model.
