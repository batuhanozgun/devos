## Acceptance

<!-- acceptance -->
Row 2's result is recorded in an evidence file under `evidence/C01/` with the common evidence envelope (plan Section 8 item 9: the source commit, the deployment configuration, the criterion version, the input, the actual observation, the raw evidence ID and the independence level); its deployment configuration names the probe environment, the routine, the repositories attached and the Claude Code version, and its raw evidence is the probe sessions' own records on their `claude/probe-c01-…` branches.

Success condition (plan C01 row 2, verbatim):

> In a routine with its connectors removed, and with the repository's permission rules, no connector tool can be called (probe: P2; the routine's layer and the guard's layer are observed apart)

It counts as met only when every part of it is observed and recorded; the record shows at least:

- in a session of each probe routine, the attempt to call a connector tool, and its answer at the routine's layer (with the repository's permission rules) apart from the guard's answer;
- that no connector tool could be called;
- no name of a service connected to Batu's account (the service-name check passes on every probe branch).

Fail path (plan C01 row 2, verbatim):

> Account-level options to Batu (with their effect on his other chats)

If the success condition is not met, a plan-change decision is taken and recorded that applies the fail path above: a plan-change record `plan/decisions/PC-<nn>.md` (plan Section 14; working order section 6) and, where the fail path sends a choice to a decision that is Batu's (working order section 8), its decision record as well.

The account-level options go to Batu as his decision, each with its effect on his other chats (working order section 8; plan Section 12 item 6).

A part that could not be observed is recorded as "could not check" (plan Section 8 item 14), never as met: it is handled as a failed part, or, where its components cannot be stood in for, it moves to the stage where they exist (plan C01, the paragraph after the probe setup), and the record names that stage and the work its fail path comes before.

A fresh-context checker that did not produce the result (same session, fresh-context subagent; declared, Ek A 5.3; PC-06) judges the record against this condition and plan C01 row 2, at the head that records it, and states its independence level.
