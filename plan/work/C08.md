**SOUL does not oblige its user to turn off their own skills** (D-029 (b), Batu, 2026-10-12, in the working session; L-263). Batu's words: "Soul’da da bu skilleri kaldırmak gerekirse bu çok mantıklı değil, çünkü soul herhangi bir kullanıcı tarafından kullanılabilir ve soul kullanıcının skillerini kaldırmak zorunda tutmamalı kullanıcıyı. Bence kalmalı skiller." ("If these skills had to be removed in SOUL too, that would not make much sense, because SOUL can be used by any user, and SOUL should not oblige the user to remove their skills. I think the skills should stay.") Criterion 2 has SOUL set up by others with their own accounts, whose account skills, plugins and connectors reach its sessions as they reach DevOS's (EV-C01-026; PC-39). C08's open-source preparation (the installation document) asks no user to turn off their skills or plugins; whatever keeps SOUL's sessions safe from them is part of SOUL (C03's statement, N-187), and the installation document states the residual instead. Deadline: C08's open-source preparation; it enters the SOUL requirements record when that is written.
