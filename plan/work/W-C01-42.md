# W-C01-42 · Keeping DevOS's three environments apart inside one account under D-020 (a): a structure sized by need

Admitted 2026-10-11 (L-185) from Batu's answer to D-020: option (a), all three environments in his account at no extra cost, with his direction to find a suitable, mechanical structure for (a), sized by need and without running into the host's automatic content review (D-020 `answer_original_tr`; D-017). Serves row 17's fail path as PC-21 item 7 takes it (W-C01-36, EV-C01-003), criteria 8 and 30, plan 6.7 and 7.3. Consumed by C02, which builds DevOS's environments and their tokens (N-131), and by C03's test 3. Deadline: before C02 builds DevOS's environments.

## Acceptance

<!-- acceptance -->
1. ✔ For each pair of DevOS's environments (working–audit, working–exam, audit–exam), the design states, citing the plan's own text (criteria 8 and 30; plan 6.7 and 7.3; Appendix A where it bears), what must not pass or happen between them, and the mechanism that meets each; for each mechanism it names its enforcement layer (ledger rule 4) and whether it holds outside the files DevOS can change (a database role or token, GitHub's settings, the platform's per-environment settings), is documented by the platform (source and reading date), or is DevOS's own.
2. ✔ It prefers, in this order: a design choice that removes the need (what must not be read is never placed where it could be read); a mechanism the platform or an external service already provides and documents; DevOS's existing parts, configured per environment. It adds no new rule family to the guard (FR-05 B) and makes no trial across environments (FR-05 A); each mechanism it proposes names the need it serves, and what it leaves out as more than the need is listed with the reason.
3. ✔ What remains kept apart only by DevOS's own measures under (a) is listed as the residual Batu accepted with D-020, re-assessed with D-003 at C03; a mechanism that makes a breach visible after the fact rather than impossible is called detection, never prevention.
4. ✔ Its consequences are carried as notes to the stages that build or test them (at least C02 and C03's test 3; C05 where exams are concerned), each with its deadline; plan text it would change is named for a plan change under Section 14 and is not changed by this item.
5. ✔ It is written at the level of need (D-017; Batu's direction in D-020's answer): configuration and information-flow design from the plan and the documentation; no probing of the platform, no listing of the account's sessions or routines, no detailed material on ways around a control.
Every result is recorded with the common evidence envelope (plan Section 8 item 9), and every fact taken from the documentation carries the claim of PC-21 item 9 (source, reading date, "not independently tested", the qualifiers). A fresh-context checker judges the result against this condition at a fixed head and states its independence level (same session, fresh-context subagent; declared, Ek A 5.3; PC-06), at the level of need (D-017).
