# W-C02-12 · The three environments and their tokens, with the real-token tests (task 7)

Batu creates `devos-calisma`, `devos-denetim` and `devos-sinav` with the settings W-C02-05 gives, runs in each the one SQL line that issues its token, stores it as W-C02-05 designs, and issues the `devos_kurulum` token and the CI key as designed ("Batu's steps" rows 2 to 4; Section 12 item 1 as the plan change adjusts it), each step in the form of Appendix E section 7, with N-136's text (S4 (d), S4 (e), S6). The builder never sees a value and tests only that each token exists and works with the expected authority (6.3). Each environment's real token is then tried from inside that environment, by the narrow step W-C02-05 designs ("Batu's steps" row 5): the Acceptance's lines "✘ With each environment's real token, an operation that requires another environment's authority is rejected" and "✘ An operation with another session's claim, and with a fake session ID, is rejected", N04's positive (the audit environment's record accepted) and its negatives, N17, and the runs with the `devos_kurulum` token N-136 names. A token that leaves its field is revoked and renewed (6.3). Owner: the executor. Deadline: before W-C02-13 and W-C02-14.
