# W-C01-32 · Routine design: the one ordinary routine of PC-21 item 1, its runs and Batu's step

Replaces W-C01-03 under PC-21 item 1. Serves: plan C01's probe setup as PC-21 item 1 changes it; the routine parts of rows 1, 2, 4, 5, 7 (session part), 8 (the marks of 5.6 and 6.8), 9 (whether hooks are active) and 13; Section 12 item 0 as its PC-21 note changes it; and the notes W-C01-03 served (N-063, N-073 and N-105, where they bear on the routine). FR-04's items 1 to 4 (a stable routine prompt that reads a checked control file on `main`; how runs start; run identity) may be reused where they serve one routine; FR-04 item 5 and the parts of item 6 that name removed components do not apply.

## Acceptance

<!-- acceptance -->
1. ✔ A design, written before any run, for one routine in `devos-kurulum`, attached to `devos` only, with every connector removed and no token: its stored prompt; the run's bans (it records only what the session observes about itself; it never writes a token, a secret or anything derived from one; it writes only to its own `claude/probe-c01-…` branch; it never lists, reads or messages the account's other sessions or routines, and reads no content from Batu's other repositories); the observable by which a run shows the environment it runs in, resting on no unstated platform fact (for example a non-secret variable of `devos-kurulum`); how it reads its steps; how runs start and how many, within the daily routine limit; and, for each row it serves, what a run records and what success and failure look like, taken from the row's success condition and fail path as PC-21 gives them.
2. ✘ The design contains no active trial across environments and no step that needs a token, a second environment or a probe schema.
3. ✔ Batu's step (create the routine; remove its connectors; start it as designed; delete it when C01 ends) is written for him step by step (Appendix E section 7), in Turkish.
4. ✔ Every input is synthetic and labelled so (plan Section 8 item 13).
Every result is recorded with the common evidence envelope (plan Section 8 item 9), and every fact taken from the documentation carries the claim of PC-21 item 9 (source, reading date, "not independently tested", the qualifiers). A fresh-context checker judges the result against this condition at a fixed head and states its independence level (same session, fresh-context subagent; declared, Ek A 5.3; PC-06), at the level of need (D-017).
