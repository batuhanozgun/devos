# W-C02-15 · Installation tooling: N-127's planted cases and N-198's decision

N-127: the planted cases X6 and Y7 added to the discipline checks' tests (`tools/test_records_disciplines.py`, `tools/test_subagent_audit.py`), whose first trigger passed twice without them (R4 "Flags", 2). N-198 (N-129 on `plan/work/C01.md`): the executor decides, with its reason, whether a reason after "no" is allowed in a discipline answer, and changes the checks if it is; such a change does not make outputs rejected before it into results (Appendix G G8 item 3). Class high (`tools/**`). Owner: the executor. Deadline: before W-C02-14 (the ledger moves) and before C02 closes.

Changes to checks, authority and policy are high-impact changes."
