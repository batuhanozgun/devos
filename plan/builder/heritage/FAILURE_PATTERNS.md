# Failure patterns of the builder (heritage)

**Status:** see `plan/ledger.md`, Governing documents. **Scope:** installation; a candidate for DevOS's learning records (C10). **What it is:** the one home of the builder's known failure patterns (`plan/builder/w-c00-12/02_memory.md` §3, family `FP-nn`; R-R7 in `04_roles.md` §4). `tools/boot_map` prints every row at session start, qualified rows first, candidates labelled. **Rules for this file:** a row's `Status` is `candidate` until someone other than its producer qualifies it; `Qualified by` then names that session or review, and `tools/check_records.py chain` fails when it equals `Produced by` or when a `qualified` row has no qualifier. Rows FP-01 to FP-10 are `briefs/w-c00-12/RUN_BRIEF.md` §5's ten patterns; rows FP-11 to FP-20 are the findings that `04_roles.md` §4 names. A finding that is one more instance of an existing pattern is added to that row's sources, not as a new row.
