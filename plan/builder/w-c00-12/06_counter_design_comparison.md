## 2. Where the two designs converge

Two designs made separately agree on these, which raises confidence in them (it does not prove them: same model family, BP-07):

1. One authoritative home per fact; other copies are generated or checked (CD §7.1; draft M-R1).
2. File records shaped like plan Ek B families, so the C02 transfer is a mapping (CD P11; draft piece 1 §2, §10).
3. "Ready" is derived, never stored; the frontier lists, for every non-ready item, why (CD §4.1; draft W-R2).
4. Typed edges: only hard prerequisites affect readiness; provenance (`discovered_from`/`from:`) never implies order; hierarchy edges do not block (both cite the `beads` study).
5. Execution, "finished" and "accepted" are separate; acceptance never by the producer (CD §5; draft W-R1).
6. A parent needs its own composition verdict (CD §4.5; draft W-R4).
7. Plan changes are candidates decided by the plan level's owner (CD §4.5; draft piece 2 §5).
8. Zoom: all thirteen stages always visible; decompose only the active branch (CD §4.3; draft piece 2 §7).
9. Open notes are fields of the item they concern (CD §4.4; draft M-R3).
10. A generated task card/brief carries goal chain, parent, siblings, downstream and notes; `create_session` is gated on a generated brief identifier (CD §5, §6.1; draft W-R5, W-R6).
11. The common floor of Ek A §2 and Ek D §2 is carried by reference, not copied.
12. Heritage has candidate and qualified states, and qualification is by someone other than the producer.
13. Typed record changes (addition/correction/supersession/retirement) (CD §7.2; draft M-R5).
14. A chain check from the always-loaded root to every authoritative record (CD §7.3; draft M-R4).
15. The standing dispatcher and the heartbeat are removed; each wait gets a self-armed `send_later` wake; S4 self-succession through the `parent_session_id` hand-over (CD §10, §16.1; draft piece 5).
16. `send_later` IDs are recorded as owned (CD §8.2; draft M-R11).
17. Roles from demonstrated demand: producer, verifier, counter-designer, probe, researcher; none of the plan's 18 DevOS roles for the builder (CD §5; draft piece 3 §2).
18. Every rule carries a scope (installation, DevOS, SOUL) and every installation mechanism names its successor stage (CD §0, §15; draft "Scope and hand-over" tables).
19. Measured timestamps, checked mechanically (CD M-15; draft M-R9).
20. `/goal` is not the stop authority; a check script's output is (CD §16.3; the operating model's R1 already works this way).

## 4. Net effect on the size of the design (revision 2; superseded by `12_tranche_plan.md` for revision 3)

Revision 1 understated the growth (critic C-15, blocking): it listed thin removals and omitted about fifteen additions. This count is complete for the mechanisms that run (hooks, scripts, checks, roles); record fields and format rules are counted where they need a check.

**Carriers.** Current system: 2 hooks (allow list, recorder), 4 scripts (`builder_check.sh`, `test_tool_allowlist.sh`, `check_service_names.sh`, `check_dispatcher_pr.sh`), a dispatcher session and 2 routines. Counter-design: 3 hooks, 7 scripts, 1 workflow, 5 role templates. **Merged design:** 3 hooks (allow list with boot, compaction and brief gates; recorder; receipts), 5 scripts (`tools/boot`, `tools/records.py`, `tools/check_records.py`, the extended stop check, the hook test; the service-name check is called by the stop check), no workflow, no dispatcher session, no standing routine, 5 role definitions (Verifier as session and as subagent, Counter-designer, Probe, Triager, Researcher; one file each, the Verifier in two forms).

**Checks inside those carriers** (the real growth): boot gate, compaction gate (instructed until observed), brief gate, reading gate, stop reasons with wake requirement, unrecorded-answer read, verdict binding, citation check, path class with sampling, squeeze, kinds, views, chain, `docstatus` and markers, one timestamp rule, map, basis staleness, probe-before-build, decomposition depth, owner reason for `class: batu`, triage presence, lens dispositions (only if T-07 passes), usage filter and Usage-row check. The hook-integrity register (D-15) is not among them. That is about twenty rules in two scripts and one hook, against about six today.

**Removed relative to today and the draft:** the dispatcher session, its heartbeat and reset routines, `check_dispatcher_pr.sh`, Appendix R2, the channel stamp and sync line proposed in the draft, the draft's status-based staleness, the inbox, the Recoverer role, and the hand-written Next action row and `DURUM.md`.

**Deferred with a re-admission trigger** (from either design): CI workflow and required checks (D-08), Actions watchdog (D-28), role profiles (D-13), deny layer (D-13), protected-path gate (D-14), hook-time usage audit (D-16), reviewer limit (D-29), verifier-frozen criteria (D-31), issue status header (D-23), push-remote check (D-38), single-writer rule (D-35).

**Judgement.** The merged design is larger than the draft and, in rules, larger than the counter-design's core; in carriers it is smaller than the counter-design (no workflow, fewer scripts). Every remaining rule names a failure that occurred or an acceptance clause. Whether twenty rules in three carriers is proportionate to the work they protect is a question the outside review must answer explicitly, and the process-overhead check at closure (operating model §4.5) measures it in practice. If the review judges it too heavy, the first candidates to cut are those whose basis is a single incident: decomposition depth (D-41), card length (D-36), the reading gate (D-12, which a §2-only import would replace).
