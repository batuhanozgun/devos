# D-010

**What Batu decided.** The installation is run by one working session. It reads the plan itself, takes the next step in plan order, splits the work and gives it to role-defined subagents and workflows (Üretici, Araştırmacı, Sınayıcı, Denetçi, Karşı tasarımcı; a Test tasarımcısı from C02), checks results with a fresh-context Denetçi, merges to `main`, records in the ledger and goes on. The builder's earlier operating model (the session chain, successor runs, leases for runs, dispatcher and heartbeat, separate review sessions) is retired, not kept beside the new way. Batu opens the working session once from the app and pastes the first message, a `/goal` for the whole installation. The full decision is the approved summary (Turkish original and English rendering named above), 39 items plus the addendum E1-E3.

**His own decisions in it.** Item 34: about one minute of auto mode in his conversation session, once, so that the transition can be reviewed by one independent review session under the rules it replaces (an exception to D-008, for that one action). Item 35: the plan changes of section G, through plan section 14 (record PC-06), including his parts of PC-04 and PC-05. Item 36: the extra usage of workflows and parallel subagents on his shared allowance.

**What it does not decide.** How the new files are written is the builder's technical work, checked by Denetçi subagents and then by the one independent review before merge. PC-01 is not Batu's decision (addendum E1); its per-stage `/goal` is dropped in the transition's plan-change record. D-009 is withdrawn: the need to start sessions returns at C01 as a narrower question.

**Facts reported by Batu on 2026-10-05.** He switched his conversation session to Auto and turned on Opus 5.5 ultracode, then switched it back to Accept edits at the builder's request, until the review session is started.
