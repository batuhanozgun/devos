# PC-36

**Authority.** W-C01-17's success condition is not met. The session's effect on the usage allowance, and the sharing with Batu's own usage as observed, could not be checked, because no reading a session has shows the fraction of a window used or separates DevOS's share from Batu's (EV-C01-024 sections 4 and 6). So this plan change applies row 10's fail path, "Capacity is worked out by estimate; the uncertainty is recorded" (plan line 954), to the plan places that rest on a usage observation. Old text is at `main` `fa6d430`, with that commit's line numbers. No acceptance condition changes: row 10 and W-C01-17's block are applied, not edited. No text labelled [Batu kararı] changes. `plan/DevOS_Kurulum_Plani.md`, `plan/Ek_G_Isleyis_Kurallari.md` and this record are class normal.
