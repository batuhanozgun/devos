# PC-23

No result of any item is affected: no acceptance block changes, and the changed texts govern work from this merge on. Old text is the text at `main` `5d0cc58`; each place below gives the replaced sentence and its replacement.

**Place 1, Appendix D section 1, the row "Evaluating the nine questions in every turn …".**
Old: "The evaluation is made at the start of every new request, work item or turn, after every material change, at a new constraint, and before the first step of a new kind of action (an external effect, a merge, a persistent or irreversible write); it cannot be skipped …"
New: "The evaluation is made at the start of every new request, work item or turn, after every material change, at a new constraint, before the first step of a new kind of action (an external effect, a merge, a persistent or irreversible write), and before entering a wait or ending work whose next step waits on a schedule, a person or an outside event (PC-23); it cannot be skipped …"

**Place 2, Appendix D section 2 (the draft `CLAUDE.md`), item "3. Thinking disciplines — trigger questions", first sentence.**
Old: "At the start of every new request, work item or turn, after every material change of plan or evidence (new information, a changed plan; a tool result only when it changes the plan or the evidence), at a new constraint, and before the first step of a new kind of action (an external effect, a merge, a persistent or irreversible write), evaluate ALL nine questions before the work or that step goes on."
New: "At the start of every new request, work item or turn, after every material change of plan or evidence (new information, a changed plan; a tool result only when it changes the plan or the evidence), at a new constraint, before the first step of a new kind of action (an external effect, a merge, a persistent or irreversible write), and before entering a wait or ending work whose next step waits on a schedule, a person or an outside event, evaluate ALL nine questions before the work or that step goes on. At a wait, also name when it ends and by what, what would end it sooner at the same quality, and whose call that is."
The nine trigger questions themselves are unchanged (PC-17's scope rule).

**Place 3, Appendix D section 3, D3 "Application" item 2.**
Old: "… Is there a simpler or more reliable way? A method that has not been explicitly set as a constraint is not equivalent to the goal."
New: "… Is there a simpler, more reliable or sooner way at the same quality? A parameter that sets when the next condition can be met (a schedule, a limit, a hand-off) is part of the method: state the time it adds and weigh it like any other method choice. A method that has not been explicitly set as a constraint is not equivalent to the goal."

**Place 4, plan 6.12 item 1 ("Premise inventory"), added after its last sentence.**
New: "A parameter that sets when dependent work can next start (a schedule, a limit, a hand-off) is a premise of any design, not only a major one: the inventory gives the time it adds on the critical path, its reason's source with that source's date, and, for a reason taken from the plan, a check against Batu's later decisions (Appendix D, D8 item 3); a design item's acceptance condition asks for these (PC-23)."

**Place 5, working order section 2, "The thinking disciplines in practice".**
Old: "… evaluates all nine trigger questions of Appendix D section 2 item 3 at the start of each work item or task and after each material change of plan or evidence; …"
New: "… evaluates all nine trigger questions of Appendix D section 2 item 3 at the start of each work item or task, after each material change of plan or evidence, and before it enters a wait or ends work whose next step waits on a schedule, a person or an outside event (PC-23); …"

**Place 6, working order section 7, the three temporary states** (as revised for CHK-C01-039 C2 and CHK-C01-040 finding 4).
Old: "… (c) a blocker the executor cannot pass. In each, first finish and merge all work that does not depend on it."
New: "… (c) a blocker the executor cannot pass; (c) also covers a scheduled or outside event the work waits on. As soon as the critical path's next step is seen to wait on one of these, question the wait at once, before other work goes on (PC-23; Appendix D section 2 item 3): when it ends and by what; what would end it sooner at the same quality; and whose call that is. A shortening that is the builder's is made then; one that needs Batu's action goes to him then as one line (section 8), and that wait is (a) until he answers, but only when it saves material time on the critical path and is not already before him or settled by a standing decision of his; otherwise the Wait line says so ("sooner: none; settled by D-nnn", or "already on issue #6"). Meanwhile, and in each state, finish and merge all work that does not depend on it."

**Place 7, working order section 7, the stop's proof.**
Old: "… carries the proof: the state named with its evidence, and the unedited output of `tools/stop_check.sh`, which must end in `STOP_CHECK PASS`."
New: the same, followed by: "The newest log entry then carries a line `- **Wait:** ends <when, by what>; sooner: <option, or none and why>; whose call: <the builder, Batu, or none>` (at the final message: `- **Wait:** none; the goal is met`), the stop message states the same, and `tools/stop_check.sh` fails without the line (PC-23)." (CHK-C01-039 C3 (b))

**Place 8, working order section 4, "Stage close".**

New: "The stage-close checker also samples the stage's discipline records (the log entries' blocks and the helpers' reports), every entry that records a wait among them (PC-23): a "no" where the discipline was needed is a finding (D-016)."

**Place 9, working order section 9, the log entry format, after "`render --check` then counts the entry as answered (N-110)."**
