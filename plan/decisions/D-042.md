**English interpretation:** as in the front matter.

**What the executor answered in the session** (in Turkish; recorded here in English, in substance; the session transcript holds the words): that the deferral had no sound basis. A date or a count reached says nothing about whether the remaining parts are needed. Turning them into notes would leave the problem D-039 named in place while calling the branch closed, which D-039 forbids ("do not return to the work until the correction is finished"). The correction would be finished today, with the method changed to fit: parallel triage of the remaining items, and the conditions written while the earlier products are checked. No binding check would be cut.

**What changes** (the executor's disposition, L-445): SB-COR plan 0.4's budget clause ("the branch closes by 2026-10-26 or after 30 binding verdicts on its parts, whichever comes first; at the budget it closes with what is merged, and the rest becomes notes") is no longer the branch's limit. The branch's limit is now: its parts as planned, finished on 2026-10-20 as the target. Reaching 2026-10-26 or 30 binding verdicts on its parts no longer closes the branch. It opens a frame review (plan 6.12 item 2; working order section 6 item 3), which looks for why the parts are not converging; that review never defers a part. The loop limits per part (working order section 6 item 2) are unchanged. Missing the target "today" is not a reason to defer or to cut a check either. Should it happen, the log says so, with the parts still open and why, and the work goes on.

**Not changed:** plan 0.3 items 1 and 6 [Batu kararı] (quality is not lowered for convenience or time; an unsolved problem is not presented as solved); every binding check and every acceptance condition; the three temporary states of working order section 7 (a usage limit, a wait for him, a blocker), which can still hold the work, and which the stop message then names.
