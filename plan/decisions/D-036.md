# D-036

**Soru (Türkçe, #6 için).** Planın ilkelerinde "Ücret, farklı bir araç ya da bir kısıtın değişmesi gerekiyorsa bu Batu'ya kazancı, gerekçesi, alternatifi ve bedeliyle sunulur" yazıyor; buradaki "farklı bir araç" yalnız paraya, hesaplarına, verilerine ya da diğer işlerine dokunan araç değişikliklerini mi kapsasın?
- (a) Evet: yalnız bunlar sana gelir; diğer araç değişikliklerine kurucu karar verir ve gerekçesini kayda yazar (önerim).
- (b) Hayır: her araç değişikliği sana gelir (bugünkü yazılış).
- (c) (a) gibi, ama diğer araç değişiklikleri de sana tek satırla bildirilir.

Cevap: `D-036: a`, `D-036: b` ya da `D-036: c`.

**What is asked (one sentence).** In your principle that "if a fee, a different tool or a change to a constraint is needed, it is presented to you with its gain, rationale, alternative and cost", should "a different tool" cover only changes of tool that touch money, your accounts, your data or your other work (a, recommended), every change of tool (b), or (a) with every other change told to you in one line (c)?

**Why it is yours.** It is your text: the principles the plan follows are labelled your decision, and only you can change them. Since D-033 we talk over the parts of your decisions that constrain the builder in technical matters. No option asks anything of you.

**What you need to know.**
- The fee and the constraint in the same sentence stay as they are under every option: an expense is never accepted on your behalf, and a need is never silently cut back.
- Read word for word, "a different tool" sends every change of a tool the plan names to you, including the many tools the plan itself marks as a proposal that "changes if something better is shown". That is a technical choice, which your principle 5 and D-033 give to the builder.
- So far no change of tool has come to you on this clause alone; the cases in the records are all about fees and constraints. The cost is in the future: the next change of a named tool would come to you.
- Under (a) "your accounts" includes the tools you yourself use (the Claude app, GitHub and the decision issue, the channels on your phone): a change of any of them still comes to you.

**Options.**
- **(a) Only changes of tool that touch money, your accounts, your data or your other work come to you (recommended).**
  - Purpose: keep your matters yours and leave the technical choice of tools to the builder.
  - Benefit: the builder can replace a tool when evidence shows a better one, as the plan's proposals allow, without waiting for you; you are asked only where your money, accounts, data or other work are touched.
  - Cost: risk: the builder could misjudge whether a change touches your matters; each change is recorded with its reason and judged by the checker of the work that makes it, and a misjudged one is a finding that reopens this decision; you hear of other changes only by reading the records; time and money: none.
  - Alternative: (c).
- **(b) Every change of tool comes to you, as written.**
  - Purpose: keep your text word for word.
  - Benefit: you see every change of tool before it is made.
  - Cost: time: a question to you, with its record, for every change of a named tool, and the work that needs it waits for your answer; quality: it asks you technical questions, which your principle 5 says you do not decide; money: none.
  - Alternative: (a).
- **(c) As (a), and every other change of a named tool told to you in one line.**
  - Purpose: as (a), while you still hear of every change.
  - Benefit: you know of every change without being asked to decide.
  - Cost: time: more lines for you to read in the batches; money: none.
  - Alternative: (a).

**Recommendation: (a).** It keeps everything in the clause that is yours (money, a constraint, the need not cut back, and any tool on your accounts or touching your data or other work) and gives the technical rest to the builder, as your principle 5, your principle 0.3 item 11 and D-033 already say. (c) is a fair choice if you want to hear of every change; it adds reading, not safety. The decision is yours.

**If you do not answer.** Nothing waits: the text stays as written, and a change of a named tool comes to you as it says. Silence is not approval: no answer means no option is taken.

**After an answer.** Under (a) or (c): plan 0.3 item 3 and its Turkish original are reworded through the plan's change route, with your Turkish summary (`plan/Summary_for_Batu_TR.md`) kept in step. Under (b): nothing changes.

**How to answer.** Write `D-036: a`, `D-036: b` or `D-036: c` on issue #6 or in the working session.
