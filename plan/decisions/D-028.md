# D-028

**What is asked (one sentence, Appendix E section 3).** Will you press Run now once on the routine's page so that its next run (P4, row 13's two unchecked parts) starts tonight rather than tomorrow at 11:07: (a) yes (recommended), or (b) no, and it runs by itself tomorrow at 11:07?

**Why it is Batu's.** The press is an action on his account, on the routine he created; the builder does not start runs of his routine.

**Options.**
- **(a) Press Run now once now, with no text (recommended).** Purpose: row 13's two unchecked parts observed, and the steps after them (the combined scenario CS1 and CS2, the routine's removal, C01's close) about 14 hours earlier. Benefit: the same run the schedule would start, from the same control file; nothing in what it does changes. Cost: one press, and one run's share of the usage, which the scheduled run would use anyway.

**Recommendation: (a)**, for the same reason as D-021, D-022 and D-027: the press saves material time on the critical path and changes nothing in what the run does; its worst case is (b).

**What you need to know.** Press once and write no text. If nothing appears after the press, do not press again; say so on issue #6. The offer stands until 2026-10-12 at 10:50 his time (07:50Z); after that the scheduled run takes P4. A press needs no reply: the run's own record shows it started.

**If you do not answer.** Nothing is lost: P4 runs by itself on 2026-10-12 at 11:07 his time.

**How to answer.** Press Run now (no reply needed), or write `D-028: b` on issue #6 or in the working session.
