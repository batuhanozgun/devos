# D-012

**What is asked** (one sentence). How should the commits the machine account might make in your research library be reported to you, independently of DevOS?

**Why it is his.** It is the second safeguard of his access decision B3 = (a), and every way to provide it is a setting in his account or a change to his repository.

**Options** (Appendix E section 3):

| | (a) Push e-mails (recommended) | (b) Read-only through an organization | (c) No monitor until C04 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Every push to the library is e-mailed to him | The machine account cannot write to the library at all | Nothing changes now |
| Benefit | Two minutes, free; DevOS cannot turn it off | Prevents instead of reporting | No action |
| Cost | E-mails for ChatGPT's pushes too; no warning if the e-mails stop; the e-mail shows the commit's author, so for an unknown commit he checks the Activity view | An organization, a repository transfer, and ChatGPT's access set up again (its route unverified) | A write by the machine account in C00–C04 would not be reported to him |
| Alternative | (b) later, at C04 | (a) now | (a) |

**What he needs to know.** The library is not attached to the working session yet (D-011 is open). The session will not attach it before this decision is answered with (a) or (b) done, or with (c). The guard already refuses pushes outside `devos`; the residual route is listed in the working order (N-028).

**If he does not answer.** Nothing is lost; the library stays unattached, and the library part of the independent plan review waits, as under D-011. Silence is not approval.

**Steps for (a)** (each with what he should see; Appendix E section 7):
1. On GitHub, open the repository `agentic-os-search` → **Settings**. *He sees the repository's settings page.*
2. In the left menu, under **Integrations**, open **Email notifications**. *He sees an "Address" box.*
3. Write his own e-mail address in "Address", leave "Approved header" empty, and press **Setup notifications**. *The page saves without an error.*
4. When ChatGPT next writes to the library, an e-mail arrives listing the commit. *If none arrives, he tells the session.*
