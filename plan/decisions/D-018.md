# D-018

**What is asked (one sentence, Appendix E section 3).** The host offers an official verification path (named in the flag notice the previous session received); should Batu enrol the account in it, so that DevOS's necessary defensive self-verification is recognised rather than flagged by the host's automatic content review?

**Why it is Batu's.** Enrolling is an account action, with terms or a cost that are not known until he looks (working order section 8).

**Options.**
- **(a) Enrol (recommended).** Benefit: the installation's defensive self-verification (C01 and later) is recognised by the host rather than flagged, so the work is less likely to be interrupted as it was for the previous working session. Cost: the terms and any cost are unknown until Batu opens the path; he decides after seeing them.
- **(b) Do not enrol.** Benefit: nothing is asked of Batu now. Cost: the builder keeps working under the adjusted verification method (D-017), which lowers but does not remove the chance that defensive verification is flagged; if it is flagged again, the work holds and the state shows in `DURUM.md`.

**Recommendation: (a)**, because it addresses the cause rather than only working around it; but it is his, and (b) loses nothing the adjusted method does not already mitigate.

**If Batu does not answer.** Nothing is lost: the installation continues under D-017's method. Silence is not approval; the question stays open in `DURUM.md`.

**How to answer.** On issue #6 or in the working session: `D-018: a` or `D-018: b`.
