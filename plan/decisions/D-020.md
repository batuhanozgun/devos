# D-020

**What is asked (one sentence, Appendix E section 3).** DevOS's audit and exam environments must be kept apart from the working environment, and inside your one Claude account the platform documents no limit between environments, so the separation would rest only on DevOS's own guard: should they stay in your account (no cost), or move to a second Claude account on the Pro plan ($20 a month)?

**Why it is yours.** It costs money and adds an account to your name (working order section 8; plan 0.3 item 3). Row 17's fail path sends this option to you with its effect on your other work.

**Options.**
- **(a) No change.** Purpose: no cost, no new account. Benefit: nothing changes for you. Cost: the audit and exam environments' separation from the working environment rests on DevOS's own guard, a check that runs inside the session and that the builder can change (the same kind of residual you accepted with D-003 until C03); criterion 30's claim ("no role can approve its own change; exam answers out of reach") is then limited to that. It is re-assessed at C03.
- **(b) A second Claude account on Pro, only for the audit and exam environments (recommended).** Purpose: put the environments that judge and examine outside the account where the working environment runs. Benefit: environments, routines and sessions belong to an account (documented), so the working environment's sessions would not have the audit and exam environments in their own account; their usage would also stop drawing on your main account's limits. Cost: $20 a month (price read 2026-10-10, subject to change); one more login to keep; its GitHub connection set up once (it may use the same machine account `batuhanozgun-devos`, which GitHub's terms allow, so GitHub would show both accounts' work as one identity; authority still comes from each environment's database token); whether Pro's usage is enough for the audit and exam work is measured in C06 and C11 (if not, a change comes to you then). The separation is an inference from documented account scope, not a stated guarantee; it is still far stronger than (a).
- **(c) A Team organization.** From $50 a month for two seats. Not recommended: the documentation keeps environments and routines personal to each member anyway, and network secrets are not available on Team.
- **(d) Enterprise.** Per seat plus usage at API rates. Not recommended: costly and open-ended for one person.

**Recommendation: (b)**, because separation of authority is what lets DevOS's reviews and exams be trusted (criterion 30), and (a) leaves it on a check DevOS can edit; the cost is small and does not touch your main account or your other work. (a) stays a reasonable choice if you prefer no new account: then the residual is recorded and looked at again at C03.

**What you need to know.** Nothing changes for your own chats or other projects under either option. Under (b), the account's setup steps (opening it, choosing Pro, connecting GitHub, and later creating the two environments in it) come to you step by step when C02 builds the environments; no key or password is ever written in the chat.

**If you do not answer.** C01 goes on. Before C02 builds DevOS's environments, the answer is needed; until then that work waits.

**How to answer.** On issue #6 or in the working session: `D-020: a` or `D-020: b` (or c, d).
