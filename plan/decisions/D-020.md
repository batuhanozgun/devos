# D-020

**What is asked (one sentence, Appendix E section 3).** DevOS's three environments (working, audit, exam) must each be kept apart from the others, and inside one Claude account the platform documents no limit between environments, so their separation would rest only on DevOS's own guard: should they (a) all stay in your account at no cost, (b) the audit and exam environments move together to one second Pro account ($20 a month, excluding tax), or (c) each of the two move to its own Pro account ($40 a month in all, excluding tax)? A Team or Enterprise organization (d, e) adds no separation here. My recommendation is (c).

**Why it is yours.** It costs money and adds accounts to your name (working order section 8; plan 0.3 item 3). Row 17's fail path sends this option to you with its effect on your other work.

**Options** (prices read 2026-10-10 on claude.com/pricing, "subject to change", excluding applicable tax).
- **(a) No change.** Purpose: no cost, no new account. Benefit: nothing new to set up. Cost: every pair of environments is kept apart only by DevOS's own guard, a check that runs inside the session and that the builder can change (the same kind of residual you accepted with D-003 until C03). Criterion 30 ("Competence is measured with exams that the role under test cannot see; no role can approve its own change") is then claimed only at that level. Effect on your other work: all of DevOS's runs, the audit's and the exam's included, count against your account's usage and limits alongside your own use (documented). Alternative: (b) or (c).
- **(b) One second Pro account for the audit and exam environments together.** Purpose: put the environments that judge and examine outside the account where the working environment runs. Benefit: the working environment is kept apart from the other two by account (an inference from documented account scope, not a stated guarantee). Cost: $20 a month; one more login; its GitHub connection set up once (see the GitHub note below for a possible extra cost). What it does not do: the audit and exam environments still share an account, so the audit environment is kept apart from the exam answers only by the guard, which bears on the same criterion 30. Effect on your other work: the audit and exam runs would draw on the second account's limits rather than yours (inference). Alternative: (c).
- **(c) Two further Pro accounts, one for the audit environment and one for the exam environment (recommended).** Purpose: keep every pair of environments in different accounts. Benefit: each pair is kept apart by account (an inference from documented account scope, not a stated guarantee). Cost: $40 a month; two more logins; two GitHub connections set up once (see the GitHub note below for a possible extra cost); with your own account that makes three, the most one phone number may verify, so if you already have another Claude account on your number, one of them needs a different number. Effect on your other work: the audit and exam runs would draw on their own accounts' limits (inference); your own account carries only the working environment's runs. Alternative: (b), at half the cost, if you accept the audit–exam residual until C03.
- **(d) A Team organization.** Purpose: one organization instead of separate personal accounts. Benefit: none beyond (b) or (c) for this purpose: the documentation keeps environments and routines personal to each member anyway. Cost: from $50 a month for two Standard seats; network secrets are not available on Team, which C02's token design may want (note N-131); whether a Team membership affects your Max account was not read. Alternative: (b) or (c).
- **(e) Enterprise.** Purpose: an organization with admin controls. Benefit: none documented for this purpose beyond (c). Cost: US$20 a seat a month billed annually plus usage at API rates, bounded only by the spend limits its admins set. Alternative: (c).

GitHub for the separate accounts: GitHub's terms allow several users to direct one machine account, so the further accounts may use the same machine account `batuhanozgun-devos`; GitHub would then show all accounts' work as one identity (inference). What no one has read yet is whether one GitHub account can be connected to more than one Claude account. If it cannot, each further Claude account needs its own GitHub identity, and since GitHub's terms allow only one free machine account besides the free personal one, that identity would be a paid GitHub account (price not read) or your personal account: a possible extra cost of (b) and (c), settled before C02 builds the environments. Authority inside DevOS still comes from each environment's own database token, not from the GitHub identity.

**Recommendation: (c).** Separation of authority between all three environments is what lets DevOS's reviews and exams be trusted (criterion 30), and (a) leaves it, and (b) leaves part of it, on a check DevOS itself can change. In my judgement (an assessment, not a measurement) account separation is much stronger than the guard alone, though the documentation does not state it as a guarantee. The cost is $40 a month and two logins you set up once. If you prefer a smaller cost, (b) is reasonable, with the audit–exam residual recorded and looked at again at C03; (a) is reasonable only if you accept all three residuals until C03.

**What you need to know.** Under (b) or (c), whether Pro's usage is enough for the audit and exam work is measured in C06 and C11; if it is not, a change comes to you then. The accounts' setup steps (opening each account, choosing Pro, connecting GitHub, and later creating the environment in it) come to you step by step when C02 builds the environments; no key or password is ever written in the chat.

**If you do not answer.** C01 goes on. Before C02 builds DevOS's environments, the answer is needed; until then that work waits.
