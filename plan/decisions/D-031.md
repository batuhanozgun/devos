# D-031

**The question** (N-129 on `plan/work/C01.md`; N-198 on `plan/work/C02.md`; W-C02-15 point 4). May a discipline answer give a reason after "no" ("Dn: no: <text>"), or does the form stay "Dn: no" with nothing after it, as working order section 9 states it now?

**What D-016 asks for.** Nine answers, one per trigger question, in order; "yes" or "uncertain" carries what was done because of it. A "no" means the question did not apply or the agent is sure it does not; D-016 asks for nothing more from it. The content of every answer is the agent's own judgment, a hint and not evidence (Appendix D section 2), which checkers sample.

**The options.**
- **(a) Keep "Dn: no" with nothing after it** (the present form). Cost: an output whose "no" carries a reason is rejected by `tools/records.py render --check` or `tools/subagent_audit.py disciplines`, and its work is given again to a fresh helper; this happened twice, in W-C01-03's round 2 (L-172, L-173; agents `aad82641009d6300f` and `a7f263717d66fe06b`). The C01 and C02 logs record no later rejection for this cause: the later `DISCIPLINES MISSING` results they record had other causes (tasks without the block, or the tool reading another message; the block given without its bold marker; a report's answers out of order; `plan/ledger/C01-log.md` lines 220, 352 and 835, read 2026-10-12).
- **(b) Allow "Dn: no: <text>".** Benefit: such outputs are kept. Costs: (1) the working order's section 9 (its log-entry line, its verdict template and its "Discipline block"), `CLAUDE.md`, every role file in `.claude/agents/` and the copy of the block in `tools/test_subagent_audit.py` change, all class high; (2) a changed block makes `disciplines` read every earlier task as not carrying it (CHK-C01-009 finding 5), so the audit of earlier helpers needs a second accepted form or loses its reading; (3) a "no" with a reason can be read as more than it says, as if the question had been worked through, when D-016 asks for that only of "yes" and "uncertain".

**Decision: (a).** The form stays "Dn: no" with nothing after it. Reason: the substance D-016 asks for is met either way; the strict form's only cost has occurred twice, both in W-C01-03's round 2 at the block's first use (L-172, L-173), and the logs record none since; option (b) widens six class-high places and breaks the reading of earlier tasks to save that occasional re-run (K-10, the process limit: every new rule, record or process shows which progress of SOUL it serves, and this widening would serve none). The checks already reject "Dn: no, <text>" and "Dn: maybe: <text>"; W-C02-15 adds a planted case for "Dn: no: <text>" in each of the two tools, expected to fail, and a one-line mutant that accepts it, so the decision is pinned by a test.

**Earlier outputs.** Nothing changes for them: outputs rejected under this form, among them those of agents `aad82641009d6300f` and `a7f263717d66fe06b` (L-173), stay rejected and are not cited as results (Appendix G G8 item 3). Since the form does not change, no earlier task or report is read differently.

**Reopen if** a stage-close sample finds that the strict form keeps costing good outputs (more than one re-run in a stage for this cause alone), or D-016's substance changes.
