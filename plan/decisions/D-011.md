# D-011

**What is asked** (one sentence). May this working session run in Auto for one short window, to attach the research library, as a one-time exception to D-008?

**Why it is his.** D-008 is his decision on the permission mode of the sessions on his account, and the library access rests on his decision B3 = (a). A platform rule unknown when he decided now makes D-008 stop the library reading that the plan and B3 assume.

**A correction first** (Appendix E sections 1.4 and 4.4). Appendix F v2.0, step 1, told him that the session adds the library later, read-only. That was wrong: in Accept edits the platform refuses the attach call without a person's approval, and nobody can give it in this session. It should have been known: L-119 had recorded the same refusal for other session tools.

**Options** (Appendix E section 3):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) one Auto window, recommended | attach the library once | C00 step 4 and the C00–C04 library reading go as the plan says; D-008 holds for all other work | Batu: two mode changes and one short message. During the window Claude Code's classifier also judges steps; it has refused a library-related command before with a label only (P5). If the attach call is refused in the window, by the classifier or by the platform, it is not retried: he switches back, and the "if he does not answer" state below applies. The window lasts until he switches back. The session makes the attach call first and tells him the result; if the guard's log still shows Auto after that, it reminds him once and goes on with its work, which the guard still decides call by call while the classifier may also refuse a step (each such refusal is logged). "Once" holds unless the attachment is lost (reopen_if) | (b), (c) |

**Recommendation and reason.** (a). It meets the plan's need with the smallest change to his decision, the same kind of exception he chose in D-010 item 34. (b) cuts or reorders a planned step and only postpones the question; (c) is broader than this need. The session starts that C01 needs will come to him as their own narrow question when C01 starts, with their number known.

**What he needs to know.**
- The attach call asks for read access. Whether the platform enforces "read" is untested (N-028), and the plan records the machine account's access to the library as technically write access (plan 0.5). What keeps the library unwritten: the rule that DevOS never writes to it (plan 0.5; `CLAUDE.md`) and the guard, which denies a plain push to anything but the builder's branches of `devos` (best-effort; a push from an attached library clone is on the guard's "not protected" list). The plan's second safeguard, an independent watcher that tells him about every machine-account commit in the library, is not in place yet (gap G-001, decided at C00 step 7). The third, removing the machine account's access, comes after C04.
- The session does not register the library as a root, so the library's own control files are not loaded as instructions. This is documented by the platform, not observed (EV-C00-001 row 3).
- Nothing else changes.

**If he does not answer.** Work that does not need the library goes on: the translation of the plan (C00 step 0), the ECC comparison from ECC's public source (step 3), the counter-design (step 5) and the premise inventory (step 6). The library part of the independent review (step 4) waits, so C00 cannot close. Silence is not approval; no default applies.

**Steps for (a)** (in Turkish to him; Appendix E section 7). The menu's place is not documented for his app; what to look for: 1. in this session, open the permission-mode menu (it shows the current mode, "Accept edits") and choose "Auto"; afterwards the menu shows "Auto". 2. write "auto" to this session; the session answers that the library is attached, or that the call was refused. 3. in the same menu choose "Accept edits" again; the menu shows "Accept edits". The session checks the mode in the guard's log before and after the call.
