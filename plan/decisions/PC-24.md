# PC-24

**Authority.** W-C01-10's probe part is not met (EV-C01-008 section 6), so this plan change applies row 4's fail path (plan line 947). Its completion branch fires on the observation alone: a subagent call can return before the subagent has finished. Old text is at `main` `680031a`, with that commit's line numbers. No acceptance condition changes. Row 4 and W-C01-10's block are applied, not edited. Every place tightens a rule. `plan/DevOS_Kurulum_Plani.md` and this record are class normal.

### 1. K-7 item 3 (line 384). Labelled: no (K-7 is a [Proposal]).
Old: "Only one writer writes to a product at any one time."
New: "Only one writer writes to a product at any one time; a writer subagent may still write until it has finished (Section 6.5 item 5), which can be long after the call that started it returns."
Old: "… so the database checks only the writer each subagent task declares (Appendix B 3.7), not the write itself."
New: "… so the database checks only the writer each subagent task declares (Appendix B 3.7), not the write itself. A declared writer stays the writer until its subagent has finished or, if it stopped, until what it wrote has been reconciled (Section 6.5 item 6)."
Reason: the fail path's second clause; P1, P3; B2-F2. Stages: C04 (note), C06.

### 2. K-7 item 4 (line 385). Labelled: no.
Old: "A session splits the work into chunks; each chunk passes to a clean-context subagent or to the next session with a structured hand-over record."
New: the same, followed by: "A chunk's result is used only after its subagent has finished (Section 6.5 item 5). A session that closes before a subagent of its own has finished names that subagent in its hand-over, and what the subagent wrote is reconciled before work on the chunk goes on (Section 6.5 item 6)."
Reason: the first and third clauses; P2 (the turn ended while the subagent ran), P5 (reclaimed machine). Stages: C06.

### 3. Section 6.5 item 5 (line 678). Labelled: no.
Old: "It is recorded with the work item (Appendix B 3.7, from C04; PC-08). Built-in helpers that do not load `CLAUDE.md` are not used for role work."
New: "It is recorded with the work item (Appendix B 3.7, from C04; PC-08). A subagent's end is known only from the status the platform gives the coordinator for it: for a foreground call, the call's own result; for a background call, the later completion notification. `completed` means it has finished; a failed or cut-off subagent has stopped (item 6). The return of a background call, which comes at once while the subagent works on, does not show the end; nor does the report the subagent hands back, which can reach the coordinator before the status (C01 #4, observed 11 October 2026; EV-C01-008). Until its subagent has finished, the coordinator does not act as if its result existed; one end that reaches it by more than one signal is acted on once. Built-in helpers that do not load `CLAUDE.md` are not used for role work."
Reason: the first clause, with the signal that defines it; P1, P2, P5. Stages: C04, C06.

### 4. Section 6.5 item 6 (line 679). Labelled: no.
Old: "**Single writer:** Parallel subagents read, research, analyse and review; a product is written by only one subagent at a time. Shared decisions are recorded before writing."
New: "**Single writer:** Parallel subagents read, research, analyse and review; a product is written by only one writer at a time, the coordinator included. No second writer starts on a product while the first may still write: a writer subagent may write until it has finished (item 5). What a subagent that stopped before finishing (failed, cut off, or lost with its session or machine) wrote in the shared checkout or branch is reconciled before work on that product goes on. Shared decisions are recorded before writing."
Reason: the second and third clauses; P3, P4 (the coordinator writes in the same checkout); K-7 item 3 already says "writer". Stages: C06.
