**The guard: a text-free denial when the service-name search times out** (CHK-C00-037 finding 7). In `.claude/hooks/tool_allowlist.py`, `lines_on_main` runs `git grep -e <line> …` over `origin/main` for the lines that name a service; both `leak_tool_checks` and `push_leak_checks` reach it. When that `git grep` ran past its own 30-second limit, the `TimeoutExpired` it raised carried its command, and so those lines, into the L1 denial's `{exc}`; the guard then withheld the whole detail, so the denial read like a match. Now the timeout becomes a `TimeoutError` with a text-free reason. The decision stays a deny under L1 (fail closed), through the existing `except (OSError, …)` of both callers; a spent call budget still raises `OverBudget` as before.
