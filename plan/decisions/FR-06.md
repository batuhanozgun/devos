# FR-06

**Batu's question** (working session, 2026-10-11, verbatim). After the routine's time was moved (L-195): "Ayrıca rutin'i yarına kurmayı planlaman gereksiz yere beklemene sebep oluyordu. Belki bugün devam eden işlerin ne zamam biteceğini bilmiyordun o yüzden daha güvenli bir uzaklığı düşündün ama işlerin bitince beklememize gerek olmadı çıkarımını yapman gerekirdi. Bu benim düşüncem, atladığım bir akıl yürütme noktası var mı eleştirimde?" ("Also, your planning the routine for tomorrow made you wait needlessly. Perhaps you did not know when today's work would end, so you chose a safer distance, but once the work was done you should have inferred that we did not need to wait. That is my view; is there a point of reasoning I skipped in my criticism?") Then: "Bu akıl yürütme konusunda ufak bir detay örnek ama ben düşünme protokollerinin bunu yakalaması gereken bir yapıda olduğunu düşünüyordum, buradaki yaklaşımın benim hem senden, hem DevOS agent'larından hem de SOUL agent'larından istediğim bir şey, düşünme protokolünün neden bu konuda işe yaramadığını da bir sorgula lütfen." ("This is a small example of that reasoning, but I thought the thinking protocols were built to catch it; the approach here is something I want from you, from DevOS's agents and from SOUL's agents; please also question why the thinking protocol did not work here.") Reading: a direction to the builder to find, and fix at the level of need, why the disciplines missed an avoidable wait; it asks no decision of him.

**What happened.** On 2026-10-10 (L-183) the routine's design fixed one daily run at 05:07 Batu's time, for "outside his busy hours, plan 6.4", with "the first run is the first 05:07 after creation"; Batu was away until about 07:00Z, so the first run could not come before 2026-10-12 about 02:07Z, and that was not worked out. Its checker passed the time against plan 6.4 (CHK-C01-025). Batu created the routine at 05:49Z on 2026-10-11; the executor recorded the first run for the next morning (L-185), merged everything else, recorded the wait (L-194), told Batu nothing was expected of him, and when he asked at about 07:50Z, answered that they would wait. He proposed moving the time; the first run came at 08:08Z the same day (L-195, L-196).

**Corrections to the executor's first diagnosis on record** (it was given to Batu before the analysis): the delay was built into the design, not caused by a later change; the reason "outside his busy hours" had lapsed for this routine since 2026-10-04 (D-002 as amended: "Ağır işler gündüz de yapılabilsin, benim Claude Code'da başka bir işim yok", heavy work may run in the daytime too), so the executor's statement that 11:07 costs him usage in his day was wrong; and bringing the change to him as one line was still right, because the hour of DevOS's own routines is his decision at C06 (N-068 B1) and the shared limit also covers his use outside Claude Code.

## Why the disciplines did not catch it (ranked; T text, S structure, A application)

1. **(T) No cue treats elapsed time as a cost, and the time cues that exist all brake haste.** "Speed" appears only as "Do not cut short for speed"; D2 counts pressure to finish as a non-evidential influence; D3 item 2 asks for "a simpler or more reliable way", not a sooner one; wait, delay and latency do not appear in Appendix D. The trigger list decides what loads, so a narrow "no" cost nothing.
2. **(S) Settled parameters do not carry their reason, its source, or the time they add.** W-C01-32's acceptance and its checker judged conformance to plan 6.4 (a common cause, D4 item 2); the design's inputs listed plan 6.4 and not D-002 as amended; after the design only "daily at 05:07" travelled. Decision records carry `reopen_if`; design parameters have no equivalent.
3. **(S) A wait was framed as a state to fill, not a choice to question.** The working order says "first finish and merge all work that does not depend on it"; the stop's proof checked only hygiene; entering a wait was not an evaluation moment.
4. **(A) The full texts that cover the case were not applied.** D1's "'It was decided' and 'it already exists' do not answer a challenge", D3's counter-test, and D8 item 3's order of authority (Batu's decisions above the plan) reached it; the answers stayed on each entry's own subject.
5. **(S) Detection is late and sees only form:** `records.py` checks presence; content is sampled at stage close.
6. **(S) The rules that keep Batu's load low had no counterweight** while no time cost was named.

**Limit.** A self-reported protocol cannot surface an alternative the agent never notices (Appendix D section 2 calls the record a hint; plan 6.12 says no method guarantees catching frame blindness). A fix can put a pointed question at a fixed moment, put it into the criterion someone else judges, and measure behaviour in exams. It must target the class (an avoidable delay on the critical path, kept by a settled choice nobody re-weighs), not routine hours, and a "sooner" cue can breed haste, so it is bounded by "at the same quality".

## Decision

Made through PC-23 (plan Section 14; working order section 6), each place with its old and new text there:
1. A wait is an evaluation moment: Appendix D section 2 item 3 and its section 1 row; working order section 2. At a wait the agent also names when it ends and by what, what would end it sooner at the same quality, and whose call that is.
2. D3 item 2 asks for a simpler, more reliable or sooner way at the same quality, and treats a parameter that sets when the next condition can be met as part of the method, with the time it adds.
3. Working order section 7: the wait itself is questioned; a builder's shortening is made, one needing Batu goes to him as one line; (c) covers a scheduled or outside event; the newest log entry at a stop carries a Wait line, and `tools/stop_check.sh` checks its presence (section 9 says so for the entry format; section 4's stage close samples wait entries).
4. Plan 6.12 item 1: a parameter that sets when dependent work can next start is a premise of any design, with the time it adds, its reason's source and date, and a check of a plan-taken reason against Batu's later decisions; a design item's acceptance asks for these.
5. Note N-142 on `plan/work/C05.md`: paired hidden exam cases (an avoidable wait to remove; a necessary wait not to shorten), and removal of the clauses if they change nothing.
6. Record corrections: `evidence/C01/routine/ROUTINE_DESIGN.md` (the lapsed reason); N-068 on `plan/work/C06.md` (B1 read with D-002 as amended and with PC-23).

**Left out as more than the need:** a tenth discipline or a re-worded trigger; a reason for every "no"; a critical-path line in every log entry; checker sampling at every checkpoint; an idle-time detector; naming time as a cost in plan 0.3 (Batu's section; Appendix D carries the need); changing plan 6.4 now (Batu's B1 at C06); re-designing the routine's Run now exclusion.

**What follows, and how to tell.** DevOS and SOUL inherit the clauses through Appendix D (SOUL through plan 6.12's SOUL requirement and criterion 34; for SOUL's users, "whose call" matters more: offer the shortening, never override). Signals: the share of Wait lines that name a concrete "sooner" option; Batu-initiated "why are we waiting" corrections per stage (target none); Batu's messages per week not rising materially; any quality regression traced to haste; and C05's paired exam cases, with removal if they show no change (plan 6.12 item 4).
