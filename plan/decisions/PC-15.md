````text

**Exit rule and loop limit** (PC-15, 2026-10-06; CHK-C00-048 condition 2). They apply from W-C00-10 round 2 on; plan Section 9 cites them at C00 step 7.
1. **Exit rule.** A C00 finding changes the plan before C01 only if it changes an action of C01 or C02, or needs a decision of Batu's. C00 step 7 ends with W-C00-10 round 2; a later C00 finding, for example from the checks of that round's plan changes, follows this rule.
2. **Loop limit.** K-7 item 7's loop limit (plan Section 4) applies to the installation's own review-and-revision cycles: an item produced, judged by a checker, revised and judged again. A cycle makes no progress when two consecutive rounds do not reduce its open blocking findings, or when the same failure recurs verbatim. For that count a finding keeps its ID across rounds, an open-finding count that is unknown is not taken as zero, and the round count is kept in the item's log entries; a verbatim repeat is a reason to stop and check, not proof (EV-C00-011 ECC-56, with the conditions of N-060 point 2). The upper limit: a second FAIL verdict on the same item opens a frame review (plan 6.12 item 2) before a third attempt.
3. **After a stop** (K-7 item 7). The work on the item stops and the reason is recorded in the log. The executor, as coordinator, takes it to a frame review (plan 6.12 item 2), or to Batu only if it is his matter (section 8). A stopped cycle never accepts the item and never changes its acceptance condition.
````

**Reason:** CHK-C00-048 condition 2 (B3-C2), accepted as written (EV-C00-021 row T-60): an exit rule for C00 step 7, in the working order cited from Section 9, and K-7 item 7's loop limit applied to the installation's own review-and-revision cycles. The rule applies from this round, as the round's own head states (EV-C00-021). Class high.
