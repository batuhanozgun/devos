**Labelled text.** No passage labelled as Batu's decision is edited; D-016 is cited, not changed. **Acceptance conditions.** None is touched: no stage's Acceptance line, no acceptance block of a `plan/work/*.md` file, no Appendix C test. The change asks more of every agent; it loosens nothing.

**Class high.** `plan/Installation_Working_Order.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/agents/` and `tools/` (`tools/merge_gate.py`); checked before the merge.

## Changed places

1. **Working order section 2, "Two layers".** After the sentence on Appendix D's hand-over rule, a new passage, "The thinking disciplines in practice": every agent (the executor, and every helper except the counter-designer) evaluates all nine trigger questions at the start of each work item or task and after each material change; "no" for a question that does not apply; "yes" or "uncertain" means reading and applying the discipline's full text; answering is mandatory and checked by tools (`tools/records.py render --check` for log entries from L-159; `tools/subagent_audit.py disciplines` for a helper's task and report); the content is the agent's judgment, sampled by checkers; every task carries the questions in the words of section 9. *Old text:* none. *Reason:* D-016 items 1 to 3.
2. **Working order section 4, step 4.** The executor takes a verdict with `tools/subagent_audit.py last` and checks with `tools/subagent_audit.py disciplines` that the task carried the questions and the verdict carries the nine answers; a helper's report the same way. *Old text:* "The executor writes its verdict verbatim to `evidence/<stage>/checks/CHK-<stage>-<nnn>.md` (section 9)." *Reason:* D-016 item 2; CHK-C00-067 finding 12 (task texts).
3. **Working order section 4, "Stage work list".** The executor reads the stage's plan text and every open note on `plan/work/<stage>.md`; each becomes an item, joins one, or stays a note with its deadline, and the work list says which. *Old text:* "The executor reads the stage's plan text in full," *Reason:* CHK-C00-066 finding 5.
4. **Working order section 4, "Stage close".** The stage-close checker samples the stage's discipline records; a "no" where the discipline was needed is a finding. *Old text:* none. *Reason:* D-016 item 3.
5. **Working order section 9, log entry format.** The template gains the line `- **Disciplines (D1–D9):** …`, and a sentence says every entry from L-159 carries it and `tools/records.py render --check` fails without it. *Reason:* D-016 item 2. D-016 item 2 says "one line per question"; a log entry puts the nine answers on one line, in order, as L-159 onwards do, so that the entry stays one bullet among its others and the tool reads one line; a verdict or a report puts them on nine lines. The substance is the same: nine answers, in order, each with what was done (CHK-C01-002 finding 4).
6. **Working order section 9, checker verdict format.** The template gains `## Disciplines (D1–D9)` between the front matter and `## Findings`. *Reason:* D-016 item 2 (a helper's report).
7. **Working order section 9, "Discipline block".** The text every task carries, verbatim, written out and never as a placeholder (CHK-C00-067 finding 12). *Old text:* none.
8. **`.claude/agents/producer.md`, `researcher.md`, `prober.md`, `checker.md`.** A section "Thinking disciplines (D-016)" before "Bans"; each output form gains the block (the checker's verdict template, between the front matter and "Findings"). The counter-designer's file is unchanged (D-016, "Not changed"). *Reason:* D-016 item 4.
9. **`CLAUDE.md`.** A rule "Thinking disciplines" after Batu's principles. *Reason:* D-016 item 4.
10. **`tools/records.py`.** `render --check` fails, and `render` warns, for every log entry from L-159 whose "Disciplines (D1–D9)" line is missing or does not answer D1 to D9 in order. Test: `tools/test_records_disciplines.py`. *Reason:* D-016 item 2.
11. **`tools/subagent_audit.py`.** A subcommand `disciplines <agent-id>`: whether the agent's task carried the discipline block and whether its last report carries the nine answers; fixed result lines only, never prompt or report text. Tests in `tools/test_subagent_audit.py`. *Reason:* N-104's decision on task texts: they can be checked, at the point the executor already takes each report.
12. **Plan header, the PC-10 note on later plan changes.** "(PC-07 to PC-17, W-C00-10; PC-18, W-C00-11)" becomes "(PC-07 to PC-17, W-C00-10; PC-18, W-C00-11; PC-19, N-104)". *Labelled:* no.
