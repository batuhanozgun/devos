# D-013

**What is asked** (one sentence). The names of services connected to your account are in old commits of the public `devos` repository: do you accept that they stay, or should the history be rewritten, or the repository recreated without it?

**Why it is his.** It is information about his accounts in a public place, and (b) and (c) change his repository and need actions in his account.

**Why now and on its own.** The Turkish summary told him this would come as a confirmation of D-003; it now comes as its own question, because D-003 accepted a different risk (a guard the builder can edit) and because the exposure grew on 5 October, after his answer.

**A mistake first** (Appendix E 4.4). On 5 October, between about 20:53Z and 21:27Z, this session's records wrote the names again into three public files. Nothing checked them automatically, and the executor did not run the check before committing. The current files were cleaned at about 22:30Z. Since then the executor runs the check before every commit; W-C00-14 will put it into the guard, but it is planned, not in force.

**Options** (Appendix E section 3):

| | Purpose | Benefit | Cost and risk | Alternative it replaces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) accept, recommended | leave the old commits as they are | nothing breaks; no action from him; every commit and pull-request link in the records keeps working | the names (not contents, not any password) stay readable in 36 old commits to anyone who looks | (b), (c) |
| (b) rewrite the history | remove the names from the commits of `main` on GitHub | the names no longer appear in the branch's history | an action in his account: turning off, then on again, the rule that refuses rewriting `main`; a one-time guard change on the builder's side, and whether the session's git route allows the push is untested. Every commit SHA cited in the records stops resolving publicly, including every verdict's `reviewed_head`; closed pull requests lose their diffs. **Recommendation and reason.** (a). The exposure is a list of names, public since 1 October. (b) breaks every reference in the records and still leaves the commits reachable; (c) costs him several actions and moves the records' references into a private archive, while copies stay.

**What he needs to know.**
- *Measured* (L-147, rechecked at about 22:40Z, counts only): the names are in 36 of the 461 commits of `main`: 9 from 1 October, 27 from 5 October. No commit message contains them. The current files are clean.
- *In force*: the executor's check before every commit, by practice. *Planned*: the same check in the guard (W-C00-14).
- *Documented*: rewriting history does not remove commits from cached views, pull requests, clones or forks; issues can be transferred between his own repositories, not from a private one to a public one.
- The names are not written here or in the issue.

**If he does not answer.** Nothing changes, and no work waits on it. Silence is not approval; the item stays open on his list.

**Steps.** (a): answer "D-013: a"; nothing else. (b) or (c): the steps, each with what he should see, come to him in one message after his answer, because they depend on his choice and each screen is first checked against GitHub's documentation.
