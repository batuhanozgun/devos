# D-013

**What is asked** (one sentence). The names of the services connected to his account stay visible in old commits of the public `devos` repository: does he accept that, or should the repository's history be removed?

**Why it is his.** It is information about his accounts in a public place.

**Options** (Appendix E section 3):

| | (a) Accept (recommended) | (b) Remove the history |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Leave the old commits as they are | Make the names disappear from the repository |
| Benefit | Nothing breaks | The names are no longer in `devos` |
| Cost | The service names (not their contents, not any password) stay readable in old commits | The repository is recreated: issue #6 with his answers and all pull-request history are lost; copies made by others remain |
| Alternative | (b) | (a) |

**What he needs to know.** Only the names of the services are exposed, in 9 old commits; current files hide them, and from now on every public write is checked for them by the guard (W-C00-14). The names are not repeated here or in the issue.

**If he does not answer.** Nothing changes, and the work is not held up by it. Silence is not approval: the item stays open on his list.
