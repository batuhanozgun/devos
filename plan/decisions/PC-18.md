**Authority.** CHK-C00-069 condition 1 (the closure verdict of W-C00-11, `evidence/C00/checks/CHK-C00-069.md`): "The executor makes a plan change under plan Section 14 ... adding an interpretation note to C00's sixth acceptance condition, as PC-08 did for criterion 26." Plan Section 14 and the working order section 6 apply.

**Written after the result.** The question arose from the probe's result (EV-C00-022 section 1), so this note is written after it. It argues only from text fixed before that result (the reasons below cite nothing later than 2026-10-05T23:36Z). Under Appendix G G8 item 3 and plan Section 14, the earlier reading of the probe stays exploratory, and the test is repeated under the reading recorded here (CHK-C00-069 condition 2). A fresh checker judges, before this change merges, whether the reading is the condition's meaning or an easing after the result; if it finds an easing, or that only reading B holds, the C00 close goes to a frame review (plan 6.12 item 2; working order section 6 item 3), not to a PASS.

**Labelled text.** C00's Acceptance line carries no label; no passage labelled as Batu's decision is edited; no Turkish original is touched. Criterion 25 ([Batu's decision]) is cited, not changed.

**Acceptance conditions.** The words of C00's Acceptance line and the acceptance block of `plan/work/C00.md` are unchanged. The note says how one condition reads in a case its words do not settle.

## Reasons, from text fixed before the result

1. **The condition's own term.** Its Turkish original says "gizli bilgi". The plan uses the same term for "keys and tokens" that Batu handles: Section 12's head, "Anahtar ve belirteç gibi gizli bilgiler hiçbir zaman sohbete yazılmaz" ("Secret information such as keys and tokens is never written into the chat"), which the condition's "or the chat" mirrors; and it says that the publishable keys "are not secret information" (6.2, "Two projects"; Turkish "gizli bilgi değildir"). The term means the keys and tokens issued for Batu's accounts and DevOS's resources.
2. **What C00 checks.** C00's purpose is "To see that the preparation is really complete" (Section 9, C00). The preparation that the condition's environment-variable part follows from handled such keys: "deleting the GitHub access key" (Section 12, "In preparation") and criterion 25, served by C00: "No Anthropic API key in the environment settings" (C00's criteria: 18, 21, 25–27, 29, 34); 11.1 lists among Batu's decisions "no Anthropic API key in the environment".
3. **Where the plan forbids a secret in an environment.** K-9 item 3: "Supabase's secret (service) key is never present in agent environments"; 6.3: environments get only the publishable key and their own environment token, and the builder never sees a token's value. C03 test 3's "there is no secret key in the agent environment" is K-9 item 3's negative test.
4. **A session's own credential visible to it.** C01 row 3: "If the session can see its own token, the threat model decides"; visibility is then "recorded as a residual risk", and the failure is a session that can read another environment's token. The plan treats a credential scoped to the session it sits in as a matter of the threat model, not as a secret that must not be there.
5. **What a ✘ condition states.** The legend of `plan/work/C00.md` (2026-10-05T23:36Z): a ✘ condition's sentence "states what must be observed (for C00's two ✘ conditions: that the thing did not happen)". The thing is a secret put where it shows, by the preparation or the builder.
6. **Reading B.** Under it, the condition could not be met by any session on the platform that the plan builds on (5.1; Batu's decision in 11.1: "DevOS running in Claude Code cloud"), for a reason no step of the installation can change, and the plan names no step that would remove such a credential. A condition the plan sets for its own first stage is not read as one that the plan's own premises make impossible, when the plan's text points the other way (reasons 1 to 5).
