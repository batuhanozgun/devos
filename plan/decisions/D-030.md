# D-030

**What is asked (one sentence, Appendix E section 3).** Will you press Run now once on the routine's page before 10:35 your time, so that the combined scenario's first run (CS1) runs now and the scheduled run at 11:07 does its second run (CS2) today instead of tomorrow: (a) yes (recommended), or (b) no, and CS1 runs by itself today at 11:07 and CS2 tomorrow at 11:07?

**Why it is Batu's.** The press is an action on his account, on the routine he created; the builder does not start runs of his routine.

**Options.**
- **(a) Press Run now once now, with no text, before 10:35 your time (recommended).** Purpose: C01's combined scenario finished today, and with it the routine's removal, your deletion steps and C01's close about one day earlier. Benefit: the same two runs the schedule would start, from the same control file; nothing in what they do changes. Cost: one press; the two runs' share of the usage, which the schedule would use anyway in the same week.
- **(b) Do not press.** Purpose: change nothing. Benefit: no action from you. Cost: CS2 and everything after it about one day later.

**Recommendation: (a)**, for the same reason as D-021, D-022, D-027 and D-028: the press saves material time on the critical path and changes nothing in what the runs do; its worst case is (b).

**What you need to know.** Press once and write no text. If nothing appears after the press, do not press again; say so in the working session or on issue #6. After 10:35 your time (07:35Z) do not press: the scheduled run at 11:07 takes CS1 then. A press needs no reply: the run's own record shows it started.

**If you do not answer.** Nothing is lost: CS1 runs by itself today at 11:07 your time, and CS2 tomorrow at 11:07.

**How to answer.** Press Run now (no reply needed), or write `D-030: b` on issue #6 or in the working session.
