# D-019

**What is asked (one sentence, Appendix E section 3).** The platform places its own credentials in every session; its documentation says they stay within the session, while the installation's earlier records suggest they may reach further, and DevOS will no longer test this itself (FR-05): do you accept this as a residual risk, as part of D-003's, until D-003 is re-assessed at C03?

**Why it is Batu's.** It is the acceptance of a residual risk to his accounts, and it is the basis on which C00's sixth condition closes. D-003, which this extends, is his decision.

**Options.**
- **(a) Accept (recommended).** Purpose: close C00's sixth condition on a stated basis and let C02 build DevOS's environments. Benefit: no further trial of the platform, which is outside DevOS's authority and is the content that interrupted three working sessions; DevOS's design does not depend on the confinement (P3). Cost: the confinement is documented by the platform but not shown, and the installation's own records doubt it; the risk is re-assessed at C03 with the audit environment. Nothing is asked of him beyond the answer.
- **(b) Do not accept.** Purpose: keep the condition open until the confinement is shown. Benefit: no risk is accepted on documentation alone. Cost: the C00 close goes to a frame review; the realistic routes are a written confirmation from the platform's provider (an action on his account) or a change at account level, and C02's work on the environments waits.

**Recommendation: (a)**, because DevOS's design does not rely on the confinement, the residual is of the same kind he accepted with D-003, and it is re-assessed at C03 with the audit environment.

**What you need to know.** "Credentials the platform places in a session" are the platform's own access keys for its channels, not DevOS's keys and not his passwords; no value of them is ever written anywhere. The question is only whether to rely on the platform's own statement about them until C03.

**If you do not answer.** Nothing is lost now: C01 continues, and C00 stays provisional as it is. Before C02 builds DevOS's environments, the answer is needed; until then that work waits. Silence is not approval.

**How to answer.** On issue #6 or in the working session: `D-019: a` or `D-019: b`.
