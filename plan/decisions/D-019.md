# D-019

**What is asked (one sentence, Appendix E section 3).** The platform places its own credentials in every session; its documentation says they stay within the session, while the installation's earlier records suggest they may reach further, and DevOS will no longer test this itself (FR-05): do you accept this as a residual risk, as part of D-003's, until D-003 is re-assessed at C03?

**Why it is Batu's.** It is the acceptance of a residual risk to his accounts, and it is the basis on which C00's sixth condition closes. D-003, which this extends, is his decision.

**Options.**
- **(a) Accept (recommended).** Purpose: close C00's sixth condition on a stated basis and let C02 build DevOS's environments. Benefit: no further trial of the platform, which is outside DevOS's authority and is the content that interrupted three working sessions. Cost: the confinement is documented by the platform but not shown, and the installation's own records doubt it. If the credentials do act beyond the session, they could reach another session, another environment, or the account's settings or services, including his other work; DevOS cannot protect the account's other sessions and services, and for its own environments it then relies on the guard hook alone (P3). The risk is re-assessed at C03. Nothing is asked of him beyond the answer.
- **(b) Do not accept.** Purpose: keep the condition open until the confinement is shown. Benefit: no risk is accepted on documentation alone. Cost: the C00 close goes to a frame review; the realistic routes are a written confirmation from the platform's provider (an action on his account) or a change at account level, and C02's work on the environments waits.

**Recommendation: (a)**, because a trial by DevOS would only reveal the risk, not reduce it: the risk exists in the same way for all his use of the platform, not only for DevOS; the step that would reduce it, separating accounts, stays open to him at any time and comes to him for DevOS's environments before C02 (row 17); the residual is of the same kind he accepted with D-003; and it is re-assessed at C03.

**What you need to know.** "Credentials the platform places in a session" are the platform's own access keys for its channels, not DevOS's keys and not his passwords; no value of them is ever written anywhere. The question is whether to rely on the platform's own statement about them until C03. If that statement were wrong, the reach would be the account's: another session, another environment, or the account's settings or services, which include his other work; DevOS can at most keep its own environments apart, and there it would rely on the guard hook alone.

**If you do not answer.** Nothing is lost now: C01 continues, and C00 stays provisional as it is. Before C02 builds DevOS's environments, the answer is needed; until then that work waits. Silence is not approval.

**How to answer.** On issue #6 or in the working session: `D-019: a` or `D-019: b`.
