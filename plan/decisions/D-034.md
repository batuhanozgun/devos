# D-034

**Soru (Türkçe, #6 için).** 4 Ekim'deki "o zaman auto mode'da çalışmayalım" kuralın bundan sonra nasıl uygulansın?
- (a) Bugünkü gibi: her Auto kullanımı senin her seferinde vereceğin bir istisna olur.
- (b) Çalışma oturumu Accept edits'te kalır; Auto yalnız platformun başka mod bırakmadığı oturumlarda (routine'lerin açtıkları, başka yol bulunamazsa) ve önceden adı yazılmış tek işlik kısa pencerelerde (kütüphaneyi yeniden eklemek gibi), kurucunun gerekçesi ve bir denetçinin kararıyla kullanılır (önerim).
- (c) Mod, çalışma oturumu dahil, kurucunun teknik seçimi olur (örneğin limit sonrası "devam" yazman gerekmesin diye denenebilir).

Cevap: `D-034: a`, `D-034: b` ya da `D-034: c`.

**What is asked (one sentence).** How should your rule of 4 October, "o zaman auto mode'da çalışmayalım", be applied from now on: (a) as today, every use of auto mode is an exception you decide each time; (b) the working session stays in Accept edits, and auto mode is used only in sessions where the platform leaves no other mode (those that routines start, if no other way is found) and in short single-action windows named in advance (such as re-attaching the library), with the builder's reason and a checker's verdict (recommended); or (c) the mode becomes the builder's technical choice, the working session included (it could be tried, for example, so that you need not type "devam" after a usage limit)?

**Why it is yours.** Your decision of 4 October (D-008) sets the mode of the sessions on your account, and the record of that decision treats the mode as yours: how agents act on your account. Since D-033, your words about how something is built are not a technical specification unless you say so, and we talk over the decisions of yours that constrain the builder. Behind the question is one only you can answer: whether "no auto mode" was your own constraint on how sessions act on your account, or a means toward your aim that every block leaves a detailed written reason. The options are that answer's practical forms: (a) keeps it as your constraint everywhere, (c) treats it as a means, and (b) keeps it as your constraint for the working session while treating as a means the cases the platform forces and single actions named in advance. Nothing here asks money of you; how any option is carried out is the builder's work, checked by a checker.

**What you need to know.**
- Your aim was auditability: every block with a detailed written reason (your text (1)). In auto mode, Claude Code's own classifier judges each step and, when it blocks one, gives only a short rule label, not a reason; on 4 October its rules also blocked the builder's ordinary merges, because they expect a person's request in the conversation. That is why the sessions left auto mode, and you accepted the stated costs: the classifier's protection against dangers nobody listed is gone, and a missing rule stops work until it is written.
- The builder's own rule file (the guard) decides every call in every mode and always writes its reason. Where the classifier acts, it is a second gate after the guard.
- What the rule costs you today: a short Auto window you open whenever the library has to be attached again; a "devam" after every usage-limit reset (the only observed way to remove it is running the working session in auto mode); and on 10 October a session that opened in auto mode waited until you switched it.
- The sessions that routines started in C01 reported auto mode by their own record: the platform gives routines no mode picker. DevOS's working, audit and exam sessions are to be started by routines. The builder's own route comes first: a documented setting meant to start such sessions in another mode, which the documentation does not say holds there and which has not been tried. If it does not hold, under (a) this comes to you as a separate question before you create DevOS's routines.
- Under any option the guard keeps writing its reasons, and a step that spends money or changes your accounts, your data or your other work still comes to you.

**Options.**
- **(a) As today: every use of auto mode is an exception you decide.**
  - Purpose: keep your rule word for word.
  - Benefit: every session acts on your account in the mode you chose; the classifier's label-only blocks stay out of the builder's work, except in the windows you open.
  - Cost: time: each new kind of Auto use needs a decision of yours, and the routine sessions' gap comes to you as its own question before DevOS's routines; quality and risk: the classifier's general protection stays off in the working session and the builder's other sessions, except in your windows (routine sessions reported auto mode in C01 whatever the rule says, and under (a) that comes to you as its own question); money: none.
  - Alternative: (b).
- **(b) Bounded: auto mode only where the platform leaves no other mode and in short single-action windows named in advance; the working session stays in Accept edits (recommended).**
  - Purpose: keep your aim and your rule for the working session, where nearly all the work runs, and stop routing to you the cases the platform forces.
  - Benefit: no further question for routine sessions or for a named window (a window still needs your switch in the app, as today); in those sessions the classifier's protection against unlisted dangers comes back, behind the guard.
  - Cost: quality and risk: in routine sessions and windows, a block by the classifier comes with a label only, the gap you asked to close, for that part of the work; the builder records those blocks, and designs around the actions the classifier may refuse there (for example merging without a person's request, N-168); each named kind needs its reason and a checker's verdict; time: small, inside the builder's work; money: none.
  - Alternative: (b) without the windows (re-attaching the library would then stay a decision of yours each time).
- **(c) Open: the mode is the builder's technical choice, the working session included.**
  - Purpose: let the builder choose the mode per kind of session by evidence, for example to try removing the "devam".
  - Benefit: possibly no "devam" after a reset, if a trial shows the working session can run in auto mode; the classifier's protection everywhere it runs.
  - Cost: quality and risk: in the working session, the classifier's blocks would come with labels only, the problem you raised, for the main body of the work; on 4 October its rules blocked the builder's merges, and whether they still would is not tested; time: a trial, and work stopped if the classifier blocks a needed step; money: none.
  - Alternative: (c) limited to a trial in one session, with the result coming back to you.

**Recommendation: (b).** Your words read as a means to your aim (text (2) answered a problem of unreadable blocks, and you pointed at the other modes), but the record of your decision also treats the mode as how agents act on your account, so the safe reading keeps it for the working session. (b) keeps your aim where the work is done, ends questions that the platform's own limits force on you, and adds no cost to you. (c)'s benefit, no "devam", is real but uncertain, and it would bring back label-only blocks to the main work, which is what you asked to remove. The decision is yours.

**If you do not answer.** Nothing waits now: the rule stays as written. Before you create DevOS's routines, the routine sessions' mode comes to you as its own question if it is still auto mode. Silence is not approval: no answer means no option is taken.

**After an answer.** The texts that state the rule change through the plan's change route, as the answer requires: D-008's title and interpretation (3), `CLAUDE.md`'s "Settings" line, the working order's opening check (section 3 item 1) and section 10, Appendix F steps 1 and 2, FR-05 premise P4 (line 9) and line 54, N-169, and the guard's rule reasons that cite D-008 as the source of the guard's own design (a class-high change).

**How to answer.** Write `D-034: a`, `D-034: b` or `D-034: c` on issue #6 or in the working session.
