# D-016

**How it came up.** Batu asked how the installation uses the reasoning layer, above all the nine disciplines of Appendix D. The executor read the records and answered: Appendix D section 2 (the draft common rules, item 3) asks for all nine trigger questions at the start of every work item and after every material change, with a record; the working order section 2 borrows Appendix D and sends its records to the ledger files until the database exists, but its log format (section 9) has no field for them; the runs before the working session wrote a "Discipline line (D1–D9)" in their log entries (last L-119), while the working session's 23 entries (L-136 to L-158) have none; the helper roles' files and `CLAUDE.md` do not mention Appendix D; and the executor had answered one question from memory without checking the source (GitHub's automatic branch deletion), which Batu caught. The executor first proposed giving helpers only the questions of their role; Batu disagreed, and the executor agreed, because Appendix D itself says to evaluate all nine and not to skip any as unimportant.

**The decision (technical).**
1. **Every agent, every work item.** The executor and every helper (producer, checker, researcher, prober) evaluate all nine trigger questions of Appendix D section 2, item 3, at the start of each work item or task and after each material change of plan or evidence. A question that does not apply is answered "no". "Yes" or "uncertain" means reading that discipline's full text (Appendix D section 3) and applying it.
2. **Mandatory, and its presence checked.** The executor's log entry carries a "Disciplines (D1–D9)" block, one line per question. A helper's final report carries the same block; the executor does not accept a report without it. A tool check confirms the block is present in log entries.
3. **The content is the agent's judgment.** Whether a "no" is right, and how a "yes" changed the work, cannot be checked mechanically. Checkers sample the records (the stage-close checker at least): a "no" where the discipline was needed is a finding. As Appendix D says, the record is the agent's own report: a hint, not evidence.
4. **Carriers.** At once: the executor's log entries from L-159, and every task the executor writes from now on. Permanently: a plan change to the working order (the reasoning layer, the log format, the stage-close check), the role files in `.claude/agents/` and `CLAUDE.md` (all class high, checked before merge), and the tool check; made after PC-15 to PC-17 merge, because they change the same file.

**Not changed.** The counter-designer role is excluded: it may not see the plan (C00 step 5), and its step is done (W-C00-09).
