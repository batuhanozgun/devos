# D-035

**Soru (Türkçe, #6 için).** 4 Ekim'de "açtığın oturumlardaki modeli Opus 5.5 ve eforu da ultracode yap. kural olarak belirle bunu" dediğin kural, kalitenin alt sınırı mı olsun, yoksa her iş için sabit ayar mı kalsın?
- (a) Alt sınır: varsayılan yine Opus 5.5 ve ultracode; kurucu yalnız mekanik işlerde (listeleme, biçim kontrolü gibi), kalitenin düşmediğini bir denetçiyle gösterdiği yerde daha hafif bir ayar kullanabilir ve gerekçesini kayda yazar (önerim).
- (b) Sabit: her oturum ve her yardımcı ajan her zaman Opus 5.5 ve ultracode'la çalışır (bugünkü gibi).

Cevap: `D-035: a` ya da `D-035: b`.

**What is asked (one sentence).** Is your rule of 4 October, Opus 5.5 at ultracode effort, "kural olarak ... geçici olmasını istemiyorum", a quality floor, under which that setting stays the default and the builder may use a lighter one only for mechanical work where a checker has shown that quality holds (a, recommended), or a fixed setting for every session and helper (b)?

**Why it is yours.** It is your rule, stated as permanent, and the model and effort spend the usage you share with DevOS. Since D-033, your words about how something is built are not a technical specification unless you say so, and we talk over the decisions of yours that constrain the builder. Only you can say whether you meant a floor or a fixed setting. Neither option asks anything of you.

**What you need to know.**
- Your own principles already set a floor of this kind: "The default effort and depth are high; less is done with a rationale" (plan 0.3 item 1), and "effort depth being high by default" is among your decisions (plan 11.1). Option (a) reads your rule the same way.
- Today every session and every helper runs on Opus 5.5 at the top effort, including helpers that only list, copy or check a format.
- At the pace of 12 October the weekly allowance may run out before it resets on 17 October, which would stop the installation and your own Claude use until then. A lighter setting for mechanical work would use less; how much is not measured.
- Sessions that routines start cannot be given a model, and the C01 routine runs did not show ultracode; so the fixed setting is not shown to hold there today. The builder settles which effort level actually applies from current documentation; that is not your question.
- Not asked separately: the repository's setting also holds in sessions you open yourself on this repository, and it overrides a lower effort you might pick there. The working session you open needs the high setting under either option. If you want your own other sessions free of it, say so.

**Options.**
- **(a) A quality floor (recommended).**
  - Purpose: keep top quality as the standing default, as you asked, and spend your shared usage only where it buys quality.
  - Benefit: mechanical work can run lighter once a checker has shown its results are as good, which saves part of your shared allowance and leaves more of it for the work that needs depth; every such choice is written down with its reason.
  - Cost: quality and risk: a kind of work judged mechanical could turn out to need depth; the checker's showing, and a return to the default when a later check finds a drop, are the safeguards; time: the builder spends some work on each showing; money: none (it lowers use).
  - Alternative: (a) for helpers only, with every session fixed.
- **(b) A fixed setting, as today.**
  - Purpose: keep your rule word for word.
  - Benefit: nothing to judge or show; the same setting everywhere the builder can set it.
  - Cost: money and risk: every helper spends the top effort on every task, mechanical ones included, which raises the chance that the weekly allowance runs out early (stopping the installation and your own use); the rule still cannot be shown to hold in routine sessions; time: none.
  - Alternative: (a).

**Recommendation: (a).** Your words asked for a permanent rule, not a temporary setting, and your own principle 0.3 item 1 already states the rule as a default with less only for a reason. (a) keeps that default everywhere design, review or judgment happens, and lets the builder save your shared usage only where a checker shows quality does not drop. (b) spends the most of your allowance for no quality gain on mechanical work. The decision is yours.

**If you do not answer.** Nothing waits: the rule stays as written. Silence is not approval: no answer means no option is taken.

**After an answer.** Under (a): D-008's title and interpretation (2), `CLAUDE.md`'s "Settings" line, Appendix F step 2 and the working order's opening check are reworded through the plan's change route to say "default" and where the record of lighter choices is; each lighter choice is a checked change. Under (b): nothing changes.

**How to answer.** Write `D-035: a` or `D-035: b` on issue #6 or in the working session.
