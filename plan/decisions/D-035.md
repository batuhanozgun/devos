# D-035

**Soru (Türkçe, #6 için).** 4 Ekim'de "açtığın oturumlardaki modeli Opus 5.5 ve eforu da ultracode yap. kural olarak belirle bunu" dediğin kural varsayılan mı olsun, sabit mi?
- (a) Varsayılan: Opus 5.5 ve ultracode varsayılan kalır; kurucu yalnız mekanik işlerde, kalitenin düşmediğini bir denetçiyle gösterdiği yerde daha hafif bir model ya da daha düşük bir efor kullanır ve gerekçesini yazar (önerim).
- (b) Sabit: kurucunun açtığı her oturum her zaman Opus 5.5 ve ultracode'la çalışır (bugünkü gibi).

Bilgi: Yardımcı ajanlar (alt ajanlar) da bugün aynı ayarla çalışıyor; bu senin sözünden değil, kurucunun kendi seçiminden geliyor, bu yüzden onların ayarını kurucu, senin "daha azı gerekçeyle yapılır" ilkene uyarak belirler: daha hafif bir ayar yalnız gerekçesiyle ve bir denetçinin onayıyla. Sözünü yardımcı ajanlar için de kastettiysen yazman yeter.

Cevap: `D-035: a` ya da `D-035: b`.

**What is asked (one sentence).** Should your rule of 4 October for the sessions the builder opens, Opus 5.5 at ultracode effort ("kural olarak belirle bunu. geçici olmasını istemiyorum"), be a default, from which the builder may depart to a lighter model or a lower effort only for mechanical work where a checker has shown that quality holds, with its reason written (a, recommended), or a fixed setting for every session the builder opens, as today (b)? The helpers' setting is the builder's, and the question tells you so in one line.

**Why it is yours.** It is your rule, stated as permanent, and the model and effort spend the usage you share with DevOS. Since D-033, your words about how something is built are not a technical specification unless you say so, and we talk over the decisions of yours that constrain the builder. Only you can say whether you meant a default or a fixed setting. Neither option asks anything of you.

**Who decided what.** Your words name the sessions the builder opens: "açtığın oturumlardaki modeli Opus 5.5 ve eforu da ultracode yap" (D-008's interpretation (2) and the guard's rule S3 read them the same way). The helpers (the subagents and workflow agents the builder uses inside a session) run on the same setting because the builder chose it: their role files take the session's model ("inherit"), and a role file's model and effort are the builder's (summary item 18, labelled the builder's technical decision). So the question covers your rule only. The helpers' setting stays the builder's under your principle 0.3 item 1 ("less is done with a rationale") and the plan's criterion 15 ("reducing it requires a rationale and another role's approval"); the question tells you this in one line, as information, and you can say otherwise at any time.

**What you need to know.**
- Your own principles already set a default of this kind: "The default effort and depth are high; less is done with a rationale" (plan 0.3 item 1; the same in Appendix E section 5), and "effort depth being high by default" is among your decisions (plan 11.1). Option (a) reads your rule the same way.
- Today every session the builder opens, and every helper, runs on Opus 5.5 at the pinned effort (`xhigh` with ultracode on), including helpers that only list, copy or check a format.
- At the pace of 12 October the weekly allowance may run out before it resets on 17 October, which would stop the installation and your own Claude use until then. A lighter setting for mechanical work would use less; how much is not measured. Most mechanical work today is done by helpers, whose setting is the builder's (above). This question bears only on the sessions the builder opens, and today it opens none whose model or effort it can choose: a session in Accept edits cannot start sessions (D-010 premise P1; working order section 2), and sessions that routines start cannot be given a model (next point). So neither option changes anything before 17 October; the answer governs the sessions the builder may open later.
- Sessions that routines start cannot be given a model, and the C01 routine runs did not show ultracode; so the fixed setting is not shown to hold there today. The builder settles which effort level actually applies from current documentation; that is not your question.
- Not asked separately: the repository's setting also holds in sessions you open yourself on this repository, and it overrides a lower effort you might pick there. The working session you open needs the high setting under either option. If you want your own other sessions free of it, say so.

**Options.**
- **(a) A default (recommended).**
  - Purpose: keep top quality as the standing default, as you asked, and spend your shared usage only where it buys quality.
  - Benefit: mechanical work in the sessions the builder opens can run on a lighter model or a lower effort once a checker has shown its results are as good, which saves part of your shared allowance and leaves more of it for the work that needs depth; every such choice is written down with its reason.
  - Cost: quality and risk: a kind of work judged mechanical could turn out to need depth; the checker's showing, and a return to the default when a later check finds a drop, are the safeguards; time: the builder spends some work on each showing; money: none (it lowers use).
  - Alternative: (b).
- **(b) A fixed setting for every session the builder opens, as today.**
  - Purpose: keep your rule as you wrote it.
  - Benefit: nothing to judge or show; the same setting in every session the builder opens and can set.
  - Cost: money and risk: a session the builder opens for mechanical work spends the top setting on it, which uses more of the weekly allowance; how much depends on how many such sessions DevOS later runs, and is not measured; the rule still cannot be shown to hold in routine sessions; time: none.
  - Alternative: (a).

**Recommendation: (a).** Your words asked for a permanent rule, not a temporary setting, and your own principle 0.3 item 1 already states the rule as a default with less only for a reason. (a) keeps that default everywhere design, review or judgment happens, and lets the builder save your shared usage only where a checker shows quality does not drop; it also matches how the helpers are handled. (b) keeps the top setting even on mechanical work where a checker has shown that a lighter one does as well. The decision is yours.

**If you do not answer.** Nothing waits: the rule stays as written for the sessions the builder opens. The helpers' setting stays the builder's either way. Silence is not approval: no answer means no option is taken.

**After an answer.** Under (a): D-008's title and interpretation (2), `CLAUDE.md`'s "Settings" line, Appendix F step 2 and the working order's opening check are reworded through the plan's change route to say "default" and where the record of lighter choices is; each lighter choice is a checked change. Under (b): nothing changes. Under either, D-008's record gets a note that his rule names the sessions the builder opens and that the helpers' setting is the builder's.

**How to answer.** Write `D-035: a` or `D-035: b` on issue #6 or in the working session.
