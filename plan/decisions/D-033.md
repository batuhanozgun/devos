# D-033

**What he decided (unasked, in the working session).** "Bu kural hem sein için hem devos hem de soul'da kullanıcı aksini belirtmediği sürece kullanıcının sözleri teknik şartname değildir. Her şey tartışmaya açıktır, benim kararlarım arasında teknik kararlar varsa ve bu seni ya da agent'ları kısıtlıyorsa konuşalım."

**Interpretation.** See `answer_interpretation_en`. The executor stated two assumptions to him in the session: "sein" is "senin" (for the builder); and the rule covers his words about how something is built, while his purpose, scope, money, accounts and other work stay binding until changed.

**What follows** (note N-261 on `plan/work/C02.md`). (1) The rule goes into DevOS's and SOUL's own rules through plan Section 14 (Appendix D's draft `CLAUDE.md` and wherever SOUL's rules are written), labelled his decision. (2) The executor lists every decision and labelled text of his whose content is technical and constrains the builder or the agents, with a proposal for each, and talks them over with him; D-032 is one of them and is withdrawn as a question (`plan/decisions/D-032.md`).
