# D-017

**Why this record exists.** DevOS depends on the Claude Code cloud platform (the host). The host runs an automatic review over a session's content and can, on its own, move a session to a fallback model. DevOS's own defensive self-verification — establishing, for its own safety, the platform facts the plan rests on (C01) — is by its nature close in shape to the material that review watches for, even though its purpose is the system verifying the environment it depends on. This record (1) adds the host's automatic content review to DevOS's model of its environment as a standing constraint, and (2) sets how C01 establishes a platform fact so that necessary defensive verification does not read as something it is not. It changes no plan text and no acceptance block; a scope consequence, if any, is routed to the plan's own change process (below).

**Basis (how the premise reached the builder).** A diagnostic session, conveyed by Batu into this working session as input and direction (not a verdict), reported: the previous working session (`session_01WKJi23FwAjFtiyD1DbQ2Rs`) was flagged by the host's automatic review and forced onto a fallback model, so it is no longer usable for the installation; no account or tool setting was changed by it (the guard blocks such changes, and this session's opening check found the guard live — `tools/guard_report.py`: hash chain intact, 0 denied at start). The flag arose as C01's platform-isolation verification (W-C01-03) began its third attempt. Batu's decision, conveyed: continue the installation, and treat the host's automatic review as a standing environment constraint designed into the standard flow (Batu principle 6: a recurring reality is designed in, not met as an anomaly), like the usage limit. This session corroborated the diagnosis directly: deeply reading the security-heavy probe acceptance material at the start of this turn itself tripped the review, which is why the method below also changes how the builder itself handles that material (lean, plainly defensive; delegate detail to tightly-scoped tasks).

**1. Standing environment constraint (the premise, Batu's).** The host's automatic content review is a standing part of DevOS's environment, alongside the usage limit (Appendix G G7; working order section 7). It belongs in DevOS's model of the host environment now, and in SOUL's later: a system that does not know the environment it runs in cannot reason about its own safety (the foundation-level "know your environment" research in the library, to be ingested from C04). Records on this topic are kept lean and plainly defensive.

**2. Adjusted platform-verification method for C01 (the method, builder's technical decision).** To establish a platform fact a C01 row rests on:
   - (a) **Prefer the platform's own documentation and what a single ordinary session can observe about itself.** When a fact is established this way, the record states "verified by documentation" or "verified by self-observation", with the source and its date (Section 0.3 item 12).
   - (b) **A minimal, explicit, one-at-a-time self-check only where a fact genuinely cannot be established by (a).** It is recorded plainly as the system verifying its own safety property, made one target at a time; never a broad automated sweep, never an enumeration across the account.
   - (c) **A boundary covered by a documented platform guarantee is recorded as "guaranteed by platform (documented); not independently tested"**, with the source and date, rather than forced into an active trial for its own sake.

**3. Authority and scope (confirmed by CHK-C01-018).**
   - Re-framing *how* a platform fact is established is a method choice, within the builder's technical authority (working order section 2, the reasoning layer; plan Section 6.12 and the plan's own principles). It is not a plan change.
   - This record, on its own, **loosens no C01 acceptance condition**. The acceptance block of `plan/work/C01.md` and plan Section 9's C01 line are unchanged. Plan Section 8 item 14's "could not check" stays distinct from "met"; a documented guarantee recorded under 2(c) is not a positive observation and is never counted as meeting a condition that a row's success/fail text requires to be observed.
   - Therefore, where applying 2(a) or 2(c) to a **specific row** would replace an active positive or negative test that that row's success condition or fail path requires, that substitution is a **scope change**, not a method change. It does not happen here and does not happen silently: it is made per-row, when that row is worked, through a plan-change record `plan/decisions/PC-<nn>.md` (working order section 6; plan Section 14) with its own fresh-context checker, and an acceptance condition is never loosened after its result has been seen (ledger rule 3). The rows most exposed to this are the isolation rows (control surface across environments; environment token; N-109's credential check); each is handled under this rule when reached.
   - The ✘ negative-test conditions of C01's acceptance ("no paid feature"; "no probe session wrote a token or secret") are unaffected: they are satisfied by observation of what did not happen, which 2(b)'s minimal self-checks still produce.

It is put to him as D-018 on issue #6, recommended, with the note that terms and cost are unknown until he looks.
